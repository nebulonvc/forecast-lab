 Summary of Results - Current Population Estimates as of October 1, 2022

Population for Japan

[Total Population]

  • The total population was 124,947 thousand, a decrease of 556 thousand compared with the previous year. The rate of decrease was 0.44 percent. The total population decreased for the twelfth year in a row.
  • The male population was 60,758  thousand, a decrease of 261  thousand (0.43 percent), while the female population was 64,189  thousand, a decrease of 294   thousand (0.46 percent). 
  • The natural change of the male population was negative for the eighteenth year in a row, and that of the female population was negative for the fourteenth year in a row.
  • The migration change of the Japanese population was negative for the two years in a row, and that of the foreign population was positive for the first time in two years.

[Population by Age Group]

  • The population under 15 years  old was 14,503 thousand (11.6  percent of the total population).
  • The population aged 15 to 64 was 74,208 thousand (59.4 percent of the total population). 
  • The population aged 65 years old and over was 36,236 thousand (29.0 percent of the total population). 

Population by Prefecture

[Population]

  • The top five prefectures in population were Tokyo-to, Kanagawa-ken, Osaka-fu, Aichi-ken and Saitama-ken. These prefectures account for 37.5 percent of the total population.
  • Tokyo-to had the largest population in 2022 (11.2 percent of the total population). 

[Rates of Population Change]

  • The population increased in 1 prefecture. Tokyo-to had the highest rate of increase in population.
  • The population decreased in 46 prefectures. Akita-ken had the highest rate of decrease in population.

[Population by Age Group]

  • The percentage of the population under 15 years old was the highest in Okinawa-ken and lowest in Akita-ken.
  • The percentage of the population aged 15 to 64 was the highest in Tokyo-to and lowest in Akita-ken.
  • The percentage of the population aged 65 years old and over was the highest in Akita-ken and lowest in Tokyo-to, and it increased in 44 prefectures.
