U.S. Department of Commerce, National Telecommunications and Information Administration (NTIA), Falling Through the Net: Defining the Digital Divide, July 1999 (Part I: Household Access). Nationwide, PC ownership is now at 42.1%, up from 24.1% in 1994 and 36.6% in 1997 (an increase of 74.7% and 15.0%, respectively). The top tier ranges from Alaska's 62.4% to Wyoming's 46.1%. The middle grouping is bounded by Arizona (44.3%) and Pennsylvania (39.3%). The low tier includes principally southern states, ranging from Oklahoma (37.8%) to Mississippi (25.7%).
