WTO Global Value Chains Sectoral Profiles / Textiles and Clothing Industry (2024). Asia is the world's top producer of textiles and clothing, representing 70.6 per cent of world exports in 2022 - a share that has increased by 11.2 percentage points since 2017. China, the world's largest exporter of textiles and clothing products, had a large proportion of domestic content in its exports in 2022, estimated at 89.1 per cent. This was mainly due to the presence of domestic supply chains encompassing all production steps. Overall, China accounted for more than 40 per cent of the value added contained in global exports from this industry in 2022, underscoring the dominance of Chinese industries along the value chain.
