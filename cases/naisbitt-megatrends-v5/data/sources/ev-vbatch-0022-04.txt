2023 FDIC National Survey of Unbanked and Underbanked Households | FDIC.gov. In response to a statutory mandate, the survey has been conducted biennially since 2009, in partnership with the U.S. Census Bureau as a supplement to the Current Population Survey. The nationally representative survey is administered to approximately 30,000 U.S. households and yields findings that are representative for the 50 states and the District of Columbia. UNBANKED HOUSEHOLDS: In 2023, 4.2 percent of U.S. households—representing about 5.6 million households—were unbanked, meaning no one in the household had a checking or savings account at a bank or credit union. BANK ACCOUNT ACCESS: In 2023, almost half of banked households used mobile banking as their primary method of bank account access Over the past decade, use of mobile banking as the primary method of account access increased almost ninefold. Increase in use of mobile banking as the primary method of account access was widespread across household characteristics, including across all age groups. In contrast, use of bank tellers fell by more than half (and use of online banking declined by more than one-third).
