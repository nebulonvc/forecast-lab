Annual Summary for 2025 Exports, Imports, and Balance (exhibit 1) For 2025, the goods and services deficit was $901.5 billion, down $2.1 billion from $903.5 billion in 2024. Exports were $3,432.3 billion, up $199.8 billion from 2024. Imports were $4,333.8 billion, up $197.8 billion from 2024. The 2025 decrease in the goods and services deficit reflected an increase in the goods deficit of $25.5 billion, or 2.1 percent, to $1,240.9 billion and an increase in the services surplus of $27.6 billion, or 8.9 percent, to $339.5 billion.
