Boroughs of New York City (Wikipedia summary of 2020 US Census and 2024 BEA county GDP): 2020 US Census population and 2024 GDP by NYC borough: The Bronx 1,472,654 ($58.323B); Brooklyn 2,736,074 ($145.934B); Manhattan 1,694,251 ($1,006.673B); Queens 2,405,464 ($143.131B); Staten Island 495,747 ($23.779B); City of New York total 8,804,190 ($1,354.061B). Compared with 1980 Census baselines, all four outer boroughs expanded significantly by 2020 while Manhattan accounted for over $1 trillion (74.3%) of the city's 2024 GDP.
