The fortieth edition of the Global Financial Centres Index (GFCI 40) was published on 16 September 2026. GFCI 40 provides evaluations of future competitiveness and rankings for 117 financial centres around the world. GFCI 40 uses 39,531 assessments from 6,147 respondents. Leading Centres: New York leads the index, with London second. Hong Kong retains third position ahead of Singapore. Shanghai is up one place to fifth, with Tokyo up four places to sixth. North America: New York is the only North American centre in the world top 10, with San Francisco, Los Angeles, Chicago, Boston, and Washington DC all in the top 20.
