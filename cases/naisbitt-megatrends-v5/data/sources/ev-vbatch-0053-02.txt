As of 31 December 2024, WTO members referred 631 disputes to the Dispute Settlement Body. Not all of these disputes required formal rulings to resolve them. A mutually agreed solution consistent with the WTO agreements is always the preferred outcome, and consultations among disputing members within the framework of WTO dispute settlement can often be sufficient to resolve the matter in dispute.
