In 2024, Ballotpedia had tracked 336 legislative proposals concerning ballot measures , initiatives , veto referendums , referrals , local ballot measures , and recall elections in 41 states during 2024 legislative sessions. Forty-one (41) bills had been enacted into law. Four additional bills were vetoed. Of the 41 bills, five were proposed constitutional amendments passed by state legislatures in 2023 and 2024 legislative sessions required voter approval in 2024 to take effect. Four were defeated by voters and one was removed from the ballot by the state supreme court and thus were not enacted despite being passed by the states' legislatures.
