Where they are spoken 
English is the largest language in the world in terms of total 1st and 2nd language speakers. In terms of 1st language speakers only, Mandarin Chinese is the largest. 
English is spread out, while Mandarin is concentrated. 
English has more 2nf language speakers, and can be found in far more countries, particularly in Africa. Because Mandarin’s size is primarily due to 1st language speakers, it is concentrated in fewer countries – primarily in Asia – that have the most 1st language speakers. English is spoken in 188 countries, a stark difference to Mandarin’s 108.
