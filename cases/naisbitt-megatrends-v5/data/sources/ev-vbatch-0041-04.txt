2026 Impact Report: Powering America's Automotive Industry. Japan Automobile Manufacturers Association (JAMA) USA, published May 4, 2026. Japanese-brand automakers are truly powering America's automotive industry. Their record high investment stats – direct employment of over 108,000, the over $70 billion in cumulative manufacturing investment, the over $4.7 billion dedicated to research and development- and the fact that today, Japanese-brand automakers produce nearly one-third of all vehicles made in the U.S. tell a compelling and clear story. Japanese-brand automakers are committed to U.S. communities, consumers, and workers. For more than four decades, Japanese-brand automakers have played a significant role in shaping U.S. manufacturing and supporting millions of American jobs. In 2025, Japanese-brand automakers and their dealership networks supported approximately 2.34 million U.S. jobs.
