World Bank Open Data API (World, NE.TRD.GNFS.ZS, Trade % of GDP): Global trade as a share of GDP rose from 37.06% in 1982 to 38.10% in 1990, 50.12% in 2000, and 60.32% in 2008, before moderating to 56.19% in 2010 and 51.87% in 2020. Following the pandemic rebound, World trade (% of GDP) peaked at 62.15% in 2022, and stood at 58.07% in 2023 and 56.72% in 2024.
