Globalization, meaning the increased internationalization of markets, inputs and investment, has had a major impact on U.S. manufacturing. Since 1980, the U.S. trade balance in manufactured goods has gone from a surplus to a deficit of nearly $500 billion. The deficit is concentrated in consumer and automotive products, but even capital goods moved into deficit in 2003. While foreign outsourcing (also known as offshore outsourcing or offshoring) has become a major issue, the report notes evidence that nearly all major industrial countries, including China, have lost manufacturing jobs since 1995.
