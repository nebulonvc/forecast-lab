HJ 6: COAL SEVERANCE TAX
OVERVIEW
COAL SEVERANCE TAX RATE
The coal severance tax, provided for in 15-35-103, MCA, is imposed on each ton of coal
produced in the state. The tax is a percentage of the contract sales price and varies based on
the heating quality of the coal and how the coal is mined.

Production of 50,000 tons or less in a calendar year is not subject to the coal severance tax.
However, if production exceeds 50,000 tons, the severance tax is levied on all production in
excess of 20,000 tons.
