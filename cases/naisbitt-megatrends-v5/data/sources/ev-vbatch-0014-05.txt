AI | 2025 Stack Overflow Developer Survey. 3.1 Sentiment and usage: AI tools in the development process. 84% of respondents are using or planning to use AI tools in their development process, an increase over last year (76%). This year we can see 51% of professional developers use AI tools daily. Do you currently use AI tools in your development process? All Respondents: Yes, I use AI tools daily 47.1%; Yes, I use AI tools weekly 17.7%; Yes, I use AI tools monthly or infrequently 13.7%; No, but I plan to soon 5.3%; No, and I don't plan to 16.2%. The biggest single frustration, cited by 66% of developers, is dealing with "AI solutions that are almost right, but not quite," which often leads to the second-biggest frustration: "Debugging AI-generated code is more time-consuming" (45%).
