Statistical Abstract of the United States: 1995 (115th Edition), U.S. Bureau of the Census, Section 13 Labor Force, Employment, and Earnings. No. 649. Employed Civilians, by Occupation, Sex, Race, and Hispanic Origin: 1983 and 1994. [For civilian noninstitutional population 16 years old and over. Annual average of monthly figures. Based on Current Population Survey.] Total employed (1,000): 1983 = 100,834 (Female 43.7%, Black 9.3%, Hispanic 5.3%); 1994 = 123,060 (Female 46.0%, Black 10.4%, Hispanic 8.8%). Managerial and professional specialty: 1983 = 23,592; 1994 = 33,847. Executive, administrative, and managerial: 1983 = 10,772 (Female 32.4%, Black 4.7%, Hispanic 2.8%); 1994 = 16,312 (Female 43.0%, Black 6.8%, Hispanic 4.9%). Management-related occupations: 1983 = 2,966; 1994 = 4,269. Professional specialty: 1983 = 12,820; 1994 = 17,536.
