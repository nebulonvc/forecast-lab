NAICS Code Description 611710: Educational Support Services. This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing non-instructional services that support educational processes or systems. Illustrative Examples: Educational consultants Educational testing services Educational guidance counseling services Student exchange programs Educational testing evaluation services. Cross-References: Establishments primarily engaged in providing job training for the unemployed, underemployed, physically disabled, and persons who have a job market disadvantage because of lack of education or job skills are classified in Industry 624310, Vocational Rehabilitation Services.
