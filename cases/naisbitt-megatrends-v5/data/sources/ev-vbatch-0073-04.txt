Updated January 2026. In 2023, the most recent year for which data is available, 309 were reported as new ESOPs, adding 56,663 active participants. In total, there were 6,609 plans identified as ESOPs in the United States, holding total assets of over $2 trillion. The number of unique companies with an ESOP is 6,411 (5,993 private companies and 418 publicly traded companies). A company may sponsor multiple plans. ESOPs cover 15.1 million participants, of whom over 10.9 million are active participants—those currently employed and covered by an ESOP. ESOPs paid out over $166 billion dollars to participants in 2023. Total contributions to ESOP accounts were over $114 billion in 2023. These numbers are sourced from the NCEO's analysis of the Private Pension Plan (PPP) Research Files made available by the Department of Labor from data reported on the Form 5500.
