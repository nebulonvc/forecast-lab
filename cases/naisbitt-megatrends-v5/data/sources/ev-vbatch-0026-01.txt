As the American Society of Interior Designers celebrates its 50th anniversary this year, the industry finds itself at a pivotal moment — one defined by rapid innovation, evolving consumer values and a renewed emphasis on sustainability and well-being.

For five decades, ASID has championed the power of design to transform lives, shaping the industry through research, advocacy and professional development. This legacy continues with the release of ASID’s 2025 Trends Outlook Report, offering key insights into the shifting priorities that will define interior design in the year ahead.

9 Trends Shaping Interior Design in 2025

+ Wellness as a Design Imperative: Interior environments are increasingly tailored to enhance physical, mental and emotional well-being. From circadian lighting and air purification systems to biophilic elements that connect occupants with nature, design is playing a critical role in fostering healthier lifestyles. Multigenerational living and adaptable environments are also on the rise, ensuring longevity, comfort and inclusivity for all users.

+ Neuro-Inclusive and Adaptive Spaces: As awareness of neurodiversity grows, designers are embracing sensory-friendly layouts, adjustable lighting and movement-friendly furniture to create environments that cater to a wider range of cognitive and sensory needs. The emphasis is on flexibility, with spaces that can be easily modified to accommodate different users.

+ The Rise of Community-Centered Living: Consumers are gravitating toward shared spaces that encourage connection — whether through co-housing models, intergenerational living arrangements or communal workspaces. In a world where remote work and social isolation remain concerns, designers are prioritizing layouts that foster collaboration, engagement and a sense of belonging.

+ Technology-Driven Personalization: AI and smart home technology are revolutionizing the design process, offering hyper-personalized solutions that cater to individual lifestyles. From intuitive lighting and climate control systems to AI-driven customization in furniture and layout design, technological integration is redefining both functionality and user experience.

+ Artisan Craft and Cultural Heritage: The appreciation for craftsmanship is making a strong comeback, with designers incorporating handmade, locally sourced and culturally significant elements into their projects. This movement aligns with a broader shift toward authenticity and storytelling in design, where every piece has a narrative and a purpose.

These trends highlight the evolving role of interior designers — not just as creators of beautiful spaces but as problem-solvers, innovators and advocates for sustainable and inclusive environments. ASID’s 2025 Trends Outlook Report underscores the industry’s responsibility to anticipate and adapt to the changing needs of society, ensuring that design remains a force for positive impact.
