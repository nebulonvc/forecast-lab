Computer and Internet Use in the United States: 2021. American Community Survey Reports, By Daniela Mejia, ACS-56, June 2024, U.S. Census Bureau. HIGHLIGHTS FROM THE ACS: Among all households in 2021, 95 percent had at least one type of computer, and 90 percent had a broadband internet subscription. Smartphones were the most common computing device, as 90 percent of households had at least one. Eighty-one percent of households had a desktop or laptop computer, followed by 64 percent of households that owned a tablet. Urban residents were more likely than rural residents to use computing devices (96 percent of urban households compared to 93 percent of rural households).
