U.S. Department of Education, National Center for Education Statistics (NCES), Digest of Education Statistics 2022, Table 325.25: Degrees in business conferred by postsecondary institutions, by level of degree and sex of student: Selected academic years, 1955-56 through 2020-21 (prepared September 2022). Bachelor's degrees in business conferred to females in the 1960s: 1960-61: 3,736 (7.6% of 48,934); 1961-62: 3,870 (7.8%); 1962-63: 4,090 (7.9%); 1963-64: 4,457 (7.9%); 1964-65: 4,917 (8.2%); 1965-66: 5,263 (8.3% of 63,639); 1966-67: 5,828 (8.3%); 1967-68: 6,991 (8.7%); 1968-69: 8,332 (8.9%); 1969-70: 9,234 (8.7% of 105,580). The 1960s ten-year annual average was 5,672 female business bachelor's degrees. In 1989-90, females earned 116,284 bachelor's degrees in business (46.8 percent of the 248,568 total), compared with 3,736 in 1960-61, 5,263 in 1965-66, and 9,234 in 1969-70. In 1990-91, females earned 117,608 business bachelor's degrees (47.2 percent of 249,165). Thus 1989-90 female business bachelor's degrees were 12.6 times the 1969-70 peak, 20.5 times the 1960s annual average, and 31.1 times the 1960-61 figure.
