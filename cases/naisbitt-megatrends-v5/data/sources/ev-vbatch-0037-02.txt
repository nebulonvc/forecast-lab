Restructuring China's Industrial Sector: Productivity and Jobs in China (Robert H. McGuckin and Matthew Spiegelman, The Conference Board; Xu Jianyi, Liu Yaodong and Jiang Yuan, Department of Industry and Transport Statistics, National Bureau of Statistics of China, Working Paper Series Vol. 2004-13, July 2004). In the textile industry both the U.S. and China lost a substantial number of jobs between 1995 and 2002. (See Figure-7) But in apparel, the trend is dissimilar – the U.S. lost jobs while China gained them. Figure-7: China Loses Textile Jobs Much Faster Than the United States. China loses 1.7 million textile jobs. Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, BLS, and TCB analysis. Notes: China data are for large and medium firms only. U.S. data are for textile mills and textile product mills. Neither China nor U.S. data include apparel assembly. The database is at the individual firm level, assembled from the 1995 Chinese Industrial Census and annual surveys for 1996-2002.
