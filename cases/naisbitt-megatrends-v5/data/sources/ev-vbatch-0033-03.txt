Review of maritime transport 2025, Chapter II: World shipping fleet and services. Japan continues to lose market share to China and the Republic of Korea, while European shipyards contribute a smaller share, specializing in building cruise, niche or high-tech ships (Arias et al., 2020). Table II.6 Deliveries of different types of newbuilt vessels, 2024 (thousands of gross tons, share percentage): China 39,118,358 (54.6%), Republic of Korea 20,090,872 (28%), Japan 9,002,442 (12.6%), Viet Nam 720,884 (1%), Philippines 668,207 (0.9%), Europe 624,672 (0.9%), Rest of the world 1,465,548 (2%), Total 71,690,983 (100%). A total of 71.7 million gross tons was added to the active fleet in 2024 (table II.6). Chinese yards delivered 54.6 per cent of tonnage while those in Japan and the Republic of Korea delivered 12.6 and 28 per cent, respectively. Container ships dominated the deliveries landscape, accounting for 41.3 per cent of total gross tonnage delivered in 2024.
