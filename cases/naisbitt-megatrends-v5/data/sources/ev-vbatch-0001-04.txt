Florida Legislature, Office of Economic and Demographic Research (EDR), Florida Population by Age Group (Source: Florida Demographic Estimating Conference, March 2021 and University of Florida BEBR, Bulletin 190, June 2021): 1980 Census actual counts for Florida: Total population 9,746,961; ages 0-4: 570,247; ages 5-17: 1,789,522 (combined under 18: 2,359,769, or 24.21% of total); ages 18-24: 1,139,942; ages 25-44: 2,450,388; ages 45-64: 2,109,157; ages 65 and up: 1,687,705 (17.32% of total population, yielding an elderly-to-youth ratio of 71.5 seniors aged 65+ per 100 youths under 18). In the 1990 Census, Florida ages 65 and up reached 2,355,938 out of 12,938,071 (18.21%), and in the 2000 Census reached 2,807,650 out of 15,982,824 (17.57%).
