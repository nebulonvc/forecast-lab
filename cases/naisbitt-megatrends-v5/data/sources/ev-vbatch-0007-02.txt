Publications Output: U.S. Trends and International Comparisons (NSF / NCSES, Science and Engineering Indicators, NSB-2023-33, December 2023). Global publication output reached 3.3 million articles in 2022, based on data from the Scopus database of S&E publications. The regions, countries, or economies with the largest volume of S&E publications in 2022 were China, with 27% of global output, and the United States, with 14%. From 2012 to 2022, the global yearly publication total grew by 59%.
