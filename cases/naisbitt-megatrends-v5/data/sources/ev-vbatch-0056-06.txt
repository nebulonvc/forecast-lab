FDIC-Insured Institutions Reported Return on Assets of 1.28 Percent and Net Income of $84.8 Billion in Third Quarter 2026 November 24, 2026 Share on Facebook Share on X Share on X Follow the FDIC on LinkedIn Share through email Print WASHINGTON—The Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC) today released the results of its latest Quarterly Banking Profile , a comprehensive summary of financial results based on reports from 4,205 insured commercial banks and savings institutions. In third quarter 2026, FDIC-insured institutions reported a return on assets (ROA) ratio of 1.28 percent and aggregate net income of $84.8 billion, a decrease of $5.3 billion (5.9 percent) from the prior quarter, but an increase of $5.5 billion (7.0 percent) from the prior year. The banking industry continued to maintain strong capital and liquidity levels, which support lending and protect against potential losses. Other key findings of the FDIC’s Quarterly Banking Profile include: Net income among community banks remained relatively unchanged from the prior quarter. Industry net interest margin increased 7 basis points from the prior quarter to 3.39 percent. Domestic deposits grew 0.9 percent, the ninth consecutive quarterly increase. Loan growth was widespread, increasing 1.9 percent from the prior quarter and 7.5 percent from the prior year. Asset quality metrics remained stable, as the past-due and nonaccrual rates increased slightly and net charge-off rate declined slightly from the prior quarter. Unrealized losses on securities increased during the quarter but remain well below the prior peak in 2023. The Deposit Insurance Fund reserve ratio increased 2 basis points to 1.50 percent.
