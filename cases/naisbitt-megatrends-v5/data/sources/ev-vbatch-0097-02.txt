U.S. Census Bureau, Historical Households Tables, Table HH-1. Households by Type: 1940 to Present (Numbers in thousands, Current Population Survey Annual Social and Economic Supplement). Selected historical figures: In 1960, total households were 52,799 thousand, family households 44,905 thousand, married couples 39,254 thousand (74.3 percent), other family male householder 1,228 thousand, female householder 4,422 thousand, and nonfamily households 7,895 thousand. In 1970, total households were 63,401 thousand, married couples 44,728 thousand (70.5 percent). In 1982, total households were 83,527 thousand, married couples 49,630 thousand (59.4 percent). In 1990, total U.S. households numbered 93,347 thousand, of which family households were 66,090 thousand (70.8 percent) and married-couple households were 52,317 thousand (56.0 percent), other family male householder 2,884 thousand (3.1 percent), other family female householder 10,890 thousand (11.7 percent), and nonfamily households 27,257 thousand (29.2 percent; male householder 11,606 thousand, female householder 15,651 thousand). Thus all non-married-couple households combined totaled 41,030 thousand (44.0 percent) in 1990.
