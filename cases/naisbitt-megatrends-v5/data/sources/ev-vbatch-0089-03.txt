Intel Corporation Form 10-K for the Fiscal Year Ended December 31, 2005 (filed February 27, 2006, SEC EDGAR): Domestic wafer manufacturing was conducted within the U.S. at our facilities in New Mexico, Oregon, Arizona, Massachusetts, Colorado and California. Separately, regarding the Intel-Micron joint venture IM Flash Technologies (IMFT) formed in early 2006: Production will take place in manufacturing facilities located in Idaho, Virginia and Utah. As part of the initial capital contribution to IMFT, Intel paid $500 million in cash, issued $581 million in notes, and owes an additional $115 million in cash in exchange for a 49% interest. In exchange for a 51% interest, Micron contributed cash and assets.
