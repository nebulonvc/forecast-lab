Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States
Secure Investment. Strong America.
The Office of Investment Security supports the U.S. Department of the Treasury’s role as Chair of CFIUS to protect national security while maintaining the United States’ open investment environment.
The Office of Investment Security fulfills the U.S. Department of the Treasury’s role as chair of the Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States (CFIUS), an interagency committee authorized to review certain transactions involving foreign investment into United States businesses and certain transactions by foreign persons involving real estate in the United States, in order to determine the effect of such transactions on the national security of the United States.
America First Investment Policy
President Trump reaffirmed the United States’ commitment to maintaining the strong, open investment environment that benefits the American economy and people, while enhancing our ability to protect the United States from new and evolving threats that can accompany foreign investment.
