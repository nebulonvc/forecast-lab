Marysville Auto Plant
Driving Quality, Advancing the Future
Producing a Legacy through Excellence and Innovation
Honda’s commitment to innovation comes to life at the Marysville Auto Plant, where advanced manufacturing and a forward-thinking approach drive our success. With decades of expertise, our team embraces flexibility and precision to build high-quality vehicles while adapting to the future of mobility. As we evolve with new technologies, including our role in Honda’s EV Hub, we remain focused on excellence and continuous improvement.
