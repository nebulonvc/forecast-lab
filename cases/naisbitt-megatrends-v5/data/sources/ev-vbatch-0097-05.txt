Claudia Goldin, Lawrence F. Katz, and Ilyana Kuziemko, The Homecoming of American College Women: The Reversal of the College Gender Gap, NBER Working Paper 12139 (2006), https://doi.org/10.3386/w12139. Women are currently the majority of U.S. college students and of those receiving a bachelor's degree, but were 39 percent of undergraduates in 1960. We use three longitudinal data sets of high school graduates in 1957, 1972, and 1992 to understand the narrowing of the gender gap in college and its reversal. Women earned 45.1 percent of bachelor's degrees in business in 1984-5 and 50 percent by 2001-2, up from only 9.1 percent in 1970-1.
