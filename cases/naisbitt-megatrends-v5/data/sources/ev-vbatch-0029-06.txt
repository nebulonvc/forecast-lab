2026 Camping & Outdoor Hospitality Report KOA's 2026 Camping & Outdoor Hospitality Report reveals a fundamental shift in how outdoor hospitality is reshaping travel and wellness: over 52 million North American households camped in 2025, exceeding pre-pandemic participation levels. These campers are driving a $66 billion economic footprint while prioritizing wellness, connection, and meaningful experiences. The 12th annual report examines travel trends, camper demographics, and generational shifts in outdoor hospitality, featuring new insights from Leave No Trace on responsible outdoor recreation. The findings highlight key industry transformations while identifying strategic opportunities for operators and stakeholders positioning themselves at the forefront of outdoor hospitality's evolution.
