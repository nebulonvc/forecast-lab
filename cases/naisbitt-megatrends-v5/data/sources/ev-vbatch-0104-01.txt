WASHINGTON, DC – Latin music’s momentum in the US continues to grow, according to the Recording Industry Association of America ® (RIAA ). In a new report, RIAA notes 2025 was the first year that Latin’s annual US revenue reached $1 billion wholesale. Last year also marked the tenth consecutive year of growth for the US Latin market, which continued to outpace the overall market. Latin music now represents 8.8% of total US recorded music revenue, reflecting its expanding commercial and cultural impact. Rafael Fernandez Jr. , RIAA’s Senior Vice President of State Public Policy & Latin Music, said, “ Today’s report underscores Latin music’s strength. With rising global reach and fresh pathways connecting artists and fans, this sector just keeps delivering as labels work to grow the market with innovative new partnerships and opportunities. It’s great to see new generations discover and build on the sounds I grew up on in Miami, taking music to new places and breaking down walls between formats, services, genres and styles so more fans than ever can experience the lure of Latin music.” RIAA 2025 YEAR-END US LATIN MUSIC REVENUE REPORT is available in English + Spanish .
