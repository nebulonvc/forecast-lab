The medical technology industry (commonly referred to as medical devices) consists of articles, instruments, apparatuses, or machines that are used in the prevention, diagnosis or treatment of illness or disease, or for detecting, measuring, restoring, correcting, or modifying the structure or function of the body for some health purpose. Typically, the purpose of a medical device is not achieved by pharmacological, immunological, or metabolic means. The industry supports almost 2 million jobs in the United States directly and indirectly. Medical technology directly accounts for over 500,000 of these jobs. U.S. medical device companies are highly regarded globally for their innovative and high technology products. Compared to several other industries, the medical device industry invests a higher percentage of yearly revenues into product innovation, reflecting the competitive nature of the industry and constant innovation and improvement of existing technologies.
