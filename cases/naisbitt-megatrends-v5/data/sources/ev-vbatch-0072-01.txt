$12 Billion U.S. Self-Improvement Market Shifts To Hybrid Model and More Virtual Services August 14, 2025 Self-improvement Market Marketdata LLC, a leading independent market research publisher since 1979, has released the 15th edition of its best-selling report a 305-page report entitled: The U.S. Market For Self-Improvement Products & Services (15th edition) . This is the ONLY publicly available analysis of this business. The report traces the market from 2003-2028 Forecast, examining these markets: Personal Coaching, Motivational Speakers, Infomercials, Apps, Holistic Institutes, Self-Help Books & Audiobooks, Training Organizations, Public Seminars, and Weight Loss Programs. According to Research Director, John LaRosa: “Traditionally, the Baby Boomers have been the main consumers of self-improvement. They still are an important group, but the tide is shifting, as Millennials now number 75 million. Millennials, Gen X and Gen Z are the future of this market, but there are few experts now catering to them.” Major Findings: Market Value… Marketdata estimates that the self-improvement market in the U.S. was worth $12.0 billion in 2024, down from $13.1 billion in 2022. The main reason for the decline was the drastic decline in commercial weight loss program revenues, due to competition from the wildly popular GLP-1 drugs. The market is forecast to grow nearly 26% over the 4-year period 2024 to 2028. Motivational Speakers… Professional speakers are doing well, taking in $1.86 billion per year. For an elite group of 19 top speakers , 2024 revenues are estimated at $350-400 million. The “average” income has risen to $101,000, and fees are on the rise. Many older speakers are dying, are retiring, or are cutting back their road tours, shifting focus to online courses, coaching, consulting, book writing, and webinars. Self-improvement Books… A popular book is still the basic starting point and platform for motivational speakers and experts. Marketdata estimates that self-improvement book sales declined from $708 million in 2022 to $387 million in 2024. The main reason is that there have been no best-selling diet books in the past 3-4 years, usually a staple genre.
