John J. Woodcock III Lemon Law Records. 1982 - 2017 Collection Summary Creator: Connecticut. John J. Woodcock III Title: Lemon Law Records Dates: 1982-2017 Abstract: This collection primarily documents John J. Woodcock's introduction, implementation and improvement of Connecticut legislation created to protect consumers from poorly made automobiles. The records also document the impact of Connecticut's first in the nation law upon other states seeking similar redress for their citizens. Series I. Lemon Laws, 1982-1991 Series II. Other States' Lemon Laws and Implementation, 1981 - 1987 Series III. Federal Attempts At Pre-emption. The New Federalism, 1985 - 1988 Biographical Note John J. Woodcock III, a Connecticut State Representative, was the proponent of the first "Lemon Law" enacted in the United States. Governor William A. O'Neill signed the bill (PA 82-287) into law on June 4, 1982. The so-called Lemon Law II passed in May 1984 establishing an "independent arbitration procedure within the Department of Consumer Protection." President Ronald Reagan's policy of deregulation left consumers subject to fraud and other abuses by manufacturers.
