Established by New Mexicans in 1958 to manage excess revenues from oil and gas production, the SIC is currently one of the largest sovereign wealth funds in the United States and among the top 30 globally. The SIC’s assets under management have grown to more than $68 billion. A portion of investment funds are sent back to New Mexico, supporting the state’s operating budget, and ensuring essential public services and fund beneficiaries receive the financial resources they need to serve New Mexicans. In fiscal year 2025, we are returning $2.6 billion to the state, or 14% of the state’s budget.
