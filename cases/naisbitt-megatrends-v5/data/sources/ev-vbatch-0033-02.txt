December 2024 crude steel production and 2024 global crude steel production totals (24 January 2025, Brussels, Belgium). World crude steel production for the 71 countries reporting to the World Steel Association (worldsteel) was 144.5 million tonnes (Mt) in December 2024, a 5.6% increase compared to December 2023, and 1,839.4 Mt for Jan-Dec 2024 (-0.9%). The 71 countries included in this table accounted for approximately 98% of total world crude steel production in 2023. Table 2.
