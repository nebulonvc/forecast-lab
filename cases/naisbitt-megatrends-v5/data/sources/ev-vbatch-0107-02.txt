U.S. Census Bureau, Centers of Population (Last Revised November 16, 2021): The concept of the center of population as used by the U.S. Census Bureau is that of a balance point. The 2020 national mean center of population is 37.415725 N, 92.346525 W, the most western and southern mean center of population point in our nation's history. The 2020 center of population is 11.8 miles from the 2010 center and 885.9 miles from the 1790 center. Hartville, Missouri, the nearest incorporated municipality to the 2020 center of population, is a town of 594 people in Wright County, Missouri.
