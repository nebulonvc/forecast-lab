ICYMI: "Unionization Petitions Increased Dramatically Under" Biden-Harris Administration (The American Presidency Project, October 16, 2024). Yesterday, the National Labor Relations Board announced that the number of workers filing for union representation has doubled under the Biden-Harris Administration—the first administration in five decades to have an increase in union petitions. Communications Workers of America (CWA): "Today's National Labor Relations Board release of data on worker union election petitions confirmed that the increase in worker organizing that our union has seen since the start of the Biden-Harris administration is part of a national movement, reversing a decades-long decline."
