Zoe Caplan and Megan Rabe, U.S. Census Bureau, 2020 Census Briefs C2020BR-07, The Older Population: 2020 (May 2023): Between 2010 and 2020, the population 65 years and over saw the largest and fastest growth in any decade since 1880 to 1890, reaching 55.8 million or 16.8 percent of the total population in 2020. This increase was largely driven by the aging of the baby boomers (those born between 1946 and 1964), the first of whom turned 65 years old in 2011. Table 1a (Population 65 Years and Over: 2000, 2010, and 2020): In 2000, total U.S. population was 281,421,906 and population 65 and over was 34,991,753 (12.4 percent of U.S. total population); in 2010, total population was 308,745,538 and 65 and over was 40,267,984 (13.0 percent); in 2020, total population was 331,449,281 and 65 and over was 55,792,501 (16.8 percent).
