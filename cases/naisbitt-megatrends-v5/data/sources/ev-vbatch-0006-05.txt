6.3 billion people now online, yet digital divides persist. ITU’s Facts and Figures 2026 shows growth in global Internet use, while 2 billion remain unconnected. Geneva, 03 November 2026. An estimated 6.3 billion people, or 76 per cent of the world's population, are using the Internet in 2026, according to Facts and Figures 2026 , released today by the International Telecommunication Union (ITU). The figure is up from a revised estimate of 6.1 billion people, or 74 per cent, in 2025. Despite the continued growth in connectivity, 2 billion people remain offline. With Internet use now estimated to extend to more than three-quarters of global population, Facts and Figures 2026 shows continued progress toward universal connectivity. At the same time, the data reveal persistent disparities in who is connected. As example, Internet use stands at 93 per cent in high income countries, compared with just 32 per cent in low-income countries. Globally, 82 per cent of urban residents have access to the Internet, compared with 63 per cent in rural areas.
