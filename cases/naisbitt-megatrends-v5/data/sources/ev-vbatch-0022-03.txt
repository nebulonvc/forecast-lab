Direct Deposit Gains as Paper Paychecks Tumble, PayrollOrg Survey Finds | Nacha. Posted on November 09, 2026. As Direct Deposit continues to be the way most Americans receive their pay, the already low number of workers getting paper paychecks fell by nearly half this year, according to new research from PayrollOrg. Their 2026 Getting Paid in America Survey , conducted during September’s National Payroll Week and released in late October, showed that 95.8% of those asked said they use Direct Deposit, up from 92.7% last year. At the same time, just 1.8% reported being paid with a paper check. While it remains the No. 2 method on payday, it’s a more than 45% drop from 2025. Earlier this year, Nacha conducted its own research on how members of Generation Z—those born starting in 1997—are using ACH. Fully 90% receive Direct Deposits, and 83% make Direct Payments.
