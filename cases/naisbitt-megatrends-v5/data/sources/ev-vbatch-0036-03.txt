China has consistently topped the production list, followed by Taiwan, Japan, India, and the United States. After reaching a peak of 44 million bicycles in 1988 (about 40 percent of the world total), Chinese production fell sharply, to 30 million in 1990. Chinese exports have increased their share from 3 percent of the value of world trade in 1981 to 8 percent in 1989 (See Table 1.2). Exports have grown particularly to the United States, the world's largest importer of bicycles. In 1990, China accounted for 10 percent of the foreign-made bicycles shipped to the U.S., thus displacing Korea as the second largest source of imports (Table 1.3).
