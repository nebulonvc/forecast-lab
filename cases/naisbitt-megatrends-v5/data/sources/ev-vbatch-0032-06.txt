WORLD TELECOMMUNICATION DEVELOPMENT REPORT 1999 Mobile Cellular Executive summary October 1999 INTERNATIONAL TELECOMMUNICATION UNION. The global boom in mobile cellular communications has been truly astounding. At the end of 1998 there were more than 300 million subscribers around the world, up from just 11 million in 1990. By the end of this decade there will be more than half a billion mobile users. Mobile cellular already accounts for almost one-third of all telephone connections.
