Energy demand from AI - Energy and AI - IEA. The outlook for energy demand from data centres. Today, electricity consumption from data centres is estimated to amount to around 415 terawatt hours (TWh), or about 1.5% of global electricity consumption in 2024. It has grown at 12% per year over the last five years. The rise of AI is accelerating the deployment of high-performance accelerated servers, leading to greater power density in data centres. While the technology sector moves quickly and a data centre can be operational in two to three years, the broader energy system requires longer lead times to schedule and build infrastructure, which often requires extensive planning, long build times and high upfront investment.
