Updated: Sep 30, 2026. Across all 50 U.S. states from 1982 to 2002 in nominal dollars, all six named states ranked among the top 10 states in absolute personal income growth: NY #2 (+$473.41B, from $234.15B to $707.56B, +202.2%), IL #5 (+$285.47B, from $146.91B to $432.38B, +194.3%), PA #6 (+$255.30B, from $140.33B to $395.63B, +181.9%), NJ #7 (+$241.01B, from $104.98B to $345.99B, +229.6%), OH #8 (+$216.45B, from $122.01B to $338.46B, +177.4%), and MI #9 (+$201.67B, from $104.75B to $306.43B, +192.5%). The 1980-2000 nominal ranking yields the exact same top-10 membership for all six states (NY #2, IL #5, PA #6, NJ #7, OH #8, MI #9). Over the extended 1982-2025 nominal window, five of the six remain in the top 10 (NY #4 +$1596.14B, IL #5 +$857.74B, PA #6 +$843.96B, NJ #7 +$751.68B, OH #9 +$682.28B), while MI drops to #14 (+$576.30B).
