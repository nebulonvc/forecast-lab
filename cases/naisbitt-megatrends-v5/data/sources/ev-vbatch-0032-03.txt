SIPRI Fact Sheet April 2025 TRENDS IN WORLD MILITARY EXPENDITURE, 2024. At $997 billion in 2024, US military expenditure was 5.7 per cent higher than in 2023 and 19 per cent higher than in 2015. The USA remained by far the largest spender in the world, allocating 3.2 times more than the second largest spender, China. The USA accounted for 37 per cent of global military expenditure in 2024 and 66 per cent of spending by North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) members (see box 1).
