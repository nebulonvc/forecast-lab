NYC Open Data (New York City Department of City Planning, dataset xywu-7bv9) official decennial census counts (1980 to 2010): NYC Total rose from 7,071,639 in 1980 to 7,322,564 in 1990, 8,008,278 in 2000, and 8,242,624 in 2010.
