By John Rush Updated 29 October 2026 3 cited sources The headline US cinemas sold about 769 million tickets in 2025 , slightly up on 2024's 761 million but 37% below 2019's 1.23 billion [1] . Attendance peaked at 1.57 billion in 2002 and drifted down for the next 17 years, before the pandemic cut 2020 to 232 million [1] . The average ticket was $11.31 in 2025 , against $9.16 in 2019 and $5.39 in 2000 [1] . Cinema United's survey matches The Numbers almost exactly up to 2022, its last published year [2] . Box office held up better than attendance because prices kept rising. Per head, each ticket sold in 2025 brought in more than twice what one did in 2000. Some of that is inflation, and some is the shift towards premium formats and recliner seating.
