Life insurers are one of the largest sources of investment capital in the nation. Life insurance industry assets are invested from coast-to-coast , generating jobs and supporting schools, businesses, hospitals and infrastructure in all 50 states and Washington D.C. These investments generate stable, dependable returns for life insurers, allowing them to keep their promises to provide American families with guaranteed life insurance benefits, retirement income, paid family leave, disability income and more.
