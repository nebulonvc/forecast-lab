Internet | Our World in Data. Many of us cannot imagine our lives without the Internet — but the technology is still young. Only 63% of the world’s population was online in 2023. The Internet provides an almost endless list of services: it allows us to communicate and collaborate worldwide, send money internationally (including remittances ), learn and educate others, form cross-border social connections , share news, and many others. On this page, you can find all our data, visualizations, and writing relating to the Internet. Hannah Ritchie, Edouard Mathieu, Max Roser, and Esteban Ortiz-Ospina (2023) - Internet.
