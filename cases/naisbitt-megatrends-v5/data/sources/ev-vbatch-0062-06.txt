National Association of Manufacturers ( NAM ) is an advocacy group headquartered in Washington, D.C., with additional offices across the United States. It is the nation's largest manufacturing industrial trade association, representing 14,000 small and large manufacturing companies in every industrial sector and in all 50 states. [ 1 ] Jay Timmons has led the organization as President and CEO [ 2 ] since 2011.
