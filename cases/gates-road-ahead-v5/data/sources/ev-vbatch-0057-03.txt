You have probably taken a short look at our Terms and Conditions , but if not, please keep in mind: We are a German company and thereby have quite a bit of freedom concerning what art you are allowed to show.
Even so: ultimately it is you who is fully responsible for all legal implications of your uploaded artworks. Please ensure compliance with copyright laws and be mindful of explicit content such as pornography and violence when completing the artwork form.
Kindly mark any such content accordingly. For marking an artwork as explicit, please:
(1) - Go to the 'additional info' tab.
(2) - There, please activate the check-box 'explicit content'
If you are in doubt, please contact us or a lawyer.
