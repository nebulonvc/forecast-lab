Wikipedia: ISDN. Once the term "broadband" came to be associated with data rates incoming to the customer at 256 kbit/s or more, and alternatives like ADSL grew in popularity, the consumer market for BRI did not develop and is effectively discontinued. However, AT&T would still have been able to have installed an ISDN BRI line anywhere in most states a normal analog line could have been placed (especially the former SBC/SWB territory) until discontinuation in 2021. In 2013, Verizon announced it would no longer take orders for ISDN service in the Northeastern United States and Verizon's remaining ISDN services were discontinued and shut down throughout 2025 and 2026. In 2021, AT&T discontinued ISDN service, marking the final consumer-facing network service provider to discontinue ISDN for home or small business use in the US.
