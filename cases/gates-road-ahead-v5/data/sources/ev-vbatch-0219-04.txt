ASTA 2024 Fact Sheet - American Society of Travel Advisors (2024)
As travel becomes more complex, demand for travel advisors (formerly known as travel agents) continues to grow. Travel advisors do more than book trips; they provide expert consultation to craft personalized itineraries that cater to unique client needs and preferences. These trusted professionals manage every aspect of a journey – whether leisure or business travel – from pre-trip planning to support during travel and follow-up upon return.
$115 Billion in gross travel agency bookings in 2023 for the United States. (Phocuswright in partnership with ASTA)
32% Year-over-year increase in agency bookings from 2022 to 2023.
50% Of travelers say they are more likely to use a travel advisor today than they were in the past. (ASTA 2023 Q1 Consumer Research)
Travel advisors book 59% of cruise bookings, 75% of tour packages, and 40% of all air travel (735,000 air tickets daily totaling $95.3 billion in annual sales in 2023, Airlines Reporting Corporation / Phocuswright and Travel Weekly 2023 Agency Study).
