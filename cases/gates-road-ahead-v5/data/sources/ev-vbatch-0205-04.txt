You can use categories in Gmail to reduce clutter and focus on emails that matter to you. When you use the “Default” inbox type, Gmail automatically sorts your emails into these categories: Primary: Emails from people you know and messages that don’t appear in other tabs. Social: Messages from social networks and media-sharing sites. Promotions: Deals, offers, and other promotional emails. Updates: Automated confirmations, notifications, statements, and reminders that may not need immediate attention. Forums: Messages from online groups, discussion boards, and mailing lists. Choose which categories to show Important: You can only choose from the current categories. You can't create your own categories. On your computer, open Gmail . At the top right, click Settings . In the “Quick settings” menu, under “Inbox type,” select Default . Click Customize . Select one or more categories. At the bottom of the window, click Save . Tips: If you have more than 250,000 emails in your inbox, you can’t use the “Default” inbox type. To get below the limit, delete or archive your emails. To show starred messages in the “Primary” category, check the box next to “Include starred in Primary.” When you turn on notifications for new mail, you only get notifications about messages in the “Primary” category.
