New! Personalized AI Learning with Khanmigo Interests (Khan Academy Blog, published September 6, 2023, updated March 21, 2025). With the introduction of Interests, Khanmigo can now incorporate your passions, making each tutoring session tailored specifically to you. Through your chat history, Khanmigo identifies the topics you are most enthusiastic about and adjusts its teaching in a way that resonates with you. By saving your top interests from past conversations, Khanmigo enriches future interactions with topics that genuinely pique your curiosity. If you opt-in to the Interests feature, your interests will automatically be determined from your chats with Khanmigo. Khanmigo Interests is designed to enhance your learning journey while respecting your privacy. For parent accounts with children, parents can view their children’s Interests and chat history.
