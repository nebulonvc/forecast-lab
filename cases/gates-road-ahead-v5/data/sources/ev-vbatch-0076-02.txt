The share of U.S. adults with high-speed broadband service at home increased rapidly between 2000 and 2010. And that growth continues today: 78% of U.S. adults now say they subscribe to a broadband internet service at home. This analysis is based on findings from the National Public Opinion Reference Survey, a Center survey of 5,511 U.S. adults. The survey was conducted Jan. 30-June 18, 2026. Home broadband use (% of U.S. adults who say they subscribe to home broadband): 3/31/2000 1%, 3/1/2001 6%, 5/19/2002 11%, 12/14/2003 19%, 6/7/2005 33%, 3/28/2006 42%.
