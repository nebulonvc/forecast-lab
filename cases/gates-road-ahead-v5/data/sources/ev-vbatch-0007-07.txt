WhatsApp Messenger , commonly known simply as WhatsApp , is an American social media , instant messaging (IM), and Voice over IP (VoIP) service accessible via desktop and mobile app . Owned by Meta Platforms , the service allows users to send text messages , voice messages , and video messages, [ 15 ] make voice and video calls, and share images , documents , user locations , and other content. It became the world's most popular messaging application in 2015, with 900 million users, [ 14 ] [ 27 ] and had more than 2 billion active users worldwide in February 2020.
