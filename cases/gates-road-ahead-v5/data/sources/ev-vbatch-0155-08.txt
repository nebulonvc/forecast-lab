Securing supply chains through vertical integration by Carlos Cordon. Published March 28, 2024. The EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) took effect in January 2023, with EU Member States required to incorporate its provisions into law by July 2024. The CSRD significantly expands the range of activities that affected companies must report, demanding oversight of both direct and indirect business relationships across the entire value chain. In response to the reporting challenges and inefficiencies presented by traditional supply chains, some companies are taking control of various roles previously held by intermediaries. This includes establishing their own farms and directly employing people in certain roles. The aim is to drastically reduce the number of steps in the value chain, which in turn will help simplify Scope 3 disclosure. Tracking these emissions is close to impossible because many farmers are suppliers of suppliers in chains that can include 10-14 different intermediaries.
