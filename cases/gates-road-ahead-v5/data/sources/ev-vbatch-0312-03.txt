It appears that the following numbers of residential customers, at a minimum, are now subscribing to broadband: 350,000 from cable television companies and 25,000 from ADSL. This amounts to 375,000 customers, or a residential penetration of approximately .4%. 375,000 residential customers, or .4% residential penetration, at the end of the second calendar year of deployment is far more than the number of customers for the telephone, color television, or cellular telephone at comparable stages.
