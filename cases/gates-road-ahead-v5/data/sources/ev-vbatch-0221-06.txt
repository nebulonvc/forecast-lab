Online banks can offer higher annual percentage yields (APYs) than traditional banks because they have lower overhead. Without physical branches, they need fewer employees and can spend less on upkeep. Competition with other online banks to draw customers away from brick-and-mortar banks also incentivizes high rates. As a result, online banks dominate Bankrate's lists of the best bank products, including the best money market accounts and the best certificate of deposit rates . 
APY's aren't just determined by competition, though. When the Federal Reserve raises or lowers the federal funds rates, bank account yields often move in the same direction . That's because a higher federal funds rate makes it more expensive for banks themselves to borrow money, so they offer better yields to attract more deposits from customers. 
During its September meeting, the Federal Reserve raised rates for the first time in 2026. The federal funds rate is now 3.75%-4.00% after the Fed held rates steady in October. This bump may mean yields on deposit accounts start to inch back up, and the banks we've rounded up here are a great place to start to find great APYs regardless of the current environment.
