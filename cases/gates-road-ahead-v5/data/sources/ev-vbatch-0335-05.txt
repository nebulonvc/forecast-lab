U.S. Census Bureau, Department of Commerce, CB26-183, FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 19, 2026: QUARTERLY RETAIL E-COMMERCE SALES, 3rd QUARTER 2026.
The Census Bureau of the Department of Commerce announced today that the estimate of U.S. retail e-commerce sales for the third quarter of 2026, adjusted for seasonal variation, but not for price changes, was $347.3 billion, an increase of 1.6 percent (±0.4 percent) from the second quarter of 2026. Total retail sales for the third quarter of 2026 were estimated at $1,941.9 billion, an increase of 1.1 percent (±0.2 percent) from the second quarter of 2026. The third quarter 2026 e-commerce estimate increased 10.4 percent (±0.9 percent) from the third quarter of 2025 while total retail sales increased 5.6 percent (±0.5 percent) in the same period. E-commerce sales in the third quarter of 2026 accounted for 17.9 percent of total sales.
Official U.S. Census Bureau seasonally adjusted historical time series (tsadprectbl3.dat, e-commerce share of total retail sales): 4th quarter 1999: 0.6%; 4th quarter 2005: 2.6%; 4th quarter 2010: 4.6%; 4th quarter 2015: 7.4%; 4th quarter 2019: 10.9%; 1st quarter 2020: 11.7%; 2nd quarter 2020: 16.0% (up 33.1% QoQ and 53.4% YoY); 4th quarter 2024: 16.9%; 3rd quarter 2026(p): 17.9%.
