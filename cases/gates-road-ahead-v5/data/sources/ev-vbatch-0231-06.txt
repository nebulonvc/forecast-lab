Learning Mode Dashboard Key Findings The percentage of public schools offering full-time in-person instruction in December (99 percent) was not significantly different from November. The percentage of public schools offering remote learning (14 percent) in December was significantly lower than in any other month this school year. The percentage of public schools offering full-time in-person instruction in June (98 percent) was not significantly different from January (97), February (99), March (99), or May (99 percent) but was lower than 99 percent in April. In February, ninety-five percent of public schools returned from the winter break with no interruption to learning.
