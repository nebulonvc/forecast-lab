SWAA November 2026 Updates. 5 November 2026. Latest survey wave included: October 2026. About 27% of Paid Days in the US in October 2026 Were Work-From-Home Days. The Pandemic Permanently Increased WFH, Equivalent to Almost 40 Years of Pre-Pandemic Growth. In the Past Year: 12% of Full-Time Employees Were Fully Remote, 62% Were Full-Time on Site, and 26% Were in a Hybrid Arrangement. About Half of Full-Time Wage & Salary Employees with 4-Year College or Graduate Degrees WFH 1+ Days Per Week.
