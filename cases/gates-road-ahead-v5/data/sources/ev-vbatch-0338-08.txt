Optimizing, not abandoning: How streamers can earn a core spot in consumers’ entertainment portfolios | Deloitte Insights (October 15, 2026). Streaming is a mainstay in US households. More than 90% of consumers say their household pays for at least one SVOD service, and subscribing households average four services, according to the data. But the cost of staying subscribed continues to rise. Subscribing households surveyed report spending an average of $76 per month across services, a 9% year-over-year increase. Many leading SVOD services have increased their monthly subscription fees at least once in the previous 12 months, with an average monthly price increase of $1.20. Despite showing some price flexibility, 45% of consumers say they pay too much for the streaming services they use, and about 50% say they are actively looking for ways to lower their monthly streaming costs. Consumers also continue to look to cheaper, ad-supported tiers and bundles to manage their entertainment portfolios. Many subscribers (65%) already have an ad-supported tier of a paid SVOD service, a cost-saving trend that has remained stable year over year.
