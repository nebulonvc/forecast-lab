Why online booking is the new standard in dentistry 
04 October 2024 
Software of Excellence explains the benefits of using an online booking system in dental practices and why they are the new normal. 
In today’s digital age, convenience and efficiency are more important than ever. Patients no longer want to wait on hold or navigate through phone trees to schedule an appointment. They expect seamless, 24/7 access to services, dental care is no exception. If your practice doesn’t offer online booking, you’re not just missing out on a fancy ‘trending’ feature – you’re falling behind. 
Shift in patient expectations 
Patient expectations have evolved dramatically. Today’s patients are used to managing their lives online – from shopping and banking to booking travel, ordering food, and even tracking sleep cycles. The dental industry is no different. 
According to Software of Excellence’s recent white paper, over 72% of patients said their practice offered online booking, with over three quarters of this group (76.1%) rating the appointment booking process as four or five out of five. In practices where patients indicated no online booking, only 59% of patients rated the booking process as four or five out of five. 
If your practice doesn’t offer this convenience, you risk losing these patients to competitors who do. 
Online booking is setting the benchmark for modern practices 
Online booking is no longer a luxury or an add-on; it’s necessary. Here’s why: 
24/7 accessibility : patients can book appointments whenever, even outside of office hours. This convenience is crucial for busy individuals who may not have time to call during the day. Software of Excellence’s data show that 47% of bookings are made outside of business hours 
Improved patient experience : offering online booking enhances the patient experience by providing a seamless and user-friendly way to manage appointments. Happy patients are more likely to return and recommend your practice to others 
Increased efficiency : online booking reduces the administrative burden on your staff. Fewer phone calls mean your team can focus on providing exceptional care rather than managing schedules 
Reduced no-shows : automated reminders and easy rescheduling options help ensure patients keep their appointments, reducing the rate of no-shows and maximising your practice’s productivity. 
Addressing concerns – will my diary fill with less profitable appointments? 
One common concern heard across the industry is: ‘Won’t my diary fill with cheap checkups, leaving no time for more profitable treatments?’ 
It’s important to understand that this concern is largely unfounded. If you believe that online booking will flood your schedule with ‘cheap’ checkups, you are wrong. Here’s why: 
Appointment management : online booking platforms allow you to categorise and prioritise diverse types of appointments. You can set specific slots for high-value treatments, ensuring these are not overshadowed by routine checkups 
Pre-appointment screening : by gathering essential patient information through pre-appointment forms, you can better identify and prioritise potentially high-value appointments
