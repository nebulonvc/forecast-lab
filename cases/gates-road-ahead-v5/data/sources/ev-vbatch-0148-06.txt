Legal hold During litigation, you might need to preserve all data associated with a user (custodian) or a Team to use as evidence for the case. You can do this by placing either a user (user mailbox) or a Team on legal hold. For a team legal hold, the team's mailbox can be put on the following holds: In-Place Hold (a subset of the mailbox or site collection through targeted queries or filtered content is put on hold), or Litigation Hold (the entire mailbox or site collection is placed on hold).
