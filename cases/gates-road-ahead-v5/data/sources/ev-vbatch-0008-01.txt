Locate a device in Find My on iPhone - Apple Support. Tap Devices, then tap the name of the device you want to locate. If the device can be located: It appears on the map so you can see where it is. If the device can’t be located: You see “No location found” below the device’s name. Below Notifications, turn on Notify When Found. You receive a notification when it’s located. Important: Make sure you allow notifications for the Find My app. If the device is online: A sound starts after a short delay and gradually increases in volume, then plays for about two minutes. If applicable, a Find My [device] alert appears on the device’s screen. A confirmation email is also sent to your Apple Account email address.
