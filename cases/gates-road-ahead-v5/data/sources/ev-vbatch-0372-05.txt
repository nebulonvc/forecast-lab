Ballotpedia's real-time, comprehensive overview of statewide ballot measures in 2026, including qualified measures, election results, trends, and analyses.
In 2026, 164 statewide ballot measures were on the ballot across 40 states. Voters approved 102 measures (62%) and defeated 62 (38%).
On November 3, voters decided 145 of those measures, approving 94 (65%) and defeating 51 (35%). The remaining 19 were spread across earlier election dates.
The total is the highest in an even-numbered year since 2018 and a 17% increase from the 140 measures in 2022, the last midterm election year. From 2010 to 2024, an average of 161 statewide ballot measures appeared on the ballot in each even-numbered year, including an average of 53 citizen-initiated measures.
The states with the most ballot measures in 2026 are Louisiana (15), California (14), and Colorado (14). Colorado also has the most citizen-initiated ballot measures, with 13.
Some of the most common topics of 2026 are constitutional rights (14), bond issues (12), and public education governance (11).
