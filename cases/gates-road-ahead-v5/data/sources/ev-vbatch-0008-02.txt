Photographer Retrieves $25K Worth of Stolen Gear Thanks to AirTags, Films Encounter on Meta Ray-Bans | PetaPixel. A photographer had over $25,000 worth of camera gear stolen from him before quickly retrieving it thanks to a well-placed AirTag. Benj Haisch filmed his encounter with the thieves on his Ray-Ban Meta Smart Glasses after the bag was stolen while traveling in Copenhagen, Denmark. Inside the bag was a goldmine of camera gear: a Sony a9 III, a Hasselblad 907X with a CFV 100C medium format back on it, a Sony FE PZ 16-35mm f/4 G lens, a Hasselblad XCD 38mm f/2.5 V lens, a Leica Summilux-M 50mm f/1.4 ASPH lens. In total, the equipment was worth over $25,000. After realizing he no longer had his bag, Haisch quickly remembered he had an AirTag stashed on his a9.
