The next steps of the problem-solving process, brainstorming potential solutions and careful analysis of advantages and disadvantages, are well suited to a meeting (Algozzine et al., 2012 ; Perkins, 2009 ). A brainstorming session is generally enhanced by having multiple active participants and a leader who records and organizes ideas, reinforces solution generation from participants, and deters evaluative comments during the idea-generation process. Similarly, meetings can be a good option for conducting a comprehensive analysis of the advantages and disadvantages of the available ideas.
