Internet Access Services: Status as of June 30, 2024. Industry Analysis Division, Office of Economics and Analytics, May 2025. Total Connections: As shown in Figure 1, total connections increased by about 2.5% between June 2023 and June 2024 to 549 million. Mobile connections increased 2.5% year-over-year to 416 million in June 2024, while fixed connections grew to 133 million – up about 2.3% from June 2023. Fixed Connections by Technology: Beginning with data as of June 30, 2022, Form 477 classifies all fixed connections into six mutually exclusive technology categories: Copper Wire, Coaxial Cable (hybrid fiber-coaxial), Optical Carrier (fiber to the premises), Satellite, Fixed Wireless, Other. Figure 4 Fixed Connections by Technology as of June 30, 2024: Coaxial Cable 59.0%, Optical Carrier 24.5%, Copper Wire 8.0%, Fixed Wireless 6.8%, Satellite 1.7%. Note: Other is summarized with Copper Wire for confidentiality reasons. Source: FCC Form 477.
