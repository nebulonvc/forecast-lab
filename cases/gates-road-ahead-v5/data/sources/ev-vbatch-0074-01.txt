Deutsche Telekom's corporate structure evolved continually over two and a half decades. With its four-pillar strategy, Deutsche Telekom and its units T-Com (fixed-line network), T-Mobile (mobile communications), T-Online (internet), and T-Systems (solutions for corporate and system customers) developed products and services for their respective market segments. As the market evolved toward integrated solutions, with mobile and broadband communications increasingly based on internet technology, Deutsche Telekom dissolved its four-pillar structure and focused its corporate structure and strategy on offering its customers integrated solutions comprised of internet-based fixed-network communication, mobile communication, and media use. Deutsche Telekom is one of the world's leading integrated telecommunications companies, with some 178 million mobile communications customers, 28 million fixed-network lines, and 20 million broadband lines. The Deutsche Telekom brand plays an important role in the company's market positioning and customer loyalty.
