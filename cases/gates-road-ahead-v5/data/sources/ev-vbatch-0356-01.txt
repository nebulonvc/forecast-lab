Playing video games is widespread among teens. The vast majority of U.S. teens (85%) say they play them. Just 15% say they never do, according to the survey conducted Sept. 26-Oct. 23, 2023. Nearly all boys (97%) say they play video games, compared with about three-quarters of teen girls. About four-in-ten U.S. teens say they play video games daily, including 23% who do so several times a day.
