Shopify Inc. Form 10-K for the Fiscal Year Ended December 31, 2025 (filed with the U.S. SEC on February 11, 2026). As of December 31, 2025, we had millions of merchants from more than 175 countries using our platform, geographically dispersed as follows: 44% in the United States, 31% in Europe, the Middle East and Africa, 16% in Asia Pacific, Australia and China, 5% in Canada and 5% in Latin America. While most merchants subscribe to our Basic and Grow plans, the majority of our gross merchandise volume ("GMV") has been generated from merchants subscribing to our Shopify Plus plan and enterprise offerings.
