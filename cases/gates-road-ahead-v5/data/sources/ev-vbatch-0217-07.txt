Vanguard Personal Advisor Official Product Page. Vanguard Personal Advisor provides automated investment management with the support of an advisor when you need it. Personal Advisor's advanced technology breaks down your investing goals and maps your progress for you, helping you see where your money stands at any point—from anywhere—on your laptop, tablet, or phone. With Personal Advisor, help is a phone call away. When life happens—including all things retirement—one of our advisors can help you navigate complex financial situations such as changes in income, adding a partner or spouse to your advised account, and tax-loss harvesting. Enrollments in Personal Advisor require an aggregate $50,000 balance or greater in eligible Vanguard Brokerage Accounts.
