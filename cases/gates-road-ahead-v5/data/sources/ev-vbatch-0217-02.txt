NCOA LegalZoom Will Review 2026. LegalZoom users can create various estate planning documents, including a will, a financial power of attorney, a revocable living trust, a healthcare directive, a medical power of attorney, and related documents. Yes, a LegalZoom will or trust will hold up in court. They are legal documents compliant in all 50 states. It took our testers an average of 40 minutes to create a will on LegalZoom. LegalZoom costs $129 for a basic will, $149 for a premium will, $399 for a basic trust, and $549 for a premium trust. Additional forms or attorney access prices vary. But we do wish attorney access were included in the packages rather than available for an additional fee. These drawbacks aside, we think LegalZoom is a great option for people new to will-making who want a user-friendly, affordable option.
