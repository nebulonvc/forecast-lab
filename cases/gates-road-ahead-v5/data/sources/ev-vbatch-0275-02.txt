You can use Focus to temporarily silence all notifications, or allow only specific notifications—ones that apply to your task, for example—and let other people and apps know you’re busy. Tip: To quickly silence all notifications, open Control Center , tap Focus, then turn on Do Not Disturb. You can also use the Reduce Interruptions Focus, which uses Apple Intelligence to show you only the most important notifications. See Use the Reduce Interruptions Focus . Note: Apple Intelligence on iOS 27 or later is available on an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or iPhone 16 model or later. Apple Intelligence is not available in all languages or regions. Usage limits may apply.* Set up a Focus Go to the Settings app on your iPhone. Tap Focus, then tap a Focus—for example, Do Not Disturb, Personal, or Work. Do any of the following: Choose which people to allow notifications from during your Focus: Tap People. To learn more, see Allow or silence notifications from specific people during a Focus . Choose which apps to allow notifications from during your Focus: Tap Apps. To learn more, see Allow or silence notifications from specific apps during a Focus . Choose whether to show silenced notification on the Lock Screen, darken the Lock Screen when the focus is on, and more: Tap Options. Choose a customized Lock Screen or Home Screen page to switch to while the Focus is on to help limit distractions: Choose the Lock Screen or Home Screen. To learn more, see Link a Focus to your Lock Screen . Have the Focus turn on automatically at a set time, location, or while using a certain app: Tap Add Schedule. To learn more, see Schedule a Focus to turn on or off . Customize how your apps and device behave when the Focus is on: Tap Add Filter. To learn more, see Add Focus filters . Use Apple Intelligence to allow important notifications to interrupt you: Turn on Intelligent Breakthrough & Silencing. Any notifications specifically allowed or silenced will always be allowed or silenced. Add Focus filters When you set up a Focus, you can add app filters that determine what information apps will show during the Focus. For example, you can choose which mail account or which calendar to use during the Focus. Go to the Settings app on your iPhone. Tap Focus, then tap the Focus you want to add filters to. Scroll down to Focus filters, then tap Add Filter. Tap an app or system filter, then select the information from that app you want to use during the Focus. Tap Add to add the filter to the Focus. Note: Focus filters don’t get synced across devices when Share Across Devices is turned on. To turn it off, go to the Settings app , then tap Focus. Create a custom Focus If you want to focus on an activity that’s different from any of the provided Focus options, you can create a custom Focus. Go to the Settings on your iPhone.
