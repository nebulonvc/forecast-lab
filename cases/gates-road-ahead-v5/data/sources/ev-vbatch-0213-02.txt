PLA fielded the Public Library Technology Survey in fall 2023. In total, 1,511 libraries completed the survey, a response rate of 16.4%. The results presented below are weighted to account for differences between the responding libraries and the universe of all U.S. public libraries. In FY 2022, the most recent year for which PLS data is available, on average libraries had 31 public computers each and those computers were used an average of 9,230 times per year. 95% of public libraries offer some kind of formal or informal digital literacy training. 95% of libraries offer e-books and/or e-audiobooks and 58% offer streaming and downloadable media for patrons. While most libraries now offer electronic materials and nationwide electronic circulation has increased every year, managing and funding that content involves many complex local decisions. Asked about the factors that influence the ability to offer digital content, the top choice for all types of libraries (selected by 85.7% overall) was the subscription cost or payment terms. For libraries to provide digital access to books is much more costly than the print equivalents. About half of libraries overall (52.3%)—and 83.8% of city libraries—identify vendor licensing terms or restrictions as a primary factor.
