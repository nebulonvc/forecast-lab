How Many Small Businesses Don't Have a Website? 2026 Statistics & Data. Published April 14, 2026, Updated May 4, 2026. As of 2026, approximately 27% of small businesses in the United States still don't have a website. In developing countries like India, this number reaches 50–60%. Globally, an estimated 40% of small businesses operate without any website. Year-over-year decline in the United States (% Small businesses without a website): 2020 36%, 2022 31%, 2024 28%, 2025 27.5%, 2026 (estimated) 27%. While the curve is bending downwards, 27% of ~33 million US small businesses still equals roughly 8.9 million businesses without an independent website.
