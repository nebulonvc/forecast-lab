Higher broadband usage and faster growth are going hand in hand, according to the latest edition of the OpenVault Broadband Insights (OVBI) report. In “Power Users Pull Ahead,” the report on broadband activity in the third quarter of 2026, OpenVault analysis shows how the fastest-growing cohorts of subscribers are those in the Power User categories of 1 TB, 2 TB or 5 TB or more, as well as those who using 500 GB or even 1 TB of upstream data. Average total usage was 695.6 GB, up 8.6% over the 640.8 GB observed in 3Q25. Average total usage has grown nearly 5x over the past 10 years, based on 3Q 2016’s 141 GB figure. As with every edition of OVBI, the 3Q26 report is based on analysis of the data traffic generated by millions of subscribers and aggregated by OpenVault’s solutions.
