To gain perspective on BYOD trends and patterns, we conducted a survey where we discovered how many employees are using their own devices for work purposes, what they do with their devices, what they access, and more. Popularly cited research claims that 80% of employees use a personal device in some capacity, indicating that, at least on paper, BYOD usage is the norm for four out of five companies. But as we’ll discuss, BYOD employee stats like these can be misleading. With that said, let’s take a deep dive into some 2026 BYOD statistics to get a better sense of the details. Key BYOD Statistics (2026) Only 11% of workers actually bring their own personal device (computer or laptop) for work - not 80%. 27% of workers regularly use their smartphone for work, and an additional 27% use it occasionally. The top three uses for personal smartphone use for work include: checking email, messaging/chat, and two-factor authentication. Only 39% of employees said that their employer requires them to install cybersecurity or management software on the personal phones they use for work. Only 31% of employees said that their company has an official BYOD policy. 57% of employers provide no reimbursement or stipend for their employees’ use of a personal device at work. Only 11% Bring Their Own Device The general narrative these days is that “everyone brings their own device” to work — or at least the vast majority do. We just mentioned one stat that said 80% of workers use a personal device on some level. And there’s a similar stat that says 83% of companies have implemented some form of BYOD policy. This type of data implies that the majority of the workforce is implementing wide-scale BYOD adoption. But in reality, this isn’t the case, and only a small number of employees actually have to use their own device for workplace productivity. More specifically, our survey found that just 11% of employees aren’t given their own device (laptop or desktop) for workplace productivity. Conversely, general employee BYOD statistics found that 89% of organizations in 2026 provided their employees with a company-owned device. The point here is that simply having a BYOD policy in place doesn’t mean that everyone is using their own personal device for day-to-day work tasks. In fact, this directly refutes the “everyone brings their own device” implication that the 83% stat leans on. The bottom line is that nearly nine in 10 workers are handed a company machine.
