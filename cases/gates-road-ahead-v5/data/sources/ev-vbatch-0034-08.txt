A whiteboard (also known as marker board, dry-erase board, dry-wipe board, and pen-board) is a glossy, usually white surface for making non-permanent markings. Whiteboards are analogous to blackboards, but with a smoother surface allowing for rapid marking and erasing of markings on their surface. The popularity of whiteboards increased rapidly in the mid-1990s and they have become a fixture in many offices, meeting rooms, school classrooms, public events and other work environments. Dry erase whiteboards are one of the most used and popular types of whiteboards. They enable one to easily erase things after writing them, making them popular in classrooms and other educational settings where communication of information should be simple. Marker pens are also used to write on whiteboards.
