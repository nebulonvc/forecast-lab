Show Hide The American Hospital Association Information Technology Supplement tracks methods hospitals use to engage in interoperable exchange with other organizations across the healthcare delivery system. Since 2018, the proportion of hospitals that often use four prominent network-based methods of sending and receiving information has consistently increased, while the proportion of hospitals that often use mail or fax has fluctuated, trending downward between some years and stabilizing between others Percent of hospitals that often or sometimes send or receive summary of care records with sources outside their hospital system by method, 2018, 2021, 2023 and 2025.
