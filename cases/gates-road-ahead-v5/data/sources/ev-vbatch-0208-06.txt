About lossless audio in Apple Music. With an Apple Music subscription, you can turn on lossless audio across all of your devices and hear every preserved detail from the original recording. Streaming lossless audio over a cellular or Wi-Fi network consumes significantly more data. Downloading lossless audio uses significantly more space on your device. Higher resolutions use more data than lower ones. To get a lossless version of music that you already downloaded from Apple Music, delete the music, turn on lossless for your device, then redownload the music from the Apple Music catalog.
