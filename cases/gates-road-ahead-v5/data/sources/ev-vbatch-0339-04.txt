Today, the Association of American Publishers (AAP) released its StatShot report covering September 2026, reflecting reported revenue for Trade (Consumer Books), Education (combines PreK-12 Instructional Materials and Higher Education Course Materials), and Professional & Scholarly Publishing.

Total revenue across all categories for September 2026 was up 9.5%, coming in at $1.5 billion as compared to September 2025. Year-to-date, compared with 2025, revenues were up 6.4%, at $9.4 billion in 2026.

Year-to-date Trade revenues were up 7.0% to $7.4 billion. Hardback revenues were up 4.3% year-to-date, coming in at $2.6 billion; Paperbacks were up 12.2%, with $2.7 billion in revenue; Mass Market was down 54.5% to $32.4 million; and Special Bindings were up 15.2%, with $187.6 million in revenue.

eBook revenues were down 3.3% compared to 2025, totaling $768.7 million. Digital Audio revenue was up 12.9% to $911.6 million, while Physical Audio revenue was down 7.7% to $4.1 million.

During September 2026, revenues from Education Materials totaled $276.6 million, up 8.2% compared with September 2025. Year-to-date Education Materials revenues totaled $1.5 billion, up 2.9% compared to the first nine months of 2025.

AAP StatShot reports the monthly and yearly net revenue of publishing houses from U.S. sales to bookstores, wholesalers, direct to consumer, online retailers, and other channels. StatShot draws revenue data from more than 1,417 publishers, although participation may fluctuate slightly from report to report.
