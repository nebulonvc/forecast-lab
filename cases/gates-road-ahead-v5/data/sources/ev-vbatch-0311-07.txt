AT&T Inc. FY2022 Form 10-K (filed February 13, 2023): The number of stockholders of record as of December 31, 2022 and 2021 was 784,110 and 817,330. The number of stockholders of record as of February 8, 2023, was 781,511. We declared dividends on common stock, on a quarterly basis, totaling $1.11 per share in 2022 and $2.08 per share in 2021. Liquidity and Capital Resources covers payment of operating expenses, spectrum acquisitions, funding capital expenditures and vendor financing payments, repayment of short-term borrowings and long-term debt, and dividend payments to stockholders.
