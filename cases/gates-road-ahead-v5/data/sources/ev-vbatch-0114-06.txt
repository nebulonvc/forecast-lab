Americans' Use of Internet, Broadband in 2026 | Pew Research Center (Published October 19, 2026). This analysis is based on findings from the National Public Opinion Reference Survey, a Center survey of 5,511 U.S. adults. When the Center began asking Americans about their internet usage in early 2000, about half of all adults were already online. Today, 97% of U.S. adults say they use the internet. The share of U.S. adults with high-speed broadband service at home increased rapidly between 2000 and 2010. And that growth continues today: 78% of U.S. adults now say they subscribe to a broadband internet service at home. Today, 16% of U.S. adults are “smartphone-only” internet users, meaning they own a smartphone but do not subscribe to a home broadband service.
