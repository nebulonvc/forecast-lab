Meet the faces behind Evaneos
Hop on board and join our team!
 Founded in 2009, Evaneos is the world's first online platform that allows travelers the freedom to create tailor-made trips directly with a local travel agent in the destination.
Our team values
Passionate about travel
 For us traveling is life. It is not uncommon to have traveled to dozens of countries when working for Evaneos. The favorite trip? The next one on the list...
Entrepreneurs
 Entrepreneurial, creative, innovative - this is what describes us best. Thinking outside the box is the key to reinventing our profession every day.
International
 International is part of our DNA: multicultural and multilingual profiles, former Erasmus students or expats - all this forms part of our team.
