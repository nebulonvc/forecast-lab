November 24, 2026 AAP September 2026 StatShot Report: Publishing Revenue Up 9.5% in September, and 6.4% Year-To-Date Trade (Consumer Books) Revenues Up 10.0% for Month of September, and Up 7.0% Year-to-Date Today, the Association of American Publishers (AAP) released its StatShot report covering September 2026, reflecting reported revenue for Trade (Consumer Books), Education (combines PreK-12 Instructional Materials and Higher Education Course Materials), and Professional & Scholarly Publishing. Total revenue across all categories for September 2026 was up 9.5%, coming in at $1.5 billion as compared to September 2025. Year-to-date, compared with 2025, revenues were up 6.4%, at $9.4 billion in 2026. The full report, including monthly and year-to-date revenue by publishing category and format, is available for purchase on AAP’s website . Trade (Consumer Books) Revenues September Trade (Consumer Books) revenues were up 10.0% in September at $1.2 billion. In terms of physical formats during the month of September, in the Trade (Consumer Books) category, Hardback revenues were up 9.2%, coming in at $510.3 million; Paperbacks were up 15.0%, with $370.3 million in revenue; Mass Market was down 34.8% to $5.5 million; and Special Bindings were up 18.7%, with $35.0 million in revenue. eBook revenues were down 2.5% to $83.5 million for the month, and revenues from the Digital Audio format were up 15.1% for September, coming in at $105.3 million. Physical Audio revenues were up 25.1% to approximately $600 thousand. Year-to-date Year-to-date Trade revenues were up 7.0% to $7.4 billion. Hardback revenues were up 4.3% year-to-date, coming in at $2.6 billion; Paperbacks were up 12.2%, with $2.7 billion in revenue; Mass Market was down 54.5% to $32.4 million; and Special Bindings were up 15.2%, with $187.6 million in revenue. eBook revenues were down 3.3% compared to 2025, totaling $768.7 million. Digital Audio revenue was up 12.9% to $911.6 million, while Physical Audio revenue was down 7.7% to $4.1 million. Religious Presses Down 8.3% for September Religious Presses revenue was down 8.3% compared to the same month in 2025, coming in at $87.2 million. Hardback revenues were down 5.7% at $57.4 million; Paperback revenues were down 9.9% to $14.4 million; eBook revenues were down 12.4%, coming in at $3.6 million; and Digital Audio revenues were down 3.4%, at $3.6 million. On a year-to-date basis, Religious Presses revenue was up 0.6% during the first nine months of 2026 when compared with the same period in 2025, coming in at $645.7 million.
