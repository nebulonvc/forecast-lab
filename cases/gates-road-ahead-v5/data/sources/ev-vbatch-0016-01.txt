INTERNATIONAL ROADMAP FOR DEVICES AND SYSTEMS 2024 UPDATE: MORE MOORE (Copyright 2024 IEEE). 1.1. CURRENT STATE OF TECHNOLOGY: A major portion of semiconductor device production is devoted to digital logic that needs to support a technology platform for two device types: 1) high-performance logic, and 2) low-power/high-density logic. Key considerations for this technology platform are speed, power, density, cost, capacity, and time-to-market. The More Moore roadmap provides an enablement view for continued scaling of MOSFETs to maintain historical trends of improved device performance at reduced power and cost and at high volume. 2025 is the 100th anniversary of the concept of the Field Effect Transistor (FET), which was first captured as a patent filed by Julius Lilienfeld on October 22, 1925. While reaching the 100th anniversary of transistor, we have seen many critical enablers for the continuous enhancement of transistor on top of geometric scaling, such as strained silicon, high-h metal gate, raised source drain, multi-gate transistor such as finFET, and now evolving to gate-all-around devices.
