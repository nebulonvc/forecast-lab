You can block phone numbers, contacts, and emails on your device. You can also filter iMessages from unknown senders and report iMessages that look like spam or junk. There are a few ways that you can block phone numbers, contacts, and emails. From the Phone app, tap Recents, then tap the phone number or contact that you want to block or the Info button in Classic view. Scroll down, then tap Block Caller. From the Messages app, open the conversation, tap the contact at the top of the conversation. Scroll down, then tap Block Caller. From the Mail app, open the email that has the contact that you want to block. Tap the contact at the top and tap View Contact Card. Then tap Block this Contact. When you block a phone number or contact, they can still leave a voicemail, but you won't get a notification. Messages that are sent or received won't be delivered. Also, the contact won't get a notification that the call or message was blocked.
