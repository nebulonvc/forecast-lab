World Bank Open Data / ITU Indicator IT.NET.BBND.P2 (Fixed broadband subscriptions per 100 people, 2000-2024). South Korea (KOR) led global early household broadband adoption with 16.62 subscriptions per 100 people in 2001 (compared with USA 4.5, Japan 3.01, Germany 2.56, UK 0.56), reached >=20 per 100 in 2002 (three years ahead of any other OECD country), and reached 46.57 per 100 in 2023. Across developed countries in Western Europe, North America, Australia, New Zealand, and Japan, the year first reaching >=20 fixed broadband subscriptions per 100 people was: 2005 (Canada, Netherlands, Norway, Sweden, Switzerland), 2006 (Belgium, France, Japan, UK, USA), 2007 (Germany, New Zealand), 2008 (Australia, Austria), 2009 (Ireland, Italy, Spain), and 2010 (Greece, Portugal). The year first reaching >=30 per 100 was: 2006 (Netherlands), 2007 (Norway, Sweden, Switzerland), 2009 (Canada, France, Germany), 2010 (Belgium, UK), 2013 (USA), 2014 (New Zealand), 2015 (Greece, Japan, Portugal), 2016 (Australia, Spain), 2020 (Ireland, Italy), and 2024 (Austria). In 2023, fixed broadband subscriptions per 100 people stood at France 48.67, Switzerland 46.84, Norway 45.44, Germany 45.38, UK 41.44, Sweden 40.73, Japan 38.63, USA 38.09, New Zealand 37.85, Spain 37.21, Australia 36.63, Ireland 31.89, Italy 31.84, and Austria 29.37.
