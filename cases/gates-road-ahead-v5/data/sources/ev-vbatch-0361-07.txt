Find & control your Web & App Activity. Web & App Activity saves your activity from some Google services, like Google Assistant, Google TV, Google Store, Google for Developers, and the Google Help Center, in your Google Account. For example, Google Assistant might use your past searches and saved places to show you better recommendations, or return results that are relevant to a general location you’ve searched from. You can turn Web & App Activity off or delete past activity at any time. When Web & App Activity is on: You can check the box next to "Include Chrome history and activity from sites, apps, and devices that use Google services." You can check the box next to "Include voice and audio activity."
