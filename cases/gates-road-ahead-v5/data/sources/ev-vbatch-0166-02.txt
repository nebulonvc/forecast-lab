Home-Based Workers in the United States: 2010. Current Population Reports, P70-132, Issued October 2012, U.S. Census Bureau. The percentage of all workers who worked at least 1 day at home increased from 7.0 percent in 1997 to 9.5 percent in 2010, according to SIPP. During this same time period, the population working exclusively from home in SIPP increased from 4.8 percent of all workers to 6.6 percent. The population working both at home and at another location increased from 2.2 percent to 2.8 percent of all workers. The percentage of workers who worked the majority of the workweek at home increased from 3.6 percent to 4.3 percent of the population between 2005 and 2010, according to the ACS. Home-based work in computer, engineering, and science occupations increased by 69 percent between 2000 and 2010. Nearly half of home-based workers were self-employed.
