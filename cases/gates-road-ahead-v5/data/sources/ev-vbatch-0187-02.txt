8 ways Amazon is delivering orders faster, from drone delivery within hours to Same-Day Delivery of fresh groceries. Written by Anny Kim. Published: October 22, 2025. Updated: June 04, 2026. Amazon delivered at its fastest speeds ever for Prime members globally in 2025, with more than 13 billion items arriving the same or next day. Through a combination of technological innovation, strategic facility placement, and specialized delivery methods, the company is setting new standards for speed and convenience. Amazon is investing over $4 billion to triple the size of its delivery network by the end of 2026, with a special focus on small towns and rural communities in the U.S. The company is expanding Same-Day and Next-Day delivery to over 4,000 smaller cities and towns across the country by the end of 2025, transforming how rural customers shop online.
