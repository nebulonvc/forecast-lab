Augmented and Virtual Reality Headsets Market Insights: Q2 2026 
Updated: October 30, 2026 
Display Glasses Carry the Market as Headset Demand Cools 
Q2 2026 Performance: Glasses Offset a Headset Decline 
Worldwide AR/VR headset shipments grew 5.1% year over year in Q2 2026, according to IDC’s Worldwide Quarterly Augmented and Virtual Reality Headset Tracker. Shipments declined 20.1% from the previous quarter as headset volumes weakened. 
Extended reality glasses drove the growth, rising 79.8% year over year to capture 46.0% of the market. Mixed reality headsets still held the largest share at 49.3% but declined 22.3%. Meta raised prices on Quest 3 and Quest 3S in April to pass through higher memory chip costs. Virtual reality devices fell 37.6%, while augmented reality grew 14.1% from a small base. 
Regional results diverged. The PRC grew 85.8% and Japan grew 88.2%, while the US and Western Europe declined 24.1% and 25.2%, respectively. Rising US inflation, driven by higher gasoline prices tied to the war with Iran, added pressure on discretionary purchases of higher-priced devices. Vendor Landscape: Meta Leads as Glasses Makers Gain Ground 
Meta remained the market leader with 45.7% share, although its shipments declined 21.6% year over year. The company reported lower Quest sales offset by strong growth in AI glasses, which signals a shift in investment from headsets toward glasses. In mixed reality, Meta held 89.6% of the category, followed by ByteDance at 6.8% and Apple at 2.6%. 
RayNeo ranked second with 12.0% share after growing 83.3%, and it led extended reality glasses with 25.2% of the category. XREAL placed third at 8.4% despite a 14.5% decline. Viture grew 95.8% to reach 7.5% share and held 63.4% of augmented reality. Rokid posted a 592.2% increase to capture 6.6%.
