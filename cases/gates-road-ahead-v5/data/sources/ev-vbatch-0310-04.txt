AT&T Inc., Form 10-K for the Fiscal Year Ended December 31, 2025 (filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on February 9, 2026). Item 1 (Business - Business Wireline - Legacy and Other Transitional Services): We offer legacy voice and other transitional services comprised of copper-based voice and data, Virtual Private Networks (VPN), wholesale, outsourcing and IP sales. Historically, a majority of our Business Wireline service revenues came from legacy copper-based voice and data and traditional products; however, over recent years those services have been declining due to secular pressures. Item 1 (Major Classes of Service - Percentage of Total Consolidated Operating Revenues for 2025, 2024, and 2023): Communications Segment Wireless service accounted for 54% in 2025 (53% in 2024, 52% in 2023); Fiber and advanced connectivity accounted for 13% in 2025 (12% in 2024, 11% in 2023); Legacy and other transitional accounted for 8% in 2025 (10% in 2024, 13% in 2023); Equipment accounted for 18% in 2025 (17% in 2024, 17% in 2023). Consolidated Statements of Income (SEC XBRL 10-K): Total consolidated operating revenues were $125,648 million in 2025 compared to $122,336 million in 2024.
