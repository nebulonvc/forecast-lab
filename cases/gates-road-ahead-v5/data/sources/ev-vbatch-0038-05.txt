The Global Findex Database 2025 - World Bank.
In 2024, 40% of adults in developing economies saved in a financial account in 2024—a 16-percentage-point increase since 2021 and the fastest rise in more than a decade. 84% of adults in LMICs own a mobile phone and 3 billion have smartphones. Access and use of financial services has increased worldwide. 79% of adults globally now have an account. Learn how mobile money and digitally enabled accounts are transforming financial behavior.
The Global Findex Database is the world's only demand-side survey on financial inclusion and a leading source of data on how adults around the world access and use financial services. The Global Findex 2025 introduces the Digital Connectivity Tracker, a new component that measures access to and use of mobile technology.
