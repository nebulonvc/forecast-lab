Para. 57: Two of the largest MVNOs are cable operators, Charter (Spectrum Mobile) with 7.8 million subscribers, and Comcast (Xfinity Mobile) with approximately 6.6 million subscribers. Altice launched its Optimum Mobile service in September 2019 and had 322,000 subscribers by the end of 2023, and Cox launched its Cox Mobile service on Verizon's network in 2023. Para. 156 Fixed Voice: The December 2023 Form 477 data show that there are approximately 20.6 million end-user switched-access lines, including approximately 8.5 million residential lines. In addition, there are approximately 64.2 million interconnected VoIP subscriptions, including approximately 24.1 million residential subscriptions. As of December 2023, residential fixed voice connections were approximately 26% switched-access and approximately 74% interconnected VoIP. The number of fixed retail switched-access lines declined over the past three years at a compound annual rate of 15.7%, while interconnected VoIP subscriptions decreased at a compound annual growth rate of 1.6%. Para. 204 & Fig. II.E.1: Traditional MVPD subscribership has been declining since 2012, when it reached 101.6 million subscribers. Collectively, the number of traditional MVPD subscribers fell from 61.9 million at the end of 2022 to 54.1 million at the end of 2023. Telephone company MVPD subscribers fell from 5.9 million in 2022 to 5.1 million in 2023 (-13.5%). Fig. II.E.3: Verizon Fios (Telephone) had 3.0 million video subscribers in 2023 (5.6% market share of traditional MVPDs). Para. 207: DIRECTV no longer accepts new customers for its legacy AT&T U-verse video service and Frontier stopped promotions for Frontier TV.
