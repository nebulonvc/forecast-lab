All-Round Compatibility
Supports HomeKit Secure Video, Amazon Alexa, and Google Home , and makes the compatible child devices support HomeKit, Alexa, Google Assistant, and more.
Local AI-Powered Recognition
Thanks to a powerful NPU-equipped processor, the Camera Hub G3 is capable of recognizing faces, gestures, and pets locally, that can be used in notifications and home automation.
2K 1296p Resolution*
The Camera Hub G3 incorporates a high-resolution 2304 × 1296 pixel sensor that results in crystal-clear image quality full of details.
Type-C Port with Video Out
The Camera Hub G3 uses the most modern and universal connector that supports low-latency, high-definition video output via UVC protocol.
Built-in Zigbee 3.0 Hub
The Camera Hub G3 is not only a camera, but also with an embedded smart home hub. It can connect up to 128 Aqara devices and enjoy home automation, remote control, and more.**
Local Infrared Control
The G3 has a built-in IR controller that can make your existing infrared-compatible devices smart and support local automations. Moreover, all the IR automations are local and will run even if there is no internet connection.
360° Viewing Angle
110° wide-angle lens coupled with the Pan and Tilt motors provides up to 360° rotation angle, supports automatic cruising, and face and pet tracking.
Dual-Band 2.4 / 5 GHz Wi-Fi
No more worrying about network type compatibility: the Camera Hub G3 supports both of the most popular Wi-Fi frequencies, and uses them to connect your Aqara child devices to the network.
* In HomeKit Secure Video mode, the camera records in Full HD 1080p and Pan/Tilt controls are not available due to the current limitations of HomeKit technology. ** Zigbee routers/repeaters such as Smart Wall Switch (with Neutral), Smart Plug, etc. are required.

The Camera Hub G3 is compatible with a wide range of smart home ecosystems and voice assistants.
Apple HomeKit, Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, IFTTT, Yandex Alice, VK Marusya are supported.
