Federal Communications Commission, Wireline Competition Bureau, Trends in Telephone Service (September 2010). Chapter 8 (Local Telephone Competition): The Telecommunications Act of 1996 (1996 Act) contemplated three vehicles for competitors to enter local telephone service markets. Since about 2002, cable companies and others have begun offering retail interconnected Voice over Internet Protocol (interconnected VoIP) service, which enables voice communications over a broadband connection and allows users both to receive calls from, and place calls to, the public switched telephone network, like traditional phone service. The service represents a rapidly growing part of the U.S. voice services market. Table 8.1 (End-User Switched Access Lines and VoIP Subscriptions, in thousands): ILEC lines declined from 181,203 in Dec 1999 to 118,503 in Dec 2008, while Non-ILEC lines and VoIP subscriptions rose from 8,194 (4.3% share) in Dec 1999 to 43,726 (27.0% share) in Dec 2008. Table 13.4 (Average Revenue per Minute for Interstate and International Calls): In 1995, average revenue per minute for interstate and international calls was $0.17 ($0.24 restated in 2008 dollars), international calls averaged $0.91 per minute, and interstate calls averaged $0.12 ($0.07 net of access and universal service cost); by 2007, interstate and international average revenue per minute dropped to $0.08 ($0.08 in 2008 dollars), international calls dropped to $0.09 per minute, and interstate calls dropped to $0.07 ($0.04 net of access and universal service cost). Table 15.1 (Telecommunications Industry Revenues, in millions): End User Toll Service revenues fell from $93,311 in 1999 to $39,259 in 2009 (preliminary), while End User Local Service revenues fell from $88,712 in 2002 to $70,142 in 2009, and End User Wireless Service revenues rose from $43,843 in 1999 to $115,505 in 2009. Table 15.4 (Gross Revenues Reported by Type of Carrier, in millions): Interexchange Carriers (IXCs) fell from $87,570 in 1999 to $37,358 in 2008; Incumbent Local Exchange Carriers (ILECs) fell from a peak of $117,885 in 2001 to $89,732 in 2008 (vs. $108,234 in 1998); Competitors of ILECs rose from $4,034 in 1998 to $26,440 in 2008; Wireless Service Providers rose from $37,032 in 1998 to $128,314 in 2008; Total Telecommunications Revenues rose from $246,392 in 1998 to $297,365 in 2008.
