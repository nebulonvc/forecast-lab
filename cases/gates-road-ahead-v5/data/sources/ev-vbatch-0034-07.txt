According to our latest research, the global whiteboard market size reached USD 5.4 billion in 2024 , with a compound annual growth rate ( CAGR ) of 6.7% from 2025 to 2033. The whiteboard market is segmented by product type into interactive whiteboards, traditional whiteboards, portable whiteboards, and others. Interactive whiteboards have gained significant traction due to their ability to transform conventional teaching and meeting environments into interactive, multimedia-rich experiences. Traditional whiteboards, while facing competition from digital counterparts, remain a staple in many classrooms, offices, and training centers worldwide. Their affordability, ease of use, and low maintenance requirements make them an attractive option for budget-conscious organizations and regions with limited access to advanced technologies. Despite the numerous growth opportunities, the whiteboard market faces certain restraints that could hinder its expansion. One of the primary challenges is the high cost of advanced interactive whiteboards, which may limit adoption in price-sensitive markets and among small to medium-sized enterprises. North America holds a substantial share of the global whiteboard market, with revenues reaching USD 1.5 billion in 2024 .
