The Use and Cost of Public Library Materials: Trends Before the COVID-19 Pandemic (JANUARY 2021). Between FY 2014 and FY 2018, the percentage of libraries offering electronic collection materials increased from 80 percent to 90 percent; median per person spending on physical materials decreased by 6 percent, while median per person spending on electronic materials increased by 31 percent; and median cost for physical items circulated increased by 11 percent, while median cost for e-circulation decreased by 26 percent. Table A3 (Median Collections Expenditures per Person by Material Type, FY 2018 and FY 2014): Overall FY 2018 median total $3.93, median physical $3.39, median electronic $0.35 (ratio of median electronic to median physical 0.1), compared with FY 2014 median total $4.08, median physical $3.59, median electronic $0.27 (percent change: total -3.6%, physical -5.6%, electronic +30.7%). In City libraries in FY 2018, median physical was $2.87 and median electronic was $1.02 (ratio 0.4). A total of 9,046 active public libraries in the United States responded to the PLS, for a response rate of 97.8 percent.
