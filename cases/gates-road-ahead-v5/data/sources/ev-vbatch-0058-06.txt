How Nest thermostats learn. To learn, your thermostat needs to have Auto-Schedule turned on. Tip: Auto-Schedule learning is on by default, but it's just one option for scheduling temperatures on your Nest thermostat. Your thermostat can start learning your temperature preferences from the day you start using it. Use the app or your thermostat to select a desired temperature. Your thermostat will remember your choice and add it to the schedule it’s building for you. On day 2, your thermostat will continue learning a personalized schedule using any temperatures you’ve set. Your thermostat will also try to learn when you’re home or away by how often you adjust the temperature or walk in front of your thermostat.
