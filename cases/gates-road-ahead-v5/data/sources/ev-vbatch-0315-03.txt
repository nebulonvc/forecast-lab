Following this meeting, DOE suspended the issuance of a proposed rule for an energy conservation standard or test procedure until after October 1, 2012 to allow industry representatives and energy efficiency advocates time to negotiate a non-regulatory agreement to improve the energy efficiency of set-top boxes. During this time, DOE continued testing and evaluating the energy efficiency of set-top boxes in support of developing a DOE test procedure.
