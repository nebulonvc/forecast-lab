Wi-Fi®’s inherent strengths enable today’s networks
Network operators around the world rely on Wi-Fi to meet user demands for faster speeds and heightened reliability. In addition to delivering these enhanced experiences, Wi-Fi allows operators to improve network coverage, manage traffic through data offload, increase network density, and provide security and privacy protections that have become a necessity as our digital world continues to evolve. Wi-Fi CERTIFIED® supports network operators by ensuring that devices meet industry standards for performance, security, and interoperability, enhancing user experience and network reliability.
