Short-stay accommodation offered via online platforms - Statistics Explained - Eurostat (Data extracted in June 2026)
In 2025, 952 million nights were booked via Airbnb, Booking and Expedia - an increase of 11.4% compared with 2024.
Paris remained the top city destination in 2025, with 26.3 million guest nights booked through these online platforms.
In 2025, guests booked 952 million nights through the platforms - up from 854 million in 2024, 719 million in 2023, 597 million in 2022 and 364 million in 2021. This marks an 11.4% increase over 2024 and reflects continued robust momentum (see Figure 1). Guest nights now stand far above the 512 million recorded in 2019.
Another key metric is the number of stays - that is, individual bookings, regardless of length and number of guests. In 2025, the 951.6 million guest nights recorded in the EU corresponded to 85.4 million stays (see Table 1). On average, that is 162 stays booked every minute, or 234 thousand each day.
