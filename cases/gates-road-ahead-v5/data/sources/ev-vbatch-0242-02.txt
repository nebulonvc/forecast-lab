What parents say about school communication Retention 63% say communication quality influenced their decision to stay at their school What parents want 64% say updates about their own child’s academic progress make them feel informed Attendance 24% rarely or never hear from the school about their child’s overall attendance Three key findings Communication quality is a driver of retention, enrollment, and advocacy. Communication quality influenced 63% of parents’ decision to stay at a school, 56% to recommend one, and 50% to enroll. Personalization is essential to engagement. 85% of parents trust school communication and 81% find it relevant, but only 68% say it feels personalized. Families have gaps in visibility into attendance information. 82% know their school’s attendance policy, yet nearly one in four rarely or never hear about their child’s overall attendance.
