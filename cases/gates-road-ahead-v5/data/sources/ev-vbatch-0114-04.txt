How much does Substack cost? - Substack, Inc. Publishing on Substack is free, no matter how many subscribers you have. If you turn on paid subscriptions, Substack keeps 10% of subscription revenue, and Stripe charges its own payment processing fees. Readers pay the monthly, annual, or founding member price the writer sets. Substack doesn't add a fee to web payments. You may pay more in two cases: some writers add sales tax or VAT on top of their price; subscriptions bought in the Substack iOS app may cost more to cover Apple fees. Fees include 10% of each subscription payment to Substack, Stripe payment processing fees (2.9% + zsh.30 per transaction fee for US cards) and Stripe Billing fee for recurring subscriptions (0.7% for recurring payments as of July 2024).
