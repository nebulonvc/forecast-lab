2022 Survey and Diary of Consumer Payment Choice: Summary Results (2023 No. 23-3), Kevin Foster, Claire Greene, and Joanna Stavins, Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta. This version: June 13, 2023. In 2022, bill payments accounted for 21 percent of all payments by number and 60 percent by value. Electronic payment methods and debit cards are used most for bill pay: 24 percent of bills by number were paid by BANP, 25 percent by OBBP, and 17 percent by debit cards, totaling two-thirds of all bill payments for the three methods (table 11 and figure 8). Thirty percent of bills were paid with cards (credit, debit, or prepaid). Checks were 11 percent of bill payments. In contrast to purchases, 85 percent of bill payments by both number and value were made remotely, either online or by mail (table 12). More than half of remote bill payments were made electronically, by BANP or OBBP, representing 70 percent of remote bill pay value. Mobile payments: About two-thirds of consumers made at least one payment using a phone or tablet in the 12 months ending in October 2022, in line with the share in 2021 (figure 7). Of the 9.9 mobile payments reported in 2022 on average, 7.1 were for purchases, 2.2 for bills, and 0.6 to pay another person (figure 8).
