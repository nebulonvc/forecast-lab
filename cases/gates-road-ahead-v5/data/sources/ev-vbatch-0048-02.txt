Pen interactions and Windows Ink in Windows apps - Microsoft Learn. Optimize your Windows app for pen input to provide both standard pointer device functionality and the best Windows Ink experience for your users. The Windows Ink platform, together with a pen device, provides a natural way to create digital handwritten notes, drawings, and annotations. The platform supports capturing digitizer input as ink data, generating ink data, managing ink data, rendering ink data as ink strokes on the output device, and converting ink to text through handwriting recognition. In addition to capturing the basic position and movement of the pen as the user writes or draws, your app can also track and collect the varying amounts of pressure used throughout a stroke.
