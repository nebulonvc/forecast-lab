AllAdvantage was an Internet advertising company that positioned itself as the world’s first " infomediary " by paying its users/members a portion of the advertising revenue generated by their online viewing habits. [ 1 ] It became most well known for its slogan "Get Paid to Surf the Web," a phrase that has since become synonymous with a wide array of online ad revenue sharing systems (see, e.g., paid to surf ). History [ edit ] AllAdvantage was launched on March 31, 1999, by Jim Jorgensen , Johannes Pohle, Carl Anderson, and Oliver Brock. [ 2 ] During its nearly 2 years of operation, it raised nearly $200 Million in venture capital and grew to more than 10 million members in its first 18 months of operation.
