We offer Cooking (our recipes product) and Games (our puzzle games product), which are available on mobile applications and websites, and Wirecutter (our product review and recommendation offering). Our Wirecutter product’s affiliate referrals (which generate revenue by offering direct links to merchants in exchange for a portion of the sale price upon completion of a transaction) contribute to Other revenues. Other revenues primarily consist of revenues from Wirecutter affiliate referrals, licensing, commercial printing, the leasing of floors in our Company Headquarters, our live events business, retail commerce, books, television and film and our student subscription sponsorship program.
