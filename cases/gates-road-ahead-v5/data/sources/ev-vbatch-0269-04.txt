Any subscription ski magazines you recommend?
Not really any ski magazines left in print.
I'm a paid subscriber to Stuart Winchester's Storm Skiing Journal . He writes articles and does podcasts. There is some free content. I'm more interested in the industry in general, resort news, as opposed to gear.
I think Ski Canada is the only mag still in print.
Ski Journal and Mountain Gazette are both wonderful, but it's true that they're not ski magazines with like, ski reviews and ads and stuff.
The Ski Journal is the true voice of skiing, a complete catalog of ski culture. An independent, quarterly, coffee-table journal created in the mountains, from Jackson Hole to Mt Baker, by and for skiers.
Mountain Gazette - When in Doubt, Go Higher
Founded in 1966, Mountain Gazette is the original outdoor magazine publishing long form journalism and stunning photography.
