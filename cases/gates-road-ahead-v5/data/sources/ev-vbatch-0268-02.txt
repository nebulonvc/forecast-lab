The Pennsylvania Lottery was established by law in 1971 to generate funding for programs that benefit older Pennsylvanians. 
Since the very first ticket was sold in 1972, the Pennsylvania Lottery has contributed more than $38.2 billion to benefit programs that include free and reduced-fare transportation services; the low-cost prescription programs PACE and PACENET; care services; and local services provided by the 52 Area Agencies on Aging. 
In the 2025-26 fiscal year, the Pennsylvania Lottery sold a total of $4.44 billion in traditional games, from which it generated a profit of more than $1 billion to benefit older Pennsylvanians. In addition, more than $3 billion in prizes were paid to traditional game winners. More than $757 million in prizes were paid out to winners of online eInstant games. 
The Pennsylvania Lottery remains the only state lottery that designates all its proceeds to programs that benefit older residents. As an agency of state government, the Pennsylvania Lottery is a successful enterprise of which all Pennsylvanians can be proud.
