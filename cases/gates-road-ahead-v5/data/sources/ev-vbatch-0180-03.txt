EST Data Inquiries Economic Indicators Division, Retail Indicator Branch: (301) 763-2713 Media Inquiries Public Information Office: (301) 763-3030 CB26-183 QUARTERLY RETAIL E-COMMERCE SALES 3rd QUARTER 2026 Notice of Revision: Quarterly retail e-commerce estimates were revised based on historical corrections and the results of the 2023 and 2024 Annual Integrated Economic Survey. For the full Adjusted and Not Adjusted estimates Time Series, see https://www.census.gov/retail/ecommerce.html. The Census Bureau of the Department of Commerce announced today that the estimate of U.S. retail e-commerce sales for the third quarter of 2026, adjusted for seasonal variation, but not for price changes, was $347.3 billion, an increase of 1.6 percent (±0.4 percent) from the second quarter of 2026. Total retail sales for the third quarter of 2026 were estimated at $1,941.9 billion, an increase of 1.1 percent (±0.2 percent) from the second quarter of 2026. The third quarter 2026 e-commerce estimate increased 10.4 percent (±0.9 percent) from the third quarter of 2025 while total retail sales increased 5.6 percent (±0.5 percent) in the same period. E-commerce sales in the third quarter of 2026 accounted for 17.9 percent of total sales. On a not adjusted basis, the estimate of U.S. retail e-commerce sales for the third quarter of 2026 totaled $335.7 billion, an increase of 0.6 percent (±0.4 percent) from the second quarter of 2026. The third quarter 2026 e-commerce estimate increased 10.4 percent (±0.9 percent) from the third quarter of 2025 while total retail sales increased 5.6 percent (±0.5 percent) in the same period. Ecommerce sales in the third quarter of 2026 accounted for 17.2 percent of total sales.
