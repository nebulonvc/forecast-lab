Americans’ Use of Internet, Broadband in 2026 | Pew Research Center (October 19, 2026). The survey was conducted Jan. 30-June 18, 2026. When the Center began asking Americans about their internet usage in early 2000, about half of all adults were already online. Today, 97% of U.S. adults say they use the internet. Internet use (% of U.S. adults who say they use the internet): 2000 52%, 2005 68%, 2008 74%, 2010 76%, 2015 86%, 2019 90%, 2021 93%, 2023 95%, 2024 96%, 2025 96%, 2026 97%. By age in 2008: Ages 18-29 89%, 30-49 84%, 50-64 72%, 65+ 38%. By community type in 2008: Urban 75%, Suburban 77%, Rural 63%. For some demographic groups – such as younger adults, those with at least some college education and those from high-income households – internet usage is near universal.
