About Us | MIT OpenCourseWare | Free Online Course Materials. Since 2001, MIT OpenCourseWare (OCW) has created new opportunities for learners and educators around the world. We're sharing Open Educational Resources (OER) from MIT and helping to lead and sustain a global revolution in free access to knowledge. MIT OpenCourseWare is a free and open collection of material from thousands of MIT courses, covering the entire MIT curriculum. Use OCW to guide your own lifelong learning, or to teach others. MIT does not offer credit or certification to users of OCW. Freely browse and use OCW materials at your own pace. There's no start or end dates, nor any requirement to create an account. MIT OpenCourseWare will continue to share the latest teaching and insights from MIT faculty, and collaborate across the dynamic open knowledge ecosystem to build a world of more vibrant, equitable and inclusive education for all.
