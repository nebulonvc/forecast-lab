About Connectivity Assist - Apple Support About Connectivity Assist Use cellular data in addition to Wi-Fi for a more reliable internet connection. Connectivity Assist uses cellular data to improve your connection when Wi-Fi is slow or unusable — in places like your backyard or driveway, or at coffee shops, airports, sports venues, and everywhere in between. Connectivity Assist appears in Wi-Fi settings — this replaces and supersedes Wi-Fi Assist, previously found in Cellular settings. If Wi-Fi Assist was on when you updated your software, Connectivity Assist is on by default. If Wi-Fi Assist was off, you can turn Connectivity Assist on manually. Turn Connectivity Assist on or off Update your iPhone to the latest iOS or iPadOS . In Settings, tap Wi-Fi, then make sure Wi-Fi is turned on. Scroll down and turn Connectivity Assist on or off. If you want to always stay on Wi-Fi for a specific router configuration (for example, your ad blocker), you can turn off Connectivity Assist for that particular network . With Connectivity Assist turned on, you might use more cellular data. If you want, you can check or reset your cellular data usage . Connectivity Assist isn't used when your iPhone is international roaming, in cellular Low Data Mode, or with some third-party apps that stream audio and video.
