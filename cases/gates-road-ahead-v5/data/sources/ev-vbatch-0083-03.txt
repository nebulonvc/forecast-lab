MVPD subscribership has been declining since 2013.667 Figure II.E.1 shows that collectively, the number of traditional MVPD subscribers fell from 75.9 million at the end of 2020 to 69.2 million at the end of 2021, which represents a loss of 6.7 million video subscribers.668 Further, between 2020 and 2021, cable MVPDs lost 3 million subscribers, DBS MVPDs lost 2.6 million subscribers, and telephone company MVPDs lost 1.1 million subscribers.669 At the end of 2021, 51.3% of U.S. households subscribed to a traditional MVPD.670
