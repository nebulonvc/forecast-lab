How Many Movie Theaters Are in the US and the World? Counts of cinema sites, screens and seats for the United States, the world and 13 national markets. Each figure comes from a national statistics office, a film agency or a trade body, with the year it describes. The United States had about 38,400 movie screens at the end of 2023, down from a peak of 41,172 in 2019 [1]. Cinema United gave 2023 as a round figure, and it is the last count it published. The US had 5,798 movie theater sites in 2020: 5,477 indoor cinemas and 321 drive-ins [2]. That was the last year the site count was published. In 1995 it was 7,744. The country has fewer buildings but more screens per building than it did 30 years ago. The world had 219,954 cinema screens in 2025, according to Omdia as quoted by UNIC, up 0.76% on 2024 [4].
