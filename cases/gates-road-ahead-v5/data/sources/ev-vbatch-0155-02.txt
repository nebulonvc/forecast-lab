Phones for Microsoft Teams. Microsoft Teams supports a portfolio of desk phones for users who require a traditional phone experience. To deliver a high-quality and reliable Microsoft Teams experience on phones, we are partnering and actively working with Yealink, Crestron, Polycom, and Audiocodes to develop and certify a wide portfolio of desk phones and conference room audio devices. Features supported by Teams phones: Hot desking : Users can get their contacts, meetings, and other preferences, just by signing into a phone. When they're done, they can sign out and leave the phone ready for the next user. Better together : Phones can lock and unlock in an integrated fashion when connected to their Windows PC running a 64-bit Teams desktop client.
