Use SharePlay to watch, listen, and play together in FaceTime on iPhone - Apple Support. With SharePlay in the FaceTime app, you can stream TV shows, movies, and music in sync with friends and family while on a FaceTime call. You can also play supported multiplayer games in Game Center with friends during a FaceTime call, and watch supported live sports events together. To watch a movie, TV show, or live sports event together, every participant needs to have access to the content on their own device, through either a subscription or purchase, on a device that meets the minimum system requirements. SharePlay may not support the sharing of some movies, TV shows, or live sports events across different countries or regions. For everyone on the call who has access to the content, the video starts playing at the same time. Each person watching the content can use the playback controls to play, pause, rewind, or fast-forward.
