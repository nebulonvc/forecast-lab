Top 10 Takeaways from NAR’s 2026 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers. November 5, 2026. By: Jessica Lautz. The NATIONAL ASSOCIATION OF REALTORS® Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers is an annual survey of recent home buyers and sellers who completed a transaction between July 2025 and June 2026. Buyer Use of Agent: Ninety percent of home buyers use a real estate agent or broker to purchase their home—the highest in four decades. Buyers want a real estate agent or broker who can help them find the right home, negotiate, and understand the real estate market. Seller Use of Agent: Ninety-two percent of home sellers use a real estate agent or broker to sell their home, an all-time high. For-sale-by-owners (FSBO) remained at a historic low of just 5% of sellers. Sixty-two percent of FSBOs knew the buyer.
