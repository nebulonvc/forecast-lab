Tap to Pay on iPhone - Apple Business.
Accept all kinds of contactless payments. Customers can pay in a variety of ways — with debit and credit cards, Apple Pay, and other digital wallets. And all you need to accept payments is an iPhone.
To set up Tap to Pay on iPhone, enable it in the payment app of your choice. To accept a payment, open the app, enter the amount, and present your iPhone to your customer. To pay, the customer holds their card horizontally or places their device directly over the payment symbol. There’s no need to invest in or manage card readers and terminals, whether you’re expanding or just starting out.
Available in over a dozen markets worldwide, Tap to Pay on iPhone with SumUp is a top payment acceptance solution for hospitality, retail, and transportation merchants on the platform. From hairstylists to taxi drivers, merchants can enable Tap to Pay on iPhone in the SumUp app in minutes — and begin accepting in-person contactless payments in stores or on the go.
