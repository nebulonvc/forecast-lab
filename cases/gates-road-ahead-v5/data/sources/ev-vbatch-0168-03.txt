U.S. Census Bureau, Department of Commerce, CB26-42, For Immediate Release Tuesday, March 10, 2026: Quarterly Retail E-Commerce Sales, 4th Quarter 2025. The Census Bureau of the Department of Commerce announced today that the estimate of U.S. retail e-commerce sales for the fourth quarter of 2025, adjusted for seasonal variation, but not for price changes, was $316.1 billion, an increase of 1.7 percent (±0.5%) from the third quarter of 2025. Total retail sales for the fourth quarter of 2025 were estimated at $1,900.5 billion, an increase of 0.4 percent (±0.2%) from the third quarter of 2025. E-commerce sales in the fourth quarter of 2025 accounted for 16.6 percent of total sales. Total e-commerce sales for 2025 were estimated at 1,233.7 billion, an increase of 5.4 percent (±1.2%) from 2024. Total retail sales in 2025 increased 3.5 percent (±0.4%) from 2024. E-commerce sales in 2025 accounted for 16.4 percent of total sales. E-commerce sales in 2024 accounted for 16.1 percent of total sales.
