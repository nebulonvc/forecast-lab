Add a hotel room key to Apple Wallet on your iPhone You can add a hotel room key to the Apple Wallet app. Then, use your iPhone or Apple Watch to view reservation details, check in, and unlock your room. To add a hotel room key on your iPhone, you need: The latest version of iOS or watchOS . An Apple ID signed in to iCloud with two-factor authentication enabled. A reservation at a hotel or resort that supports this feature. The hotel or resort app and a valid account. How to add a hotel room key on your iPhone Open the hotel or resort app and sign in to your account. Open your reservation. Tap Add to Apple Wallet. Follow the instructions on the screen to add your hotel room key. When you add your hotel room key on your iPhone, it will be automatically added to your eligible paired Apple Watch. If your hotel room key wasn't added to your eligible Apple Watch, you can add it to your watch by adding it again from the hotel or resort app. Express Mode is turned on automatically, which allows you to use your hotel room key by simply holding your device against the lock . When your stay is over, your hotel room key is deactivated remotely and may be archived for more efficient management of passes in Apple Wallet. You can also manually remove hotel passes from Apple Wallet. Some hotel room keys previously added can be added again directly in the Wallet app: On your iPhone, open the Wallet app. Tap the Add button . Tap Previous Cards. Tap your hotel key and follow the onscreen instructions. Use your hotel room key After you add a hotel room key to the Apple Wallet app, you can use your iPhone or Apple Watch to unlock your room by simply holding your iPhone or Apple Watch against the lock. Depending on your iPhone model, you might be able to open your room after your iPhone battery runs out . You can also view your reservation details and check in.
