FOR RELEASE AT 10:00 AM EST, THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 19, 2026

QUARTERLY

Selected Services Total
Advance U.S. selected services total revenue for the third quarter of 2026, adjusted for seasonal variation
but not for price changes, was $6,531.3 billion, an increase of 1.0 percent (± 0.4 percent) from the second
quarter of 2026 and up 6.5 percent (± 0.6 percent) from the third quarter of 2025. The first quarter of
2026 to second quarter of 2026 percentage change was not revised from the preliminary estimate of 3.0
percent (± 0.4 percent).

Information
Advance U.S. information sector revenue for the third quarter of 2026, not adjusted for seasonal variation
or price changes, was $733.2 billion, an increase of 4.0 percent (± 0.6 percent) from the second quarter of
2026 and up 13.8 percent (± 1.3 percent) from the third quarter of 2025. The first quarter of 2026 to
second quarter of 2026 percentage change was revised from the preliminary estimate of 4.2 percent (± 0.8
percent) to 4.3 percent (± 0.6 percent).
Finance and insurance
Advance U.S. finance and insurance revenue for the third quarter of 2026, not adjusted for seasonal
variation or price changes, was $2,016.6 billion, an increase of 0.2 percent (± 0.6 percent)* from the
second quarter of 2026 and up 5.2 percent (± 0.6 percent) from the third quarter of 2025. The first
quarter of 2026 to second quarter of 2026 percentage change was revised from the preliminary estimate
of 4.1 percent (± 0.4 percent) to 4.2 percent (± 0.4 percent).
Real estate and rental and leasing
Advance U.S. real estate and rental and leasing revenue for the third quarter of 2026, not adjusted for
seasonal variation or price changes, was $277.9 billion, an increase of 1.0 percent (± 0.4 percent) from the
second quarter of 2026 and up 5.3 percent (± 1.1 percent) from the third quarter of 2025. The first
quarter of 2026 to second quarter of 2026 percentage change was revised from the preliminary estimate
of 11.7 percent (± 0.9 percent) to 11.4 percent (± 0.9 percent).
Professional, scientific, and technical services
Advance U.S. professional, scientific, and technical services revenue for the third quarter of 2026, not
adjusted for seasonal variation or price changes, was $861.7 billion, an increase of 1.3 percent (± 1.5
percent)* from the second quarter of 2026 and up 5.6 percent (± 1.7 percent) from the third quarter of
2025. The first quarter of 2026 to second quarter of 2026 percentage change was revised from the
preliminary estimate of 3.6 percent (± 1.3 percent) to 3.2 percent (± 1.3 percent).
