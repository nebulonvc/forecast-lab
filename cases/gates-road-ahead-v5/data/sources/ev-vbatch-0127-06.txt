TactSuit Pro User Manual. Specifications: Product Name TactSuit Pro, Model Name BHTV32M1D, Feedback Point Total 32 (front : 16, rear : 16), Haptic Motor * 32, Bluetooth Dongle included. with bHaptics Player for PC (Windows): Windows 10, 11. 1. Install the bHaptics Player for PC. 2. Launch the bHaptics Player for PC and follow the on-screen guide to pair TactSuit Pro with your Windows PC. 3. Play Natively or MOD Supported Content. For Non-Natively Supported Content: You can enjoy content that doesn't natively support TactSuit Pro by using the Audio-to-Haptics feature in bHaptics Player for PC, which automatically generates haptic feedback based on the audio of the content.
