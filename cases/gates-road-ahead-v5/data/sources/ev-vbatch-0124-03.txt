Presence and Cybersickness in Virtual Reality Are Negatively Related: A Review (Published 2019 Feb 4, PMC6369189). In order to take advantage of the potential offered by the medium of virtual reality (VR), it will be essential to develop an understanding of how to maximize the desirable experience of “presence” in a virtual space (“being there”), and how to minimize the undesirable feeling of “cybersickness” (a constellation of discomfort symptoms experienced in VR). These confounding factors emerge in part due to the rapid rise of VR, the multifactorial nature of both CS and presence, and the influence of other modulating factors such as sensory mismatch, display and interaction factors. First, there is more compelling evidence in support of a negative association between CS and presence than alternative relationships.
