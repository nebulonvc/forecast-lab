Etsy, Inc. Form 10-K for the Fiscal Year Ended December 31, 2025 (filed with the U.S. SEC on February 19, 2026). As of December 31, 2025, the Etsy marketplace had 86.5 million active buyers and 5.6 million active sellers. Additionally, for the year ended December 31, 2025: 74% of GMS came from U.S. buyers and 26% came from buyers outside of the United States. There were more than 100 million items for sale on Etsy. Consolidated GMS in 2025 was $11,916.9 million, of which Etsy marketplace GMS was $10,460.7 million or 87.8% of the total.
