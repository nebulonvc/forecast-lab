eBook Royalties - Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing Help. If you select the 35% royalty option, your royalty will be 35% of your list price without VAT for each unit sold. If you select the 70% royalty option, your royalty will be 70% of your list price without VAT, less delivery costs (average delivery costs are zsh.06 per unit sold, and vary by file size), for each eligible book sold to customers in the 70% territories, and 35% of the list price without VAT for each unit sold to customers residing outside the 70% territories. 70% royalty applies when the buyer is located in one of the 70% eligible territories. 35% royalty applies when the buyer is located outside those territories.
