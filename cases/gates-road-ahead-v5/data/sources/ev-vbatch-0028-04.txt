Geneva, 03 November 2026 — An estimated 6.3 billion people, or 76 per cent of the world's population, are using the Internet in 2026, according to Facts and Figures 2026 , released today by the International Telecommunication Union (ITU). The figure is up from a revised estimate of 6.1 billion people, or 74 per cent, in 2025. Despite the continued growth in connectivity, 2 billion people remain offline. At the same time, the data reveal persistent disparities in who is connected. As example, Internet use stands at 93 per cent in high income countries, compared with just 32 per cent in low-income countries. Mobile network coverage: An estimated 97 per cent of the global population lives within reach of at least a 3G network. However, coverage by more advanced technologies remains highly unequal. While 5G reaches 59 per cent of the global population, coverage ranges from 86 per cent in high-income countries to just 4 per cent in low-income countries.
