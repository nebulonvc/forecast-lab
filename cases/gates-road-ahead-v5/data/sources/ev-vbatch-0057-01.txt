How to edit the Guided Tour?
Before entering the exhibition, each visitor is given the choice of walking through the room by themselves, or to be taken from artwork to artwork in a pre-set tour of the room - the Guided Tour . Switching between these as a visitor is easily done in the menu on the right-hand side.
The numbers of the placeholders on the wall determine their sequence in the tour, set per default for each room, also the entry point.
Editing the Default Sequence:
You can alter this existing tour sequence with the curation menu on the left-hand side of the screen as follows:
When in curation mode of your exhibition, access the tool with 'Tour editor'. The column shows the current order of tour stations for each artwork in the room. Now you can adjust their sorting with the grabber on the left, or the arrows on the right of the images (move up or down).
The tool also offers these choices in addition:
hiding empty placeholders
displaying the duration of a view
recording a certain tour position, inserted automatically as a view
modifying the default duration of how long a piece is shown
playing the tour for confirmation of the changes
An alternative access to the tool is from a specific artwork's detail view , shown next to the in-room-editor .
The number here indicates the current tour position, and you can assign any other number to it manually with the arrows provided.
