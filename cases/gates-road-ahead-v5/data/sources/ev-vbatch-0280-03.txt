Know how familiar face detection works
Important:
To use familiar face detection, you need a
Google Home Premium subscription
.
Familiar face detection is not available for cameras based in Illinois.
Basics
Important:
Before you save any of your camera’s face data, check your local privacy laws. If required, get the person’s permission first before you save their face data.
Whenever your camera detects a face, the activity is marked in your camera's recorded video in the Google Home app or Nest app.
When your camera detects an unfamiliar face, the app should ask whether it’s someone you know. You’ll have the option to save the person’s data in the familiar face library. This helps your camera recognize the face as someone you know.
We also use additional signals (body size, clothing color, etc.) to help make Familiar Faces work better when a subject's face is not visible.
Based on which Nest camera model you have, you can change settings and manage the familiar face library in the Google Home app, Nest app, or both.
Face library
Whenever your camera recognizes a face, photo snapshots of the person’s face will be saved in the familiar face library. From there, you’ll be able to edit the face profile of each person and manage photos.
All home members in the app have access to the familiar face library, and may be able to categorize faces your camera detects.
Recorded video & camera events
When your camera recognizes a face, the activity is marked in its recorded video. You can find this information when you review specific camera events, or when you filter an activity in your camera’s video history.
Notifications
Each home member can change whether they get familiar face notifications.
If you turn familiar face detection off, your camera can still send alerts when it detects a person. However, it will not recognize faces.
How familiar face detection works with 2 or more cameras
You can turn familiar face detection on or off for each camera in its settings.
Nest cameras in the same home share the same familiar face library. If you add a new camera to the same home, it uses the same familiar face library as the other cameras in your home. This means it won’t take as long to recognize certain faces.
You can only use familiar face detection for the cameras with a
1st gen Nest Aware subscription
. Cameras without a subscription do not have familiar face detection.
