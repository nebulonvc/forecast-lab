Worst year on record: internet shutdowns in 2024. Launching today, February 24, 2025, Access Now and the #KeepItOn coalition’s new report, Emboldened offenders, endangered communities: internet shutdowns in 2024 , exposes how authorities imposed at least 296 internet shutdowns in 54 countries , causing chaos across borders, exacerbating trauma during conflict, and fracturing the lives of millions of people around the globe. The worst year on record: authorities implemented at least 296 internet shutdowns in 54 countries — a 35% increase compared to the previous high in 2022 ( 40 countries); The majority of internet shutdowns were experienced by people in four countries: millions of people in India, Myanmar, Pakistan, and Russia, lived through at least 203 shutdowns — 69% of the global total; A surge in platform blocks: a record-breaking total of 71 were recorded in 35 countries, with X being the most blocked platform in 2024;
