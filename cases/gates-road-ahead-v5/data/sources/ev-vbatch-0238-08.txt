In 2021, some 97 percent of 3- to 18-year-olds had home internet access, according to the American 
Community Survey (ACS). Specifically, 93 percent had access through a computer,

and 4 percent relied on a smartphone for home internet access.

The remaining 3 percent had no internet access at home. When compared with the percentage with home internet access overall, there was more variation by race/ethnicity, parental education, and household income in the percentage of 3- to 18-year-olds who had access to the internet through a computer.
