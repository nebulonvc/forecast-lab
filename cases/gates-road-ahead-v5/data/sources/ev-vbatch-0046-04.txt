TV Viewing Cools Off in Nielsen’s August Gauge Reports as Sports Take a Breather and School Resumes. NEW YORK – October 22, 2026 – Television viewing cooled off in August following a two-month surge fueled by the FIFA World Cup 2026, according to Nielsen’s August 2026 reports of The Gauge and Media Distributor Gauge. Streaming Represents 49.6% of TV Watch-Time. However, while time spent with each Gauge category declined between July and August, cable and streaming still managed to gain share, capturing 18.9% and 49.6% of television, respectively. YouTube bucked the monthly downturn to capture a platform-record 15.8% of TV as monthly viewing climbed 6%, keeping the streamer in the lead in Nielsen’s Media Distributor Gauge. Disney Streaming and Paramount Streaming each added 0.1 share point to finish with 4.8% and 2.3% of TV, respectively. Netflix moved up to No. 3 with 7.7% of TV, narrowly outpacing NBCU+Versant by a margin of 0.03 share points. Broadcast ultimately lost 2.0 share points to finish with 17.5% of TV.
