Barring a miracle, the 2020s are on track to become a lost decade for far too many developing economies, the World Bank Group’s Chief Economist, Indermit Gill, writes in his Foreword. Virtually half of all developing economies have failed since 2019 to advance on the most rudimentary promise of development: narrowing the income gap with the world’s most prosperous economies. But prospects for the 2030s are bright: the economic forces now gathering—AI, energy transformation, and deeper regional integration—are powerful enough to unlock transformative progress in the next decade.
