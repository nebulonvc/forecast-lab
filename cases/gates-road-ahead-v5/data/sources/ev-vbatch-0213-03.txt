The Public Library Association’s 2023 Public Library Technology Survey tells us that library internet download speeds are increasingly common at 100MBps or 1Gbps, but still 28.4% of libraries overall and 35.4% of town/rural libraries reported subscribing to internet connections that do not meet the 2024 federal definition of broadband (100 Mbps download and 20 Mbps upload). Historically, approximately 50-60% of libraries nationwide participate in E-Rate each year. For 2025 just over 6,000 libraries applied for almost $150 million. According to the PLA’s 2023 Public Library Technology Survey nearly half (48.1%) of libraries used the E-Rate program to pay for technology.
