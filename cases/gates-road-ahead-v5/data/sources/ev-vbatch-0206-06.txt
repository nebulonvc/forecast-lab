RIAA YEAR-END RECORDED REVENUE REPORT 2025 (United States Wholesale Dollar Value, In Millions, net after returns). Total Revenue reached a record high of $11,535.3 million in 2025 (up 3.1% from $11,188.1 million in 2024). Paid Subscriptions reached 106.5M accounts, generating $6.4B. Paid Subscriptions represent more than half of total US revenue (55.3%). Total Streaming Revenue reached $9,474.5 million in 2025 (+3.1% from $9,185.2 million in 2024), and Total Digital Revenue reached $9,747.2 million (+3.0%). Total Physical revenue was $1,381.0 million in 2025 (+5.0% from $1,315.5 million in 2024), driven by Vinyl reaching $1,042.9 million (+9.3%, 46.8 million units, marking 19 YEARS of vinyl growth), while CD revenue fell 7.8% to $312.4 million (29.5 million units, -11.6%).
