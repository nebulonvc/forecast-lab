Amazon Prime Video Removes Watch Party Feature, Days Before New Fallout Series Starts | EarlyGame (By Malena Rose, April 10, 2024). After Twitch recently removed their Amazon Watch Party feature on April 2, the popular feature now disappeared from Amazon Prime Video completely, leaving many of their subscribers in confusion because they never announced the feature's removal. Especially during the COVID-19 pandemic in the past years, watch party features have become increasingly popular. It allows users to watch their favorite shows and series simultaneously with their friends and families all around the world. So even without the pandemic, Amazons Watch Party feature is popular among long distance friends, partners, and family members. It brings people from all over the world together and allows us to connect with each other and enjoy typical home activities together even if a distance brings us apart. Sadly... with the pandemic situation gradually improving in recent months and years, the usage has also declined.
