Hard Drive Cost Per Gigabyte, Backblaze, November 29, 2022. When Backblaze purchased hard drives back in 2009, we paid over $0.11 per gigabyte. In 2017, when we did a review of the cost of hard drives, the cost per gigabyte had fallen to just below $0.03 per gigabyte. From 2017 to November 2022, the average cost per gigabyte decreased by 56.36% for all of the drives ($0.033 down to $0.0144). Overall, the drop in the average price per gigabyte was from $0.114 in 2009 to just $0.014 as of November 2022. That's a difference of $0.100 (one thin dime) over the period. That equates to an 87.4% decrease since 2009. The cost per gigabyte has continued to fall over the past 13 years we've been tracking our drive purchases. This was in spite of the Thailand drive crisis which started in 2011, as well as the Coronavirus pandemic.
