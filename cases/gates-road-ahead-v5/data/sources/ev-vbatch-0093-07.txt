Regulatory & Policy Center
Wi-Fi Alliance® has made it our mission to promote policies that protect and open critical unlicensed spectrum resources for Wi-Fi®. This Regulatory and Policy Center provides the latest information related to our efforts and offers resources supporting Wi-Fi’s immense socioeconomic value.
Wi-Fi Alliance® is committed to protecting valuable unlicensed spectrum resources
Unlicensed spectrum – where Wi-Fi® operates – is one of society's most vital resources and a crucial component to connecting everyone and everything, everywhere while also delivering immense socioeconomic value. Wi-Fi Alliance's regulatory priorities focus on ensuring favorable policies and unlicensed spectrum decisions to support the growth, innovation, and reliability of Wi-Fi. These priorities align with the needs of diverse industries and global connectivity goals. Wi-Fi Alliance will continue to advocate for fair spectrum policies that foster a robust ecosystem, economic growth, and unparalleled connectivity.
