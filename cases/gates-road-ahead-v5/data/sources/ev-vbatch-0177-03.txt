The world’s most used social media platforms
Different data sources offer different perspectives on social media use, so there is currently no definitive answer to the question, “ what’s the most used social media platform in the world? ”
Each of these different perspectives is still valuable though, so let’s take a closer look at what the data tells us.
Self-declared social media platform use
When it comes to the platforms that people say they use, the latest research from GWI finds that Facebook is the world’s most widely used social media platform.
In GWI’s Q2 2026 survey, 58.1 percent of adult internet users aged 16 and above reported using Facebook within the past month, which puts the platform just 1.5 percentage points ahead of its nearest rival.
Instagram ranks second, with 56.6 percent of GWI’s survey respondents saying that they used the Meta platform within the past month, while YouTube ranks third, with 56.2 percent .
Meta platforms claim the remaining places in the top 5, with 54.9 percent of online adults saying they use WhatsApp each month, and 37.9 percent saying they used Messenger at least once in the past 30 days.
TikTok places sixth, with 36.1 percent of internet users over the age of 16 saying they’ve used the short video platform within the past month.
However, opt-in research that logs the apps that people actually open on their smartphones offers different perspectives.
For example, App Intelligence data from Similarweb suggests that WhatsApp had the greatest number of monthly active users in August 2026, giving the platform an index of 100 [ note that the top platform in this particular ranking will always have an index of 100 ].
YouTube ranked second, with Similarweb’s data indicating that the video platform’s audience is roughly 96.2 percent the size of WhatsApp’s active user base.
Facebook ranked third, with an index score of 81.9 , while stablemate Instagram placed fourth, with an index of 77.9 .
TikTok ranked fifth with an index score of 72.0 , and TikTok is the last platform in this Similarweb ranking that has an active user base that is at least half the size of WhatsApp’s.
Meanwhile, Manochi analysis of data published in the advertising resources of the world’s top platforms indicates that YouTube reports the largest social media advertising audience.
