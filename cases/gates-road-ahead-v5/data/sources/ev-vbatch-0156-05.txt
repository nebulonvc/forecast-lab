Accenture plc Form 10-K for the Fiscal Year Ended August 31, 2026 (Filed with the U.S. SEC on October 16, 2026). Competition: Accenture operates in a highly competitive and rapidly changing global marketplace. Our clients typically retain us on a non-exclusive basis. Our competitors include large multinational IT service providers, including the services arms of large global technology providers; offshore IT service providers in lower-cost locations, particularly in India; accounting firms and consultancies that provide consulting, managed services and other IT solutions and services; solution or service providers that compete with us in a specific geographic market, industry or service area. Revenues and Type of Work: Consulting revenues, which include strategy, management and technology consulting and technology integration consulting, were $36.9 billion in fiscal 2026 and $35.1 billion in fiscal 2025, out of total revenues of $74.2 billion. New bookings were $84.5 billion in fiscal 2026, including Consulting new bookings of $40.9 billion. Market Environment and Business Optimization Costs: While the discretionary environment is unchanged, clients continue to prioritize large-scale transformations, which include becoming AI-ready. During the first quarter of fiscal 2026, we completed our six-month business optimization program and recorded $308 million and $615 million during fiscal 2026 and 2025, respectively, primarily for employee severance.
