The Broadband Equity Access and Deployment Program (BEAD) , funded by the IIJA, is a $42.45 billion federal grant program that aims to connect every American to high-speed internet by funding partnerships to build infrastructure. In June 2023, NTIA announced allocation amounts for all 56 states and territories. States and territories can use their allocated funds for these eligible uses: Planning for the deployment of high-speed internet, including conducting research, collecting data, outreach, and training Deploying or upgrading internet infrastructure in unserved or underserved areas, or improving service to community anchor institutions Installing internet and Wi-Fi service in multi-unit residential buildings Internet access, adoption and use programs Workforce readiness programs to meet the needs of the infrastructure programs Other activities that increase meaningful use of constructed infrastructure, at the discretion of the Assistant Secretary Award Recipients State and Territory BEAD Plans and Milestones Click Here BEAD State and Territory Progress Dashboard Click Here Policy and Waiver Notices BEAD Supplemental Deployment Policy Notice Download Policy Notice Download 11/06/2026 Amendment BEAD Model Zero Outlay Agreement Download Model Agreement BEAD Restructuring Policy Notice Download Policy Notice Download Frequently Asked Questions Performance Measures for BEAD Last Mile Networks Policy Notice and Primer Download Notice and Primer Programmatic Waiver of Tribal Consent Deadline Download Notice Final Proposal Resources BEAD Final Proposal Guidance and Templates Download Guidance and Templates BEAD Final Proposal Funding Request Guidance and Samples Download Guidance and Samples Program Resources Notice of Funding Opportunity (NOFO) Download Now BEAD Subgrantees: Protect Your Rights Download Now BEAD Frequently Asked Questions Download Here Learn More About CostQuest Licensing View Licensing Details BEAD Program Buy America Waiver Released Learn More about the Build America, Buy America Waiver National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA) Resources and Tools View NEPA for BEAD Resources View General NEPA Resources Technical Assistance Find information, technical assistance, and documentation to help manage your award.
