Many remote workers say they’d be likely to leave their job if they could no longer work from home. The analysis is based primarily on the responses of 2,315 U.S. adults who are working part time or full time, have only one job or have multiple jobs but consider one their primary job, and have a job that can be done from home. The data was collected as part of a larger survey of workers conducted Oct. 7-13, 2024. Among employed adults who have a job that can be done from home, 75% are working remotely at least some of the time, according to a recent Pew Research Center survey. Nearly half of workers in this group (46%) say that if their employer no longer allowed them to work from home, they would be unlikely to stay at their current job.
