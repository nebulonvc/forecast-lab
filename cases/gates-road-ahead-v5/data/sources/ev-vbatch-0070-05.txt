Americans’ Use of Internet, Broadband in 2026 | Fact Sheet | October 19, 2026 | Pew Research Center. The internet represents a fundamental shift in how Americans connect with one another, gather information and conduct their day-to-day lives. For more than 25 years, Pew Research Center has documented the internet’s growth and distribution in the United States. This analysis is based on findings from the National Public Opinion Reference Survey, a Center survey of 5,511 U.S. adults. We allowed people to respond online, by mail or by phone. The survey represents the views of all U.S. adults. The survey was conducted Jan. 30-June 18, 2026. Its overall margin of error is plus or minus 1.8 percentage points. When the Center began asking Americans about their internet usage in early 2000, about half of all adults were already online. Today, 97% of U.S. adults say they use the internet.
