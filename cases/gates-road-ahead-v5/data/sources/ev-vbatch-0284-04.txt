A smart vending machine is a vending machine that is connected to the internet and reports what it is doing. It takes cashless payment, sends every sale, stock level and fault to a cloud dashboard in real time, and can be managed remotely — prices, planograms, access rules and alerts — without a visit. The cabinet and spirals are the same as any other machine; what makes it “smart” is the connectivity and software behind it. Vending on Track builds that layer: the telemetry hardware, the vending management software and, where needed, the machine itself.
This is the cheapest route, and it works on most fleets. Most vending machines built after 2000 have an MDB (Multi-Drop Bus) port — the standard bus that links the controller to the coin mech, note reader and card reader.
