Arlington, VA–The American Alliance of Museums’ Annual National Snapshot of United States Museums shows signs of renewed momentum for the museum field after last year’s setback. Fifty-four percent of museums surveyed reported more visitors than a year earlier, while 23% reported fewer. The share drawing at least as many visitors as in 2019 edged up from 45% in last year’s survey to 49% this year. More than half reported a stronger operating bottom line in 2025 than in 2019, and 44% reported an increase in school visits over the past two years following the steep decline during the height of the pandemic.
