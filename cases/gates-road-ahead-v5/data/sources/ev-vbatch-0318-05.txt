NEW YORK – October 22, 2026 – Television viewing cooled off in August following a two-month surge fueled by the FIFA World Cup 2026™, according to Nielsen’s August 2026 reports of The Gauge™ and Media Distributor Gauge. However, while time spent with each Gauge category declined between July and August, cable and streaming still managed to gain share, capturing 18.9% and 49.6% of television, respectively. Overall streaming usage was down 3.2% in August, but because the decrease was smaller than that of total TV usage (-4.4%), the category gained 0.6 share points over July. Broadcast viewing declined 14% compared to July as the category settled into its typical August slowdown ahead of football season.
