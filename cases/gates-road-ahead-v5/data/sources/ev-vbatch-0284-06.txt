Today, realtor.com ® launched Street Peek, which uses the location and orientation of the user combined with a proprietary algorithm to allow smartphone users to point their Android device at homes, and instantaneously surface property details about homes in the realtor.com ® database.
This augmented reality feature gives home shoppers the information they need to know about homes on any street.
Realtor.com ® ’ s patent-pending Sign Snap feature , which first debuted on Android in January, is now available on iOS. Like Street Peek, Sign Snap leverages a smartphone’s technology to put home details in the palm of your hands.
Sign Snap taps into a smartphone’s GPS and image recognition capabilities, allowing users to snap a photo of a For-Sale sign to immediately display the listing details of a home.
Interested in trying out Street Peek? Take a stroll through your neighborhood, grab your Android phone, and frame a home or group of homes within the app to experience a real-world view of the environment with information overlays from the realtor.com ® database.
When panning their phones around a neighborhood, users can view price bubbles that will pop up over houses that appear within the Street Peek view finder. Users will be able to view helpful information such as the listing or rental price, the recently sold price, estimated value, and the number of bedrooms and bathrooms on the framed properties.
