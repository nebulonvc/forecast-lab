Plan a drive Schedule a drive for a future time and get a reminder when it's time to go. Before planning a drive, make sure to turn on "Time to leave" notifications . First, log in to your Waze account if you haven’t done so already. Then, follow these steps: Go to the Waze Live map Under "Driving directions", enter your starting point and destination. Waze will provide you with up to three best routes. Click Leave now and change it to Arrive at Select the day and time you want to arrive at your destination Note: If you select Leave now , Waze will send you an instant notification on your mobile device. Tap it to start navigating. Click Save to app Your planned drive is now synced with your mobile device. Waze will send you a notification when it's time to leave. Manage your planned drive You can view or change your planned drives from your mobile device. To learn how, click the Android or iPhone & iPad tab above. Got questions? I scheduled a ride in the Live map but I don’t see it in the app Try signing in to your account by clicking the Log in button in the top-right corner of the Live map. Learn more about Waze sign-in . Note: Once you are signed in, you may need to click Save to app again. Can I schedule a drive from a different start point? Yes. You can schedule a drive from a different start point than your current location . Can I add a stop to my drive? You can add a stop before or during your drive but not when planning a drive.
