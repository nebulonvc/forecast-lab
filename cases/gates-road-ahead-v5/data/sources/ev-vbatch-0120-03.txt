How Much Does Maya Cost in 2026? Pricing Guide
Autodesk Maya costs $255 per month, $2,010 per year, or $6,025 for a three-year subscription in the US. Eligible independent artists can also get Maya Indie for $330 per year, while qualified students and educators may access Maya for free through Autodesk’s Education plan.
The cheapest option depends on your eligibility and how often you use Maya. Here are the current prices, the differences between each plan, and the additional costs to consider when working on 3D projects.

Maya Pricing Plans Compared
Monthly Subscription: $255 per Month
The monthly subscription is the most flexible paid option. You can avoid committing to a full year, but the cost adds up quickly.
Using Maya for 12 months at $255 per month costs $3,060. That is $1,050 more than the annual subscription.
Choose monthly billing if you need Maya for a short, clearly defined project and the total cost is lower than committing to a longer plan. For continuous use, compare the annual price before subscribing.
Artists who need Maya temporarily and do not qualify for a lower-cost option.
Annual Subscription: $2,010 per Year
An annual subscription costs $2,010, equivalent to $167.50 per month. Compared with paying monthly for a full year, you save $1,050.
This is a practical option for professional artists, freelancers who do not qualify for Maya Indie, and studios that use Maya regularly.
If you expect to use Maya throughout the year, annual billing costs less than paying month by month. You should also compare the three-year price if you expect to keep using the software long term.
Professionals and studios that need the full commercial version of Maya throughout the year.
Three-Year Subscription: $6,025
A three-year subscription costs $6,025, or about $2,008 per year when averaged across the full term.
It is worth considering if Maya is a core part of your production workflow and you expect to use it for the entire subscription period. The main trade-off is the longer commitment, so it is less suitable if your software needs may change soon.
Established studios and professionals with predictable, long-term Maya requirements.
