Who we are We are the non-profit organization behind Wikipedia. The Wikimedia Foundation supports hundreds of thousands of people around the world in creating the largest free knowledge projects in history. The work of volunteers helps millions of people around the globe discover information, contribute knowledge, and share it with others no matter their bandwidth. Wikipedia is a free, collaborative encyclopedia written in over 300 languages by volunteers around the world. Wikimedia Commons is the world’s largest free-to-use library of illustrations, photos, drawings, videos, and music. As a nonprofit, Wikipedia and our related free knowledge projects are powered primarily through donations.
