Substack is an American online platform that provides publishing, payment, analytics, and design infrastructure to support subscription newsletters. It allows writers to send digital content directly to subscribers. Authors can decide to make subscribing to their newsletter free or paid, and to make specific posts publicly available to non-subscribers. By November 2024, Substack had four million paid subscriptions. In March 2025, Substack announced that it had five million paid subscriptions. In October 2025, Substack reported that 50,000 creators were earning money on its platform, of whom at least 50 were making more than $1 million. Substack takes a 10% share of paid subscription revenue, a fee that some writers have found increasingly expensive as their audiences grow.
