How Americans View Data Privacy: 1. Views of data privacy risks, personal data and digital privacy laws. By Colleen McClain, Michelle Faverio, Monica Anderson and Eugenie Park (October 18, 2023). When managing their privacy online, most Americans say they trust themselves to make the right decisions about their personal information (78%), and a majority are skeptical that anything they do will make a difference (61%). Far fewer mention being overwhelmed by figuring out what they need to do (37%) or say privacy is not that big of a deal to them (29%). Another 21% are confident that those with access to their personal information will do what is right. Roughly four-in-ten Americans say they are very worried about companies selling their information to others without them knowing (42%) or people stealing their identity or personal information (38%). Fewer are apprehensive about law enforcement monitoring what they do online (15%).
