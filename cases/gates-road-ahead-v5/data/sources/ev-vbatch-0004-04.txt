Digital economy and technology
The rapid advancement of digital technologies has transformed economies and reshaped the way businesses, governments and individuals interact globally. The digital economy, driven by innovation, connectivity and technological disruption, plays a pivotal role in shaping economic growth, productivity and societal change. This collection of thematic insights explores key aspects of the digital economy and technology, offering a comprehensive understanding of its evolving landscape.

Over $28 trillion of e-commerce sales in 2024
E-commerce sales, trillions of dollars, 2024
UN Trade and Development,
Based on data from 46 developed and developing economies which account for three quarters of global GDP.
Businesses located in 46 developed and developing economies that account for three quarters of global GDP recorded nearly $28 trillion in e-commerce sales in 2024.
This includes sales to customers both within the same economy and abroad. A global measure of e-commerce would be even higher.
Among the economies covered, the
United States of America, Europe and China dominate e-commerce sales
. This reflects the presence of complex and digitalized supply chains supporting manufacturing, retail and other business activities, as well as these economies' central role in global value chains.

E-commerce sales by businesses continue growing rapidly
Business e-commerce sales value and GDP, in current prices (index, 2016=100)
UN Trade and Development,
Based on a sample of 46 developed and developing economies, accounting for three quarters of global GDP.
Business e-commerce sales have grown rapidly since 2016
, based on statistics available across 46 developed and developing economies that generate three quarters of global GDP. This
trend accelerated during the COVID‑19 pandemic, when both businesses and their customers turned to e-commerce to mitigate disruption to other sales channels.
Sales in 2024 were 5.5% higher than in 2023
, representing a slight easing in the rate of increase. The continued growth of e-commerce reflects the benefits that online sales channels offer businesses, enabling them to reach customers beyond their immediate location, including internationally, and to receive orders at any time.

E-commerce is growing faster in developing economies
Average annual growth in business e-commerce sales value, percentage, 2016-2024
UN Trade and Development,
Based on data representing 10 developing and 36 developed economies.
e-commerce sales by businesses in developing economies grew more than twice as fast as in developed economies
, based on a sample of economies with available data.
In developed economies, online ordering is widely used as a sales channel. While adoption continues to increase, and the value of e-commerce sales is rising, digital ordering has already been adopted by many businesses for which it is well suited.
In many developing economies, digitalization is progressing rapidly, including through the adoption of digital ordering processes, although adoption remains less widespread than in developed economies.
