Overview and key findings of the 2024 Digital News Report | Reuters Institute for the Study of Journalism (June 17, 2024, Nic Newman). This 13th edition of our Digital News Report is based on data from six continents and 47 markets. Many of the immediate challenges are compounded by the power and changing strategies of rival big tech companies, including social media, search engines, and video platforms. Our data show little growth in news subscription, with just 17% saying they paid for any online news in the last year, across a basket of 20 richer countries. In some countries we find evidence of heavy discounting, with around four in ten (41%) saying they currently pay less than the full price. Prospects of attracting new subscribers remain limited by a continued reluctance to pay for news, linked to low interest and an abundance of free sources.
