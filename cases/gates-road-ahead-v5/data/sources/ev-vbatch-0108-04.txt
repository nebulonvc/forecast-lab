Published on 09 June 2026 Author Michael Kozlowski

Amazon has been promising that one of its new software features would be available soon since it was announced back in 2025. Story So Far is a new function that just went live today on all modern Kindle e-readers and the Kindle apps for Android and iOS.

Amazon’s Story So Far is an AI-powered catch-up feature that generates spoiler-free summaries of a book’s plot and characters, tailored specifically to the page you are currently on. It lets you seamlessly jump back into complex, paused, or long books without having to backtrack or remember every detail.

The tool looks only at the text you have already read to generate a clean, clear synopsis and is spoiler-free. Story So Far covers thousands of popular, best-selling English-language e-books that are purchased from the Amazon bookstore or Kindle Unlimited for USA customers only.

To determine whether a book has Story So Far available, look for the “Read recap” button that appears when you press and hold a book in your Kindle Library. You can access Story So Far at any time by tapping the three-dot menu in the book while reading. If you want to try out Story So Far, it is only compatible with Kindle e-readers running the latest software update 5.19.4.0.1 or above.

I think Story So Far is a unique system for people who juggle multiple books at once and do not want any spoilers for chapters you haven’t read yet. I used to use X-Ray for books to find people, places, and things, along with all pages that reference them; however, it is easy to find spoilers.
