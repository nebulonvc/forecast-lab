For all of the studios, the windows that follow theatrical are: Premium Electronic Sell-Through (PEST) , the price is usually $24.99 to $29.99 to purchase a title, and the premium period lasts 2 to 4 weeks; Premium Video-on-Demand (PVOD) , from $14.99 to $19.99 per rental for a 48-hour viewing period, it’s simultaneous with PEST and lasts 2 to 4 weeks before the prices drop; “Regular” sales and rental — after the premium period ends, prices drop to around $19.99 for sales, and $2.99 to $5.99 for rentals, depending on the format and interest in the film; Then, streaming and pay-TV ; Then, ad-supported free TV . - With Disney’s strong films and long windows, they’re able to negotiate the best box office split with theater owners, as high as 65% for a big movie that plays for many weeks. Not every release gets those terms, but the hits do. That’s the business decision Disney has made: longer windows, and stay out of theatrical’s way.
