THE 2025 VIRTUOSO® LUXE REPORT - A Guide to Key Trends and Insights in Luxury Travel
More than 2,200 travel advisors from Virtuoso-affiliated agencies across 58 countries contributed their extensive knowledge, professional experience and client requests to offer insight into travel trends in the year ahead.
In 2025, Virtuoso clients continue to prioritize exploration and personalized experiences, but they are increasingly focused on maximizing value without compromising quality. While luxury travelers are willing to spend more, they are also mindful of rising costs and want to ensure their investment enhances their travels, making their relationship with a trusted travel advisor all the more important.
