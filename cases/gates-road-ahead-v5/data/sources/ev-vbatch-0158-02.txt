Indeed Apply makes it easier for job seekers to complete applications directly on Indeed, which can help reduce drop-off and increase completed applications. Worldwide, employers who used Indeed Apply received 5X more completed applications than those who did not.[1]
Below, we explain what Indeed Apply is, how it works and how it can support your hiring process.
Optimize your new and existing job descriptions to reach more candidates
Indeed Apply, also known as Indeed Easy Apply or Indeed Quick Apply, is designed to make it easier for candidates to apply to your jobs. Instead of being redirected to a career site or other external application experience, job seekers can easily apply directly on Indeed using their Indeed Profile, which includes their resume and other credentials.
For employers, this can mean fewer candidate drop-offs and more completed applications. It can also create a smoother experience for candidates, which may help deliver higher-quality applications and connect you with the right applicants faster.
Indeed Apply is the default application method for all jobs posted directly on Indeed (hosted jobs).
For jobs pulled from an applicant tracking system (ATS) or career site (indexed jobs), you can enable Indeed Apply through Indeed Apply Sync, which connects completed applications back to your ATS.
You may see the most value from Indeed Apply if you want to:
Attract more relevant candidates and increase the volume of completed applications
Reduce drop-offs where clicks don’t result in applications
Gain clearer visibility into application data
Indeed Apply works differently depending on how your jobs are posted. If you post directly on Indeed, the process is built in from start to finish. If you post through an ATS or career site, you can still take advantage of Indeed Apply by connecting your system with Indeed.
In both cases, the goal is the same: to make it easier for candidates to apply and for you to manage applications more efficiently.
Hosted jobs are jobs posted directly on Indeed. You manage applications and candidate communications in Indeed, with built-in analytics to track performance.

The primary way to use Indeed Apply for indexed jobs is through an integration with one of Indeed’s 350+ supported ATSs. Your indexed jobs will continue to be managed in your ATS, but can also be tracked in Indeed, giving you flexibility in how you oversee applications.
With an Indeed Apply Sync integration:
New jobs in your ATS can post automatically to Indeed
Applications submitted on Indeed flow directly into your ATS
You can use screener and qualification questions set up in your ATS, as well as Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) questions
Job data, application information and analytics stay in sync
This approach can be particularly helpful if you:
Want all Indeed applications to flow directly into your ATS with source tracking
Rely on insights like application volume and application completion rates to guide your hiring decisions
Need to hire quickly or fill high-volume or evergreen roles
Compete in industries where speed is critical, and you want candidates to apply before they move on to other employers
Want to improve the quality of applications by using your ATS’s screening and qualifying questions
Prefer to let Indeed guide more relevant job seekers to apply to your jobs
Getting started with Indeed Apply is simple.
For hosted jobs, you don’t need to do anything. Indeed Apply is included automatically when you post directly on Indeed.
For indexed jobs, setup usually requires enabling an ATS integration so applications can sync between Indeed and your system.
Depending on your ATS, the integration may be built by Indeed or by your ATS provider. In either case, the experience is designed to work the same way for your team so you can turn on Indeed Apply and manage candidates in a consistent workflow.
When you use Indeed Apply, you may benefit from these key advantages:
You not only see more applications completed, but also increase your chances of reaching quality applicants. In the US, jobs using Indeed Apply are, on average, 2.4X more likely to lead to a hire than jobs that do not use Indeed Apply.[2]
For indexed jobs worldwide, those with Indeed Apply turned on receive an average of 40% more apply starts per job than those without Indeed Apply.[3]
A streamlined, mobile-friendly process helps reduce drop-offs and makes it easier for job seekers to apply from any device.
