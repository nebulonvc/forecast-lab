To free up bandwidth for additional services (e.g., more digital channels, more HD channels, more VOD programming, and faster Internet speeds), many cable MVPDs are transitioning from the delivery of their programming in an analog format to an all-digital format. At the end of 2013, this all-digital transition had reached approximately 57 percent of the collective footprints of the top eight cable MVPDs.

At the end of 2013, facilities-based telephone MVPD service was available to 46.4 million homes (34.7 percent of the 133.8 million U.S. homes).
