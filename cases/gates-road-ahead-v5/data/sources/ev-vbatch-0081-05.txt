January 3, 1997, 12:30 am ET. CNET News.com. ISDN rate fight to flare up. Administrative law judge Kim Malcolm will hold a hearing to review her November ruling that Pac Bell shouldn’t be allowed to raise rates. The meeting follows Malcolm’s preliminary ruling on November 22, which concluded that Pacific Bell hadn’t demonstrated why it should be allowed to raise rates for Integrated Services Digital Network connections. Her draft ruling stated that Pac Bell should retain the current rate of $24.50 per month instead of the $32.50 that the utility had proposed. She also recommended off-peak usage at no additional charge for up to 200 hours, instead of the 20 hours proposed by Pac Bell. Besides Intel, companies including Compaq Computer and the Utility Consumer Action Network applauded Malcolm’s ruling.
