Office for the web (formerly Office Web Apps) opens Word, Excel, OneNote, and PowerPoint documents in your web browser. Office for the web makes it easier to work and share Office files from devices with a supported browser and an internet connection. Microsoft 365 customers with Word, Excel, OneNote, or PowerPoint can view, create, and edit files on the go. Available plans For detailed plan information on subscriptions that enable users for Office for the Web, see the Microsoft 365 business plan comparison and Microsoft 365 Enterprise plan comparison .
