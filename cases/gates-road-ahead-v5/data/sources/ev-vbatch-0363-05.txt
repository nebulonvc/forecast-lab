MyLifeBits - Microsoft Research. MyLifeBits Established: November 2, 2001. MylifeBits is a lifetime store of everything. It is the fulfillment of Vannevar Bush’s 1945 Memex vision including full-text search, text & audio annotations, and hyperlinks. There are two parts to MyLifeBits: an experiment in lifetime storage, and a software research effort. The experiment: Gordon Bell has captured a lifetime’s worth of articles, books, cards, CDs, letters, memos, papers, photos, pictures, presentations, home movies, videotaped lectures, and voice recordings and stored them digitally. He is now paperless, and is beginning to capture phone calls, IM transcripts, television, and radio. We have collaborated with the WWMX team to get a mapped UI, and with the SenseCam team to digest and display SenseCam output. External Papers: Gordon Bell and Jim Gemmell, A Digital Life, Scientific American, March 2007.
