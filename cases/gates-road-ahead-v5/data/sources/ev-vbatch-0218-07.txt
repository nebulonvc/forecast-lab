The term bleisure has been a travel trend that has been gaining in popularity over the last few years. The term describes the growing trend in recent years of employees who want to combine business and leisure travel. A U.S. survey found that 84% of travelers wanted to include some vacation time to their next corporate trip , and 48% had taken a bleisure trip in the past year. ( American Hotel & Lodging Association ) 41% of travel managers have noted a rise in colleagues asking to add a longer stay to their work trips for leisure purposes ( GBTA ) 55% of business travellers took at least two blended trips in 2024, and 73% of employees view bleisure as a corporate perk.
