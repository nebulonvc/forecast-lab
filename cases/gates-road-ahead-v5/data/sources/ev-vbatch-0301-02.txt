US beats Singapore and the Netherlands for the top spot in national AI readiness. But 2026 rankings find national AI sophistication alone does not predict corporate AI leadership. The United States has emerged as the world’s leading AI-ready economy while American companies dominate the ranks of global corporate AI leaders, according to two new rankings from IMD. 2026 World Digital and AI Ranking puts Singapore and the Netherlands just after the US in the top three spots.
