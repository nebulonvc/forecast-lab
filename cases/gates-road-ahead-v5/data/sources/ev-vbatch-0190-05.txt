Fact Sheet | October 19, 2026. Americans’ Use of Internet, Broadband in 2026. This analysis is based on findings from the National Public Opinion Reference Survey, a Center survey of 5,511 U.S. adults. The survey was conducted Jan. 30-June 18, 2026. Its overall margin of error is plus or minus 1.8 percentage points. Internet use over time: When the Center began asking Americans about their internet usage in early 2000, about half of all adults were already online. Today, 97% of U.S. adults say they use the internet. Historical share of U.S. adults who say they use the internet: 2000 52%, 2005 68%, 2010 76%, 2015 86%, 2021 93%, 2023 95%, 2024 96%, 2025 96%, 2026 97%.
