The following graph and table are a summary of League statistical information for Broadway shows from the 1980-81 season to the present.
*Beginning with the 2009-10 season, "Gross" represents gross gross and "Attendance" represents total attendance. For seasons prior, these numbers represent net gross and paid attendance, respectively.
** Playing Weeks represent the overall number of weeks of performance logged by all productions during the season.
***Since statistics are reported weekly, the Broadway season of 52 weeks amounts to 364 days. Therefore, a week is added to the Broadway season every seven years.
**** New productions reflect productions that officially opened within that season.
