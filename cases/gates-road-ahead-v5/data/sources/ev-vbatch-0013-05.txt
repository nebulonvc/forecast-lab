Obsoletes: 8478 Facebook Category: Informational February 2021 ISSN: 2070-1721 Zstandard Compression and the 'application/zstd' Media Type Abstract Zstandard, or "zstd" (pronounced "zee standard"), is a lossless data compression mechanism. This document describes the mechanism and registers a media type, content encoding, and a structured syntax suffix to be used when transporting zstd-compressed content via MIME. Despite use of the word "standard" as part of Zstandard, readers are advised that this document is not an Internet Standards Track specification; it is being published for informational purposes only.
