Study on the payment attitudes of consumers in the euro area (SPACE 2024) - European Central Bank (published 2025-02-07).
The 2024 SPACE results are based on information collected directly from 50,000 euro area consumers by the market research company Ipsos in two rounds of online and telephone interviews. The first survey round took place between September and November 2023, while the second was conducted between April and June 2024.
Cash remains the payment method used most frequently in shops, although its use declined further. The share of digital payments continues to increase, but at a slower pace, with cards still being the most popular method. The share of mobile apps is on the rise. Over half of euro area consumers prefer to use cards and other digital payment methods. However, a majority of consumers consider it important to have the option to pay with cash. The possibility to pay with electronic means increased in all euro area countries.
