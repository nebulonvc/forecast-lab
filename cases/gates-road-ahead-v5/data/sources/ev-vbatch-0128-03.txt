PubMed PMID 43002378. Journal of Bioethical Inquiry, 2026-11-09. Title: Constructing Neurorights in the Era of Clinical Brain-Computer Interfaces : China's Emerging Governance Pathway. Abstract: In March 2026, China approved the world's first implantable brain-computer interface (BCI) medical device for clinical use outside clinical trials, a milestone that raises urgent questions about how legal and ethical systems should protect the brain and mind. This article examines China's emerging neurorights governance pathway, which combines the interpretive expansion of civil law personality rights, administrative ethics guidelines, and local legislative experimentation in Shenzhen. Its speed and flexibility are ethically valuable, but its long-term legitimacy depends on transforming ethical principles into enforceable rights, reducing regulatory fragmentation, and institutionalizing meaningful participation by patients, disabled persons, and wider publics.
