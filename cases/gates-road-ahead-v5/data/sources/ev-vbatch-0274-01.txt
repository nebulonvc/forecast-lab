TV Viewing Cools Off in Nielsen’s August Gauge Reports as Sports Take a Breather and School Resumes. Streaming Represents 49.6% of TV Watch-Time. NEW YORK – October 22, 2026 – Television viewing cooled off in August following a two-month surge fueled by the FIFA World Cup 2026™, according to Nielsen’s August 2026 reports of The Gauge™ and Media Distributor Gauge. However, while time spent with each Gauge category declined between July and August, cable and streaming still managed to gain share, capturing 18.9% and 49.6% of television, respectively. YouTube bucked the monthly downturn to capture a platform-record 15.8% of TV as monthly viewing climbed 6%, keeping the streamer in the lead in Nielsen’s Media Distributor Gauge. Broadcast ultimately lost 2.0 share points to finish with 17.5% of TV. Overall cable usage declined 3%, but the category gained 0.2 share points to represent 18.9% of total TV watch-time. The August 2026 interval spanned five weeks, from 07/27/2026 through 08/30/2026.
