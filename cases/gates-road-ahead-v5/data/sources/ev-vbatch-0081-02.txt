Entrepreneur (March 1, 1997): Small Business: Rev It Up. If you want options for upgrading, Cardinal Technologies’ Cardinal Voyager is a 33.6 Kbps modem that is upgradeable to ISDN and will also be upgradeable to 56.6 Kbps when that technology becomes available in the next few months via software downloadable from the Web. The price ranges from $149 to $199, with the ISDN upgrade at $79; prices for the 56.6 Kbps upgrade were not available at press time. Ask your local phone company whether it offers a service called ISDN basic rate interface (BRI). This service provides two 64 Kbps channels that can behave as a single 128 Kbps data pipeline. The BRI does not require any special wiring, but the phone company must configure the switching equipment. ISDN service costs up to $300 for installation, with monthly service charges ranging from $35 to $100, depending on which region of the country you’re in. Connect a digital modem. ISDN, or digital, modems, such as U.S. Robotics’ Sportster ISDN 128K Terminal Adapter, start at about $250. These “modems” are actually network adapters and require an NT-1 unit to complete the connection.
