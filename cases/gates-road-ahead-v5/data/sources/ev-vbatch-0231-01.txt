World map of usage in 2018 2. Equalizing access to college Students’ ability to achieve their goals shouldn’t depend on their families’ ability to pay for test prep. In 2018, 2.3 million students used Khan Academy to prepare for the SAT. 3. Doing more to reach the students who need it most Lack of access to quality education is a persistent barrier to progress in the United States. This lack of opportunity disproportionately affects low-income families and students of color. To help make sure that all students have equal access, Khan Academy partners with teachers and school districts, particularly those who are reaching underserved students. Right now the population of students using Khan Academy mirrors the population of students in the United States. And we want to do even more to bring academic resources to those who need it.
