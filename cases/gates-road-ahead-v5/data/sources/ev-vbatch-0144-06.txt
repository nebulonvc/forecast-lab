Is it time to say goodbye to Zoom fatigue? (CORDIS, European Commission, October 9, 2025). Virtual meetings became the new normal in work settings a few years ago. Now they’re seemingly a daily part of life at the office. A study carried out by Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz (JGU) in Germany explored whether online meetings are more draining than physical ones. The findings were published in the ‘Journal of Occupational Health Psychology’. The research team recruited 125 participants to examine how exhausting online meetings were compared to other types of meetings. The researchers gathered data on 945 meetings, of which 62 % were video meetings. Overall, the results showed that video meetings under 44 minutes were less exhausting than other meetings.
