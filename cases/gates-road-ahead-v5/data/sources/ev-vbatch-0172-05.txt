Pew Research Center: Trends and Facts on Online News | State of the News Media (Published November 10, 2023; citing eMarketer, U.S. Ad Spending Estimates, March 2023). In 2022, according to eMarketer estimates, digital advertising grew to $245 billion, an increase from $221 billion in 2021 and $161 billion in 2020. It was estimated to comprise nearly three-quarters of all advertising revenue (72%), compared with 70% in 2021 and 64% in 2020. Display ad revenue began to level out among all formats except video ads which continued to rise in 2022. Video ads were the largest segment of this market in 2022 at $73.8 billion, growing 17% from the previous year. Banner ad revenue decreased for the first time in 2022 dropping to $47.6 billion from $49.4 billion in 2021. Note: Rich media other ads are those with interactive or audio/video components, but not full video. Sponsorship ads are those in which the advertiser sponsors content.
