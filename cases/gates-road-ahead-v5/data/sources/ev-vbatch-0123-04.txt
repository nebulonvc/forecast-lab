Video as a tool is used by 91% of businesses and is considered integral to the marketing strategy of 93% of marketers. Of course, barriers to adoption still exist for some marketers, with most citing it’s too expensive and that they don’t feel it’s needed – which is certainly a contradiction to the amount of people who rely on video. However, 67% who did not use video marketing last year plan to join the party in 2026.
