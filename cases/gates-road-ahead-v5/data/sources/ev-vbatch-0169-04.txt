Booking Holdings Inc. Form 10-K for the Fiscal Year Ended December 31, 2025 (filed with the U.S. SEC on February 18, 2026). Booking.com is the world's leading brand for booking online accommodation reservations, based on room nights booked, with operations worldwide and headquarters in the Netherlands. At December 31, 2025, Booking.com offered accommodation reservation services for approximately 4.4 million properties in over 220 countries and territories and in over 40 languages, consisting of approximately 500,000 hotels, motels, and resorts and approximately 3.9 million homes, apartments, and other unique places to stay. In 2025, Booking.com offered flights in over 55 markets.
