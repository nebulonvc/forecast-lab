Disney has become the latest studio to announce the end of its home entertainment membership rewards program. Started as Disney Movie Rewards in 2006, and retooled as Disney Movie Insiders in 2019, the program allowed fans who bought tickets to Disney movies in theaters or purchased Disney movies and TV shows on DVD, Blu-ray and digitally to earn points that could be redeemed for merchandise such as gift cards, movies, posters, pins, hats, shirts, books, toys and even studio tours. Disney began informing members at the end of August that the program will sunset on Dec. 4. The ability to earn new points ended Sept. 6, and the last day to redeem them will be Dec. 3, according to the announcement. Member services will shut down Dec. 17. Sony also announced it will close its loyalty program at the end of the year. Universal Pictures shut down its rewards program at the end of July. Disney earlier shut down its Disney Movie Club online membership store in July.
