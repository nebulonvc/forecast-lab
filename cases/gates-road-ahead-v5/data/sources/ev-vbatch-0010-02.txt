News consumption across all platforms
A large majority of U.S. adults (88%) say they at least sometimes get news from digital devices like a smartphone, computer or tablet. This includes 59% who say they do so
– up slightly from 2025, when 56% of Americans said they often get news from digital devices.
Digital devices are by far the most common way Americans get news, although a majority (63%) also get news from television at least sometimes. This includes 31% who say they
get news from TV, a share that has stayed fairly steady over the last few years.
Americans turn to radio and print publications for news less frequently. As of 2026, 12% of U.S. adults say they often get news from radio, and 6% say the same about printed newspapers or magazines. Both of these shares are roughly the same as last year.
