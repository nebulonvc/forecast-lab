Dec. 1, 1997. isdn terminal adapter. The Model 464 internal isdn terminal adapter for PC applications provides 128-kbit/sec data-communications links between users and remote locations. The built-in NT1 network termination allows direct connection to the isdn line through the U-interface. The adapter card includes a 16-bit isa bus interface. List price is $99. Telebyte Technology Inc. Greenlawn, NY.
