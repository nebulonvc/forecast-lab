From: Department for Education Published: 13 January 2026 Get emails about this page Print this page Applies to England What is changing The public switched telephone network ( PSTN ), integrated services digital network ( ISDN ) and copper broadband services will be switched off by January 2027 . The switch-off is industry-led and your local network supplier should contact you well in advance of the switch-off. To prepare for this, schools, colleges and other public-sector estates should audit their sites to identify where these are still used. Where appropriate, they should switch to a digital service, such as voice over internet protocol ( VoIP ).
