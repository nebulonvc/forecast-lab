ESA ESSENTIAL FACTS 2025: 205.1 million Americans now play video games regularly. 83% of U.S. households have played at least one video game device in the past 12 months. Video Game Platforms Played Among Total U.S. Households: Mobile 72%, PC 54%, Console 42%, Virtual Reality 12%. Video Game Platforms Played Among Individual Gamers (Among total players 8+): Mobile 82%, Console 47%, PC 45%, Virtual Reality 10%. GAME COMMUNITIES: 89% of total players (ages 8-90) have played video games online (alone or with others). 72% of total players (ages 8-90) have ever played video games with others (online or offline). 55% of all players (ages 8-90) play video games with others weekly. SPENDING TRENDS: Total Consumer Spending on Video Games in 2024 (Source: Circana): $59.3 billion ($51.3 billion on content, $4.9 billion on hardware, $3.2 billion on accessories).
