Every figure here was checked on the page or document it comes from on 14 November 2026. The most complete official figure is UNCTAD's: businesses in 43 economies sold almost $27 trillion online in 2022, business and consumer sales combined, and most of it was B2B. In the US in 2022, manufacturers shipped $4.83 trillion of goods through ecommerce and merchant wholesalers sold $3.76 trillion online, together about 76% of US ecommerce, against $997.5 billion for retail (US Census Bureau E-Stats). Globally, UNCTAD estimated B2B was 82% of ecommerce in 2019.
