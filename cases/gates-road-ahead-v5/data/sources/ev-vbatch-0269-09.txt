HOW BIG PICTURE SKIING HELPS YOU IMPROVE
Ski days are precious. If you want to improve you need a simple focus. Video ski tutorials from Tom get results fast. Watch a lesson before skiing and have a plan ready to action on snow.
Most lessons only teach you what it should look like. You will improve faster if you are also guided on what good skiing should feel like. This is backed up by the ecological approach to learning.
Have fun no matter what the conditions
Have something to work on no matter where you ski or what the conditions are like. We have drills exercises that make even the most basic runs interesting or the bad weather days a learning experience.

SPEND MORE TIME MAKING BETTER TURNS
Our days on snow are valuable and we never want to waste a day. You can spend more time making better turns if you focus on learning when you are off snow. The Big Picture Skiing video library contains hundreds of videos on every topic imaginable. Each video contains explanations on how to help you improve along with helpful cue cards to review the key points. Days after a lesson you may have questions you want your instructor to answer. With a Big Picture Skiing membership you can leave a question or comment with Tom and receive an answer to keep you progressing. Using your time wisely allows you to make faster progress. Having your coach at your fingertips gives you flexibility with snow conditions and choosing what to work on based on what the day presents you.
Improve your body awareness
If you want your skiing to feel good you need to learn how to move your body better. We have lessons that help you achieve this.
You could be making the right move, just at the wrong time. Many problems are fixed by addressing when you time a movement.
We have lessons filmed with real skiers like you. Watch them improve giving you the confidence to try the steps yourself.
Become a self sufficient learner
Learn to better analyse your own skiing. This helps you become a more self sufficient learner and to truly understand the mechanics of skiing.
Tom Gellie is an internationally recognised ski coach, biomechanics expert, and founder of Big Picture Skiing - An online learning platform for intermediate to expert skiers. His coaching combines modern ski technique, video analysis, and task based learning grounded in the ecological approach. Tom helps skiers understand how to use ski design, body mechanics, and effective drills to improve carving, control, and all-mountain performance. He's worked with World Cup racers, instructors, and passionate recreational skiers around the world.
