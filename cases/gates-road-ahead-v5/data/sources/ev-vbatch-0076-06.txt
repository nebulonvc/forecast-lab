The transition from TDM to IP technology has been ongoing for over a decade as the subscriptions to voice-only local exchange telephone service (switched access lines) has fallen from nearly 141 million lines in December 2008 to 27 million in June 2022. A linear model predicts that switched access lines will be fully phased out in the near future. See FCC, Voice Telephone Services Report, (Aug. 18, 2023), https://www.fcc.gov/voice-telephone-services-report (Reference row 13 "Local exchange telephone service (Switched Access Lines)" shows that there were 140,958,000 subscriptions in December 2008 which declined steadily year-over-year to 27,207,000 subscriptions in June 2022).
