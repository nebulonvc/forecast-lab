The brains behind the first camera [EN] In-house quality While we were developing our first network cameras, there was no market for image processing chipsets. We made the strategic choice of designing our own and tailoring it for video security purposes. The Axis Real Time Picture Encoder (ARTPEC) was born and could support image processing, compression, video analysis and networking functionalities in an optimized way. By using our own chipset we have been able to master light and stream management in our products. The history of ARTPEC [EN] Open from the core Our AXIS 2100, the first network cameras that used ARTPEC-1, was also revolutionary because of its internal device software that was based on a completely new embedded operation system, today known as embedded Linux. To enable fast and efficient development of a portfolio of products we chose to scrap the proprietary RTOS (Real-Time Operating System) and use μClinux that we believed was right for Axis to grow with.
