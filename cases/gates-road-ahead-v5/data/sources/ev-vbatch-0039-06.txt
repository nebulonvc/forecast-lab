Unlock trusted insights into passenger behavior and preferences
Since 2012, IATA’s Global Passenger Survey (GPS) has been the leading source of global insights into passenger satisfaction, behaviors, and expectations. With over 10,000 responses from 200 countries, GPS provides aviation organizations and key stakeholders with a comprehensive understanding of passenger preferences and emerging trends. Its representative sample is based on global passenger traffic by region, ensuring reliable, actionable insights.
The GPS 2026 Report delivers in-depth insights into passenger behaviors, attitudes, and preferences, segmented by region, traveler type, and demographics. It captures what matters most to today’s travelers, from their views on biometric technology to growing eco-conscious travel trends.
Additionally, GPS reveals what passengers want and need at every key touchpoint—from booking and check-in to security and baggage collection—offering a comprehensive view of the entire passenger journey.
Access trusted passenger insights through a rigorous methodology
GPS stands apart through its rigorous methodology, global reach, and trusted collaboration with IPSOS. As the only survey of its kind, GPS offers invaluable insights to help aviation organizations stay ahead of evolving passenger expectations and enhance the travel experience.
Here are some of our latest insights from GPS 2026:
Data shows that respondents in 2026 are increasingly embracing digital travel solutions while seeking smoother and more efficient journeys. Other insights include:
Biometrics go mainstream: 58% of respondents used biometric identification during their airport journey in the past 12 months, up from 50% in 2025. Satisfaction with biometric use reached a record 87%.
Digital identity gains momentum: 84% of passengers are interested in using a digital passport for online check-in, while 82% would like to share a digital passport with airlines during booking to enable more personalized services.
AI is reshaping travel planning: Nearly half of respondents (49%) have already used AI tools to plan or book travel, with passengers using AI to search for flights, find the best fares, and discover new destinations.

IATA’s GPS is ideal for anyone seeking a clearer picture of aviation travelers’ preferences and behaviors alongside industry trends. GPS users include: airlines, airports, airline suppliers, tourism companies, government agencies, media, academic organizations, and consultants.
How can organizations leverage GPS insights?
The data from the Global Passenger Survey equips airlines, airports, OEMs, tourism companies, governments, media, and academic organizations with the insights needed to understand passenger preferences and satisfaction levels.
By analyzing key touchpoints—from booking to baggage collection—stakeholders across these diverse segments can make informed decisions that guide research projects and enhance strategies. While some may focus on optimizing service offerings, others may leverage these insights to inform policy decisions or academic research, ultimately leading to improved passenger experiences and better alignment with evolving traveler needs.
