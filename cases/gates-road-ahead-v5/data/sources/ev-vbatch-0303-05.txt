World Bank Open Data / ITU Indicator IT.NET.USER.ZS (Individuals using the Internet, % of population, 1995-2023). Across developed countries in Western Europe, North America, Australia, New Zealand, and Japan, internet penetration by 2023 converged to a high band of 85.01% to 99.0%: Norway 99.0%, Switzerland 97.34%, Netherlands 97.01%, Ireland 96.87%, Australia 96.12%, Sweden 95.7%, UK 95.45%, Spain 95.45%, Austria 95.33%, Belgium 94.63%, Canada 94.15%, USA 93.53%, New Zealand 93.33%, Germany 92.48%, Italy 87.03%, France 86.84%, Portugal 85.79%, Greece 85.01%, Japan 85.01% (and South Korea 97.42%). However, rollout milestone timing differed by far more than 1-2 years: the year first reaching >=50% internet users was 2000 (Canada, Norway), 2001 (Australia, New Zealand, Sweden, Switzerland), 2002 (Netherlands, UK, USA), 2003 (Germany), 2004 (Austria, Belgium, Japan), 2006 (Ireland, Spain), 2007 (France), 2010 (Italy, Portugal), and 2011 (Greece). The year first reaching >=70% was 2002 (Norway, Sweden), 2005 (Canada, Netherlands, Switzerland, UK), 2006 (Germany), 2007 (Japan, USA), 2008 (Australia, Austria, France, New Zealand), 2013 (Spain), 2016 (Portugal), and 2018 (Greece, Italy). The year first reaching >=85% ranged from 2006 (Sweden) and 2007 (Netherlands, Norway) to 2016 (Australia, USA), 2021 (France), 2022 (Italy), and 2023 (Greece, Portugal).
