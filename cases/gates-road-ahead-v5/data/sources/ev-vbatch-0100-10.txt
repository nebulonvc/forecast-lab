ic-content-overview-technical-approaches-digital-content PUBLICATIONS Reducing Risks Posed by Synthetic Content An Overview of Technical Approaches to Digital Content Transparency Published November 20, 2024 Author(s) Bilva Chandra , Jesse Dunietz , Kathleen Roberts , Yooyoung Lee , Peter Fontana , George Awad Abstract This report examines the existing standards, tools, methods, and practices, as well as the potential development of further science-backed standards and techniques, for: authenticating content and tracking its provenance; labeling synthetic content, such as using watermarking; detecting synthetic content; preventing generative AI (GAI) from producing child sexual abuse material or producing non-consensual intimate imagery of real individuals (to include intimate digital depictions of the body or body parts of an identifiable individual); testing software used for the above purposes; and auditing and maintaining synthetic content.
