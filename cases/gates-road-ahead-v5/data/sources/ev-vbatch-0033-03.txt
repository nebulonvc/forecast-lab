The display in modern monitors is typically an LCD with LED backlight, having replaced CCFL backlit LCDs by the 2010s. Before the mid-2000s, most monitors used a cathode ray tube (CRT) as the image output technology. A monitor is typically connected to its host computer via DisplayPort, HDMI, USB-C, DVI, or VGA. Monitors sometimes use other proprietary connectors and signals to connect to a computer, which is less common.

Multiple technologies have been used for computer monitors. Until the 21st century, most used cathode ray tubes but they have largely been superseded by LCD monitors.

TFT-LCD is a variant of LCD which is now the dominant technology used for computer monitors.
