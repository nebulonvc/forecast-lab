Market for smart thermostats will reach unit sales of 8.1 million in 2030, with annual revenues exceeding $1.1 billion
Top smart thermostat brands include Google Nest, Honeywell Home (Resideo), and ecobee
Parks Associates’ latest market assessment,
Smart Thermostat Market Assessment 2025
, forecasts that smart thermostat sales will grow over the next five years. In 2030, the US market for smart thermostats is projected to achieve sales of 8.1 million units, with annual revenues exceeding $1.1 billion. Current adoption of current smart thermostats is 16-17% of US internet households, and the top smart thermostat brands purchased in the last year are Google Nest and Honeywell Home.

Smart thermostat adoption is driven by desire for remote control and energy cost concerns: 61% of households cite high energy bills.
37% of smart thermostat owners identify as Early Majority tech buyers, up from 26% in 2020. They are practical buyers, expecting value from their purchases, rather than seeking out new tech like Early Adopters and Innovators.
32% of smart thermostat owners view energy insights in their thermostats today, but this information is likely limited to just HVAC use.
