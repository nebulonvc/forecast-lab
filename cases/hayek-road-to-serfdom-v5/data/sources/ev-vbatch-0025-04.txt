I am now in my fourth month as a member of the MPC, and I would like to thank Bloomberg for hosting this occasion, and providing the venue and opportunity to break my self-imposed vow of silence. At various points over the past few months, I have been asked for my thoughts on this new challenge that I have taken on. I am delighted to have joined a team with an excellent track record of success in economic management to date. While the MPC has been in charge of monetary policy, the UK economy has built on and consolidated the period of low inflation and sustained growth which began in the mid-1990s.
