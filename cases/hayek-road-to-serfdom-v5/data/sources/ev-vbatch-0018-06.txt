The Progress Party focuses on law and order as well downsizing the bureaucracy and the public sector ; it identifies as an economic liberal party which competes with the left to represent the workers of Norway. [ 39 ] [ 40 ] The Progress Party calls for a strict immigration policy , integration of immigrants and for the removal of illegal immigrants or foreigners who commit crimes. During its time in coalition government from 2013, the party oversaw the creation of a Minister for Integration and increased the process of deporting failed asylum seekers or migrants with criminal convictions. [ 41 ] It has been described as anti-immigration ; [ 42 ] nevertheless, the FrP also supports free migration to and from the European Union through the European Economic Area as well as helping refugees through the United Nations Convention Relating to the Status of Refugees .

The Progress Party was founded by Anders Lange in 1973 as an anti-tax protest movement . Its development was greatly influenced by Carl I. Hagen , the party's long-standing leader between 1978 and 2006. [ 43 ] [ 44 ] Siv Jensen served as the party leader between 2006 and 2021. [ 45 ] She was succeeded by her deputy leader, Sylvi Listhaug on 8 May 2021. [ 46 ]

The Progress Party was founded at a meeting at the movie theater Saga Kino in Oslo on 8 April 1973, [ 47 ] attended by around 1,345 people. [ 47 ] An address speech was held by Anders Lange , after whom the party was named the Anders Lange's Party for a Strong Reduction in Taxes, Duties and Public Intervention , commonly known as Anders Lange's Party ( ALP ). [ 48 ] Lange had some political experience from the interwar era Fatherland League and was part of the Norwegian resistance movement during the Second World War . [ 47 ] Since the end of the war, he had worked as an independent right-wing political editor and public speaker. [ 47 ] Lange held his first public speech as chairman of ALP at Youngstorget in Oslo on 16 May the same year. ALP was to a large extent inspired by the Danish Progress Party , [ 49 ] which was founded by Mogens Glistrup . Glistrup also spoke at the event, which gathered around 4,000 attendees. [ 50 ]

Originally, Anders Lange wanted the party to be an anti-tax protest movement rather than a common political party. The party had a brief political platform on a single sheet of paper that on one side listed ten things the party was "tired of", and on the other side ten things that they were in favour of. [ 51 ] The protest was directed against what Lange claimed to be an unacceptable high level of taxes and subsidies . [ 52 ] In the 1973 parliamentary election , the party won 5% of the vote and gained four seats in the Norwegian parliament . The main reasons for the success has later been seen by scholars as a mixture of tax protests, the charisma of Anders Lange, the role of television, the aftermath of the 1972 European Community membership referendum and the political development in Denmark. [ 53 ] The first party conference was held in Hjelmeland Municipality in 1974, where the party established its first political conventions. [ 54 ]
