Arghiri Emmanuel and Unequal Exchange (Monthly Review, published 2025-03-01, DOI: 10.14452/mr-076-10-2025-03_3). Abstract: Torkil Lauesen delves into the legacy of celebrated Arghiri Emmanuel, whose theory of unequal exchange resonates well into the twenty-first century. Introduced in 1962, Emmanuel's critique of Ricardian and neoliberal capitalism further illuminated the Marxist concept value as it relates to global exchange and the ongoing exploitation of the Global South by the Global North.
