The proportion of UK employees who were trade union members rose from 22.0% in 2024 to 22.4% in 2025. The number of employees in the UK who were trade union members rose by 192,000 on the year to 6.6 million. Trade union membership levels as reported by the unions listed in Great Britain reached their peak in 1979 (13.2 million) and declined sharply through the 1980s and early 1990s. From 1996 onwards the rate of decline slowed significantly, with occasional years of slight growth interspersed with the general annual reductions in membership. Union membership as a proportion of employees has fallen from 32.4% in 1995 to 22.4% in 2025. In 2025, the overall proportion of employees who are members of trade unions continued to be significantly higher in the public sector (48.5%) relative to the private sector (12.1%).
