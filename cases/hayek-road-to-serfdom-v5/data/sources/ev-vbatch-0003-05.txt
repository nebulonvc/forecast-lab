When Margaret Thatcher’s Conservatives won the 1979 general election, nationalised industries represented 10% of the economy and 14% of capital investment. After the recent ‘winter of discontent’, the Conservatives were concerned that strong trade unions and public ownership were hampering productivity and profitability.
