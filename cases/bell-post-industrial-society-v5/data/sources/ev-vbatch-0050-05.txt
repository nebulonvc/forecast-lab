Our World in Data, Healthcare Spending (first published June 2017, minor text updates March 2024). The impact that scientific developments had on healthcare expenditure is epitomized in the U.S. experience: in recent decades, as treatment possibilities expanded rapidly, expenditure on healthcare increased - in any way you want to measure it: private and public, both per capita and as a share of gross domestic product. Global expenditure on healthcare as a share of world income has been increasing, steadily but slowly over the course of the last couple of decades. Regionally, high-income countries spend - and have been spending - a much larger share of their GDP on healthcare.
