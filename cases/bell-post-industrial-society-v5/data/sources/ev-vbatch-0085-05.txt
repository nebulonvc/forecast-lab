U.S. Bureau of the Census, Current Population Reports, Series P-25, No. 976, Issued October 1985
Patterns of Metropolitan Areas and County Population Growth: 1980 to 1984
The metropolitan areas continue to grow at about the 1 percent annual rate that prevailed during the 1970's. However, the average annual rate of change for nonmetropolitan areas as now defined has fallen from 1.3 percent per year in the 1970's to only 0.8 percent annually during the 1980-84 period.
This apparently restores a pattern of predominantly metropolitan population growth which had extended for more than a century until the dramatic turnaround of the 1970's.
