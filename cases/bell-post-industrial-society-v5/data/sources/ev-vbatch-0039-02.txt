ERIC Number: EJ265494. Title: Evaluating the 1980 Projections of Occupational Employment. Authors: Carey, Max L.; Kasunic, Kevin. Source: Monthly Labor Review, v105 n7 p22-30 Jul 1982. Abstract: Bureau of Labor Statistics' 1980 estimates were on target for professional and service occupations, fairly accurate for farm, craft, clerical, and sales occupations, but significantly off the mark for managerial and administrative, nonfarm labor, and operative occupations. (JOW)
