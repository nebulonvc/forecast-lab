National Center for Science and Engineering Statistics (NCSES), National Science Foundation (NSF), Doctorate Recipients from U.S. Universities: 2023 (NSF 24-336, released December 2, 2024). Table 1-3 (Selected years, 1993-2023): Economics rose from 930 in 1993 to 1,001 in 1998, 932 in 2003, 1,091 in 2008, 1,183 in 2013, 1,244 in 2018, and 1,290 in 2023; Sociology, demography, and population studies moved from 535 in 1993 to 579 in 1998, 612 in 2003, 624 in 2008, 663 in 2013, 695 in 2018, and 627 in 2023. Table 1-7 (Research doctorate recipients, 2013-23): Economics increased 9.0% from 1,183 in 2013 to 1,290 in 2023; Sociology, demography, and population studies declined 5.4% from 663 in 2013 (peak 762 in 2015) to 627 in 2023. Among U.S. citizens and permanent residents (2013-23), Economics fell 9.5% (505 to 457) and Sociology, demography, and population studies fell 7.7% (535 to 494).
