Shapiro Share X LinkedIn Facebook Bluesky Threads Email Link Working Paper 26669 DOI 10.3386/w26669 Issue Date January 2020 Revision Date November 2021 We measure trends in affective polarization in twelve OECD countries over the past four decades. According to our baseline estimates, the US experienced the largest increase in polarization over this period. Five countries experienced a smaller increase in polarization. Six countries experienced a decrease in polarization. We relate trends in polarization to trends in potential explanatory factors.
