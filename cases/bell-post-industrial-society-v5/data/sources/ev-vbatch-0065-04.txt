U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS), Occupational Projections and Training Data, Bulletin 2020 (April 1979), Chapter 1 and Appendix Table B-1: Employment, 1976 and 1985 (Projected), and average annual openings, by occupation, 1976-85. Chapter 1 states for Professional and technical workers: Employment in this major group is expected to rise from 13.3 to 15.8 million workers, or about 18 percent, between 1976 and 1985. Appendix Table B-1 reports estimated 1976 employment and projected 1985 employment: Engineers: 1,133,000 in 1976 (projected 1,415,000 in 1985, 25.0 percent change). Under Other scientific and technical occupations, Table B-1 lists Drafters: 320,000 in 1976 (projected 420,000 in 1985); Engineering and science technicians: 586,000 in 1976 (projected 760,000 in 1985, 29.9 percent change); Surveyors: 52,000 in 1976 (projected 74,000 in 1985).
