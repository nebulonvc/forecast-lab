U.S. Bureau of the Census, Statistical Abstract of the United States: 1982-83 (103rd edition, December 1982), Section 13: Labor Force, Employment, and Earnings, p. 386, No. 648. Occupation of Employed Workers, by Sex and Race: 1960 to 1981 (Civilians 16 years old and over. Annual averages of monthly figures. Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Employment and Earnings, monthly, and unpublished data). Total employed (1,000): 1960 = 65,778; 1970 = 78,678; 1975 = 85,846; 1979 = 98,824; 1980 = 99,303; 1981 = 100,397. Operatives, except transport (1,000): 1975 = 9,751 (11.4 percent); 1979 = 11,111; 1980 = 10,565; 1981 = 10,540 (10.5 percent). Transport equipment operatives (1,000): 1975 = 3,252 (3.8 percent); 1979 = 3,671; 1980 = 3,531; 1981 = 3,476 (3.5 percent). Combined operatives employment stood at 14,096 thousand in 1980 (down from 14,782 thousand in 1979) and edged down further to 14,016 thousand in 1981. Farm workers (1,000): 1960 = 5,176; 1970 = 3,127 (4.0 percent); 1975 = 2,955 (3.4 percent); 1979 = 2,737; 1980 = 2,741; 1981 = 2,749 (2.7 percent).
