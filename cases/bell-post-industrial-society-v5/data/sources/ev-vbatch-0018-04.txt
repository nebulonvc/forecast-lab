Economic Report of the President, Transmitted to the Congress January 1976, Council of Economic Advisers (p. 79, Source: Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics). UNEMPLOYMENT: The unemployment rate averaged 8.5 percent in 1975, sharply above the 5.6 percent rate in 1974 and the previous post-World War II high of 6.8 percent in 1958. The unemployment rate increased rapidly, from 5.6 percent in the third quarter of 1974 to 8.1 percent in the first quarter of 1975, and reached a peak of 8.7 percent in the second quarter. By the fourth quarter the rate had declined to 8.5 percent.
