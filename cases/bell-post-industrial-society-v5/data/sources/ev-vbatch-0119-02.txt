Regulators' Budget. Published May 19, 2026. Authored by Matias Vesperoni, Finn Dobkin. This report presents data extracted from the Budget of the United States on the outlays and personnel devoted to writing, administering, and enforcing federal regulations. The latest report covers fiscal years 1960 through 2024. While overall regulatory spending and staffing have continued to rise in recent years, this growth has been uneven across agencies and policy areas. Social regulation and homeland security drive most of the expansion, whereas economic regulation grew at a more modest rate over the same period. This report tracks the spending and staffing of 77 federal departments and agencies from Fiscal Year (FY) 1960 to FY 2024. Accompanying dataset regulatory_agency_budget_outlays_by_fy.csv (constant 2012 dollars, millions): FY 1973 total 14178 (economic_regulation 2286, social_regulation 11892); FY 2024 total 83094 (economic_regulation 13156, social_regulation 69937, tsa 8666).
