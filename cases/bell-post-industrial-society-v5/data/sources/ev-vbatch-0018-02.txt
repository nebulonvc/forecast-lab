NBER Working Paper No. 486, June 1980, Victor R. Fuchs, Economic Growth and the Rise of Service Employment. Table 1 presents the distribution of employment by sector in the United States since 1870 and shows its relation to per capita GDP (in 1970 U.S. dollars). Nonetheless, the dramatic fall of Agriculture, the rise and then fall of Industry, and the steady rise of Service employment is clearly discernible. Table 1.
