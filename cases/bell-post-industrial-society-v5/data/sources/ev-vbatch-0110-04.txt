Your Work and Research: Summary • Since 2012, the average estimated time taken away from research by pre-award and post-award requirements related to federally-funded research has increased from 42.3% to 44.3%. • The increase appears to predominantly affect the amount of time spent preparing proposals and interim/final research reports. • Although an estimated 39% of the time spent on proposal preparation may contribute to a PI’s scholarship, 50% or more of proposal preparation time is a complete waste due to rejections.
