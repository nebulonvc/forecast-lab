NBER-CES Manufacturing Industry Database (1958-2018, version 2021a), U.S. Census Bureau Annual Survey of Manufactures and Census of Manufactures compiled by Becker, Gray, and Marvakov (2021), data files nberces5818v1_n2012.csv (364 six-digit 2012 NAICS industries) and nberces5818v1_s1987.csv (459 four-digit 1987 SIC industries), page last updated 2021-03-17. Annual production worker share (prode / emp) under 2012 NAICS: 1973: 76.32% (13,957.4k / 18,287.3k), 1980: 73.11%, 1985: 70.91%, 1990: 70.58%, 1995: 72.05%, 2000: 71.72%, 2005: 70.08%, 2010: 69.23%, 2015: 69.96%, 2018: 71.38% (8,362.3k / 11,714.4k). Under 1987 SIC: 1973: 75.38%, 1980: 71.96%, 1990: 68.99%, 1995: 70.34%, 2000: 71.76%, 2010: 69.24%, 2018: 71.38%. In the NBER-CES Census-based dataset (2012 NAICS), production workers' share of total manufacturing employment (prode / emp) fell from 76.32% in 1973 to 70.58% in 1990, rose to 72.05% in 1995, dropped to 69.23% in 2010, and then rebounded to 71.38% in 2018.
