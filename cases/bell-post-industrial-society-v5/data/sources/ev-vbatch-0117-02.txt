Federal Regulatory Growth — QuantGov (Mercatus Center at George Mason University). Regulatory Growth and Accumulation: The volume of federal restrictions has steadily grown over time. In 1970 there were about 400 thousand restrictive words in the Code of Federal Regulations . Now, there are over 1 million.
