(2017) Employment by economic sector dataset (lastUpdated: 2017-04-24; timespan: 1801-2015) for the United States: In 1973, agriculture employed 3,181,000 (3.82%), industry employed 25,347,000 (30.40%), and services employed 54,846,000 (65.78%) out of 83,374,000 total employed.
