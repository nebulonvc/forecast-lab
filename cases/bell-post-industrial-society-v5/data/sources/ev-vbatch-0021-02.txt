European Parliamentary Technology Assessment (EPTA) Network official page. Welcome to the network of parliamentary technology assessment! The currently 23 members of EPTA give advice to their parliaments on topical issues such as nanotechnology, brain research, mobility pricing, or future energy systems. Their projects use various methods and draw on insights from citizen panels, stakeholders, workshops as well as the foremost experts in the relevant fields. Full Members include Austria, Catalonia, European Parliament, France, Germany, Greece, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Norway, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom; Associate Members include Chile, Council of Europe, Denmark, Japan, Korea, Lithuania, Poland, Portugal, USA, Wallonia. The 2026 EPTA conference and the Council meeting took place in Athens 2-3 November 2026 with the main topic of sustainable urban mobility. New Projects from EPTA members include The potential and risks of using AI agents (DE) and The transformation of law-making through AI-assisted processing of legal norms.
