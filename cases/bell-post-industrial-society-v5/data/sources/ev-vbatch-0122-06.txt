New CPI Data Shows Collision Repair Inflation Significantly Outpacing Consumer Prices. Automotive Body Parts Association (ABPA), October 27, 2025. New federal inflation data shows that collision repair costs are rising significantly faster than everyday consumer expenses. For drivers, insurers, and repair facilities, the trend means higher repair bills, longer cycle times, and greater strain on the collision repair economy. According to the latest U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics data, the All-Items Consumer Price Index (CPI) rose 3.0% year-over-year in September 2025. However, over the same period, the overall category of Motor Vehicle Maintenance and Repair shows a 7.7% year-over-year increase, meaning automotive repair inflation continues to run at more than double the rate of overall consumer inflation. Diving deeper into the subcategories, Motor Vehicle Body Work ticked in at 5.4% and Motor Vehicle Repair by itself was at an 11.5% rate. This widening gap illustrates the disproportionate financial burden facing drivers. CCC's Crash Course Q3 data shows that parts costs continue to be a major driver of repair inflation, with average part prices rising 4.4% year-over-year in March–May 2025 and accelerating to 5.0% in June and 6.6% in July.
