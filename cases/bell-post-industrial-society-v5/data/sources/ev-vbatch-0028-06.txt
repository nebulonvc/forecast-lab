Physical or manual skills, as much in demand as social or analytical skills some three decades ago, are fading in importance. Not coincidentally, employment is rising faster in jobs calling for greater preparation, whether through education, experience or other forms of training. These changes have played out surely and steadily in recent decades. A key factor is the decline in manufacturing employment, by about a third just since 1990.
