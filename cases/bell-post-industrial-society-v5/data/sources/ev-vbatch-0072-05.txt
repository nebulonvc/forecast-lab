National Science Board and National Center for Science and Engineering Statistics (NCSES), Academic Research and Development: Academic R&D in the U.S. R&D Enterprise (NSB-2023-26, published October 5, 2023). In 2021, U.S. academic R&D constituted a greater share of U.S. R&D funded by the federal government—about 28%—than its 11% share of overall national R&D ( Figure URD-1 and Figure URD-2 ). In 2021, academia accounted for nearly 60% of federal funding for basic research, 27% for applied research, and nearly 6% for experimental development (NCSES NP 2021 : Table 7 , Table 8, and Table 9 ). Academia’s share of U.S. R&D exceeded 14% in 2004. Although the amount of academic R&D rose after 2004, it represented 11% of national R&D spending in 2021, continuing a decline in share that began in 2010.
