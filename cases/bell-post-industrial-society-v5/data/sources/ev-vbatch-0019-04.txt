National Science Board, Science & Engineering Indicators, NSB-2024-7 (April 2024): Production and Trade of Knowledge- and Technology-Intensive Industries. Table KTI-1 (page 8): U.S. KTI industries, by value added and share of GDP, 2018-22 (billions of dollars): All manufacturing 2,373.3 (2018) and 2,894.3 (2022); KTI manufacturing 1,295.2 (2018) and 1,594.5 (2022), including Computer, electronic, and optical products (ISIC 26) 308.0 (2018) and 380.0 (2022), Chemicals and chemical products (ISIC 20) 211.1 (2018) and 258.4 (2022), Pharmaceuticals (ISIC 21) 165.6 (2018) and 233.7 (2022). Page 10: KTI services industries' share in total U.S. KTI value added increased from about a third to 45% from 2002 to 2022, with the KTI manufacturing share declining from over two-thirds in 2002 to 55% in 2022. Page 26: China's manufacturing KTI value added accounted for about a third of global manufacturing KTI value added in 2022, compared with 22% in 2012 and 8% in 2002 (Figure KTI-6). In contrast, the United States accounted for around 20% of global manufacturing KTI value added since 2011, down from 25% in 2006 and from about 30% in 2002. The shares for Japan and Germany also declined over this period. Page 27: In terms of specific industries, the United States had the largest 2022 global shares of value added in two KTI manufacturing industries—air and spacecraft (57%) and medical and dental instruments (37%). China had the largest shares in the other six KTI manufacturing industries, including chemicals and chemical products (excluding pharmaceuticals) (37%) and computer, electronic, and optical products (including semiconductors) (31%). The United States continues to lead global value added by KTI services industries, reaching a peak of 40% in 2022. Page 31: China has been the leading KTI manufacturing exporter country since 2008 based on current U.S. dollars, with a share of 20.9% in 2022 ($2.4 trillion), followed by those of the United States and Germany (both with $1.1 trillion).
