U.S. Census Bureau, Census 2000 Brief C2KBR-24: Educational Attainment: 2000 (by Kurt J. Bauman and Nikki L. Graf, Issued August 2003). The education levels of the United States population reached an all-time high, according to Census 2000. Of the 182.2 million people aged 25 and over on April 1, 2000, 80 percent had a high school diploma or more, and 24 percent had completed at least a bachelor’s degree.
