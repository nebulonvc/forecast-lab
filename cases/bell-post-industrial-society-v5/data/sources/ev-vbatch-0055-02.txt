NHE Fact Sheet | CMS NHE Fact Sheet Historical NHE, 2024: NHE grew 7.2% to $5.3 trillion in 2024, or $15,474 per person, and accounted for 18.0% of Gross Domestic Product (GDP).
Medicare spending grew 7.8% to $1,118.0 billion in 2024, or 21 percent of total NHE.
Medicaid spending grew 6.6% to $931.7 billion in 2024, or 18 percent of total NHE.
Private health insurance spending grew 8.8% to $1,644.6 billion in 2024, or 31 percent of total NHE.
The largest shares of total health spending were sponsored by the federal government (31 percent) and the households (28 percent). The private business share of health spending accounted for 18 percent of total health care spending, state and local governments accounted for 16 percent, and other private revenues accounted for 6 percent.
