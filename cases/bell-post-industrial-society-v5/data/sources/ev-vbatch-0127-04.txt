KTI industries have accounted for about a 10th of U.S. gross domestic product (GDP) since 2002 (11% in 2022, or $2.9 trillion of $25.7 trillion in current U.S. dollars, based on S&P Global detailed value-added industry data and BEA GDP statistics) (BEA 2023c; Table KTI-1 ; Table SKTI-1 ; Table SAKTI-2 ). This relatively modest share reflects that the U.S. economy is relatively less manufacturing intensive and that most KTI industries are in the manufacturing sector given its higher R&D intensity (Wolfe 2022). ​ See Thomas (2022) for comprehensive economic statistics on U.S. manufacturing. Indeed, in 2022, manufacturing industries overall accounted for 11.2% of U.S. GDP, compared with 55.6% for total private services .
