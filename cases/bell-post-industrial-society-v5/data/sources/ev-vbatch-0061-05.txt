CHP systems can provide cost savings for industrial and commercial users and substantial emissions reductions for the State of California. Development of fuel cells for commercial use began in earnest in the 1970’s for stationary power and transportation applications. Although several fuel cell designs are under development, only the phosphoric acid fuel cell (PAFC) is commercially available. The price of the most competitive PAFC is still around $3000/kW which is still too high for most industrial and commercial applications.
