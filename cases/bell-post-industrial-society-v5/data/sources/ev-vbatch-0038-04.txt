Council of Economic Advisers, Economic Report of the President (February 1982), p. 266, Table B-29. Noninstitutional population and the labor force, 1929-81 (Thousands of persons 16 years of age and over; annual averages, CPS industry classification). 1974: Civilian labor force = 91,949; Civilian employment total = 86,794; Agricultural employment = 3,515 (4.05 percent of total civilian employment); Nonagricultural employment = 83,279. 1975: Civilian employment total = 85,846; Agricultural employment = 3,408; Nonagricultural employment = 82,438. 1979: Civilian employment total = 98,824; Agricultural employment = 3,347; Nonagricultural employment = 95,477. 1980: Agricultural employment = 3,364 (3.39 percent of total civilian employment of 99,303); Nonagricultural employment = 95,938. 1981: Agricultural employment = 3,368; Nonagricultural employment = 97,030. Under this CPS industry classification, agricultural employment declined from 3,515 thousand (4.05 percent of total civilian employment) in 1974 to 3,364 thousand (3.39 percent of 99,303 thousand) in 1980.
