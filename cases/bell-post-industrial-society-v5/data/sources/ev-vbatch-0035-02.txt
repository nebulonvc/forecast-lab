NBER-CES Manufacturing Industry Database (1958-2018, version 2021a), Becker, Randy A., Wayne B. Gray and Jordan Marvakov (2021), National Bureau of Economic Research and U.S. Census Bureau Center for Economic Studies (CES), page last updated 2021-03-17. This database is a joint effort between the National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER) and U.S. Census Bureau's Center for Economic Studies (CES), containing annual industry-level data from 1958-2018 on output, employment, payroll and other input costs. Aggregated U.S. manufacturing employment (emp, thousands): under 1987 SIC (nberces5818v1_s1987.csv), 1970=18,408.6, 1973=18,871.3, 1979=19,750.4, 1980=19,300.7, 1985=17,502.9; under 2012 NAICS (nberces5818v1_n2012.csv), 1970=17,838.3, 1973=18,287.3, 1979=19,106.0, 1980=18,625.1, 1985=16,812.9. In 1980, total U.S. manufacturing employment in the NBER-CES database was 19,300.7 thousand under 1987 SIC (459 four-digit industries) and 18,625.1 thousand under 2012 NAICS (364 six-digit industries), well below 22 million under both classifications.
