Measuring America: Our Changing Landscape
Change in Rural and Urban Population Size: 1910-2010
Source: U.S. Census Bureau, 1910 to 1990 censuses, www.census.gov/population/censusdata/urpop0090.txt; Census 2000, Table P002; 2010 Census, Table P2.
1960: Urban Population 125,268,750 (69.9%), Rural Population 54,054,425 (30.1%).
1970: Urban Population 149,646,629 (73.7%), Rural Population 53,565,297 (26.3%).
1980: Urban Population 167,050,992 (73.7%), Rural Population 59,494,813 (26.3%).
1990: Urban Population 187,053,487 (75.2%), Rural Population 61,656,386 (24.8%).
2000: Urban Population 222,360,539 (79.0%), Rural Population 59,061,367 (21.0%).
2010: Urban Population 249,253,271 (80.7%), Rural Population 59,492,267 (19.3%).
