U.S. Bureau of the Census, Current Population Reports, Series P-23, No. 45, July 1973: Persons in Engineering, Scientific, and Technical Occupations: 1970 and 1972 (sponsored by the National Science Foundation; 1970 Census and Feb-July 1972 follow-up survey). The net result of changes in employment status between 1970 and 1972 was that a slightly higher proportion of persons in over half of the occupation groups were unemployed in 1972 than in 1970 (see table 1). However, within the eight occupation groups only 12 of the 31 individual occupations also showed an increase in the percent unemployed between 1970 and 1972 with aeronautical and aerospace engineers having the highest unemployment rate in both years (3.8 percent in 1970 and 6.1 percent in 1972).
