National Center for Education Statistics (NCES), Digest of Education Statistics 2022, Table 325.92: Degrees in economics, history, political science and government, and sociology conferred by postsecondary institutions, by level of degree: Selected academic years, 1949-50 through 2020-21. This table was prepared October 2022. Economics degrees conferred: 1972-73 Bachelor's 14,770, Master's 2,225, Doctor's 845; 2020-21 Bachelor's 34,636, Master's 4,779, Doctor's 1,245. Sociology degrees conferred: 1972-73 Bachelor's 35,436, Master's 1,923, Doctor's 583; 2020-21 Bachelor's 26,291, Master's 1,283, Doctor's 573. Earlier benchmark 1967-68: Economics Bachelor's 15,193, Master's 1,916, Doctor's 600; Sociology Bachelor's 21,710, Master's 1,193, Doctor's 367. From 2015-16 to 2020-21, Economics degrees changed +3.5% (Bachelor's), +20.9% (Master's), +7.1% (Doctor's), while Sociology degrees changed -6.1% (Bachelor's), -5.9% (Master's), -11.3% (Doctor's).
