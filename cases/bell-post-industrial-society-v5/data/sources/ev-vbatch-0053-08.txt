Home Research Working Papers The Federal Government's Discretionary… The Federal Government's Discretionary Spending Karen Dynan , Douglas Elmendorf & Theresa Gullo Share X LinkedIn Facebook Bluesky Threads Email Link Working Paper 35257 DOI 10.3386/w35257 Issue Date May 2026 This paper examines federal discretionary spending, including its place in the overall budget, its composition, and the economic and political forces shaping its size. Both defense and nondefense discretionary spending show no trend relative to national output over the past three decades, reflecting underlying factors rather than explicit targets. This stability implies a less favorable fiscal outlook than appears in official projections. Because discretionary spending is generally set annually, it will face continuing pressure from concerns about rising federal debt, a challenge compounded by the erosion of the structured budget process envisioned in the Budget Act fifty years ago.
