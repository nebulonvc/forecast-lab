Activities of U.S. Multinational Enterprises, 2024 Worldwide employment by U.S. multinational enterprises increased 0.9 percent to 44.2 million workers in 2024 (preliminary) from 43.8 million workers in 2023 (revised), according to statistics released today by the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis on the operations and finances of U.S. parent companies and their majority-owned foreign affiliates. Employment in the United States by U.S. parents increased 0.1 percent to 29.9 million workers in 2024. U.S. parents accounted for 67.6 percent of worldwide employment by U.S. multinational enterprises (MNEs), down from 68.1 percent in 2023. Employment abroad by foreign affiliates of U.S. MNEs increased 2.7 percent to 14.3 million workers and accounted for 32.4 percent of employment by U.S. MNEs worldwide. U.S. parents accounted for 21.7 percent of total private industry employment in the United States in 2024, down from 21.9 percent in 2023. Employment by U.S. parents was largest in manufacturing, in “other industries,” led by transportation and warehousing, and in retail trade. Employment abroad by foreign affiliates was largest in India, Mexico, and the United Kingdom. Worldwide current-dollar value added of U.S. MNEs increased 8.0 percent to $7.4 trillion in 2024. Value added by U.S. parents , a measure of U.S. parents’ direct contribution to U.S. gross domestic product, increased 8.5 percent to $5.7 trillion. U.S parents accounted for 21.8 percent of total U.S. private-industry value added, up from 21.2 percent in 2023. Foreign affiliate value added increased 6.5 percent to $1.8 trillion. Value added by foreign affiliates was largest in the United Kingdom, Ireland, and Canada. Worldwide expenditures for property, plant, and equipment of U.S. MNEs increased 7.1 percent to $1.2 trillion. Expenditures by U.S. parents accounted for $968.7 billion, representing 36.5 percent of all U.S. private-business capital expenditures. Expenditures by foreign affiliates accounted for $216.2 billion. Worldwide research and development (R&D) expenditures of U.S. MNEs increased 10.1 percent to $622.7 billion. U.S. parents accounted for expenditures of $536.5 billion, representing 69.6 percent of business R&D performed in the United States. Foreign affiliates accounted for expenditures of $86.2 billion. Additional statistics on the activities of U.S. parent companies and their foreign affiliates , including sales, balance sheet and income statement items, compensation of employees, trade in goods, and more are available on BEA’s website. More industry detail for U.S. parents and more industry and country detail for foreign affiliates are also available on the website. Updates to the statistics Statistics for 2023 were revised to incorporate newly available and revised source data. Preliminary statistics for 2023 were released in August 2025.
