TEMPORARY ASSISTANCE FOR NEEDY FAMILIES (TANF) - Georgetown Center on Poverty and Inequality. REPORT | October 2022: Re-Envisioning TANF: Toward an Anti-Racist Program That Meaningfully Serves Families. The current TANF program provides very little cash assistance and is marked by stark racial disparities, but it has the potential to reduce child poverty, increase economic security, and advance racial equity. REPORT | August 2021: The Lasting Legacy of Exclusion: How the Law that Brought Us Temporary Assistance for Needy Families Excluded Immigrant Families & Institutionalized Racism in our Social Support System. In 1996, the Personal Responsibility and Work Opportunity Act (PRWORA) radically transformed our system of social supports. In addition to decimating cash assistance for families, the law’s immigrant exclusions exacerbated economic and racial inequities and harmed children and families in the 25 years since.
