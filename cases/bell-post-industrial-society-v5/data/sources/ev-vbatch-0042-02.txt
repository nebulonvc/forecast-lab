Table 104.10. Rates of high school completion and bachelor's degree attainment among persons age 25 and over, by race/ethnicity and sex: Selected years, 1910 through 2023
Total, 25 years old and over:
High school completion or higher: 1960: 41.1% | 1970: 55.2% | 1975: 62.5% | 1980: 68.6% (standard error 0.20) | 1985: 73.9%
Bachelor's or higher degree (footnote 4: Data for years prior to 1992 are for persons with 4 or more years of college, even if they did not complete a degree): 1960: 7.7% | 1970: 11.0% | 1975: 13.9% | 1980: 17.0% (standard error 0.16) | 1985: 19.4%
NOTE: For 1960 and prior years, data were collected in April. For later years, data were collected in March. Data are based on sample surveys of the noninstitutionalized population, which excludes persons living in institutions (e.g., prisons or nursing facilities).
SOURCE: U.S. Department of Commerce, Census Bureau, U.S. Census of Population: 1960, Vol. I, Part 1; Current Population Reports, Series P-20, various years; and Current Population Survey (CPS), Annual Social and Economic Supplement, selected years, 1970 through 2023.
