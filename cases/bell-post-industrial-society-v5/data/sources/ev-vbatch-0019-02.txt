U.S. Bureau of the Census, Statistical Abstract of the United States: 1979 (100th Edition), Section 13 Labor Force, Employment, and Earnings, pages 416-417. No. 687. EMPLOYED PERSONS, BY RACE, SEX, AND OCCUPATION: 1972 AND 1978 [Civilians 16 years old and over]. Total employed: 81,702 thousand in 1972 and 94,373 thousand in 1978. Professional, technical, and kindred workers: 11,459 thousand in 1972 and 14,245 thousand in 1978. Computer specialists: 273 thousand in 1972 and 428 thousand in 1978. Engineers: 1,102 thousand in 1972 and 1,265 thousand in 1978 (including Civil 154 and 160; Electrical and electronic 287 and 329; Industrial 170 and 206; Mechanical 191 and 216). Life and physical scientists: 230 thousand in 1972 and 273 thousand in 1978. Social scientists: 141 thousand in 1972 and 255 thousand in 1978. Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Employment and Earnings, monthly, and unpublished data.
