2024 MEASUREMENT EFFORT. A total of 73 health plans, four fee-for-service (FFS) Medicaid states, and Traditional Medicare participated in the 2024 HCPLAN Measurement Effort representing almost 282.9 million or 92.7% of people covered by an insurance plan in the commercial, Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, or Traditional Medicare lines of business (LOB). The 2024 results highlight payments made during CY 2023 for all lines of businesses. In 2023, 28.5% of U.S. health care payments flowed through downside risk contracts (Categories 3B-4) across all lines of business (LOBs). For All LOB, 54.8% of payments were Categories 1 and 2, 16.7% were Category 3A, 14.3% were Category 3B, 14.2% were Category 4, and Categories 3B and 4 together accounted for 28.5% of payments. For Commercial, 60.8% were Categories 1 and 2, 17.6% were Category 3A, 13.4% were category 3B, 8.2% were Category 4, and Categories 3B and 4 together accounted for 21.6% of payments. For Medicare Advantage, 35.7% were Categories 1 and 2, 21.3% were Category 3A, 9% were category 3B, 34% were Category 4, and Categories 3B and 4 together accounted for 43% of payments. For Medicaid, 56.3% were Categories 1 and 2, 22.6% were Category 3A, 11.9% were category 3B, 9.2% were Category 4, and Categories 3B and 4 together accounted for 21.1% of payments. For Traditional Medicare, 58% were Categories 1 and 2, 8.3% were Category 3A, 21.8% were category 3B, 11.9% were Category 4, and Categories 3B and 4 together accounted for 33.7% of payments. Per the HCPLAN APM Framework definitions, Category 1 is Fee-for-service with no link to quality, and Category 2 is Fee-for-service linked to quality.
