U.S. Bureau of the Census, Statistical Abstract of the United States: 1981 (102nd edition, December 1981), Section 13: Labor Force, Employment, and Earnings, p. 401, No. 673. Occupation of Employed Workers, by Sex: 1960 to 1980 (Civilians 16 years old and over. Annual averages of monthly figures. Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Employment and Earnings, monthly). Total employed (in thousands): 1960 = 65,778; 1965 = 71,088; 1970 = 78,627; 1975 = 84,783; 1979 = 96,945; 1980 = 97,270. Farmworkers (in thousands): 1960 = 5,176 (7.9 percent); 1965 = 4,053; 1970 = 3,126 (4.0 percent); 1975 = 2,936; 1979 = 2,703; 1980 = 2,704 (2.8 percent of total employed). Operatives, exc. transport (in thousands): 1975 = 9,637; 1979 = 10,909; 1980 = 10,346 (10.6 percent of total employed). Transport equipment operatives (in thousands): 1975 = 3,219; 1979 = 3,612; 1980 = 3,468 (3.6 percent of total employed). Combined operatives employment in 1980 on the pre-revision CPS basis was 13,814 thousand (14.2 percent of total employed), down from 14,521 thousand in 1979.
