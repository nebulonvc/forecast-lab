Measuring Occupational Prestige on the 2012 General Social Survey (Tom W. Smith and Jaesok Son, NORC at the University of Chicago, GSS Methodological Report No. 122, October, 2014). Each study measured occupational prestige for a large number of occupations on a nationally-representative sample of adults in the United States. On the social-standing ladder, occupational prestige ran from 1 for bottom to 9 for top. A total of 860 occupations were rated.
