National Center for Science and Engineering Statistics (NCSES), National Science Foundation, Doctorate Recipients from U.S. Universities: 2022 (NSF 24-300, published September 28, 2023). The number of research doctoral degrees awarded by U.S. institutions increased from 52,194 in 2021 to 57,596 in 2022, according to the Survey of Earned Doctorates (SED) ( figure 1 ). Since the survey’s inception in 1957, there has been an upward trend in the number of doctorates awarded—with an average annual growth of 3.1% punctuated by periods of slow growth and some declines. Doctorates awarded by U.S. colleges and universities: 1958–2022 (Figure 1 official data table nsf24300-fig001.xlsx, Total research doctorates): 1970: 29,498; 1973: 33,755; 1977: 31,716; 1978: 30,875; 1979: 31,238; 1980: 31,019; 1981: 31,355; 1985: 31,295; 1990: 36,065; 1995: 41,747; 1999: 41,101; 2000: 41,369; 2022: 57,596.
