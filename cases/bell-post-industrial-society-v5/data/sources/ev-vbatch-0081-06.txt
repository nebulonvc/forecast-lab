There’s a strong incentive to caricature Friedman and JM, as it’s easy to push your view of how capitalism should be run if the status quo appears stuck in the dark ages. But the JM article is far more nuanced than commonly portrayed. Advocates of stakeholder capitalism, such as me, should use it to guide us rather than ridiculing it.
What Did Jensen And Meckling Actually Say?
While their focus was on ownership structure, a key starting point was what they viewed as the goal of the firm. It is to maximize shareholder ‘welfare’ (which they sometimes refer to as ‘utility’), not short-term profits. In my
on Friedman, I highlighted how his advocated goal of shareholder value is critically different from short-term profits— shareholder value is the present value of all future cash flows, including those in the very long term.
