Number of teachers in elementary and secondary schools, and faculty in degree-granting postsecondary institutions, by control of institution: Selected years, fall 1970 through fall 2027 (Numbers in thousands). SOURCE: U.S. Department of Education, National Center for Education Statistics, Statistics of Public Elementary and Secondary Day Schools, 1970 and 1975; Higher Education General Information Survey (HEGIS), Fall Staff survey, 1970 and 1975. This table was prepared May 2018. Fall 1970: Total all levels 2,766 (Public 2,373; Private 393); Elementary and secondary teachers 2,292 (Public 2,059; Private 233); Postsecondary faculty 474 (Public 314; Private 160). Fall 1975: Total all levels 3,081 (Public 2,641; Private 440); Elementary and secondary teachers 2,453 (Public 2,198; Private 255); Postsecondary faculty 628 (Public 443; Private 185).
