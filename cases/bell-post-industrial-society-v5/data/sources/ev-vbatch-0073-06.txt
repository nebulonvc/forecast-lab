Research Applied to National Needs, better known as RANN, was an operation that stemmed from the Daddario-Kennedy amendment giving the agency authority to conduct applied research. In response to the amendment, in 1969, the Foundation established a modest applied research program, named Interdisciplinary Research Relevant to Problems of Our Society (IRRPOS), to which Congress appropriated $6 million in fiscal year 1970.
