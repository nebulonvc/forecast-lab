U.S. Bureau of the Census, Statistical Abstract of the United States: 1979 (100th Edition), Section 20 Science, page 625. No. 1055. SCIENTISTS AND ENGINEERS, BY FIELD, EMPLOYMENT STATUS, AND SEX: 1974 TO 1978 [In thousands, except percent. Represents the total science and engineering population in the United States]. Total, all fields: 1974 Total 2,482 (In labor force 2,288); 1976 Total 2,706 (In labor force 2,452); 1978 Total 2,741 (In labor force 2,508; Outside labor force 237). Engineers, total: 1974 Total 1,292 (In labor force 1,228); 1976 Total 1,375 (In labor force 1,268); 1978 Total 1,396 (In labor force 1,285). Scientists, total: 1974 Total 1,190 (In labor force 1,058); 1976 Total 1,330 (In labor force 1,182); 1978 Total 1,347 (In labor force 1,224). Field breakdown in 1974 and 1976: Physical scientists 248 (1974) and 281 (1976); Mathematical scientists 101 and 110; Computer specialists 170 and 179; Environmental scientists 79 and 86; Life scientists 266 and 314; Psychologists 109 and 123; Social scientists 217 and 237. Source of tables 1055 and 1056: U.S. National Science Foundation, U.S. Scientists and Engineers: 1974 (NSF 76-329); Detailed Statistical Tables, U.S. Scientists and Engineers: 1976 (NSF 79-305); and 1978.
