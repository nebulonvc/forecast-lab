The efficiency record of 64.2% was verified in May 2024,together with a second record as the most powerful combined cycle plant of almost 850 MW. In addition, with fast gas turbine ramping of 85 MW/minute, Keadby-2 provides important flexibility to complement non-dispatchable renewable generation. Construction of Keadby-2 began in August 2018 and it was officially handed over in March 2023. It has been calculated to contribute over £1b to the UK economy over its lifetime. At the heart of the gas turbine is the advanced can-annular combustion system (ACE). This is a dry-low emissions system based on lean-premix technology which has been modified from earlier designs.
