Margaret Thatcher's premiership (1979–90) embodied the UK's first neoliberal phase, unleashing market values such as freedom and entrepreneurialism, trade union reform and decreasing state support for faltering industries (Jenkins, 2007 ; Wistow, 2022 ). Whilst neoliberal ideas were once regarded as somewhat outlandish and unrealistic (Marquand and Seldon, 1996 ; Slobodian, 2018 ), as we will see, subsequent neoliberal governments extended Thatcher's reforms into areas of society previously untouched by market logic (Telford and Wistow, 2020 ).
