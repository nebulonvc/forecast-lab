Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco — Total Factor Productivity (Updated 11/9/2026). This site presents a real-time, quarterly series on total factor productivity (TFP) for the U.S. business sector, adjusted for variations in factor utilization – labor effort and capital’s workweek. Nevertheless, the utilization-adjusted quarterly series is an improvement over more “naive” measures of TFP as a high-frequency indicator of technological change.
