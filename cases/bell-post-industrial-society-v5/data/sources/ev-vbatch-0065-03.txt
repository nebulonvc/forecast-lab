National Science Foundation, Division of Information Science and Technology (prepared by King Research Inc.), A Chart Book of Indicators of Scientific and Technical Communication in the United States (December 1977), Figure 4: Estimated number of scientists and engineers (1960-1980). In 1975, there were just over 2 million scientists and engineers in the United States. Slightly more than half (1.1 million) are engineers. Life, social and physical scientists account for about 10 percent each. The remainder in decreasing numbers are computer scientists, psychologists, mathematical, and environmental scientists. A net addition of more than 50 thousand scientists and engineers is expected every year through 1980.
