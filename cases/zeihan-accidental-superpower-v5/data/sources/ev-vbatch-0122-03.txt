Country Reports on Terrorism 2023: Saudi Arabia In 2023 the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia continued to work closely with U.S. and international counterparts to deploy a comprehensive and well-resourced counterterrorism strategy that included strong security measures, threat detection and deterrence, measures to counter terrorist financing, and counter-radicalization efforts. Saudi Arabia hosted the Defeat-ISIS Coalition Ministerial in June and made a substantial financial contribution to the Coalition. There were fewer terrorist attacks in Saudi Arabia in 2023 in comparison with recent years. The threat of Houthi cross-border attacks from the conflict in Yemen was reduced dramatically by the ongoing truce-like conditions in Yemen. However, the Houthis exploited the October 7 Hamas attack on Israel and subsequent Israeli operations in Gaza to initiate a series of long-range aerial attacks against Israel, which posed a threat to Saudi Arabia, as some projectiles entered Saudi airspace. 2023 Terrorist Incidents: Terrorist incidents fell sharply in 2023, even when compared with 2022’s already low number. A truce between Saudi Arabia and the Houthis, beginning in April 2022, largely held through the course of 2023, despite technically expiring in October 2022. Isolated incidents of direct and indirect fire engagements against Saudi border guards and Saudi-led Coalition (SLC) forces stationed near the Saudi-Yemeni border resulted in several deaths to SLC forces, including several Saudi security force personnel. The Houthis initiated long-range aerial attacks against Israel and maritime traffic in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in response to the Israeli incursion into Gaza after the October 7 Hamas attack. Some of the systems launched against Israel crossed Saudi airspace. Notable terrorist attacks in 2023 included the following: On June 28 a lone actor shot and killed a Nepalese security guard at the U.S. Consulate General in Jeddah, before the attacker was himself killed by Saudi security personnel. This attack had no apparent connection to organized terrorism, but the targeting of the consulate suggests the attacker may have had political motives. On September 25 a drone attack against Bahraini forces on the Yemeni border killed five Bahraini soldiers. Legislation, Law Enforcement, and Border Security: No substantial changes were made in 2023.
