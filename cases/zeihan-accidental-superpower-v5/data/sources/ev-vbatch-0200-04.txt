U.S. Geological Survey, Mineral Commodity Summaries, January 2025: NICKEL (Data in metric tons, nickel content, unless otherwise specified). Estimated global nickel mine production decreased to an estimated 3.7 million tons in 2024, even though production in Indonesia increased by an estimated 8%. Production in Australia and the Philippines declined by an estimated 26% and 20%, respectively, after multiple companies reduced or halted production owing to unfavorable market conditions related to declining prices and increased production in Indonesia. In New Caledonia, production decreased by an estimated 52% owing to widespread unrest in addition to reduced global nickel prices. In June, a company began commercial production at a new nickel sulfide mine in Kalumbila, Zambia. World Mine Production (2023 / 2024e): United States 16,400 / 8,000; Australia 149,000 / 110,000; Brazil 82,700 / 77,000; Canada 159,000 / 190,000; China 117,000 / 120,000; Indonesia 2,030,000 / 2,200,000; New Caledonia 231,000 / 110,000; Philippines 413,000 / 330,000; Russia 210,000 / 210,000; Other countries 340,000 / 300,000; World total (rounded) 3,750,000 / 3,700,000.
