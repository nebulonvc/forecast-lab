Additive Manufacturing of Metals and Multi-Material (Fraunhofer IGCV). Complex multi-material components in one assembly step: The production of multi-material parts requires an advanced additive manufacturing process that combines multiple materials in one process. This technology is particularly useful when different parts of a component require different properties, e.g. the combination of conductive materials such as copper or aluminum with insulating ceramics. The multi-material solutions developed at Fraunhofer IGCV aim to integrate different materials into a single component to achieve optimal functionality. Fraunhofer IGCV has developed a three-material processing technique using powder bed fusion (PBF), enabling the production of battery cell housings from aluminum, copper, and ceramic in a single manufacturing step. A key feature of the technique is its ability to incorporate ceramics as electrical insulation alongside conductive metals in a single powder bed fusion process. Although the process involves longer build times and requires postprocessing through milling, the advantages of design flexibility and supply chain agility present a strong alternative to traditional manufacturing methods.
