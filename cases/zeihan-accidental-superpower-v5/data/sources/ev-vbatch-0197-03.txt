National Bureau of Statistics of China, Investment in Real Estate Development from January to October 2026, released 2026-11-17 10:00. From January to October, the investment in real estate development was 5,721.5 billion yuan, a year-on-year decrease of 21.6% (calculated on a comparable basis; see Annotation 5 for details). From January to October, the floor space of buildings under construction by real estate development enterprises was 5,681.64 million square meters, a year-on-year decrease of 13.0%. From January to October, the floor space of newly built commercial buildings sold was 613.58 million square meters, a year-on-year decrease of 13.7%, of which the floor space of residential buildings sold decreased by 14.2%. From January to October, the funds for investment this year for real estate development enterprises were 6,153.0 billion yuan, a year-on-year decrease of 21.9%. Among them, domestic loans were 814.8 billion yuan, down 33.4%.
