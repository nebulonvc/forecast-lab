Office of the United States Trade Representative (USTR) Press Release, November 18, 2026: Today, Ambassador Jamieson Greer delivered virtual remarks for the Responsible Business Alliance's Annual Conference in San Jose, California. The conference convened hundreds of Alliance members, as well as representatives from a wide range of industries, to discuss enhancing supply chain resilience and boosting global competitiveness for American workers and businesses. "As the United States Trade Representative, my mandate is clear: advance a trade policy that puts American workers and businesses first," said Ambassador Greer. "A fundamental part of that agenda is ensuring that American workers and producers can compete on a level playing field."
