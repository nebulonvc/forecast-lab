Ukrainian forces have largely offset Russia’s territorial gains since March 2026 while conducting successful counteroffensive operations on several sectors of the front, according to an assessment by the Institute for the Study of War (ISW). According to ISW on September 12, Ukrainian forces in late 2025 and throughout 2026 have increasingly challenged Russia’s battlefield initiative, marking the first sustained shift of this kind since Ukraine’s Kursk offensive in August 2024. ISW assessed that since March, Ukrainian operations have reduced Russia’s net territorial gains to nearly zero. Ukrainian forces have carried out counteroffensive operations in the Kupiansk, Oleksandrivka, and Lyman directions, disrupting several Russian operational objectives. Russia continues to make advances in some areas. ISW reported limited but significant Russian gains in and around Kostiantynivka, as well as advances east of Sloviansk and Kramatorsk. Earlier, ISW assessed that at Russia’s August 2026 rate of advance, its forces would need until October 2032 to capture the remaining Ukrainian-held territory in the Donetsk region, far beyond Moscow’s stated goal of taking the entire region by the end of 2026.
