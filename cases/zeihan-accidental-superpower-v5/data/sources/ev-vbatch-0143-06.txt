DDG Nordquist: Global trade is adapting, not retreating Global trade has demonstrated remarkable resilience despite geopolitical tensions, supply chain disruptions and trade restrictions, with businesses adapting through diversification and new trading partnerships, Deputy Director-General DJ Nordquist said at the 16th Asian Logistics, Maritime and Aviation Conference in Hong Kong, China, on 16 November. Highlighting stronger-than-expected trade growth and the continued importance of WTO rules, she called for more resilient global supply chains, deeper international cooperation and reforms to ensure the multilateral trading system remains fit for today's challenges. The text of her speech is below.
