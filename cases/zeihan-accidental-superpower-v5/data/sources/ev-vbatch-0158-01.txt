Statistics Canada. Table 17-10-0005-01 Population estimates on July 1, by age and gender (DOI: https://doi.org/10.25318/1710000501-eng, Release date: 2026-09-23). Canada, Total - gender: On July 1, 2014, All ages = 35,434,066; 20 to 39 years = 9,628,265 (27.17% of total); 60 to 64 years = 2,171,320 and 65 years and older = 5,541,888 (15.64%), totaling 7,713,208 aged 60 and older (21.77%).
