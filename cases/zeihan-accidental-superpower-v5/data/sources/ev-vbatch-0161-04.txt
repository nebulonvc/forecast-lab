This HillStudy provides an overview of the distribution of federal government revenues and expenditures among Canada’s provinces between 2015 and 2024, based on Statistics Canada’s provincial and territorial economic accounts. This analysis shows that federal revenues and expenditures generally increased between 2015 and 2024, with the COVID-19 pandemic producing a temporary surge in spending and widening fiscal deficits. In 2024, federal revenues per capita were higher than the national average in only three provinces: Alberta, Ontario and British Columbia. In 2024, per capita federal transfers to persons in British Columbia, Alberta and Ontario were below the national average of $4,123, while Newfoundland and Labrador had the highest level of transfers, at $7,236 per capita. In 2024, the federal government provided a total of $94.6 billion to provinces and territories under these major transfers. Conversely, Ontario, British Columbia and Alberta obtained the lowest amounts per capita. In 2024, Alberta and British Columbia were the only two provinces where federal revenues exceeded federal expenditures.
