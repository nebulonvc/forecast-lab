Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System (2022): 2021 Annual Report — Monetary Policy. Over the first half of 2021, progress on vaccinations has led to a reopening of the economy and strong economic growth, supported by accommodative monetary and fiscal policy. In the first quarter, real gross domestic product (GDP) increased 6.4 percent, propelled by a surge in household consumption and a solid increase in business investment but restrained by a substantial drawdown in inventories as firms contended with production bottlenecks. Despite having increased since February, mortgage rates for households remain near historical lows. The FOMC has continued to keep the target range for the federal funds rate at 0 to 1/4 percent since the previous report. From June 2020 until November 2021, the Federal Reserve expanded its holdings of Treasury securities by $80 billion per month and its holdings of agency mortgage-backed securities by $40 billion per month.
