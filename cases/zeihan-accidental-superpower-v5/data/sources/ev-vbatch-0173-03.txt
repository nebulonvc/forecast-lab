BIS Total Credit Statistics (WS_TC, 2014-Q1 to 2026-Q1): Mexico credit to households and NPISHs (% of GDP, Q.MX.H.A.M.770.A) edged up from 14.2% in 2014-Q1 to 16.9% in 2024-Q4 and 17.4% in 2026-Q1, remaining far below the Euro Area (Q.XM.H.A.M.770.A) at 60.5% in 2014-Q1 and 50.2% in 2026-Q1. In contrast, Mexico credit to general government at nominal value (% of GDP, Q.MX.G.A.N.770.A) rose substantially from 30.6% in 2014-Q1 to 46.0% in 2024-Q4, 49.2% in 2025-Q4, and 50.1% in 2026-Q1.
