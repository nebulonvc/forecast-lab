Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED / U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis NIPA quarterly series, seasonally adjusted annual rate through 2026 Q3): Federal government customs duties (B235RC1Q027SBEA) rose from $36.73 billion in 2014 Q1 and $38.72 billion in 2014 Q4 to $85.36 billion in 2019 Q4, $89.10 billion in 2024 Q4, $270.59 billion in 2025 Q2, $366.65 billion in 2025 Q4, and $275.30 billion in 2026 Q3. Meanwhile, U.S. exports and imports of goods and services (EXPGS + IMPGS) expanded from $5,197.3 billion ($2,343.6B exports + $2,853.7B imports, 30.22% of GDP) in 2014 Q1 to $8,721.2 billion ($3,752.1B exports + $4,969.2B imports, 26.42% of $33,010.0B GDP) in 2026 Q3.
