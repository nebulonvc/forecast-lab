Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, Distributional Financial Accounts (DFA), dfa-generation-levels-detail.csv and dfa-generation-shares.csv (released September 18, 2026; data through 2026:Q2). BabyBoom net worth increased from $45.35 trillion (56.1% of U.S. household net worth) in 2014:Q4 to $97.40 trillion (52.5% of U.S. household net worth) in 2026:Q2. BabyBoom financial assets rose from $38.44 trillion in 2014:Q4 to $77.75 trillion in 2026:Q2; Corporate equities and mutual fund shares held by BabyBoom grew from $10.33 trillion (26.9% of financial assets; 52.5% of all U.S. household equities) in 2014:Q4 to $35.22 trillion (45.3% of financial assets; 54.5% of all U.S. household equities) in 2026:Q2. Meanwhile, BabyBoom Deposits plus Money market fund shares rose from $4.66 trillion (12.1% of financial assets) in 2014:Q4 to $9.60 trillion (12.3% of financial assets) in 2026:Q2, while BabyBoom DC pension entitlements grew from $3.80 trillion (65.6% share of all U.S. DC pensions) in 2014:Q4 to $6.25 trillion (44.0% share) in 2026:Q2. Over the same period (2014:Q4 to 2026:Q2), GenX financial assets expanded from $10.99 trillion to $37.81 trillion (net worth share rising from 15.5% to 25.7%) and Millennial financial assets expanded from $1.52 trillion to $15.76 trillion (net worth share rising from 1.8% to 11.2%).
