U.S. Geological Survey, Mineral Commodity Summaries, January 2025: IRON ORE (Data in thousand metric tons, usable ore, unless otherwise specified). Import Sources (2020–23): Brazil, 47%; Canada, 30%; Sweden, 13%; Bahrain, 3%; and other, 7%. Development of one of the world’s largest high-grade iron ore deposits, located in Guinea, was expected by yearend 2024 after its ownership was split into a partnership that included the Government of Guinea and multiple international steel companies. Production was expected to start in 2025, and a full production rate of 60 million tons per year was expected by 2028. World Mine Production (usable ore, 2023 / 2024e): United States 44,700 / 48,000; Australia 953,000 / 930,000; Brazil 445,000 / 440,000; Canada 59,400 / 54,000; Chile 18,100 / 18,000; China 278,000 / 270,000; India 278,000 / 270,000; Iran 85,400 / 90,000; Kazakhstan 28,900 / 30,000; Mauritania 14,100 / 15,000; Mexico 8,500 / 8,000; Peru 20,900 / 21,000; Russia 90,900 / 91,000; South Africa 63,200 / 66,000; Sweden 28,900 / 28,000; Turkey 16,800 / 18,000; Ukraine 41,700 / 42,000.
