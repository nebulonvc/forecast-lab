Financial Stability Board (27 November 2025): 2025 List of Global Systemically Important Banks (G-SIBs). The Financial Stability Board (FSB), in consultation with the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS) and national authorities, has identified the 2025 list of global systemically important banks (G-SIBs). The list uses end-2024 data, and is based on a methodology agreed upon in July 2018 and implemented for the first time in the end-2021 G-SIB assessment. The list for 2025 includes 29 G-SIBs, the same banks as in the 2024 list but with different allocation of the banks to buckets (see Annex). G-SIBs as of November 2025 allocated to buckets: Bucket 4 (2.5%): JP Morgan Chase; Bucket 3 (2.0%): Bank of America, Citigroup, HSBC, Industrial and Commercial Bank of China; Bucket 2 (1.5%): Agricultural Bank of China, Bank of China, Barclays, BNP Paribas, China Construction Bank, Goldman Sachs, Groupe Crédit Agricole, Mitsubishi UFJ FG, UBS; Bucket 1 (1.0%): Bank of Communications (BoCom), Bank of New York Mellon, Deutsche Bank, Groupe BPCE, ING, Mizuho FG, Morgan Stanley, Royal Bank of Canada, Santander, Société Générale, Standard Chartered, State Street, Sumitomo Mitsui FG, Toronto Dominion, Wells Fargo. One bank has moved to a lower bucket: Deutsche Bank has moved from bucket 2 to bucket 1.
