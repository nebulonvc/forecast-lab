This is a list of the 30 largest container shipping companies as of April 2026, according to Alphaliner, ranked in order of the twenty-foot equivalent unit (TEU) capacity of their fleet. In January 2022, Mediterranean Shipping Company (MSC) overtook Maersk for the container line with the largest shipping capacity for the first time since 1996. Top 5 container shipping companies by fleet capacity and market share (Alphaliner, April 2026): Rank 1: Mediterranean Shipping Company (MSC), Switzerland, 7,318,632 TEU, 1,000 ships, 21.6% market share. Rank 2: Maersk, Denmark, 4,647,588 TEU, 735 ships, 13.7% market share. Rank 3: CMA CGM, France, 4,273,202 TEU, 723 ships, 12.6% market share. Rank 4: COSCO Shipping Lines (COSCO), China, 3,593,746 TEU, 554 ships, 10.6% market share. Rank 5: Hapag-Lloyd, Germany (includes United Arab Shipping Company, UASC), 2,400,946 TEU, 291 ships, 7.1% market share.
