Bank for International Settlements (BIS) Global Liquidity Indicators (WS_GLI) — USD denominated credit (bank loans & debt securities) to non-bank borrowers located outside the US (USD millions, quarterly). Key observations: 2014-Q4: 9,377,884.14; 2018-Q4: 11,565,516.98; 2019-Q4: 12,121,856.96; 2020-Q4: 12,739,073.08; 2021-Q4: 13,465,594.24; 2022-Q4: 12,814,617.52; 2023-Q4: 12,759,309.35; 2024-Q4: 13,127,739.86; 2025-Q4: 14,264,166.94; 2026-Q2: 15,132,723.96. After reaching a local peak of USD 13.47 trillion in 2021-Q4 and contracting/stagnating during 2022-2024, offshore USD non-bank credit broke to new all-time highs of USD 14.26 trillion in 2025-Q4 and USD 15.13 trillion in 2026-Q2.
