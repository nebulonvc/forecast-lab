In nominal USD terms, United States GDP rose from $17.608 trillion in 2014 (21.94% of $80.244 trillion world GDP) to $29.298 trillion in 2024 (26.24% of $111.669 trillion world GDP), widening its lead over the European Union ($15.764T or 19.65% in 2014 to $19.501T or 17.46% in 2024), Japan ($4.986T or 6.21% to $4.190T or 3.75%), and China ($10.675T or 13.30% to $18.730T or 16.77%). In PPP terms (current international dollars), however, the United States share of world GDP declined from 15.84% ($17.608T of $111.150T in 2014) to 14.66% ($29.298T of $199.814T in 2024), whereas China expanded from 15.97% ($17.754T) to 19.14% ($38.239T) and the European Union reached 14.38% ($28.728T). World exports of goods and services increased from $23.860 trillion in 2014 to $32.471 trillion in 2024, and world trade as a percentage of GDP stood at 56.72% in 2024 compared with 58.03% in 2014.
