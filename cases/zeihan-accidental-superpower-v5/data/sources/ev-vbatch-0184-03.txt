In 2022, China's population shrank for the first time since the famine of the Great Leap Forward (1959–1961). China has one of the world's lowest fertility rates, and its population decline is accelerating. By 2050, China's population could lose 250 million people from its 1.4 billion (as of 2026)—nearly three-quarters of the United States' 2026 population (see Figure 1). Figure 1.
