The Guardian (2026-10-14): Russia arrests Japanese journalist on allegations of spying. Russia has arrested a Japanese journalist on the far east island of Sakhalin and accused him of spying, in the latest sign of rising tensions between Moscow and Tokyo. Russian foreign ministry spokesperson Maria Zakharova identified the reporter as Yuki Imai, from the Hokkaido Shimbun newspaper, adding that he had been accused of “conducting espionage activities on Russian territory”. Japan’s foreign ministry summoned the Russian ambassador in Tokyo, Nikolay Nozdrev, over the arrest later on Wednesday. Imai, a reporter at the newspaper’s bureau in the city of Yuzhno-Sakhalinsk on Sakhalin, was reportedly preparing to return to Japan when he was arrested for allegedly collecting information on Russia’s defence capabilities, according to Russia’s Tass news agency. Russia’s FSB security agency claimed the journalist had intended to pass the information on to Japanese intelligence.
