Mexican drug cartels are leading suppliers of cocaine, heroin, methamphetamine, and other illicit narcotics to the United States. The trade fuels rampant corruption and violence in Mexico, contributing to tens of thousands of homicides in the country each year. Since 2006, the United States has provided Mexico with billions of dollars in security and counternarcotics assistance as authorities targeted major cartel leaders. In February 2026, Mexico’s military killed Nemesio Rubén Oseguera Cervantes (“El Mencho”), the most‑wanted leader of Mexico’s Jalisco New Generation Cartel (CJNG), setting off a wave of violence and instability.
