Import-related measures covered USD 2.0 trillion (8.0 per cent)) of world imports in the first half of 2026, while export-related measures covered USD 794 billion (3.2 per cent) in world exports. The report covers trade and trade-related measures up to 30 June 2026, presenting annual data for 2025 and semi-annual data for the period January to June 2026, while also tracking the accumulation of measures introduced since 2009 and still in force. As of 30 June 2026, the stockpile (1) of import-related restrictive and other non-facilitating measures introduced since 2009 and still in force, covered USD 5.4 trillion of trade, equivalent to 21.3 per cent of world imports. The stockpile of such export-related measures reached USD 1.5 trillion, covering 6.3 per cent of world exports. Both figures represent the highest levels recorded since the WTO trade monitoring began.
