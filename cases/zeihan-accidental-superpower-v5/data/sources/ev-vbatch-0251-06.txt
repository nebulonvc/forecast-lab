What is the National Delta Programme? (Government of the Netherlands, National Delta Programme). The government protects the Netherlands, now and in the future, against flooding and ensures there is enough fresh water. In addition, the government is working to make our country climate-resilient and water-robust. Sea levels are rising (possibly faster and faster) and the land is subsiding. Nearly 60 percent of our country could be flooded, including the largest cities and part of the economic centre. Proper protection against floods from the sea, rivers and lakes is therefore important. The Netherlands has to be climate-resilient and water-robust by 2050.
