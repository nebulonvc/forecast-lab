Now, turning to the referendum. Yesterday, Albertans voted overwhelmingly in favour of remaining a province of Canada. The voter turnout was historic and a powerful testimony of the passion Albertans feel for our country and our province. I’d like to thank every Albertan who took the time to exercise their democratic right to vote and the thousands of election workers and volunteers who made this entire process possible. The question of whether Alberta should remain a province within Canada was put on the ballot because of Alberta’s citizen-initiated referendum process. Two petitions were put forward by the people and received hundreds of thousands of signatures from Albertans. Out of respect for that democratic process, that question was added to the referendum. The debate regarding our province’s future in Canada has been many years in the making, as Albertans have struggled to address many long-standing and legitimate grievances with Ottawa. This province wide discussion took place at coffee shops, water coolers, online and at family dinner tables. The debate was passionate at times, but for the most part, the various campaigns and dialogue have been civil and respectful. This is another sign of the strength of our democracy. And at the end of it all, the people of Alberta cast their votes in an open and democratic referendum. And the resulting vote from Albertans has given our government a clear mandate. Alberta will remain a province of Canada. Albertans will continue to be Canadians. And our government will work cooperatively with Albertans, the federal government and our fellow provinces to secure a prosperous future for our province and our country. And I want to make it clear tonight that for as long as I am Premier, I will honour this mandate from Albertans and will not be holding another referendum on this issue. It is time to move forward together as a strong and free Alberta within a united Canada. Make no mistake, our government will continue to boldly assert our province’s autonomy by exercising the provincial rights guaranteed to us under Canada’s Constitution.
