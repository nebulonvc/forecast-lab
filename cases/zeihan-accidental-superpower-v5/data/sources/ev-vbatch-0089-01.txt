Capital Markets Fact Book. Published on: August 13, 2026. The volume of gross activity (purchases and sales) in U.S. securities by foreign investors increased to $176.3 trillion, +31.0% Y/Y in 2025. Section 2 – U.S. Capital Markets: In 2025, long-term fixed income issuance increased by 9.7% Y/Y to $11.5 trillion. In 2025, the value of U.S. households’ liquid financial assets increased 12.1% Y/Y to $80.4 trillion.
