U.S. Geological Survey (USGS), Mineral Commodity Summaries, February 2026, Cement chapter, World Production and Capacity (thousand metric tons, estimated). China cement production was estimated at 1,800,000 thousand metric tons in 2024 and 1,700,000 thousand metric tons in 2025, while China clinker capacity was estimated at 1,900,000 thousand metric tons in 2024 and 1,800,000 thousand metric tons in 2025. By comparison, India cement production was 440,000 in 2024 and 470,000 in 2025 (clinker capacity 380,000 and 400,000), and the United States cement production was 85,000 in 2024 and 84,000 in 2025. World total (rounded) cement production was 3,900,000 thousand metric tons in 2024 and 3,800,000 thousand metric tons in 2025, and world total clinker capacity was 3,800,000 thousand metric tons in both 2024 and 2025.
