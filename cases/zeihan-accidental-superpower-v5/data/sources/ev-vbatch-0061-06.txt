World Bank Open Data API (lastupdated: 2026-11-11): China Exports of goods and services (current US$, NE.EXP.GNFS.CD), Exports of goods and services (% of GDP, NE.EXP.GNFS.ZS), and GDP growth (annual %, NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG), 2014-2025. China's total exports of goods and services expanded by 66.8% from $2,462.83 billion in 2014 ($2,655.61 billion in 2018, $3,705.07 billion in 2022) to $3,828.87 billion in 2024 and a record $4,108.47 billion in 2025 (+7.3% year-on-year despite the steep drop in direct U.S. imports). Exports as a share of China's GDP stood at 23.07% in 2014, 18.77% in 2018, 20.44% in 2024, and 21.07% in 2025. China's annual real GDP growth remained positive throughout the window: 7.46% in 2014, 6.76% in 2018, 2.34% in 2020, 8.57% in 2021, 3.13% in 2022, 5.42% in 2023, 4.96% in 2024, and 4.96% in 2025.
