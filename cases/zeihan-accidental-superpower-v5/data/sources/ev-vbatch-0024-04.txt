The White House, January 20, 2025. WITHDRAWING THE UNITED STATES FROM THE WORLD HEALTH ORGANIZATION. Section 1. Purpose. The United States noticed its withdrawal from the World Health Organization (WHO) in 2020 due to the organization’s mishandling of the COVID-19 pandemic that arose out of Wuhan, China, and other global health crises, its failure to adopt urgently needed reforms, and its inability to demonstrate independence from the inappropriate political influence of WHO member states. In addition, the WHO continues to demand unfairly onerous payments from the United States, far out of proportion with other countries’ assessed payments. Sec. 2. Actions. (a) The United States intends to withdraw from the WHO.
