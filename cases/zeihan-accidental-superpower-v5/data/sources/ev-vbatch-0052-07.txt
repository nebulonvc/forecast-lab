BIS Annual Economic Report 2025 (Published 29 Jun 2025) — At a crossroads: policy challenges in a shifting world. The soft landing for the global economy that was coming into view suddenly seems more elusive. Long-established trade relationships began to fray as a wave of larger than expected tariff announcements in early April hit the global economy. Growth forecasts were cut, and financial markets were jolted as policy uncertainty surged. Even as some of the initial shock has dissipated, a sense of apprehension hangs in the air. Long-standing economic relationships that have sustained global prosperity for decades are now under strain.
