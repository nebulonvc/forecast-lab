Quarterly Population Report Second Quarter 2014 (Alberta Treasury Board and Finance, September 26, 2014). All data are from Statistics Canada. For the fourteenth straight quarter, Alberta led all provinces in population growth. Alberta’s population increased by about 35,000 or 0.86% in the second quarter, to 4,121,692 as of July 1. Year-over-year, Alberta’s population expanded by 2.9%, nearly triple the national rate (1.1%). With a median age of 36 years, Alberta continues to be the youngest among the provinces. Alberta recorded the lowest proportion of seniors (65+) at 11.4% and the highest proportion of working-age (15-64) population at 70.4%. The 13,204 net interprovincial migrants to Alberta between April and June represented the second largest second quarter net inflow on record.
