Imports of Goods: Balance of Payments Basis (BOPGIMP), U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis via FRED (dateModified: 2026-11-04, Millions of Dollars, Monthly). Combined with U.S. goods imports from China (IMPCH), U.S. total goods imports rose from $2,385.5 billion in 2014 and $2,555.7 billion in 2018 to $3,292.5 billion in 2024, $3,451.5 billion in 2025, and $2,810.2 billion in January-September 2026. Over the same period, China's share of U.S. goods imports declined sharply from 19.64% in 2014 and a peak of 21.44% in 2017 (21.07% in 2018) to 16.40% in 2022, 13.76% in 2023, 13.37% in 2024, 8.94% in 2025, and 7.65% in January-September 2026.
