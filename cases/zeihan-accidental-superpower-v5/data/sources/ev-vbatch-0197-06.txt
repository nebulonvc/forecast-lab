National Bureau of Statistics of China, Industrial Production Operation in October 2026, released 2026-11-17 10:00. In October, the total value added of industrial enterprises above the designated size increased by 4.8% year on year in real terms. In terms of three sectors, in October, the value added of the mining industry increased by 0.7% year on year, that of the manufacturing industry increased by 5.4%. Table Industrial Production of Enterprises Above Designated Size in October 2026 (October Y/Y growth %, January-October Y/Y growth %): Manufacturing 5.4% (Jan-Oct 5.7%); Textile Industry 2.0% (Jan-Oct 3.0%); Manufacture of Non-metallic Mineral Products -2.3% (Jan-Oct -2.9%); Smelting and Pressing of Ferrous Metals -1.7% (Jan-Oct 1.4%). Major product outputs: Cement (10,000 tons) October 14,375 (-1.3% Y/Y), Jan-Oct 127,229 (-7.9% Y/Y); Crude Steel (10,000 tons) October 7,083 (-1.6% Y/Y), Jan-Oct 79,561 (-2.8% Y/Y); Rolled Steel (10,000 tons) October 11,161 (-5.1% Y/Y), Jan-Oct 117,348 (-2.6% Y/Y).
