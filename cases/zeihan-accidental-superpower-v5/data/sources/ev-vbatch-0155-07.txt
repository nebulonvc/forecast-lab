Employment figures largely unchanged in 2025 - German Federal Statistical Office (Destatis). Press release No. 001 of 2 January 2026. After record employment last year, number of employed down slightly by 5,000. Employment gains in 2025 only in the service sector; losses recorded in industry and construction. Number of unemployed in 2025 up 161,000 on an annual average. On an annual average, there were roughly 46.0 million persons in employment in 2025 whose place of employment was in Germany (domestic concept). According to a first estimate of the Federal Statistical Office (Destatis), the average number of persons in employment in 2025 was therefore down slightly - by 5,000 (0.0%) - on the previous year. The highest number of persons in employment since German unification in 1990 was recorded in 2024. With the exception of the pandemic year of 2020, the number of persons in employment had risen consistently since 2006.
