European Commission - Press release. Eurobarometer shows record high trust in the EU, and strong support for the euro and a common defence and security policy. Brussels, 28 May 2025. 52% of Europeans trust the EU, the highest result since 2007. The level of trust is highest among young people aged 15-24 (59%). At the same time, 36% of Europeans say they trust their national government and 37% say they trust their national parliament. Three quarters of respondents (75%) - the highest level in more than two decades - say they feel they are citizens of the EU. In addition, more than six in ten EU citizens (62%) are also optimistic about the future of the EU. Almost seven in ten respondents (69%) agree that the European Union is a place of stability in a troubled world. Background: The Standard Eurobarometer 103 (Spring 2025) was conducted between 26 March and 22 April 2025 across the 27 Member States. Overall, 26,368 EU citizens were interviewed face-to-face.
