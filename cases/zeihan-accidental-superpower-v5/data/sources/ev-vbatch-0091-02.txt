Aircraft Carrier Fujian, Commissioned! Source: China Military Online. Time: 2025-11-07 16:47:54. China's PLANS Fujian (Hull 18), the first catapult-assisted aircraft carrier, was officially commissioned on Nov. 5, 2025. With a full-load displacement of over 80,000 metric tons and electromagnetic catapult launch and recovery capabilities, its entry into service marks the dawn of China's three-aircraft-carrier era.
