Eurostat Statistics Explained: Living conditions in Europe - housing (Data extracted: November 2025). In 2024, 68.4% of people in the EU lived in owner-occupied homes, while 31.6% lived in rented accommodation. In 2024, 8.2% of the EU population spent 40% or more of their household disposable income on housing, with the highest rates recorded in Greece (28.9%) and Denmark (14.6%), and the lowest in Cyprus (2.4%) and Croatia (3.7%). In 2024, 9.2% of people in the EU could not afford to keep their homes adequately warm, a decrease from 10.6% in 2023.
