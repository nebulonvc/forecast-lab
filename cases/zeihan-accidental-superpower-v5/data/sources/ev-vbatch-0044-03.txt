The first agreement under the U.S.-Taiwan Initiative on 21st Century Trade was signed on June 1, 2023, and entered into force on December 10, 2024. Pursuant to section 6(d) of the United States-Taiwan Initiative on 21st-Century Trade First Agreement Implementation Act (Act), the U.S. Trade Representative submitted to Congress a report on Taiwan’s implementation of the first agreement. Pursuant to section 6(d)(3) of the Act, the report is available here.

U.S. goods and services (exports plus imports) trade with Taiwan totaled an estimated $283.6 billion in 2025, up 52.9 percent ($98.1 billion) from 2024.

U.S. goods trade (exports plus imports) with Taiwan totaled an estimated $256.1 billion in 2025. U.S. goods exports to Taiwan in 2025 were $54.8 billion, up 28.9 percent ($12.3 billion) from 2024. U.S. goods imports from Taiwan totaled $201.4 billion in 2025, up 73.2 percent ($85.1 billion) from 2024. The U.S. goods trade deficit with Taiwan was $146.6 billion in 2025, a 98.7 percent increase ($72.8 billion) over 2024.

U.S. services trade (exports plus imports) with Taiwan totaled an estimated $27.5 billion in 2025. U.S. services exports to Taiwan in 2025 were $14.9 billion, up 8.6 percent ($1.2 billion) from 2024. U.S. services imports from Taiwan in 2025 were $12.6 billion, down 3.4 percent ($438.0 million) from 2024. The U.S. services trade surplus with Taiwan was $2.3 billion in 2025, a 248.5 percent increase ($1.6 billion) over 2024.
