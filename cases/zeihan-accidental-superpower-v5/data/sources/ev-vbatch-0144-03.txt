EU’s gross domestic product up by 1.5% in 2025. In 2025, real gross domestic product (GDP) of the EU increased by 1.5%, after an increase of 1.1% in 2024. This information comes from data on GDP and main components published by Eurostat today. Compared with 2024, GDP increased in all EU countries in 2025, with the highest increases recorded in Ireland (+12.3%), Malta (+4.0%) and Cyprus (+3.8%). In contrast, the smallest increases were recorded in Germany and Finland (+0.2% each) and Hungary (+0.4%).
