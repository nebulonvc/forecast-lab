6. World nuclear forces — SIPRI Yearbook 2025. At the start of 2025, nine states—the United States, Russia, the United Kingdom, France, China, India, Pakistan, the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea (North Korea) and Israel—together possessed approximately 12 241 nuclear weapons, of which 9614 were considered to be potentially operationally available. The USA and Russia together possess almost 90 per cent of all nuclear warheads, and both have extensive programmes under way to modernize and replace their nuclear warheads as well as their delivery systems and nuclear weapon production facilities. Russia, China, India, Pakistan and North Korea deploy dual-capable missiles and all are believed to be modernizing these capabilities.
