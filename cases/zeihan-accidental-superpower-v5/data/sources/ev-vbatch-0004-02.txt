US Trade 2025: Annual Census Bureau Statistics | CargoFax
The United States imported US$3.41 trillion of goods in 2025, up 4.6% from 2024, and exported US$2.18 trillion, up 5.7%, according to US Census Bureau data compiled by CargoFax. The trade deficit was US$1.23 trillion, compared with a deficit of US$1.20 trillion in 2024.
Annual totals are the sum of the 12 monthly totals in the CargoFax monthly report data, compared with the same 12 months of 2024. US monthly imports peaked in March 2025 at US$342 billion; the lowest month was November 2025, at US$261 billion.
