World Bank World Development Indicators (Indicator FR.INR.LEND: Lending interest rate %, 2014-2025; lastupdated 2026-11-11): High-inflation developing economies recorded double-digit or much higher lending rates — Argentina (ARG) rose from 24.01% in 2014 to 95.89% in 2023 and 46.02% in 2025; Brazil (BRA) stood at 32.01% in 2014, 43.60% in 2023, and 45.33% in 2025; South Africa (ZAF) peaked at 11.62% in 2024 (10.69% in 2025); Mexico (MEX) peaked at 11.59% in 2023 and eased to 8.79% in 2025. However, major Asian developing economies remained in single digits: China (CHN) at 4.35% through 2024, India (IND) at 8.57% in 2022, Indonesia (IDN) declining from 12.61% in 2014 to 8.50% in 2025, Malaysia (MYS) at 4.82% in 2025, and Thailand (THA) at 4.99% in 2025. Reporting developed economies remained in low single digits: South Korea (KOR) peaked at 5.19% in 2023 (4.19% in 2025) and Switzerland (CHE) peaked at 2.96% in 2024 (2.82% in 2025).
