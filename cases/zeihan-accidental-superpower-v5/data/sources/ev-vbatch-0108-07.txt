Kiel Institute for the World Economy, Ukraine Support Tracker, Release 31 (Update from October 8, 2026; Country Summary in EUR billion covering bilateral allocations between January 24, 2022 and August 31, 2026 across 41 countries and EU institutions). Total bilateral allocations reached EUR 382.89 billion, including EUR 185.88 billion in military allocations. Major donors led the coalition: United States allocated EUR 115.38 billion total (EUR 64.62 billion military), EU institutions (Commission and Council) allocated EUR 99.77 billion total (EUR 8.87 billion military, EUR 87.61 billion financial), Germany allocated EUR 30.84 billion total (EUR 25.00 billion military), and the United Kingdom allocated EUR 22.80 billion total (EUR 16.18 billion military). By country in the same Country Summary (EUR) table, Sweden allocated EUR 11.33 billion total (EUR 9.27 billion military), Poland allocated EUR 5.89 billion total (EUR 4.45 billion military), and Romania allocated EUR 0.54 billion total (EUR 0.41 billion military), representing EUR 17.75 billion combined or 4.6% of total tracked bilateral allocations.
