Canada Real GDP Per Capita (Drained.ca, rewritten from Statistics Canada’s tables: real GDP per person, Canada beside the United States, and Alberta). Alberta's real GDP per person, in 2017 dollars, was $83,797 in 2014 and $75,017 in 2024, 10.5% lower. Most of the fall came in the 2015-16 oil-price collapse. It fell again by 1.3% in 2023 and 1.5% in 2024, while the population grew 3.9% and 4.6%.
