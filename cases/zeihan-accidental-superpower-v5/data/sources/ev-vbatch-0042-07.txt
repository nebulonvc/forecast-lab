Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering, DoD Additive Manufacturing Strategy, January 15, 2021. The Office of the Secretary of Defense Manufacturing Technology (OSD ManTech) Program Office in the Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering (OUSD(R&E)) released its first-ever comprehensive Additive Manufacturing (AM) Strategy in January 2021. "Additive manufacturing offers DOD unprecedented supply chain agility while enabling our developers to sustain technological dominance for our Warfighters," said Robert Gold, Director of the Technology and Manufacturing Industrial Base (TMIB) Office. The Strategy outlines how AM supports our Nation's continued economic and defense dominance in three key ways: Modernizing national defense systems to improve performance using AM-designed equipment; Increasing materiel readiness to rapidly prototype and produce direct parts, thereby reducing the risk of obsolete hardware; and Enabling Warfighters to employ AM in operational environments.
