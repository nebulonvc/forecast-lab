Ukraine is a sovereign, democratic nation that has decided freely that it wants to join our European family.
Ukraine applied for EU membership at the end of February 2022, only days after Russia launched its full-scale unprovoked and unjustified aggression.
The EU is working with our Ukrainian partners to facilitate the accession process and support them in fulfilling the membership criteria.
