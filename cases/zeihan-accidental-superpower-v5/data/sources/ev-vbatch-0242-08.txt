World Bank Open Data (WDI) cross-country distribution of non-U.S. economies (excluding World Bank regional aggregates; 216 non-U.S. economies in database), comparing 2014 to 2024 and 2025. Real GDP per capita (constant 2015 US$, indicator NY.GDP.PCAP.KD): From 2014 to 2024 across 198 non-U.S. economies with data in both years, 161 economies (81.3%) recorded higher real GDP per capita in 2024 than in 2014, while 37 economies (18.7%) declined. From 2014 to 2025 across 185 non-U.S. economies with 2025 data reported, 156 economies (84.3%) recorded higher real GDP per capita than in 2014, while 29 economies (15.7%) declined. Total Real GDP (constant 2015 US$, indicator NY.GDP.MKTP.KD): From 2014 to 2024 across 198 non-U.S. economies, 182 economies (91.9%) expanded and 16 (8.1%) contracted; from 2014 to 2025 across 185 non-U.S. economies, 174 economies (94.1%) expanded and 11 (5.9%) contracted.
