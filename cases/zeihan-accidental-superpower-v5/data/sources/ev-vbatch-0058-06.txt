World GDP rose from 128,707,260,097,480 ($128.71 trillion) in 2014 to 132,979,475,190,066 in 2015, 137,269,733,552,341 in 2016, 142,488,808,750,999 in 2017, 168,473,707,388,178 in 2023, 174,159,721,560,705 in 2024, and 180,028,791,385,506 ($180.03 trillion) in 2025, a cumulative increase of +39.87%.
