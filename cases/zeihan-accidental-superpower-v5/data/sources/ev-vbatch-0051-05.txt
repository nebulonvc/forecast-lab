World Platinum Investment Council (WPIC), Platinum Quarterly Q3 2026, 18th November 2026 (data prepared by Metals Focus). Total platinum demand is expected to decline by 21% year-on-year to 6,898 koz in 2026f. Net disinvestment of 398 koz, driven by ETF liquidations and exchange stock outflows, accounts for most of the decline, together with weaker jewellery demand.
