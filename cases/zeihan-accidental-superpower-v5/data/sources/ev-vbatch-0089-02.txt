World Bank Open Data API (Indicator CM.MKT.LCAP.CD: Market capitalization of listed domestic companies, current US$; Source: World Federation of Exchanges database). World (1W) total market capitalization increased from $67,177,254,463,959.20 in 2014 to $115,086,929,890,000.00 in 2024 and $141,297,217,290,000.00 in 2025 (+110.3% from 2014 to 2025). United States (US) market capitalization increased from $26,330,589,190,000.00 in 2014 (39.2% of World) to $62,185,685,320,000.00 in 2024 (54.0% of World) and $68,937,866,210,000.00 in 2025 (48.8% of World), far exceeding second-largest China (CN) at $6,004,947,670,000.00 in 2014 and $15,508,858,500,000.00 in 2025 (11.0% of World). Complementarily, World gross capital formation (NE.GDI.TOTL.CD) rose from $20.77 trillion in 2014 to $29.36 trillion in 2024.
