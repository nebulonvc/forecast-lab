Annual Population Report, Alberta 2024-25 (Government of Alberta, Office of Statistics and Information, Treasury Board and Finance, September 25, 2025; Source: Statistics Canada). As of July 1, 2025, Alberta’s population topped five million (5,029,346). Alberta had the highest population growth rate across the country (2.5%), significantly above Canada’s 0.9%. Although interprovincial migration to Alberta moderated compared with the record-setting level set last year, the province continued to be the leading destination for interprovincial migrants, attracting 28,138 newcomers in 2024-25. This was the highest net interprovincial migration among all the provinces and territories this year. On July 1, 2025, the median age for the Canadian population was 40.6 years, while Alberta was younger at 38.1 years. Alberta’s relative youth is a longstanding pattern -the 2024 median age in Canada was 40.3 years, while Alberta was 37.9. Relative to Canada, Alberta’s population is composed of greater proportions of children and working age persons, and a smaller proportion of seniors (Tables 3-4). Despite its relative youthfulness, Alberta’s population is aging. The share of school age children (age 0-14) was 17.4% in 2025, a decline from 17.7% in 2024. The working age (age 15-64) constituted a share of 67.0% in 2025, a slight decrease from 67.2% in 2024, while seniors (age 65+) increased to 15.5% compared to 15.1% in 2024.
