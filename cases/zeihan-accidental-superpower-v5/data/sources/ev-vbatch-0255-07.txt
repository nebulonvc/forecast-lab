World Bank Open Data API (lastupdated: 2026-11-11), Spain (ESP) and Portugal (PRT) official indicators 2014-2025: Total population (SP.POP.TOTL): Spain grew from 46,460,733 in 2014 to 49,355,143 in 2025; Portugal grew from 10,401,062 in 2014 to 10,804,871 in 2025. Net migration (SM.POP.NETM): Spain recorded positive net migration every year from 2016 to 2025 (2019=+464,783; 2022=+299,779; 2023=+119,099; 2024=+111,674; 2025=+96,630); Portugal recorded positive net migration every year from 2017 to 2025 (2021=+69,606; 2022=+68,696; 2023=+29,102; 2024=+20,648; 2025=+16,472). Cereal production (AG.PRD.CREL.MT): Spain dropped from 27,320,900 MT in 2020 to 19,292,230 MT in 2022 and 11,928,280 MT in 2023 during severe drought, then rebounded to 20,862,990 MT in 2024 (above 20,581,146.05 MT in 2014); Portugal moved from 1,333,258 MT in 2014 to 1,017,320 MT in 2024. Food production index (AG.PRD.FOOD.XD, 2014-2016=100): Spain dipped to 98.81 in 2022 and 96.04 in 2023 before rebounding to 108.84 in 2024 (vs 94.50 in 2014); Portugal rose from 93.66 in 2014 to 127.80 in 2024. Food exports vs food imports share of merchandise trade (TX.VAL.FOOD.ZS.UN / TM.VAL.FOOD.ZS.UN): Spain food exports rose from 15.74% in 2014 to 18.65% in 2024 (exceeding food imports at 12.46% in 2024); Portugal food exports rose from 12.18% in 2014 to 14.36% in 2024 (imports 15.22%).
