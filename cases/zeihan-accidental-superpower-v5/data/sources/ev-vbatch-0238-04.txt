Chechen Head Ramzan Kadyrov has instructed his son Adam to oversee waste management in the republic, according to the state-run Chechnya Today news agency, which quoted Kadyrov’s remarks during a live broadcast on Tuesday. The teenager was offered the responsibility of collecting the republic’s bins during the conference, which was held on his 19th birthday.
One of the journalists addressing the Chechen Head during Kadyrov’s annual conference raised residents’ complaints about irregular waste collection, overflowing bins and problems with waste collection charges from Onyx, the regional operator. Kadyrov asked his son to contact the officials responsible and ensure that the situation improved. Adam responded that he would take the necessary measures.
However it is far from his first role in his father’s government. The teenager has served as secretary of Chechnya’s Security Council since April 2025 and at 17 began to lead a group responsible for the security of his father, the head of the republic. He has also been assigned to oversee the work of Chechnya’s Interior Ministry, the Russian University of Special Forces in Gudermes, and two Defence Ministry battalions taking part in Russia’s war against Ukraine.
While Adam Kadyrov has never worked in municipal services, in recent weeks he has been making more frequent public visits to industrial facilities, including a waste-processing complex. In the first three weeks of November alone, he visited such sites in the republic five times. previously he had rarely taken part in such inspections.
For example, on 9 November he inspected the construction site of the Technopark waste-processing complex as part of the work of an interdepartmental commission on environmental safety and on 13 November, he inspected the results of riverbank reinforcement works on the Fortanga River in Achkhoi-Martan.
Waste collection problems in Chechnya are not new. In September 2025, residents of Grozny complained that rubbish had not been collected from some streets for weeks, bins were overflowing and it was difficult to get through to the waste collection service.
