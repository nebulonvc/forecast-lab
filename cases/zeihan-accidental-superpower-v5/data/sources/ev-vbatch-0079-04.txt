Eurostat estimates that 13 773 million people in the EU [ 1 ] , of whom 11 555 million in the euro area ( EA ) [ 2 ] , were unemployed in September 2026. Compared with August 2026, unemployment increased by 105 000 in the EU and by 122 000 in the euro area. Compared with September 2025, unemployment increased by 471 000 in the EU and by 420 000 in the euro area.
In September 2026, the euro area seasonally adjusted unemployment rate was 6.5%, stable compared with August 2026 and up from 6.3% in September 2025. The EU unemployment rate was 6.2% in September 2026, also stable compared with August 2026 and up from 6.0% in September 2025.
In September 2026, 3 022 million young people (under 25 years) were unemployed in the EU, of whom 2 469 million were in the euro area. In September 2026, the youth unemployment rate was 15.5% in the EU, up from 15.4% in the previous month, and 15.4% in the euro area, also up from 15.1% in August 2026. Compared with August 2026, youth unemployment increased by 44 000 in the EU and by 69 000 in the euro area. Compared with September 2025, youth unemployment increased by 45 000 in the EU and by 80 000 in the euro area.
In September 2026, the unemployment rate for women was 6.4% in the EU and the unemployment rate for men was 6.0%, both stable compared with the previous month. In the euro area, the unemployment rate for women was 6.7%, stable compared with August 2026, and the unemployment rate for men was 6.4%, up from 6.3% in the previous month.
Registered unemployment of refugees from Ukraine
After the outbreak of Russian aggression in Ukraine in February 2022, people fleeing the war have been given temporary protection in the EU under the EU Temporary Protection scheme . The rights for beneficiaries of the scheme include access to employment , subject to rules applicable to the profession and to national labour market policies and general conditions of employment.
Eurostat invited EU countries and EFTA countries to provide data on people fleeing the war in Ukraine with respect to their situation on the labour market. In particular, monthly data were collected on those refugees that are registered as unemployed in the national public employment services.
Since April 2022, a significant number of countries have been able to provide data on registered unemployed refugees. The data coverage comprised up to 19 EU countries and Switzerland for July and August 2026, while for September 2026 data were not yet available for some of those countries. In general, data are available for border countries with Ukraine and the largest EU countries, except Italy, although the coverage varies over the months. The data only cover people registered in the national public employment services and not necessarily all people seeking a job at a certain point in time.
