U.S. Census Bureau Press Release (2023-05-25): 2020 Census Demographic Profile and Demographic and Housing Characteristics File (DHC). Between 2010 and 2020, median age in the U.S. grew older due to an increase in the older population. In 2020, there were 55.8 million people age 65 and over in the United States (16.8% of the total population), up 38.6% from 40.3 million in 2010. In 1970, after all the Baby Boomers (1946-1964) had been born, half of the population was younger than 28.1 years old. By 2020, the median age was 38.8, an increase of more than 10 years over the past five decades. In 2020, the population age 45 and over accounted for 42% of the total population, up from 27% in 1940, the census before the Baby Boom began.
