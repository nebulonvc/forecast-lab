French President Emmanuel Macron talks with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy in Paris, France, on July 13, 2026. French President Emmanuel Macron has championed the idea of European strategic autonomy since he rose to power nearly a decade ago. To the repeated chagrin of more than a few of his European and US counterparts, Macron pushed for greater European agency and capabilities at every turn. Next year, he will end his time in the Élysée having won the argument. Whether Europe can finally translate Macron’s vision of strategic autonomy into practice, however, is far from certain. Ukraine will be an important test case. On July 14, Macron chose to mark his final Bastille Day by highlighting Europe’s strategic awakening.
