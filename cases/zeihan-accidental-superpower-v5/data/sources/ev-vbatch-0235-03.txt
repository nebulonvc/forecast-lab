It was the beginning of the Jalisco Cartel New Generation (Cartel de Jalisco Nueva Generación – CJNG ). The group trafficked cocaine, but its core business was methamphetamine. That business went through an unprecedented boom, and, according to the United Nations, amphetamine-type stimulants are consumed by more than 30 million people, ranking it third in global use behind marijuana and opiates, in large part due to the ingenuity, power, and reach of groups like the CJNG.
