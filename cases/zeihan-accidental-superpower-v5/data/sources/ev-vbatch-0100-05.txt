Trends in World Military Expenditure, 2024 — SIPRI Fact Sheet (April 2025). The five biggest spenders in 2024 were the United States, China, Russia, Germany and India, which together accounted for 60 per cent of world military spending. The USA’s military spending was $997 billion in 2024, while China’s was an estimated $314 billion. Russia’s military spending grew by 38 per cent in 2024 to an estimated $149 billion, equivalent to 7.1 per cent of Russia’s GDP.
