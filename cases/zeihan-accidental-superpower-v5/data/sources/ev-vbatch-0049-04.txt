Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, Open Market Operations (FOMC Federal Funds Target Range): After holding the target range at 0-0.25% from December 16, 2008 to December 17, 2015 and cutting back to 0-0.25% on March 16, 2020, the Federal Reserve raised rates sharply throughout 2022 and 2023: March 17, 2022 (0.25-0.50%), May 5, 2022 (0.75-1.00%), June 16, 2022 (1.50-1.75%), July 28, 2022 (2.25-2.50%), September 22, 2022 (3.00-3.25%), November 3, 2022 (3.75-4.00%), December 15, 2022 (4.25-4.50%), February 2, 2023 (4.50-4.75%), March 23, 2023 (4.75-5.00%), May 4, 2023 (5.00-5.25%), and July 27, 2023 (5.25-5.50%). Subsequent adjustments brought the target range to 4.25-4.50% by December 19, 2024, 3.50-3.75% by December 11, 2025, and 3.75-4.00% on September 17, 2026.
