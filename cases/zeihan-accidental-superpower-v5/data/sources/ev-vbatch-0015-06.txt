OECD and BLS labor market series retrieved from Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) from January 2014 through October 2026: United States Employment Rate Aged 55-64 (OECD/FRED LREM55TTUSM156S, 154 monthly observations): rose from 60.75% on 2014-01-01 to 63.75% on 2019-10-01, dipped to 59.36% on 2020-07-01, and climbed to 65.69% on 2026-10-01. Japan Employment Rate Aged 55-64 (OECD/FRED LREM55TTJPM156S, 153 monthly observations): surged from 68.15% on 2014-01-01 to 77.21% on 2019-10-01 and reached 80.30% on 2026-09-01. Euro Area Employment Rate Aged 55-64 (OECD/FRED LREM55TTEZQ156S, 36 quarterly observations): rose from 51.22% in 2014-Q1 to 60.94% in 2019-Q4 and 62.98% in 2022-Q4. By contrast, United States Labor Force Participation Rate 55 Years and Over (BLS/FRED LNS11324230, 154 monthly observations) moved from 39.9% on 2014-01-01 and 40.2% on 2019-10-01 down to 37.2% on 2026-10-01 as older cohorts aged into 65+ and 75+ brackets.
