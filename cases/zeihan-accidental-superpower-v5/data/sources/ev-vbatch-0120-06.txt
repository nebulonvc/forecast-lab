Makkah Defence Alliance to be institutionalised, Pakistan to head secretariat for 3 years: FO (Dawn / Reuters, published August 31, 2026). Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Turkiye have agreed to further institutionalise their cooperation under the Makkah Agreement to strengthen collective deterrence and defence, with the alliance's secretariat to be initially headed by Pakistan for three years, the Foreign Office (FO) said on Monday. The decision was taken at the first meeting of the Strategic Political and Defence Committee of the Makkah Agreement, held in Istanbul. According to the FO, a secretariat headed by a secretary general will be established in Saudi Arabia to support the alliance's work under the agreement. The three countries also agreed to take steps to further institutionalise their cooperation in a manner that would enhance their solidarity, ensure the credibility and effectiveness of their collective deterrence and defence, and contribute to regional efforts and cooperative security towards a just, inclusive and lasting peace. Acting with a sense of regional ownership, the three countries reiterate their determination to work together within the framework of the Makkah Agreement to achieve sustainable peace and stability in their region.
