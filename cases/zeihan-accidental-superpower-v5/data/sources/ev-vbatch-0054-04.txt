The October 2026 Senior Loan Officer Opinion Survey on Bank Lending Practices (SLOOS) addressed changes in the standards, terms, and demand for bank loans to businesses and households over the past three months, which generally corresponds to the third quarter of 2026. Over the third quarter, banks reported that lending standards for commercial and industrial (C&I) loans to large and middle-market firms were basically unchanged, while standards tightened for C&I loans to small firms. In addition, banks reported having eased or left basically unchanged most queried terms for C&I loans to firms of all sizes. As important reasons for weaker demand, banks cited decreasing customer investment in plant or equipment products and customer borrowing shifting to more attractive bank or nonbank funding sources. Foreign banks reported having left standards basically unchanged and stronger demand for C&I loans.
