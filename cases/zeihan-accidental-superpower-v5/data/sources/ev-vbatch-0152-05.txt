Excessive Deficit Procedure and France. France is subject to an Excessive Deficit Procedure. Ongoing procedure: 2024 - Report by France on action taken (as part of its 2026 Annual Progress Report) 07.05.2026; Report by France on action taken (as part of its 2026 Draft Budgetary Plan) 25.11.2025; Report by France on action taken (as part of its 2025 Annual Progress Report) 30.04.2025; 126(7) Council Recommendation with a view to bringing an end to the situation of an excessive deficit / Press release 21.01.2025; 126(7) Commission Recommendation for a Council Recommendation to end the excessive deficit situation 26.11.2024; 126(6) Council decision on the existence of an excessive deficit 26.07.2024; 126(3) Commission report 19.06.2024.
