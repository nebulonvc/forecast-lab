World Bank Open Data API (SIPRI Military Expenditure Database: MS.MIL.XPND.CD current USD, MS.MIL.XPND.GD.ZS % of GDP; Armed forces personnel MS.MIL.TOTL.P1). United States (US) military expenditure (current USD) increased from $647,789,000,000 in 2014 to $1,004,901,000,000 in 2024 and $954,387,000,000 in 2025 (+47.3% compared to 2014). US military expenditure as a share of GDP moved from 3.68% in 2014 to 3.43% in 2024 and 3.12% in 2025, remaining well above the 2025 World average (2.48%) and European Union average (2.18%). European Union (EU) military expenditure more than doubled from $207,250,469,525 in 2014 (1.32% of GDP) to $460,556,066,326 in 2025 (2.18% of GDP). World (1W) total military expenditure rose from $1,757,307,252,758 in 2014 (2.23% of GDP) to $2,856,012,677,498 in 2025 (2.48% of GDP). US armed forces personnel (MS.MIL.TOTL.P1) stood at 1,381,250 in 2014 and 1,395,000 in 2020.
