UNDP Human Development Index (HDI) country-level series via Our World in Data (human-development-index.csv, 2014 to 2023). Across 191 countries with both 2014 and 2023 HDI observations, 175 countries (91.6%) recorded a higher HDI in 2023 than in 2014, 15 countries (7.9%) recorded a lower HDI, and 1 country was unchanged. Sample country values from the CSV: Albania rose from 0.797 (2014) to 0.810 (2023); Algeria rose from 0.732 to 0.763; Australia rose from 0.937 to 0.958; Austria rose from 0.919 to 0.930; Bangladesh rose from 0.600 to 0.685; while Afghanistan edged down from 0.497 to 0.496. Cross-check with Our World in Data / World Bank GDP per capita PPP (gdp-per-capita-worldbank.csv, 2014 to 2025): across 188 countries with both 2014 and 2025 observations, 158 countries (84.0%) had higher real GDP per capita (PPP) in 2025 than in 2014, and 30 countries (16.0%) had lower values.
