U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), Country Analysis Executive Summary: Japan, Last Updated: October 2020. Overview: Japan was the fifth-largest oil consumer and fourth-largest crude oil importer in the world in 2019. Consumption: Japan’s oil consumption was an estimated 3.7 million b/d in 2019, making it the fifth-largest petroleum consumer in the world behind the United States, China, India, and Russia. However, oil demand in Japan fell by more than 1 million b/d between 2012 and 2019 (Figure 2). Structural factors such as a declining and aging population, high energy efficiency measures, and an expanding fleet of hybrid and electric vehicles continue to reduce oil demand. Crude oil imports declined 13% on an annual basis during the first seven months of 2020.
