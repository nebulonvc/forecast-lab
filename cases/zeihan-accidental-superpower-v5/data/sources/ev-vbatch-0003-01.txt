Share of world merchandise trade on MFN tariff terms
The share of world merchandise trade conducted on non-discriminatory most-favoured nation (MFN) terms stands at 72% (as of end-July 2026), showing the continued importance of MFN tariffs in the multilateral trading system. This total is down from 80% at the start of 2025 due to a significant increase in the use of tariff actions in recent years.
Global trade statistics
The WTO’s non-discrimination principle of MFN treatment remains a cornerstone of the global trading system: under MFN terms, any trade advantage granted by a WTO member to one trading partner must be extended immediately and unconditionally to all other members. Enshrined in Article I of the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT 1994) and applicable to all 166 members of the WTO, including separate customs territories, MFN terms promote stability, predictability and fairness in global trade relations.
Analysis conducted by Gonciarz and Verbeet (2025), based on official 2022 imports and tariffs, indicated that the MFN share of global trade stood at just over 80%. An updated analysis, using official 2024 imports and tariff data and taking account of tariff actions recorded up to the end of July 2026, indicates the share is approximately 72%.
Share of trade under MFN terms remains significant
Global merchandise imports can be categorized according to tariff treatment: MFN duty-free trade, MFN dutiable trade, trade eligible for preferential treatment but conducted under MFN terms, trade benefiting from preferential tariffs, trade affected by anti-dumping or countervailing duties, and trade affected by other tariff actions.
