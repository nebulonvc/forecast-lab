Russia's 2021 Census Results Raise Red Flags Among Experts And Ethnic-Minority Activists — RFE/RL (February 05, 2023). According to the official results, even the number of self-reported ethnic Russians declined from 111 million in 2010 to 105.6 million in 2021. In interviews with RFE/RL, census workers confirmed that much of the data provided was fictional, with many entries filled from residency-registration lists leaving the ethnicity question unanswered. Such anecdotal evidence and the census results themselves have prompted many experts to caution against using the 2021 census at all.
