HARBIN -- Daqing Oilfield, China's largest onshore oilfield, said that its oil and gas production at home and abroad had exceeded 40 million tons of oil equivalent in 2022. The figure, which included 30 million tonnes of crude oil and more than 5.5 billion cubic meters of natural gas last year, has been maintained for 20 consecutive years since 2003, according to Daqing Oilfield, a subsidiary of the China National Petroleum Corporation. It has also made new breakthroughs in resources exploration in the basins of Songliao, Sichuan and Tarim.
