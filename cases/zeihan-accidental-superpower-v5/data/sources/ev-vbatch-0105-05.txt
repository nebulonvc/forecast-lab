Poland’s Defence Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz told a news conference in Warsaw that two missiles and a jet-powered drone fell on Polish territory during Russia’s overnight attack on November 20. The missiles came down at Nowy Lipowiec and Rudnik nad Sanem, while the jet-powered drone fell at Molodycz, all in the country’s southeast.

Maybe perhaps allies don’t want to use it too often, because Article 4 is also quite serious. It has been invoked not often, and it loses its value if it’s constantly invoked.”
Analysts say a direct confrontation between Russia and NATO is unlikely for now.
Lucas said the bigger danger is a wider campaign of hybrid, or “grey zone”, warfare, including cyberattacks and disinformation, rather than direct strikes. “That’s where the real threat here is right now, rather than direct Russian strikes,” he said.
