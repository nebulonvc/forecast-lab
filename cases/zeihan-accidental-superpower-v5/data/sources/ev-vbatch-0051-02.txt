FAO Cereal Supply and Demand Brief, Release date : 06/11/2026. At 2 999 million tonnes, world cereal production (including rice in milled equivalent) is forecast to retreat from the 2025 record in 2026, while remaining at the second-highest level on record. World cereal utilization in 2026/27 is forecast at 2 971 million tonnes, up 5.6 million tonnes, or 0.2 percent, from 2025/26. Food consumption accounts for most of the increase, with food use of both wheat and rice rising by just over 4.0 million tonnes, or 0.8 percent and 1.0 percent, respectively, slightly below the pace of global population growth. Overall rice utilization is expected to increase by 0.6 percent to 558.8 million tonnes, exceeding anticipated production.
