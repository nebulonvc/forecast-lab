World Bank Open Data API / SIPRI (lastupdated: 2026-11-11): United States Military expenditure (current US$, MS.MIL.XPND.CD) and Military expenditure (% of GDP, MS.MIL.XPND.GD.ZS), 2014-2025. U.S. military expenditure increased from $647.79 billion (3.68% of GDP) in 2014 and $682.49 billion (3.30% of GDP) in 2018 to $916.01 billion (3.29% of GDP) in 2023, $1,004.90 billion (3.43% of GDP) in 2024, and $954.39 billion (3.12% of GDP) in 2025, demonstrating that American hard power has not become overwhelmingly absent globally.
