It is part of a larger, ongoing effort led by CPRA to adapt Louisiana’s coast in the face of future environmental change. The 2023 Coastal Master Plan is the fourth plan developed by the state to achieve comprehensive coastal restoration and risk reduction goals. As approved by the Louisiana Legislature, this plan directs the state’s coastal activities.
