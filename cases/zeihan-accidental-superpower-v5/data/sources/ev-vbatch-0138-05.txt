PRESS RELEASE Monetary developments in the euro area: December 2024 29 January 2025 Annual growth rate of broad monetary aggregate M3 decreased to 3.5% in December 2024 from 3.8% in November Annual growth rate of narrower monetary aggregate M1 , comprising currency in circulation and overnight deposits, increased to 1.8% in December from 1.5% in November Annual growth rate of adjusted loans to households increased to 1.1% in December from 0.9% in November Annual growth rate of adjusted loans to non-financial corporations increased to 1.5% in December from 1.0% in November
