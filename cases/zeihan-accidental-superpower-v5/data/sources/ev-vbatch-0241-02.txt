Who Has What: Countries with Armed Drones (New America, World of Drones). Over three dozen countries in this database have armed drones, but not all of them produced their UCAVs at home. Many countries seeking armed drone capability without the capacity to develop UCAVs domestically have turned to foreign suppliers such as China. Chinese-made drones have been used extensively to combat extremism outside of China, but the Chinese military has avoided conducting lethal strikes themselves.
