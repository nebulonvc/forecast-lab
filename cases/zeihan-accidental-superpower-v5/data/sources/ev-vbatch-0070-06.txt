World Bank Open Data API (FAO source, lastupdated: 2026-11-11) — Arable land (hectares, indicator AG.LND.ARBL.HA) and Land under cereal production (hectares, indicator AG.LND.CREL.HA), 2014–2024: Between 2014 and 2024, Argentina expanded arable land from 39.93 million ha to 46.84 million ha (+6.91 million ha) and land under cereal production from 12.03 million ha to 17.66 million ha (+5.63 million ha). Brazil expanded arable land from 53.89 million ha to 55.64 million ha (+1.75 million ha) and land under cereal production from 21.85 million ha to 27.78 million ha (+5.93 million ha). Kazakhstan expanded arable land from 29.47 million ha to 30.54 million ha (+1.07 million ha) and cereal land from 14.59 million ha to 16.06 million ha (+1.47 million ha). China expanded land under cereal production from 94.98 million ha to 100.29 million ha (+5.31 million ha), while its arable land changed from 115.90 million ha to 108.47 million ha. Over the same 2014–2024 period, United States arable land declined from 155.96 million ha to 151.55 million ha (-4.41 million ha) and land under cereal production declined from 57.98 million ha to 54.08 million ha (-3.90 million ha).
