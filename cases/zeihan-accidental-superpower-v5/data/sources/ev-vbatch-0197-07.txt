World Steel Association (worldsteel), October 2026 crude steel production, published 24 November 2026. World crude steel production for the 70 countries reporting to the World Steel Association (worldsteel) was 142.4 million tonnes (Mt) in October 2026, a 1.1% decrease compared to October 2025. Top 10 steel-producing countries: China produced 70.8 Mt in October 2026, down 1.6% on October 2025. India produced 14.5 Mt, up 3.6%. The United States is estimated to have produced 7.5 Mt, up 8.4%. Japan produced 6.7 Mt, down 2.3%. Russia is estimated to have produced 5.4 Mt.
