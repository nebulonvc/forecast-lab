My latest analysis,
available at Finances of the Nation
, finds that net outflows from Alberta in 2024 were more than 6 percent of the province’s GDP. That is roughly $30 billion, or over $6,100 per person. It is down from about 8 percent of GDP in 2015, and from a modern peak of over 9 percent nearly 20 years ago.
Graphic credit: Janice Nelson
Still, Alberta stands out. Per person, its net contribution is roughly double that of the next highest province, British Columbia, and many times greater than Ontario’s. You have to go back to the early 1990s to find a time when Ontario contributed more per person than Alberta.
