BTI 2026 China Country Report (Bertelsmann Stiftung). Monopoly on the use of force: score 9 out of 10. China’s administrative system comprises four levels: provincial, prefectural, county and township. Key leadership positions are determined by the CCP’s Organization Department, a key agency of China’s Central Committee. While the central government is able to push through its political priorities, its effectiveness depends on how closely central and local interests align. In areas where interests diverge, such as environmental protection, implementation gaps frequently prevent policies from being fully realized. Under Xi Jinping, policy enforcement has tightened, with enhanced oversight of local implementation.
