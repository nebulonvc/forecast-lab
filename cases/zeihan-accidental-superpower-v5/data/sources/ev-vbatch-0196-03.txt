Energy Production in December 2025 
2026-01-20 10:00 Print | Large | Medium | Small 
In December, the raw coal and crude oil production by industrial enterprises above the designated size remained stable, the production of natural gas grew steadily, and electricity production maintained its growth. 
I. Raw Coal, Crude Oil and Natural Gas Production and Related Information 
The raw coal production remained stable with a slight decrease. In December, the raw coal production by industrial enterprises above the designated size was 440 million tons, a year-on-year decrease of 1.0%. The average daily output was 14.10 million tons. 
From January to December, the raw coal production by industrial enterprises above the designated size was 4.83 billion tons, a year-on-year increase of 1.2%.
