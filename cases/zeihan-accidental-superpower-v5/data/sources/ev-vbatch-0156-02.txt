Press release No. 060 of 25 February 2026. WIESBADEN – The financial deficit (net borrowing) of general government amounted to 119.1 billion euros in 2025 according to provisional calculations. The Federal Statistical Office ( Destatis ) also reports that the government deficit was therefore 3.9 billion euros higher than in the previous year (2024: 115.3 billion euros). Measured as a percentage of gross domestic product (GDP) at current prices, the deficit ratio for 2025 was 2.7% (2024: also 2.7%). The financial deficit of central government, totalling 79.6 billion euros, accounted for around two-thirds of the general government deficit in 2025. The financial deficit of central government increased by 18.6 billion euros compared with the previous year (2024: 60.9 billion euros). In 2025, general government revenue as defined in national accounts amounted to 2,140.2 billion euros. Compared with a year earlier, it therefore increased by 5.7%, or 115.8 billion euros. This development was driven, in particular, by the increase in social contributions, which rose by 8.9%.
