Autumn 2025 Economic Forecast shows continued growth despite challenging environment. 17 November 2025. Key figures: GDP EU: 2025: 1.4%; Euro area: 2025: 1.3%. Inflation EU: 2025: 2.5%; Euro area: 2025: 2.1%. Unemployment EU: 2025: 5.9%; Euro area: 2025: 6.3%. Economic growth exceeded expectations in the first nine months of the year, with real GDP growth outperforming the annual expansion projected in spring. Continued growth in the third quarter is testimony to the resilience of the European economy and its ability to navigate unprecedented shocks.
