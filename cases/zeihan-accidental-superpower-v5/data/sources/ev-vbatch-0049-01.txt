World Bank Press Release (January 6, 2016): Anemic recovery in emerging markets to weigh heavily on global growth in 2016. Global economic growth was less than expected in 2015, when falling commodity prices, flagging trade and capital flows, and episodes of financial volatility sapped economic activity. Developing economies are forecast to expand by 4.8 percent in 2016, less than expected earlier but up from a post-crisis low of 4.3 percent in the year just ended. Growth is projected to slow further in China, while Russia and Brazil are expected to remain in recession in 2016. The South Asia region, led by India, is projected to be a bright spot. There is greater divergence in performance among emerging economies. Economic activity in Russia is projected to contract by 0.7 percent in 2016 after shrinking by 3.8 percent in the year just ended. The current recession in Brazil is expected to extend into 2016 but a return to growth is expected in 2017. Growth in China is forecast to ease further to 6.7 percent in 2016 from 6.9 percent in 2015.
