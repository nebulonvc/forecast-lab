Press Information Bureau, Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation, Government of India. Posted On: 05 JUN 2026 4:00PM by PIB Delhi. PROVISIONAL ESTIMATES OF ANNUAL GROSS DOMESTIC PRODUCT FOR 2025-26 AND QUARTERLY ESTIMATES OF GROSS DOMESTIC PRODUCT FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER (JANUARY-MARCH) OF 2025-26. Provisional Estimates for the Financial Year 2025-26 shows Real GDP Growth Rate of 7.7%. In Q4 (January-March) of FY 2025-26, Real GDP has been estimated to grow by 7.8%. Real GDP or GDP at Constant Prices is estimated to attain a level of ₹323.12 lakh crore in the FY 2025-26, against the First Revised Estimate (FRE) of GDP for the year 2024-25 of ₹299.89 lakh crore. The growth rate in Real GDP during 2025-26 is estimated at 7.7% as compared to 7.1 % in 2024-25.
