Global trade is expanding again, but the forces shaping it are undergoing significant change. Geopolitical tensions, evolving trade policies and rising uncertainty are altering trade patterns and restructuring global value chains. The report shows that trade reached a new high in 2025, even as these structural shifts continue. Global trade in goods and services surpassed $35 trillion in 2025 – a new all-time high. Goods made up roughly three quarters of the total. Services accounted for the remainder. After two years of decline, globalization strengthened in 2025, with the trade-to-GDP ratio rising again. These shifts point to a system that is less uniform and more fragmented, with a growing focus on resilience.
