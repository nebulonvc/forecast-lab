Our World in Data / FAOSTAT Crop Yields dataset (China, 2014-2024; wheat-yields.csv and rice-yields.csv). China wheat yield rose from 5.2435 tonnes per hectare in 2014 to 5.9396 tonnes per hectare in 2024 (+13.3%), and China rice yield rose from 6.8132 tonnes per hectare in 2014 to 7.1545 tonnes per hectare in 2024 (+5.0%). Annual China wheat yield (tonnes/ha): 2014: 5.2435; 2015: 5.3927; 2016: 5.3965; 2017: 5.4841; 2018: 5.4166; 2019: 5.6298; 2020: 5.7421; 2021: 5.8106; 2022: 5.8554; 2023: 5.7811; 2024: 5.9396. Annual China rice yield (tonnes/ha): 2014: 6.8132; 2015: 6.8913; 2016: 6.8657; 2017: 6.9169; 2018: 7.0266; 2019: 7.0601; 2020: 7.0432; 2021: 7.1135; 2022: 7.0796; 2023: 7.1368; 2024: 7.1545.
