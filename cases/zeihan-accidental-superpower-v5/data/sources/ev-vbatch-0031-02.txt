China Economic Update - December 2025. Executive Summary. China’s economy sustained its growth momentum in Q3, supported by resilient exports even as signs of domestic demand weakness reemerged. Real GDP grew by 1.1 percent q/q, seasonally adjusted (sa), in Q3, compared to 1.0 percent in Q2, bringing year-to-date growth to 5.2 percent y/y. Despite trade policy uncertainty, demand from developing countries sustained export growth. Accommodative policies have supported domestic demand this year. However, household spending remains cautious amid a soft labor market and wealth effects from a cooling housing market. There are also signs that the effect of the consumer goods subsidies has diminished, with retail sales increasing by an average of 0.06 percent month-on-month (sa) over July-October, compared with 0.2 percent in Q2. The property sector downturn has deepened in recent months, while manufacturing and infrastructure investment slowed due to profit pressures and tighter local government finances, respectively.
