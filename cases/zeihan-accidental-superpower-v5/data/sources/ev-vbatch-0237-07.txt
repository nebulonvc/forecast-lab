The outcome of the midterm elections in the United States filled the studios of the Russian state media outlets with unadulterated sorrow. During his radio show Full Contact on November 4, state TV host Vladimir Solovyov grimly surmised , “Trumpushka failed.” Appearing on the state TV program Sunday Evening With Vladimir Solovyov last week, the head of RT Margarita Simonyan dramatically exclaimed that US President Donald Trump was America’s last hope. In reality, it seems to be the Kremlin’s last hope of achieving Ukraine’s unconditional capitulation by severing US support. The US still sells weapons to Ukraine and provides intelligence , although military and financial aid was largely terminated in early 2025, and Starlink satellite services continue to be limited , But Moscow wants much more. Pundits and experts explicitly say that they aim to reach and influence the American president , believing their words on state media are conveyed to Trump by the US Embassy in Russia. This causes glee and schadenfreude to Russia’s talking heads.
