The Ukraine Facility is a pivotal instrument within the European Union's strategy to address the multifaceted challenges confronting Ukraine in the wake of Russia's war of aggression. This dedicated support mechanism, which entered into force on 1 March 2024 and covers the years 2024 to 2027, offers up to €50 billion in stable and predictable financial support. The Commission disburses €1.8 billion to Ukraine through the Ukraine Facility, bringing total EU support to Ukraine’s state budget to €24.5 billion.
