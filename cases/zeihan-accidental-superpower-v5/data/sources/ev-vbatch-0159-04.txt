World Bank World Development Indicators (WDI, lastupdated 2026-11-11) demographic aging series (Population ages 65 and above, % of total population, SP.POP.65UP.TO.ZS) for 2014-2025: Canada's share rose continuously from 15.62% in 2014 to 17.53% in 2019, 19.36% in 2023, 19.80% in 2024, and 20.26% in 2025 (an increase of 4.64 percentage points). The United States' share rose from 14.01% in 2014 to 15.67% in 2019, 17.43% in 2023, 17.93% in 2024, and 18.39% in 2025 (an increase of 4.38 percentage points). Throughout 2014-2025 Canada remained older than the United States and the gap widened slightly from 1.61 percentage points to 1.87 percentage points, though the United States also experienced substantial population aging.
