U.S. Imports of Goods by Customs Basis from China (IMPCH), U.S. Census Bureau via FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (dateModified: 2026-11-04, Millions of Dollars, Monthly, Not Seasonally Adjusted). Annualized totals from monthly customs data show U.S. goods imports from China were $468.5 billion in 2014, $483.2 billion in 2015, $462.4 billion in 2016, $505.2 billion in 2017, peaking at $538.5 billion in 2018 (monthly average $44.88 billion), then falling to $449.1 billion in 2019 and $432.5 billion in 2020 before rebounding to $504.2 billion in 2021 and $536.3 billion in 2022. Subsequently, U.S. goods imports from China contracted to $427.2 billion in 2023, $440.3 billion in 2024, and plunged 29.9% to $308.7 billion in 2025 (monthly average $25.72 billion). In January-September 2026, imports totaled $215.0 billion (monthly average $23.89 billion, down 47% from the 2018 monthly average), with monthly values of $23.51B in May, $25.15B in June, $27.07B in July, $27.13B in August, and $31.48B in September 2026.
