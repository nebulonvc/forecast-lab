Trade and Market Share Overview • China’s 2025 calendar-year imports totaled 2.67 MMT of corn, 4.56 MMT of sorghum, 10.45 MMT of barley, and 3.99 MMT of wheat, for a combined 21.67 MMT of the four cereals—down 26 MMT, or 55%, from the 47.8 MMT imported in 2024. Corn recorded the steepest contraction, falling 81% from the previous year.
