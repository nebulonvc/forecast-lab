World Trade Statistics 2025
In 2025, world trade in goods and commercial services, on a balance of payments basis, increased by 8% to US$ 34.89 trillion, up from 4% growth in the previous year. Goods trade grew by 8%, and services trade grew at the same pace. The share of services in global trade reached 27.5%, its highest level since 2005, reflecting the continued resilience and expanding role of services in global trade.
