As we are entering the fifth year of the full-scale war, Ukraine faces unprecedented demographic challenges. Since 2014, the population has declined by an estimated 10 million, and fertility rates have fallen below 1.0. This poses a threat to human capital and the country's sustainable development. UNFPA supports the Ukrainian government and local actors in Ukraine in strengthening its capacity to understand and anticipate the population dynamics, and ensure they have the skills, tools, political will and public support to manage it. This should help Ukraine mitigate potentially negative effects for individuals, society, economy and the environment, and harness the opportunities that come with demographic change. UNFPA is doing this through modernization of national data systems, advancing strategic planning, and implementing inclusive policies on human capital development. Transformation of Demographic Policy: National Demographic Strategy until 2040 In September 2024, the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine officially approved the Strategy of Demographic Development of Ukraine until 2040 , which is developed with UNFPA’s technical and advocacy support, marks a shift from fertility-boosting measures to a holistic demographic resilience approach, including the following key areas: Family-friendly policies and childcare support. Reducing premature mortality and promoting active longevity. Increasing inclusivity of the labor market, including IDP integration. Return migration and reintegration of Ukrainians abroad.
