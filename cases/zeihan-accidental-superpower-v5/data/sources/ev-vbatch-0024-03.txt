European Commission - Economic forecast for Germany. After several years of stagnation, the economy is set to expand by 1.3% in 2026, 1.0% in 2027 and 0.7% in 2028. Meanwhile, increased uncertainty and high inflation are dampening private consumption, and rising labour, energy and financing costs are holding back private investment. Since the COVID-19 pandemic, Germany’s recovery has been among the weakest in advanced economies. In 2025, relatively strong consumption growth was offset by weak exports and private investments, partly reflecting competition from China and the effects of US tariffs. This left economic activity broadly stagnant. In late 2025, the economy began to regain momentum, supported by the ramp-up in public spending.
