STATISTICAL COMMUNIQUÉ OF THE PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA ON THE 2024 NATIONAL ECONOMIC AND SOCIAL DEVELOPMENT (National Bureau of Statistics of China, February 28, 2025). According to preliminary estimates, the gross domestic product (GDP) in 2024 was 134,908.4 billion yuan, up by 5.0 percent over the previous year. At the end of 2024, the national population reached 1,408.28 million, a decrease of 1.39 million over that at the end of 2023. Of this total, urban permanent residents numbered 943.50 million. There were 9.54 million births in 2024 with a crude birth rate of 6.77 per thousand; and there were 10.93 million deaths with a crude death rate of 7.76 per thousand. The natural growth rate was -0.99 per thousand.
