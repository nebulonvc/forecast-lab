The Sinaloa Cartel and Jalisco New Generation Cartel, now designated along with six other transnational criminal organizations as foreign terrorist organizations, are the primary groups producing the illicit synthetic drugs driving U.S. drug poisoning deaths and trafficking these drugs into the United States. The Sinaloa Cartel (Cártel de Sinaloa, aka CDS) is one of the world’s most powerful drug cartels. CDS associates, facilitators, and affiliates operate in almost all 50 U.S. states. U.S.-based affiliates use social media platforms and encrypted messaging applications to advertise and distribute their deadly products. The Jalisco New Generation Cartel (Cártel de Jalisco Nueva Generación, aka CJNG) is one of Mexico’s most powerful, influential, and ruthless transnational criminal organizations. CJNG associates, facilitators, and affiliates operate in almost all 50 U.S. states. Rely on U.S.-based criminal groups for distribution. (Page 38) Transportation: Transported through the SWB and later relying on U.S.-based criminal groups for distribution (Page 56). Mara Salvatrucha, better known as MS-13, is an extremely violent international criminal gang founded in Los Angeles, California in the 1980s by El Salvadoran immigrants. MS-13 members also engage in retail-level drug trafficking, robbery, prostitution, extortion, firearms offenses, and other crimes.
