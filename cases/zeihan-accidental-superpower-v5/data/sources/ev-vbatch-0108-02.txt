Bulgaria and the euro - Economy and Finance - European Commission. Bulgaria joined the European Union on 1 January 2007 and adopted the euro on 1 January 2026. Bulgaria joined the euro area on 1 January 2026. EUR 1 = 1.95583 Bulgarian lev. The dual display of prices in lev and euro became compulsory on 8 August 2025 and will apply until 8 August 2026. In order to protect consumers and address their concerns about unjustified price increases in the changeover period, Bulgaria relies on the strict application of consumer protection law and information campaigns.
