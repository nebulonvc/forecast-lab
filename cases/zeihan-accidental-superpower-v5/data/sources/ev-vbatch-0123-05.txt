U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), Japan Country Analysis Brief Data (Figure 1, 2023): Figure 1. Total petroleum and other liquids consumption (million barrels per day). 2013: 4.524493; 2014: 4.287786; 2015: 4.169181; 2016: 4.036516; 2017: 3.945266; 2018: 3.851586; 2019: 3.763537; 2020: 3.360814; 2021: 3.414942; 2022: 3.368910. Between 2013 and 2020, Japan's total petroleum and other liquids consumption declined by 1.164 million barrels per day (from 4.524 million b/d to 3.361 million b/d), and remained at 3.415 million b/d in 2021 and 3.369 million b/d in 2022.
