National Bureau of Statistics of China, Industrial Capacity Utilization Rate in the Third Quarter of 2026, released 2026-10-20 10:00. In the third quarter of 2026, the capacity utilization rate of industrial enterprises above the designated size nationwide was 73.1%, up by 0.1 percentage points from the second quarter of 2026 and down by 1.5 percentage points from the same period of the previous year. In terms of three sectors, in the third quarter of 2026, the capacity utilization rate of the mining industry was 69.5%; that of the manufacturing industry was 73.3%; that of the production and supply of electricity, heat power, gas and water was 72.6%. In terms of major industries, in the third quarter of 2026, the capacity utilization rate of the textile industry was 75.1%, that of the manufacture of non-metallic mineral products was 61.2%, that of the smelting and pressing of ferrous metals was 75.1%. For the first three quarters of 2026, Industry capacity utilization rate was 73.2% (-1.0 percentage point Y/Y), Manufacturing was 73.5% (-0.9 pp), Textile industry was 75.4% (-2.2 pp), Manufacture of non-metallic mineral products was 59.2% (-2.6 pp), and Smelting and pressing of ferrous metals was 76.9% (-3.2 pp).
