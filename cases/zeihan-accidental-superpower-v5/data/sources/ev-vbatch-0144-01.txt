International trade in goods - an overview. In 2025, the value of trade in goods between EU Member States was 1.6 times as high as the value of trade in goods between the EU and non-EU countries. Both EU exports and imports increased beween 2024 and 2025. Between 2012 and 2016, the value of extra-EU imports fell, while the value of EU exports continued to grow. After 2016, imports also started to grow again, reaching €1 940 billion in 2019. In that same year, exports reached €2 132. In 2020, largely due to the COVID-19 pandemic, both imports (-11.6%) and exports (-9.4%) fell sharply. However, since then, they have more than recovered, with exports reaching €2 646 billion and imports reaching €2 532 billion in 2025.
