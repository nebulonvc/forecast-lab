According to the latest regional economic update, economic growth in Sub-Saharan Africa has remained broadly stable despite heightened geopolitical tensions and regional and domestic headwinds. However, the outlook remains challenging. The conflict in the Middle East, trade policy uncertainty, financial market volatility, cuts in foreign aid, and rising debt-service burdens continue to weigh on growth prospects. Public debt has stabilized, with the median debt-to-GDP ratio at 57% in 2025, but debt vulnerabilities remain high. Debt-service costs continue to put pressure on government budgets, limiting the resources available for infrastructure, human capital, social protection, and other development priorities.
