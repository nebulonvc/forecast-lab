Bangladesh Overview | World Bank (updated October 26, 2026). Bangladesh is highly vulnerable to climate change and natural disasters such as floods and cyclones. The World Bank Group’s Country Partnership Framework (CPF) for Bangladesh for FY2023–2027 supports the country’s ambition to achieve upper-middle-income status. To address disasters and climate risks, $1.4 billion from the World Bank has strengthened resilience for about 6.7 million people through shelters, roads, embankments, protective infrastructure, and afforestation.
