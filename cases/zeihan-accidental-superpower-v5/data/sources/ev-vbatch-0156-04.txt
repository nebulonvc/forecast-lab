Eurostat Euro indicators, 21 October 2026: Government debt at 88.8% of GDP in euro area. At the end of the second quarter of 2026, the general government gross debt to GDP ratio in the euro area (EA21) stood at 88.8%, an increase compared with 88.4% at the end of the first quarter of 2026. In the EU , the ratio also increased from 82.5% to 82.9%. Compared with the second quarter of 2025, the government debt to GDP ratio increased in both the euro area (from 87.3% to 88.8%) and the EU (from 81.4% to 82.9%). The highest ratios of government debt to GDP at the end of the second quarter of 2026 were recorded in Greece (140.8%), Italy (139.9%), France (119.1%), Belgium (110.2%) and Spain (101.4%), and the lowest ratios were recorded in Estonia (26.1%), Denmark (27.1%), Luxembourg (28.2%) and Bulgaria (28.7%).
