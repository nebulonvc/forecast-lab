Reference Date: 24-November-2026. The 2026/27 season started with improved availability of irrigation water, reflecting the abundant rainfall during the previous season. As of early November, national reservoirs were filled to nearly 66 percent of capacity, compared with about 30 percent a year earlier. Total cereal production in 2026 is estimated at about 9 million tonnes, nearly 70 percent above the five-year average and almost double the drought-affected 2025 output. The sharp recovery reflects favourable weather conditions as abundant rainfall amounts from November 2025 onwards restored soil moisture levels and replenished water reservoirs, supporting crop development throughout the growing season. Cereal import requirements in the 2026/27 marketing year (July/June) are forecast at 7.5 million tonnes, about 22 percent below the average, largely reflecting the above-average 2026 wheat output.
