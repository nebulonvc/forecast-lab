Alberta is one of the most educated places in the world (ATB Financial, March 23, 2021, citing Statistics Canada Education Indicators). In this regard, Alberta does quite well. As of 2020, 55 per cent of Albertans aged 25 to 64 have completed a post-secondary (a.k.a. tertiary) program. Just 20 years ago, the proportion of Albertans aged 25 to 64 with a post-secondary credential was 37 per cent, for an 18 percentage point jump since 2000. Most of this growth has been at the university level. The proportion of Albertans aged 25 to 64 with a Bachelor’s degree went from 13 to 23 per cent and the proportion with a Master’s degree or higher went from 5 per cent to 10 per cent. Over the same period and for the same age group, the proportion of Albertans who did not complete high school went from 15 per cent in 2000 to 7 per cent in 2020.
