Statistics Bureau of Japan - Summary of the Results - Current Population Estimates as of October 1, 2024. The total population was 123,802 thousand, a decrease of 550 thousand compared with the previous year. The rate of decrease was 0.44 percent. The total population decreased for the fourteenth year in a row. The population under 15 years old was 13,830 thousand (11.2 percent of the total population). The population aged 15 to 64 was 73,728 thousand (59.6 percent of the total population). The population aged 65 years old and over was 36,243 thousand (29.3 percent of the total population).
