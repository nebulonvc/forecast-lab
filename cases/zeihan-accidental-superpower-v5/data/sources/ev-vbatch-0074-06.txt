FRED / U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) Industry GDP Data: Gross Domestic Product for Water Transportation (NAICS 483, USWATTRANNGSP) compared with U.S. annual nominal GDP (GDPA) fell as a share of GDP from 0.0957% in 2014 ($16,843.0 million out of $17,608.1 billion) to 0.0720% in 2019, 0.0599% in 2020, 0.0881% in 2024 ($25,899.0 million out of $29,381.0 billion), and 0.0850% in 2025 ($26,240.0 million out of $30,861.3 billion). By contrast, broader Value Added by Industry: Transportation and Warehousing as a Percentage of GDP (VAPGDPT) edged up from 3.0%–3.1% in 2014 to 3.3% in 2024 and 3.2% in 2026 Q2.
