China - Global Hunger Index (GHI) - peer-reviewed annual publication designed to comprehensively measure and track hunger at the global, regional, and country levels. In the 2026 Global Hunger Index, China records a score below 5, indicating a low level of hunger. The 2026 GHI score for East & Southeast Asia is 8.1, compared with 18.1 for the world. Child stunting has fallen from 20.0 percent of children under five in 2000 to 4.5 percent, a reduction of 15.5 percentage points.
