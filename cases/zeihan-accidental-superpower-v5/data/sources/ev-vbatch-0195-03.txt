The Southern Theater Command ( Chinese : 南部战区 ; pinyin : Nánbù zhànqū ) is one of the five theater commands of the People's Liberation Army . Founded on 1 February 2016, its predecessor was the Guangzhou Military Region . Its jurisdiction includes Guangdong , Guangxi , Hunan , Yunnan , Hainan and the South Sea Fleet , as well as the Special Administrative Regions of Hong Kong and Macau .
