Mexico asked for “one of these drones” because it does not yet have the capabilities for certain types of platforms the US government has, Sheinbaum said. The intelligence goes to the Defense Ministry, the Navy or the Security Ministry, and Mexican forces alone plan and carry out the operation. President Trump has pressured Sheinbaum to crack down on the cartels, the AP reported, and she said on Monday that US forces are not allowed to operate on the ground in Mexico. US Northern Command had said on social media that its Joint Interagency Task Force-Counter Cartel “supported the targeting effort that led to this capture,” Bloomberg reported , and the Justice Department release credits the Homeland Security Task Force without mentioning aircraft. At least two US agencies have been reported flying MQ-9s over Mexico. CNN reported in February 2025 that CIA MQ-9 Reaper drones were flying unarmed over Mexico , and in August 2025 flight trackers followed a Customs and Border Protection MQ-9 about 600 miles (966 km) into Mexico .
