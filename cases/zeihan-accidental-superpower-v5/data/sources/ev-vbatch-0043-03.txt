DragonFly IV | A new era in 3D electronics printing (Nano Dimension). A multi-material, multi-layer 3D printer that generates entire circuits in one step – including substrate, conductive traces, and passive components. Additively Manufactured Electronics (AME) removes human error and lengthy manual operations. The DragonFly™ System can print antennas and arrays using conductive nano-particle silver and dielectric inks. Vertically-stacked Integrated Circuits: Stacked ICs in an AME structure have a higher circuitry density than traditional PCBs. DragonFly IV+ Tech Specs and Capabilities: Dimensions 1,400mm x 800mm x 1,800mm; Network Connectivity Ethernet TCP/IP 10/100/1000; Operational Temperature 18°C (64°F) to 25°C (77°F).
