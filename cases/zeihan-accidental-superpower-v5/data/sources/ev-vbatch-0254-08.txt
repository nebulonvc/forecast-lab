C3S/ECMWF and WMO, 2026: C3S-WMO European State of the Climate 2025. In 2025, storms and flooding in Europe caused at least 21 deaths and affected an estimated 14,500 people. Wildfires caused at least a further three deaths and affected around 500 people. Heatwaves impacted Europe from the Mediterranean to the Arctic Circle, including Europe’s second most severe heatwave on record in July. In Europe, 2025 was marked by a range of extreme and notable events, from widespread drought in spring and summer to heatwaves from the Mediterranean to the Arctic Circle, wildfires in southern Europe and storms across the continent.
