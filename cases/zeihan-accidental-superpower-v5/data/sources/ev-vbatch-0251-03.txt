State of the Climate in Latin America and the Caribbean 2023 (WMO Publication, 8 May 2024). Key messages: Warmest year on record; Drought, heatwaves, rainfall and floods undermine economic development; Sea level rise threatens coastal areas and glacier retreat accelerates. The WMO State of the Climate in Latin America and the Caribbean 2023 report confirmed that it was by far the warmest year on record. Sea levels continued to rise at a higher rate than the global average around much of the Atlantic region, threatening coastal areas and small island developing States.
