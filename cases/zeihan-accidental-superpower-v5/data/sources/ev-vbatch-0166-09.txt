The Daily — Consolidated Canadian Government Finance Statistics, 2024 (Statistics Canada, November 21, 2025). In 2024, Alberta's surplus increased from $5.2 billion in 2023 to $7.1 billion as revenue growth (+$6.5 billion) outpaced the growth in expenses (+$4.5 billion). Oil and gas royalties increased by $2.8 billion (+14.5%) to reach $21.8 billion. Alberta (1.5%) and Nova Scotia (1.2%) reported the only surpluses among all provinces. Alberta recorded the lowest fiscal burden at 9.9%. Major federal government transfers to provinces include equalization payments, the Canada Health Transfer and the Canada Social Transfer. Among all provinces, Ontario received the lowest grants per capita ($2,392) in 2024, followed by Alberta ($2,547) and British Columbia ($2,585). Alberta (30.3%), Nova Scotia (39.8%) and British Columbia (40.3%) had the lowest gross debt-to-GDP ratio in 2024. Alberta (2.1%) and British Columbia (3.8%) recorded the lowest net debt-to-GDP ratios. The lowest net debt per capita was in Alberta ($1,972), followed by British Columbia ($2,871).
