U.S. weekly mortgage rate averages as of 11/19/2026
30-year Fixed-Rate Mortgage: 7.28%

Mortgage Rates Average 7.28%
The 30-year fixed-rate mortgage averaged 7.28% as of November 19, 2026, up from last week when it averaged 7.11%. A year ago at this time, the 30-year FRM averaged 6.26%.
The 15-year fixed-rate mortgage averaged 6.66%, up from last week when it averaged 6.45%. A year ago at this time, the 15-year FRM averaged 5.54%.
The PMMS results are based on data from our Loan Product Advisor (LPA): mortgage rates collected from thousands of loan applications submitted to Freddie Mac from lenders across the country when a borrower applies for a mortgage.
Since April 1971, Freddie Mac shared the average 30-year fixed-rate mortgage rate with the PMMS. On November 17, 2022, the mortgage rate gathering process was updated. Instead of surveying lenders, we now use data from our Loan Product Advisor (LPA).
