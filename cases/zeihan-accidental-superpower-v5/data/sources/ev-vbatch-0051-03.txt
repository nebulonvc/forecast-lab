USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 - LEAD (January 2025). Events, Trends, and Issues: During the first 9 months of 2024, the average North American price for lead was 110 cents per pound, 4% less than the annual average price of 114.1 cents per pound in 2023. Estimated U.S. apparent consumption of refined lead decreased by 7% from that in 2023, and the net import reliance decreased to 28% from 33%. According to the International Lead and Zinc Study Group, global refined lead production in 2024 was forecast to increase by 2.4% to 13.5 million tons and refined lead consumption to increase by 0.2% to 13.1 million tons.
