In talks in Jeddah on March 11, 2025 the Ukrainian delegation expressed readiness to accept the U.S. proposal to enact an immediate, interim 30-day ceasefire. To date, we have provided $66.9 billion in military assistance since Russia launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine on February 24, 2022, and approximately $69.7 billion in military assistance since Russia’s initial invasion of Ukraine in 2014. We have now used the emergency Presidential Drawdown Authority on 55 occasions since August 2021 to provide Ukraine military assistance totaling approximately $31.7 billion from DoD stockpiles.
