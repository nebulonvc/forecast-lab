World Bank Open Data API (Brazil, 2014-2024). Brazil container port traffic (IS.SHP.GOOD.TU) rose from 9,436,275 TEU in 2014 to 13,893,906 TEU in 2024 (+47.2%), while Brazil exports of goods and services (NE.EXP.GNFS.CD) increased from 270.46 billion USD in 2014 to 392.11 billion USD in 2024.
