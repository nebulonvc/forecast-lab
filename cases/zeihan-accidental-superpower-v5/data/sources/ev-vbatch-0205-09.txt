Vienna, 21 October 2026: Total fees paid to smugglers on sea routes to Europe reached an estimated US$388-509 million in 2025, according to new research released by the UN Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC) today. UNODC’s Observatory on Smuggling of Migrants has provided updates on migrant smuggling along the four main sea routes to Europe in 2025: the Central Mediterranean, Eastern Mediterranean, Western Mediterranean and the Northwest African Atlantic . Around 1 90 ,000-20 9 ,000 people were the object of smuggling offences along these routes in 2025. The largest number of offences were committed on the Central Mediterranean route ( 84 ,000 -100,400 people), followed by the Eastern Mediterranean (48,400–51,300 ), the Northwest African Atlantic ( 33 , 0 00) and the Western Mediterranean (2 4 , 5 00). Not all those attempting irregular entry on sea routes are smuggled . UNODC estimates that 72-8 6 per cent of people departing on the Central Mediterranean route in 2025 were smuggled, versus 73-83 per cent of people on the Northwest African Atlantic route and 57-90 per cent on the Western Mediterranean route. On the Eastern Mediterranean route, meanwhile, an estimated 75 per cent of those departing from Türkiye to Greece and Italy and 77-90 per cent on the route from Libya to the Greek islands of Crete and Gavdos were smuggled . Many of the people who were the object of smuggling offences did not arrive at the destination. Among all those departing on the sea routes in 2025 , 10 4 , 2 00 people w ere intercepted at sea and returned , and 3, 404 lost their lives or were reported missing. The average smuggling f ee received by smugglers varied widely depending on the route , the migrant’s nationality and the country of departure , as well as the conditions of travel .
