A Longer View: East Asia and Pacific Economic Update, April 2025. Developing East Asia and Pacific (EAP) is growing faster than most of the world but slower than before the pandemic. The World Bank projects that growth in EAP will slow down to 4.0 percent in 2025 from 5.0 percent in 2024. Economic performance in the region is being shaped by three critical external developments: Increasing global economic policy uncertainty, especially regarding trade; Increasing trade restrictions; Slowing global growth. Short-term uncertainty should not obscure long-term trends in global economic integration, the environment, and demographics that will affect trade, growth, and jobs in the EAP region. Policies to face up to the major challenges of de-globalization, aging and climate change.
