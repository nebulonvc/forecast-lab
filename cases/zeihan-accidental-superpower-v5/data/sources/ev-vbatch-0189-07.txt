Joint Operations Command (Japan) - Wikipedia. Recommendations to establish the JJOC was mentioned as early as 2022 when the Defense Buildup Plan (DBP) paper was released, which called for the suggestion of a creating a joint headquarters to "build a system capable of seamlessly conducting cross-domain operations." In February 2024, the government approved a bill that would amend the Self-Defense Forces Law (SDF Law) and the Ministry of Defense Establishment Law, which calls for the establishment of a joint operation command. A ceremony was held on March 24, 2025, at the JMOD. Lt. Gen. Nagumo was officially appointed as the JJOC's first commander. The JJOC commands the three branches of the JSDF and is in charge of commanding units in the Ground Component Command, the Self-Defense Fleet, the Air Defense Command, the Cyber Defense Command, and the Space Operations Group.
