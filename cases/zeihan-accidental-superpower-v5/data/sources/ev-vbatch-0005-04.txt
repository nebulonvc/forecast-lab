The European Union aims to phase out imports of Russian natural gas by the fourth quarter of 2027, bringing to an end more than five decades of reliance on Russia for a substantial share of the bloc’s gas needs. The EU legislation was adopted in December 2025 and its implementation is already under way, with imports under short-term contracts prohibited since late April for Russian LNG – and since mid-June for Russian pipeline gas. Imports of Russian LNG are to halt completely by 1 January 2027, with remaining pipeline gas imports ending no later than 1 November 2027. Overall, the regulation is expected to reduce Russian pipeline gas and LNG deliveries to the EU by around 33 billion cubic metres (bcm) per year between 2025 and 2028.
