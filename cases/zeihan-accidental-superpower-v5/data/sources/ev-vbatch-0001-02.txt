IEA (2025), Global Energy Review 2025, IEA, Paris. Global energy demand grew by 2.2% in 2024, a notably faster rate than the annual average of 1.3% seen between 2013 and 2023. Electricity demand grew more rapidly than both overall energy demand and GDP, increasing by 4.3% in 2024. Renewables accounted for the largest share of the growth in total energy supply (38%), followed by natural gas (28%), coal (15%), oil (11%) and nuclear (8%). Global energy demand rose by 2.2% in 2024, reaching nearly 650 EJ.
