Eurostat News articles, 10 September 2026: 4.43 million under temporary protection in July 2026. On 31 July 2026, a total of 4.43 million non-EU citizens who fled Ukraine had temporary protection status in the EU. The EU countries hosting the highest number of beneficiaries of temporary protection from Ukraine were Germany (1 290 225 people; 29.1% of the EU total), Poland (953 060; 21.5%) and Czechia (395 225; 8.9%). Compared with the end of June 2026, the number of people under temporary protection increased in 23 of the 26 EU countries with available data, with the 3 largest absolute increases observed in Czechia (+4 415; +1.1%), Romania (+4 055; +1.9%) and Germany (+3 995; +0.3%). The 3 countries that registered decreases were Poland (-8 110; -0.8%), France (-475; -1.0%) and Cyprus (-110; -0.4%). On 31 July 2026, Ukrainian citizens represented over 98.5% of the beneficiaries of temporary protection in the EU. Adult women made up 43.4% of the total, adult men 27.1%, and minors almost one-third (29.4%) of all beneficiaries.
