Political Parties and Political Participation : The ruling MPLA party dominated all political institutions, giving its members advantages in obtaining civil service and government leadership jobs. Opposition parties asserted government control of most media outlets favored the MPLA. There were reports of traditional leaders in rural areas limiting political participation to a single party. By law, no political party was permitted to limit party membership based on ethnicity, race, or gender. The law contained several provisions that criminalized discrimination based on skin color, race, and ethnicity. Reports of racial or ethnic violence were rare.
