HARBIN -- Daqing Oilfield, one of China's largest energy production bases, reported rises in both crude oil and natural gas production in the first half of this year. The oilfield's domestic crude oil output increased by 10,900 metric tons year-on-year from January to June, while its natural gas production rose by 222 million cubic meters compared with the same period last year. In 2024, the oilfield saw its annual natural gas output surpass 6 billion cubic meters, a year-on-year increase of 155 million cubic meters, marking the 14th consecutive year of growth. Since discovered in 1959 in the northeastern province of Heilongjiang, Daqing Oilfield has produced more than 2.5 billion tons of crude oil, accounting for 36 percent of the country's total onshore output.
