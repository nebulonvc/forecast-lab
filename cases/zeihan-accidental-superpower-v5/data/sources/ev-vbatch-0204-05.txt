Government expenditure on social protection. Statistics Explained. Data extracted on 20 August 2026. Source : Statistics Explained - 08/09/2026. In the EU in 2024, expenditure on social protection stood at C 3 540 billion, equivalent to 19.6% of gross domestic product (GDP) and 40.0% of total expenditure of general governments. By far the most significant group in this division, 'old age' (10.7% of GDP and 21.8% of total expenditure), relates mainly to pension payments. Compared to 2010, expenditure on 'social protection' has increased at the EU level as a share of total expenditure from 38.8% of total expenditure in 2010 to 40.0% of total expenditure in 2024. Over half of the increase between 2023 and 2024 at EU level in social protection expenditure was due to the COFOG group 'old age (increase of + C 137 billion).
