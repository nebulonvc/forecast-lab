In U.S., Millennials pass Gen X in debt, latest Equifax Global Credit Trends shows
A generational credit shift is underway, according to the Equifax first half 2026 Global Credit Trends report . According to the latest report, Millennials in the U.S. now hold a larger share of debt than Gen X. Also in the U.S., Gen Z is the fastest-growing credit group.
In the first half of 2026, Millennials’ share of U.S. debt caught up with and passed that of Gen X. A surge in mortgage borrowing fueled the change as older generations paid down debt. Here is how each generation’s share of U.S. debt changed from January 2020 to May 2026:
Baby Boomers: Down from 30.3% to 20.1%
Traditionalists: Down from 4.5% to 1.9%
Mortgages now make up 70% of Millennial debt, and 30% of Millennial borrowers hold one.
