CBO Estimates $2.0 Trillion Deficit for Fiscal Year 2026. Oct 8, 2026. The United States borrowed $2.0 trillion in FY 2026, including $28 billion in the month of September, according to the latest Monthly Budget Review from the Congressional Budget Office (CBO). The following is a statement from Maya MacGuineas, president of the Committee for a Responsible Federal Budget: Fiscal Year 2026 has come to an end and CBO confirmed our estimate that the deficit totaled $2 trillion. That’s over $200 billion more than last fiscal year and ranks among the highest deficits in our history – and the highest ever outside of a war or recession. It is no surprise that interest rates have climbed so much over the past few months. And with the nation’s major trust funds – the largest being the Social Security retirement trust fund – facing insolvency in the coming years, both sides of the aisle should work together to restore solvency and avoid the automatic across-the-board benefit cuts.
