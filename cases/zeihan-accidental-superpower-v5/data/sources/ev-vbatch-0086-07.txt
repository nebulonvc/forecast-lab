The Lobito Corridor is a railway and logistics infrastructure project that aims to connect the Port of Lobito on the Atlantic coast of Angola to the Central African Copperbelt in the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC) and Zambia , with the aim of facilitating the transport of goods between Central Africa and global markets. The project is supported via the G7 Partnership for Global Infrastructure and Investment (PGI) by the United States and the European Union through its Global Gateway initiative. Overview [ edit ] The central axis of the Lobito Corridor is the Benguela railway . The project further envisions the rehabilitation of the Congolese stretch of the railway from the border town of Dilolo via Kolwezi and Lubumbashi to Kapiri Mposh
