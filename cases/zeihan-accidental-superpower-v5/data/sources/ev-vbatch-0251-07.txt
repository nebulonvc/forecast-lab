Sea Level Rise and Future Projections in the Baltic Sea (Journal of Marine Science and Engineering, published 29 July 2023). This article aimed to provide an overview of relative and absolute sea level rise in the Baltic Sea based on different studies, where researchers have used data from tide gauges, satellite altimetry, sea level rise, and land uplift models. To determine the relative sea level rise in the coastal area of the Baltic Sea, the rates from land uplift models were compared to ESA’s BalticSEAL absolute sea level rise model. It was found that the relative sea level rise between 1995–2019 was −5 to 4.5 mm/yr (based on NKG2016LU) in the Baltic Sea. In addition, the southern area is more affected by relative sea level rise than the northern part.
