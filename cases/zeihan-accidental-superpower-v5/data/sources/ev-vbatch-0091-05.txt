MENA FCV ECONOMIC SERIES BRIEF, Issue #2 | February 2025. The Deepening Red Sea Shipping Crisis: Impacts and Outlook. Summary: The Red Sea crisis has emerged as a critical flashpoint of the conflict in the Middle East, upending global trade and maritime transport, port activity in the MENA region, and ecological balance of the Red Sea. By end-2024, about a year after the onset of the crisis, vessel traffic through the strategic Suez Canal and Bab El-Mandeb Strait—which used to carry 30 percent of world container traffic—had plummeted by three-fourths, forcing ships to detour around the Cape of Good Hope, where navigation volumes surged by over 50 percent. The disruption of maritime transportation in the Red Sea has driven up global shipping costs, with the sharpest increases observed on routes directly affected by the hostilities.
