Alberta Referendum – October 19, 2026 Three Orders in Council set a standalone referendum for ten questions for October 19, 2026. O.C. 160/2026 , approved May 28, 2026, set out Question 10 and ordered the results of the referendum on this question are not to be binding. O.C. 110/2026 , approved March 31, 2026, set out Questions 1 to 5 and ordered the results of the referendum on these questions are not to be binding. O.C. 109/2026 , approved March 31, 2026, set out constitutional Questions 6 to 9.
