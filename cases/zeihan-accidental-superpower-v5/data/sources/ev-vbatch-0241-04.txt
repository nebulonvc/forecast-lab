China nominal GDP rose from $10.675 trillion in 2014 to $19.498 trillion in 2025, with real GDP growth moderating from 7.46% in 2014 to 4.96% in 2024 and 4.96% in 2025. European Union nominal GDP rose from $15.764 trillion in 2014 to $21.243 trillion in 2025, with real GDP growth slowing sharply to 0.44% in 2023 before recovering to 1.09% in 2024 and 1.54% in 2025. Russia nominal GDP moved from $2.059 trillion in 2014 down to $1.277 trillion in 2016 and $2.561 trillion in 2025, while Russia real GDP contracted in 2015 (-1.97%), 2020 (-2.65%), and 2022 (-1.44%), rebounded in 2023 (4.07%) and 2024 (4.92%), and slowed to 1.00% in 2025. United States nominal GDP expanded from $17.608 trillion in 2014 to $30.770 trillion in 2025, with real GDP growth of 2.79% in 2024 and 2.16% in 2025.
