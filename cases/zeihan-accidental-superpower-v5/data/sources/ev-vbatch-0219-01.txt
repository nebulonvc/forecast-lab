Country Reports on Terrorism 2024. Bureau of Counterterrorism, U.S. Department of State. Foreword: In 2024, the United States and the international community confronted a serious and complex range of terrorist threats. Global terrorist organizations affiliated with the Islamic State of Iraq and Syria (ISIS) and al-Qa’ida maintained networks extending across Africa, the Middle East, South and Central Asia, and within Europe. ISIS and its branches and affiliates maintained a global presence, plotting and carrying out attacks in the Middle East, Africa, Russia, Asia, Europe, and in the United States. ISIS attempted a resurgence in Iraq and Syria, its affiliate in South Asia launched deadly operations in Europe, and its affiliates in Africa expanded. With the Syrian government experiencing a significant transition in late 2024, vulnerabilities existed that hindered efforts to prevent an ISIS resurgence. An estimated 8,700 ISIS fighters, including 5,400 Syrians, 1,500 Iraqis, and 1,800 fighters from more than 70 other countries of origin, remained in Syrian Democratic Forces’ (SDF) controlled detention facilities. The Crocus City Hall attack in Krasnogorsk, Russia, which killed more than 145 people, and the bombings in Kerman, Iran, which killed 95 people, highlighted ISIS-Khorasan’s (ISIS-K) growing reach and ability to carry out mass casualty attacks.
