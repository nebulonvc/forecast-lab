National Bureau of Statistics of China, National Economy was Generally Stable with Innovation-Led and High-Quality Development in October 2026, released 2026-11-16 10:00. In October, the total value added of industrial enterprises above the designated size grew by 4.8 percent year on year, or 0.21 percent month on month. In terms of sectors, the value added of mining increased by 0.7 percent year on year, that of manufacturing increased by 5.4 percent. In the first nine months, the total profits made by industrial enterprises above the designated size were 6,033.7 billion yuan, up by 15.5 percent year on year. In the first ten months, the investment in fixed assets (excluding rural households) reached 36,482.4 billion yuan, down by 8.1 percent year on year; the investment in fixed assets was down by 5.1 percent with the investment in real estate development deducted. In October, the total value of imports and exports of goods was 4,868.3 billion yuan, an increase of 31.2 percent year on year. Specifically, the total value of exports was 2,790.5 billion yuan, up by 28.7 percent.
