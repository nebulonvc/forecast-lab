The San Francisco Waterfront Flood Study is a multi-year effort led by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers in collaboration with the City of San Francisco. USACE and the City have reached an important milestone - the release of the Recommended Plan, which proposes where, how high, and how expensive it will be to build coastal flood defenses that will manage sea level rise for the next century. Why is this an important miles The Recommended Plan evolved from the best ideas, approaches, and public input from the Draft Plan, released for public review in 2024. The proposed solutions are estimated to cost $17.8 billion in 2026 dollars, and, if approved by Congress, the Federal government may pay up to 65% of the construction cost. The Recommended Plan includes an Implementation Strategy for how to build the project in phases over time and a Monitoring and Adaptation Plan to track evidence of changing hydrologic and meteorologic conditions that will trigger future adaptation actions.
