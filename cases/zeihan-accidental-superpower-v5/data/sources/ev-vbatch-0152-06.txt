Press release 11 June 2025. The number of armed conflicts in the world reached a historic high in 2024. This is shown by new data from the Uppsala Conflict Data Program (UCDP) at Uppsala University. UCDP recorded 61 active conflicts involving at least one state in 2024, up from 59 the previous year and the highest number since statistics began in 1946. Eleven of these reached the level of war, defined as a conflict causing at least 1,000 battle-related deaths in a year. In total, it is estimated that nearly 160,000 people died in organised violence during the year. The war in Ukraine continues to be the world’s deadliest conflict, with around 76,000 battle-related deaths in 2024.
