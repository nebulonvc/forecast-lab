World Bank Open Data API (Indicators SP.DYN.TFRT.IN Fertility rate, total, births per woman; SP.POP.1564.TO.ZS Population ages 15-64, % of total population, 2014-2024). United States total fertility rate declined from 1.863 in 2014 to 1.843 (2015), 1.821 (2016), 1.766 (2017), 1.730 (2018), 1.706 (2019), 1.641 (2020), 1.664 (2021), 1.657 (2022), 1.617 (2023), and 1.627 in 2024. United States working-age population (ages 15-64) share fell steadily from 66.68% in 2014 to 64.75% in 2024. Among other major aging nations from 2014 to 2024, total fertility rates fell in China (1.769 to 1.013), Japan (1.42 to 1.15), South Korea (1.205 to 0.748, after a 2023 low of 0.721), Germany (1.47 to 1.36), Italy (1.38 to 1.18), and Russia (1.75 to 1.418); Hungary temporarily rose from 1.45 in 2014 to 1.63 in 2021 before falling back to 1.41 in 2024. Working-age population (ages 15-64) shares also declined from 2014 to 2024 in China (72.01% to 69.33%), Germany (65.86% to 62.89%), Italy (64.47% to 63.48%), Japan (60.80% to 58.78%), South Korea (73.28% to 70.16%), and Russia (70.36% to 65.54%).
