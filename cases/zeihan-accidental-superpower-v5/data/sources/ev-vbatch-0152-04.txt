In this release, Eurostat, the statistical office of the European Union, is providing government deficit and debt data for the years 2022-2025, based on figures reported by EU Member States in the second notification in 2026, for the application of the excessive deficit procedure (EDP). In 2025, all Member States, except Cyprus (+3.6%), Denmark (+2.9%), Greece (+2.0%), Ireland (+1.8%) and Portugal (+0.7%), reported a deficit. The highest deficits were recorded in Romania (-7.8%), Poland (-7.2%), Belgium (-5.2%) and France (-5.1%). Eleven Member States had deficits equal to or higher than 3% of GDP. At the end of 2025, the lowest ratios of government debt to GDP were recorded in Estonia (24.0%), Luxembourg (26.0%), Denmark (27.2%), Bulgaria (29.9%), Ireland (34.8%), Sweden (35.1%) and Lithuania (39.3%). Twelve Member States had government debt ratios higher than 60% of GDP, with the highest registered in Greece (145.4%), Italy (136.7%), France (115.7%), Belgium (108.1%) and Spain (100.5%).
