Germany’s tank brigade participates in its first exercise in Lithuania. The new German tank brigade that forms the backbone of NATO forces in Lithuania is holding its first large-scale battle drills in the forests of Pabrade Training Area. German Army tank crewmen stand on top of their Leopard 2 main battle tank as other armoured units move out from Pabrade Training Grounds in Lithuania during Exercise Freedom Shield 26. Freedom Shield is the first large-scale exercise for the German Army's Tank Brigade 45. The brigade is the first German military unit to be permanently stationed abroad since World War II. Once fully operational in 2027, it will add thousands of soldiers and around 2,000 vehicles to the Allied forces in Lithuania. The German Army has led the multinational battlegroup there since it was established in 2017.
