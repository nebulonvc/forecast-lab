World Bank Open Data (World Development Indicators, lastupdated: 2026-11-11), demographic aging and global savings series 2014-2025: Population ages 65 and above (% of total population, SP.POP.65UP.TO.ZS) in High income countries (HIC) increased steadily from 16.27% in 2014 to 17.84% in 2019, 18.17% in 2020, 18.52% in 2021, 18.83% in 2022, 19.20% in 2023, 19.60% in 2024, and 20.00% in 2025. In the United States (USA), the share of population ages 65 and above rose from 14.01% in 2014 to 16.07% in 2020, 16.92% in 2022, 17.93% in 2024, and 18.39% in 2025. World gross savings as a percentage of GDP (NY.GNS.ICTR.ZS) remained stable across 2014-2024: 26.74% in 2014, 26.51% in 2019, 25.69% in 2020, 27.05% in 2021, 27.16% in 2022, 26.18% in 2023, and 26.05% in 2024 (2025 not yet reported).
