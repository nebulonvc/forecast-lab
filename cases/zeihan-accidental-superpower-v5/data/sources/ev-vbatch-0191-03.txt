New trade-restrictive measures surge but WTO members step up trade facilitation efforts
The pace of newly-introduced tariffs and other non-facilitating measures rose sharply in the first half of 2026, while the stockpile of such measures reached a record level, according to the WTO Director-General's latest annual overview of developments in the international trading environment. At the same time, WTO members introduced a significant number of trade-facilitating measures, helping to sustain global trade flows despite heightened geopolitical tensions and economic uncertainty.

Stockpile of other trade and trade related import and export measures introduced since 2009 and in force as of the reporting date
In 2025, WTO members and observers introduced 1,229 new trade and trade-related measures, according to thereport. During the first half of 2026 alone, a further 1,048 measures were recorded, reflecting a substantially higher monthly pace of trade policy activity. The increase was broad-based across the membership and not limited to the largest trading economies. These trends were also identified by theTrade Policy Activity (TPA) Index, which WTO and IMF economists updated in July.
