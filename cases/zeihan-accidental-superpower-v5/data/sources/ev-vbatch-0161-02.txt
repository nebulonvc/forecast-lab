A new Léger–Le Journal–TVA poll suggests the Parti Québécois (PQ) is gaining ground in Quebec, while support for sovereignty has fallen to its lowest level on record. According to the poll, support for Quebec sovereignty has dropped to 26 per cent, marking a historic low. Nearly three-quarters of respondents aged 55 and over expressed the least support for sovereignty. The poll also suggests Quebecers are increasingly rallying behind Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney, with the prime minister receiving stronger support in Quebec than in any other province. Approximately 61 per cent of respondents expressed support for Carney. The findings indicate that if an election were held today, the Parti Québécois would form a majority government, with approximately 33 per cent of Quebecers indicating they would vote for the party. This represents a five-point increase compared with the fall election. Methodology: The online poll was conducted from November 13 to 16, 2026, among 1,013 Quebec voters. For comparison purposes, a probabilistic sample of this size would have a margin of error of at most ±3.08 percentage points, 19 times out of 20.
