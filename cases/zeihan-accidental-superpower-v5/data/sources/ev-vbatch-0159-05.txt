Statistics Canada, The Daily (Released: 2015-04-02), Canadian international merchandise trade: Annual review, 2014. Trade with the United States increases: Exports to the United States rose 11.6% to $400.0 billion in 2014. The United States' share of Canada's total exports increased to 75.7% in 2014 from 74.8% in 2013. Meanwhile, imports from the United States increased 12.0% to $350.7 billion, as the share of imports from the United States reached 66.9% compared with 64.4% in 2013. Consequently, Canada's trade surplus with the United States widened from $45.4 billion in 2013 to $49.3 billion in 2014. Exports to countries other than the United States advanced 6.6% to $128.8 billion.
