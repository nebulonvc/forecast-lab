Sanctioning Iranian Regime Insiders Who Profit from Stolen Oil Wealth Press Statement Thomas "Tommy" Pigott, Spokesman Office of the Spokesman November 20, 2026 Today, the United States took action against four corrupt Iranian regime insiders who illicitly sell oil to help fund the regime’s military and its support for terrorism. Today’s action is part of Operation Economic Outcast, a whole-of-government campaign to cut off illicit money flows that sustain the Iranian regime. The United States will continue working to deny Tehran the revenue it uses to destabilize the region, threaten our partners, and pursue its dangerous ambitions. Today’s designations are being taken pursuant to Executive Order (E.O.) 13902, which targets the Iranian petroleum, petrochemical, and aviation sectors, among other key sectors of the Iranian economy, and E.O. 13224, as amended, a counterterrorism authority.
