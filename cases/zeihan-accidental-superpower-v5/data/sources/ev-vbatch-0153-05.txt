Böll EU Brief 04/2026 By Roderick Kefferpütz. German Rearmament: Handle with Care - Public Attitudes in Six EU Member States. Germany is rearming. How do its European partners feel about it? A new Böll EU Brief presents what is, to our knowledge, the first public survey of its kind, with more than 9,000 people in France, Poland, Italy, Greece, Lithuania and Czechia. Two thirds support German rearmament, but backing varies by country and does not always come with trust. The top concern is the rise of extremist forces in Germany. Asked who should lead the defence of Europe, the largest group wants the EU to share that role, with no single country in charge. Key findings: There is broad but uneven support for German rearmament. 67% of respondents support German rearmament. Support is strongest in Lithuania (87%) and France (79%), but considerably weaker in Italy (52%) and Greece (48%). Yet support does not equate to trust or confidence that Germany would come to an ally’s defence. The rise of extremist political forces in Germany emerges as respondents’ single most important concern (25%).
