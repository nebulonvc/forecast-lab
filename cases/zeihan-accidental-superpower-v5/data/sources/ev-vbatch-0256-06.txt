Value of Agricultural Commodities Produced, Australia, 2019-20 financial year. Released 14/05/2021. Reference period 2019-20 financial year. Value of agricultural products: The gross value for Australian agriculture increased slightly to $61 billion in 2019-20 despite drought conditions. Crops: The total gross value of crops decreased by 5% to $28 billion due to a large fall in the value of cereal and broadacre crops, particularly wheat. Drought conditions impacted broadacre crops in New South Wales, Queensland and Western Australia. Results were mixed for horticultural crops. Key crop results for 2019-20: $5.4 billion for fruit and nuts (up 9% from 2018-19), $4.9 billion for wheat (down 20%), $4.2 billion for vegetables (down 4%), $3.0 billion for barley (steady), $2.5 billion for hay (up 24%), $1.4 billion for canola (up 1%), $252 million for cotton (down 78%).
