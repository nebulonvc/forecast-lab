Bolivia (Plurinational State of) - Global Hunger Index (GHI) 2026. Bolivia's 2026 Global Hunger Index score is 14.2, a moderate level of hunger. For context, Latin America & Caribbean has a regional GHI score of 7.4; the global score is 18.1. GHI severity scale: <= 9.9 low; 10.0-19.9 moderate; 20.0-34.9 serious; 35.0-49.9 alarming; >= 50.0 extremely alarming. The Global Hunger Index (GHI) is a tool designed to comprehensively measure and track hunger at global, regional, and national levels, reflecting multiple dimensions of hunger over time.
