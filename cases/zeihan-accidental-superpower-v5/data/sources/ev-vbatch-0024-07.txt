Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, FEDS Notes, July 18, 2025. The International Role of the U.S. Dollar – 2025 Edition (Carol Bertaut, Bastian von Beschwitz, and Stephanie Curcuru). For most of the last century, the preeminent role of the U.S. dollar in the global economy has been supported by the size and strength of the U.S. economy, its stability and openness to trade and capital flows, and strong property rights and the rule of law. And the U.S. has increased its level of tariffs and thereby somewhat reduced its openness to trade flows. Given that most data on international dollar usage is available with a lag, this note is not yet able to show any potential results of the change in the U.S. credit rating or changes to tariff rates in 2025. According to our index combining various factors, the dollar's international usage is little changed over the past 5 years and far exceeds the U.S. share of global GDP and trade (see Figure 1). It also plays an outsized role in areas of financial innovation, such as being the dominant anchor for stablecoins.
