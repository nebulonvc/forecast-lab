Severing the Financial Lifelines of Iran’s Shadow Banking Network Press Statement Thomas "Tommy" Pigott, Spokesman Office of the Spokesman November 23, 2026 Today, the United States is taking action against more than two dozen exchange houses, front companies, and individuals in Iran, the United Arab Emirates, Türkiye, and Hong Kong that support Iran’s shadow banking system. This action is part of Operation Economic Outcast, the whole-of-government campaign to sever the economic lifelines that sustain the Iranian regime. The Iranian regime uses its shadow banking networks to seek to fund the purchase of weapons and support terrorist proxies across the Middle East. The regime continues to prioritize funding terror and destabilization abroad while the Iranian people pay the price at home. The United States will continue to use every diplomatic and economic tool available to deny the Iranian regime the resources it needs to threaten our forces, our partners, and global security.
