Office of the United States Trade Representative (USTR), Mexico Trade Summary. Mexico has consistently been one of the top two trading partners for the United States. Most recently, in 2024, Mexico was the second-largest destination for U.S. exports and the top source of U.S. imports. U.S. goods and services (exports plus imports) trade with Mexico totaled an estimated $964.1 billion in 2025, up 3.7 percent ($34.7 billion) from 2024. U.S. goods trade (exports plus imports) with Mexico totaled an estimated $871.6 billion in 2025. U.S. goods exports to Mexico in 2025 were $337.3 billion, up 0.8 percent ($2.8 billion) from 2024. U.S. goods imports from Mexico totaled $534.3 billion in 2025, up 6.2 percent ($31.2 billion) from 2024.
