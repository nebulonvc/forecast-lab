Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries of Japan (MAFF), FY2025 Summary of the Annual Report on Food, Agriculture and Rural Areas in Japan (May 2026). Special Feature: Responses for Stable Supply of Rice. Retail price and negotiated transaction price of rice rose. Imports increased due to rising domestic rice prices. Factors behind rising rice prices and responses were verified in August 2025. As production fell below demand and private-sector stocks declined in the summer of 2024, rice prices soared due to shortages caused by an increase in purchases. Complete self-sufficiency was achieved in rice in FY1966, but the production continued to increase amid declining consumption, resulting in oversupply, and full-scale production adjustments began in 1971.
