Letterpress printing remained in wide use for books and other uses until the second half of the 20th century. It was only in the late 20th century (circa 1980), through digital typesetting and offset lithography, that letterpress became obsolete as a widespread commercial process. Letterpress has survived thanks to a small number of commercial printers, print workshops, artisan printers, and hobby printers. Status: ENDANGERED. Current number of professionals for whom it is the main income: 11-20.
