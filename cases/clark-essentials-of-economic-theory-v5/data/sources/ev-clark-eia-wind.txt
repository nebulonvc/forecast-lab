In the late 1800s and early 1900s, small wind-electric generators were widely used. Rural electrification in the 1930s extended power lines and the number of wind turbines declined. In the early 1980s, thousands of wind turbines were installed in California. The share of U.S. electricity generation from wind grew from less than 1% in 1990 to about 10.5% in 2025. In 1990, 16 countries generated about 3.6 billion kWh of wind electricity. In 2024, at least 135 countries generated about 2,511 billion kWh.
