Tim Gleig is a cordwainer, a shoemaker, a cobbler, founder and sole maker of Two Five Footwear, a Melbourne-based custom shoemaking brand. Shoemaking is a dying craft. Throughout the 20th century the Australian industry was vibrant, until tariff cuts in the 1980s and free-trade deals in the 2000s. Few footwear factories survive in Melbourne, but a handful of sole traders have been keeping the dream alive.
