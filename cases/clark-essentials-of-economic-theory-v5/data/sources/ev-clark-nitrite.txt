Source: United States ex rel. Norwegian Nitrogen Products Co. v. United States Tariff Commission, 274 U.S. 106, https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/274/106
The Tariff Act of September 21, 1922, section 315, let the President change duties after a Tariff Commission investigation so as to equalize differences in costs of production, within 50 percent of the rate.
In October 1922 the American Nitrogen Products Company petitioned for a 50 percent increase in the duty on imported sodium nitrite. The commission investigated costs at home and abroad. The opinion states that on May 6, 1924 the President proclaimed a duty found necessary to equalize those costs, and also refers to a hearing date of September 10, 1926. Those two dates are both in the opinion text and are not reconciled here.
The petition is for a 50 percent increase, not a statement that a 50 percent duty was asked where 20 percent would suffice.
