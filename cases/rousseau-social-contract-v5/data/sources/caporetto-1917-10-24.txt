I. Introduction: the astounding disaster 1
Two hours after midnight on 24 October 1917, German and Austrian batteries opened a bombardment along a 30-kilometre front in the Julian Alps, where Italy had six divisions in the line. The weight and accuracy of shelling were unprecedented; gun lines, observation

posts and communications were shattered.
