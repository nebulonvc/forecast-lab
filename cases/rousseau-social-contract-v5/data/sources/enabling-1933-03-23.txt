' Law to Remedy the Distress of People and Reich ' ) [ 2 ] was a law that gave the German Cabinet —most importantly, the chancellor , Adolf Hitler —the power to make and enforce laws without the involvement of the Reichstag or President Paul von Hindenburg . By allowing the chancellor to override the checks and balances in the constitution, the Enabling Act of 1933 was a pivotal step in the transition from the democratic Weimar Republic to the totalitarian dictatorship of Nazi Germany . Background [ edit ] On 30 January 1933, Adolf Hitler, leader of the Nazi Party (NSDAP), was appointed Chancellor, the head of the German government.
