One economic trigger that may have contributed was the bad grain harvest of 1788. Depending on the account, this was the result of either droughts or hailstorms. In any case, since threshing was a major source of seasonal employment, unemployment rose through autumn and winter while the price of grain rose steadily. “Vagabonds” started to move across town and country, asking for work or bread. It is unclear how hard famine hit the populace
