The Land Tax was first introduced in 1692. Because the quotas never went up, over time it became a smaller proportion of the revenue, some seventeen per cent in the 1790s, and eleven per cent by the 1820s. In time, the tax became a nominal sum more inconvenient to collect than onerous to pay.
