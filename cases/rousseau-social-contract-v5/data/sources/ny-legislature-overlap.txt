Although a usurpation of all powers by one of the three coordinate 
branches of government would result in the ultimate destruction of our 
republic, as experience has taught us, there is nevertheless a certain 
sphere in each of the coordinate branches within which each of the 
others operates-an overlapping of powers, so to speak.
