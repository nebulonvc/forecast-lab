I have imagined for aesthetic purposes
that our race will destroy itself. There is today a very earnest
movement for peace and international unity; and surely with good fortune
and intelligent management it may triumph. Most earnestly we must hope
that it will. But I have figured things out in this book in such a
manner that this great movement fails.
