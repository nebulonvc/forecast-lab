Synthesis:
Alexander Hamilton, in Federalist No. 11 (November 24, 1787), argued that a united, efficient government could soon build a federal navy. Even if it could not match Europe’s great navies, it would have “respectable weight” in deciding between two rival powers—especially in the West Indies, where a few timely ships of the line could determine a campaign.
