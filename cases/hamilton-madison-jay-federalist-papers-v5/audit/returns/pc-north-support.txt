Synthesis:
In Federalist No. 5, written in November 1787, Alexander Hamilton argued that if the states split into regional confederacies, the northern one would likely soon become “unquestionably more formidable” than the others because of its geographic and economic advantages. He warned this would make southern states fear and envy it, producing conflict rather than a reliable mutual defense.
