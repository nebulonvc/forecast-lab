In the 1790s the United States had only a small, newly reestablished navy—initially six frigates—so it lacked the scale to be a respectable naval power alongside Britain, France, and other maritime states. Its immediate purpose was protecting commerce from Barbary corsairs and European interference, not competing with major European fleets.
