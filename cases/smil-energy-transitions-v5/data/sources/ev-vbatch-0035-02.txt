Climate Change Agreement Analysis 1. Main Report: Security of Electricity Supply Towards and After 2030. Energistyrelsen, January 2022. The SOES in Denmark will be challenged in the years around and immediately after 2030, because of 1) Increase in demand due to electrification of heating and transport as well as more data centers and Power-To-X facilities. 2) Increase in intermittent renewable capacity and closure of thermal power plant. Both result in an increased probability of power shortage. In particular, there is an unexploited potential in increased flexible electricity demand, increased capacity on interconnectors and increased electricity savings on inflexible electricity demand.
