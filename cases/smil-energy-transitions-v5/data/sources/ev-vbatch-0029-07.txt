U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), Use of energy explained: Energy use for transportation. In 2025, petroleum products accounted for about 89% of the total U.S. transportation sector energy use. Biofuels contributed about 6%, most of which were blended with petroleum fuels (gasoline, diesel fuel, and jet fuel). Natural gas accounted for about 5%, and nearly all was used as a fuel for natural gas pipeline compressors. Electricity use by mass transit systems was less than 1% of total energy consumption by the transportation sector. On an energy content basis, gasoline (excluding fuel ethanol) accounted for 52% of total energy consumption by the U.S. transportation sector in 2025. Distillate fuels, mostly diesel (excluding biofuels blended with diesel), accounted for 22%, and jet fuel accounted for 13%.
