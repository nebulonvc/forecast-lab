IEA (2025), Global Energy Review 2025, IEA, Paris. In 2024, 80% of the growth in global electricity generation was provided by renewable sources and nuclear power. Together, they contributed 40% of total generation for the first time, with renewables alone supplying 32%. New renewables installations hit record levels for the 22nd consecutive year, with around 700 GW of total renewable capacity added in 2024, nearly 80% of which was solar PV. Oil’s share of total energy demand fell below 30% for the first time ever, 50 years after peaking at 46%. Oil demand from global road transport fell slightly, driven by declines in China. Global coal demand rose by 1%. Power generation was the main driver of growth as high temperatures pushed up electricity consumption for cooling.
