A Wind Turbine Efficiency Limit Higher than the Lanchester (Betz) Limit. A generalized actuator-disk analysis is developed that retains both the axial and tangential velocity components of the flow through the blade passage. The optimized solutions predict power coefficients that exceed the classical Betz limit (reaching 78% at its peak) while remaining fully consistent with the governing conservation laws. The analysis shows that the classical efficiency limit is a consequence of the one-dimensional flow assumption rather than a fundamental upper limit imposed by the governing conservation laws.
