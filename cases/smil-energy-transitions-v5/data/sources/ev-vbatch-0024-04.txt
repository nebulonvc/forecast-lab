IEA (2025), Global Energy Review 2025 - Oil. In other emerging market and developing economies in Asia, fuel demand continues to rise. India (+3.4%) was the largest single global oil demand growth source in 2024, while demand in Southeast Asia (+2.6%) also rose significantly. India’s dynamic economy has underpinned the advances of recent years, with urbanisation and rising car ownership lifting oil demand 11.6% higher in 2024 than 2019. Gasoline use in the world’s most populous nation increased by 41.7% (+310 kb/d) from 2019 to 2024.
