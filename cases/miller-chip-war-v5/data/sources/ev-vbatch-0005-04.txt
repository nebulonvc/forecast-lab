According to the latest data released by the General Administration of Customs of China, both the quantity and amount of imports and exports of integrated circuits (ICs) in China will decrease in 2023. It is worth noting that China is continuously increasing its local chip production to cope with long-term development in the future.   In terms of import data, China will import a total of 479.5 billion integrated circuits in 2023, a decrease of 10.8% compared to 2022; The import amount was 349.4 billion US dollars, a decrease of 15.4%.
