When SEMICON West gathered in San Francisco in 2023, the semiconductor industry was forecasting a path to $1 trillion in annual revenue by 2030. Three years later, the industry has already blown past that number. At the SEMI Market Symposium, the association projected 300mm fab equipment spending of $149 billion this year, rising to $227 billion by 2029, with construction beginning on 20 new 300mm fabs or production lines in 2026 alone. Patrick Vandenameele, CEO of imec, noted that AI infrastructure can keep growing by adding accelerators and racks, but adding hardware isn’t scaling in the traditional semiconductor sense. The industry isn’t abandoning transistor scaling. It’s adding another level above it, where performance depends on how effectively different technologies can be combined into a functional system.
