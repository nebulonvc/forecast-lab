Dell’Oro sees RAN market flat through 2030, but the mix is shifting (September 29, 2026). After a couple of strong years, RAN revenue fell about $9 billion between 2021 and 2024 and was flat in 2025. The reason for the decline, Pongratz argued, is that 5G delivers more capacity per dollar than 4G did — roughly a 2x boost — while mobile data traffic is growing only 20% to 30% a year. That gives operators an opportunity “to slow things down for a bit,” he said. And it does not have to mean GPU RAN. Today’s adopters are pragmatic, he said: mostly single-purpose, non-GPU deployments on distributed 5G architectures that lean on the existing install base to improve performance, energy efficiency and operations.
