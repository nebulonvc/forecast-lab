Shipments of x86 CPUs declined quarter-over-quarter and year-over-year in the third quarter as demand for client processors dropped sharply due to high component prices, while server CPU unit sales grew by a whopping 20% year-over-year, according to the latest report from Mercury Research. Intel regained overall CPU market share from AMD for the first time since Q2 2025, largely due to surprisingly strong performance in desktop PCs. Meanwhile, AMD continued to set new records in the mobile and server CPU segments, the report claims. In total, the industry supplied 68.93 million x86 CPUs, Jon Peddie Research reports .
Unlike the bleak client CPU market, server CPUs are thriving, with shipments of data center processors reaching 8.13 million units in Q3 2026, according to Jon Peddie Research. The market again grew 20% year-over-year, the highest annual growth rate in nearly five years, according to Mercury Research.
In the server CPU segment, AMD continued its winning spree as its unit share reached a record 35.5%, up from 34.5% in Q2 2026 and 27.8% in Q3 2025. Intel remained the dominant supplier with a 64.5% share, down 1% sequentially and 7.7% year-over-year.
Interestingly, Intel managed to slow AMD's advance in the core data center CPU market by apparently substantially increasing shipments of its Xeon SP processors, McCarron believes. When the comparison is limited to AMD's EPYC and Intel's Xeon SP processors, AMD's unit share reached 46.8%, up just 0.4% sequentially, which represents AMD's smallest sequential gain over the past year, as the company's quarterly share gains by this metric averaged 3% over the preceding three quarters.
Shipments of x86 CPUs declined both sequentially and year-over-year in Q3 2026 to 68.93 million units as rising component prices weighed on client PC demand, while server CPU shipments grew more than 20% year-over-year.
Intel regained overall x86 CPU market share from AMD for the first time since Q2 2025, largely thanks to a spectacular comeback in the desktop CPU segment, where its share reached 70.1%. Meanwhile, AMD gained 0.3% of the mobile CPU market, now commanding 29.2%, its highest share ever. Furthermore, AMD reached a record unit share in the server CPU segment, where it now commands 35.5%.
