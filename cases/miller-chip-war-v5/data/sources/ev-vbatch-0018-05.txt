John David Minnich, a professor at the London School of Economics, argued that Chinese officials were unable, and perhaps unwilling, to leverage market access to impose JV or tech transfer requirements on leading semiconductor firms. Foreign-sourced chips were critical to China’s export manufacturing-led growth, and Chinese policymakers were unwilling to risk this export engine by imposing market barriers to force non-Chinese companies to transfer advanced technologies for market access, although many firms agreed to set up less advanced facilities.
