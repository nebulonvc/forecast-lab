TrendForce’s latest foundry industry research reveals that the combined revenue of the world’s top 10 foundries rose 11.5% QoQ to nearly US$53.49 billion in 2Q26, setting another record high. Among the top 10 foundries, TSMC maintained its lead with 2Q26 revenue nearing $40.2 billion, a 12.1% increase QoQ, capturing a 72.5% market share. Strong demand for AI server GPUs and XPUs kept its 5/4nm and 3nm capacities fully booked. Additionally, the initial inventory buildup for new iPhones supported sales, and 2nm contributed revenue for the first time. Samsung Foundry ranked second. Revenue edged up 1.8% QoQ to $3.26 billion. However, Samsung’s market share declined to 5.9% as competitors grew at a faster pace. SMIC ranked third, with revenue surging 20% QoQ to more than $3 billion. Its market share edged up to 5.4%, narrowing the gap with Samsung. HuaHong Group ranked sixth, with 2Q26 revenue rising 3.5% QoQ to more than $1.27 billion. Nexchip slipped to ninth place despite a 6.4% QoQ increase in revenue to $447 million.
