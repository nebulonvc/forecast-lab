On Thursday, TSMC’s Co-Chief Operating Officer Zhang Xiaoqiang stated at the North America Technology Symposium that the company has no plans to purchase ASML’s latest High-NA EUV lithography machines, citing their price of over €350 million per unit as too high. Although the equipment can improve chip manufacturing precision to below 1.4nm, TSMC believes its existing EUV systems are still sufficient to meet production needs. As ASML’s largest customer, TSMC’s decision could impact the company’s planned large-scale production rollout in 2027–2028, as well as its €60 billion revenue target for 2030.
