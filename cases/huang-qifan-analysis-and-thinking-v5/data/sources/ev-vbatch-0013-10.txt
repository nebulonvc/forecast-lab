As matters now stand, if the Hong Kong dollar-US dollar interest rate differential persists, the automatic interest rate adjustment mechanism under the Linked Exchange Rate System (LERS) will lead to a further easing of the Hong Kong dollar, and could lead to a triggering of the weak-side CU. Should this occur, the Hong Kong dollar Aggregate Balance will decrease, pushing up Hong Kong dollar interest rates, and stabilising the Hong Kong dollar exchange rate within the 7.75 to 7.85 Convertibility Zone. This is all part of the design and effective functioning of the LERS. As to whether and when the weak-side CU will be triggered, it is hard to make a precise prediction given the influence of the range of factors mentioned above. The current global economic environment and financial conditions are highly uncertain and global capital flows could swing unpredictably. The HKMA will continue to closely monitor financial markets and maintain Hong Kong’s monetary stability under the LERS. Banks will normally take into account factors such as funding supply and demand, interbank rates and other reference interest rates, as well as their own funding cost structures to assess whether, and if so by how much, Hong Kong dollar deposit and lending rates should be adjusted.
