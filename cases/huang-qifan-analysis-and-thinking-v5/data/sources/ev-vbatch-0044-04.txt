SpaceX has selected Qualcomm to supply radio and baseband technology for Starlink Mobile’s terrestrial 5G network in the US. Deployment is due to start in 2027, with “tens of thousands” of sites planned over a multi-year rollout. The agreement will see Qualcomm provide its Dragonwing cellular infrastructure platforms, including the QRU100 and QDU100 for radio-unit and distributed-unit (RU and DU) processing, alongside commercial-grade physical-layer RAN software, to support Starlink Mobile’s base station architecture. The platforms combine ‘massive MIMO’ radio technology with baseband processing and Layer 1 software, with an emphasis on capacity and power efficiency.
