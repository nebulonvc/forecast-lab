Today, the United States reached an unfortunate milestone: $40 trillion in gross debt. Looking ahead, we are adding debt faster than ever and are currently on pace to borrow an additional $1 trillion every five months. On May 16, 2025, Moody’s Ratings downgraded the U.S. credit rating from Aaa, its highest rating, to Aa1, a tier below, citing the inability of the nation to address large and growing deficits. With this revision, Moody’s joined Fitch and Standard & Poor’s (S&P), which downgraded the United States in 2011 and 2023, respectively. Financial institutions continue to treat U.S. debt as a safe asset class, but successive downgrades reveal that this privilege is at risk.
