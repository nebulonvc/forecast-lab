World Bank China Economic Update: Advancing Reforms, Enhancing Prospects (December 2025, published December 11, 2025). Despite trade policy uncertainty, exports continued to expand, including strong growth in emerging markets, helping offset domestic demand weakness. Growth was driven by stronger exports to developing countries, which helped offset a 27-percent decline in shipments to the United States (Figure 3A). China could complement short-term stimulus with reforms that can help address long-term drivers of low household consumption, subdued private investment, and local government financial constraints. The challenges in the property sector, subdued earning prospects, labor market softness, and trade policy uncertainty could last longer than expected and weigh on consumption and investment.
