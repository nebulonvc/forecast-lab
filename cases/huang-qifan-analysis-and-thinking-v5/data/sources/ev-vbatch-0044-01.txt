REDWOOD CITY, Calif. – November 19, 2026 – According to a recently published report from Dell’Oro Group, the trusted source for market information about the telecommunications, security, networks, and data center industries, the Radio Access Network (RAN) market continued to improve in the third quarter of 2026. Worldwide RAN revenue increased at a mid-single-digit rate year-over-year (Y/Y), marking the fastest quarterly growth in nearly five years and reinforcing the broader stabilization trend. Additional highlights from the 3Q 2026 RAN quarterly report: Regional performance was mixed, with robust growth in the Middle East and Africa (MEA) and Asia Pacific (APAC), excluding China, contributing to the stronger-than-expected results. The competitive landscape remains highly concentrated. The top five RAN suppliers accounted for 97 percent of worldwide revenue in 3Q 2026. The top five RAN suppliers by worldwide revenue remain Huawei, Ericsson, Nokia, ZTE, and Samsung. Relative to 2025, Ericsson’s 1Q26-3Q26 RAN revenue share is up approximately one percentage point, while Samsung is down over the same period.
