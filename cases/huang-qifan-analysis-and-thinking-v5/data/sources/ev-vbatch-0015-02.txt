BEIJING, April 3 (Xinhua) -- China's Ministry of Finance said on Thursday that it deeply regrets and does not recognize a ratings downgrade by Fitch Ratings. The statement by the ministry came after the ratings company said in a report on Thursday that it has downgraded China's long-term foreign-currency issuer default rating to 'A' from 'A+', with a stable outlook. The downgrade was announced despite Fitch's recognition that China has a more robust economic growth prospect and a key position in global trade based on communications between the company and the Chinese side, the ministry said in a statement, while adding that the downgrade is biased and does not fully and objectively reflect the actual situation in China.
