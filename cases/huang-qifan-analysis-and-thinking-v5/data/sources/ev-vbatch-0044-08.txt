Spanish telecom giant Telefónica has confirmed it is removing Huawei equipment from its 5G networks in Spain and Germany, aligning with national regulations that restrict the use of Chinese technology in core telecommunications infrastructure. However, the company will continue using Huawei gear in Brazil, where no such restrictions exist. “In both Germany and Spain, we are reducing our exposure to Huawei following the rules we have in these countries,” said Emilio Gayo, Telefónica’s chief operating officer, in comments to Reuters. He added that Telefónica’s presence in the U.K. already has “very, very low” exposure to Huawei due to existing bans.
