Developments in the Federal Reserve's Balance Sheet and Money Markets With the Federal Reserve's net asset purchases concluding in March, the size of the balance sheet has been roughly stable at $9 trillion since February 2022 ( figures A and B ). At its May 2022 meeting, the FOMC announced plans for significantly reducing the size of the Federal Reserve's balance sheet starting June 1. Balance sheet reduction, along with increases in the target range for the federal funds rate, firms the stance of monetary policy. A.
