The Internet Archive is fighting for universal access to quality information. As an independent nonprofit, we build and maintain all our own systems, but we don’t charge for access, sell user information, or run ads. Instead, we're powered by online donations averaging about $14. We'd be deeply grateful if you'd join the one in a thousand users that support us financially.
