DIGITAL ECONOMY BEA’s Digital Economy Satellite Account measures the digital economy’s contribution to U.S. GDP, improves measures of high-tech goods and services, and offers a more complete picture of international trade. It includes valuing digital-enabling infrastructure, e-commerce transactions, and digital media.
