AMR occurs when bacteria, viruses, fungi and parasites do not respond to these medicines. As a result of drug resistance, infections become difficult or impossible to treat, increasing the risk of disease spread, severe illness, disability and death. One in six laboratory-confirmed bacterial infections causing common infections in people worldwide in 2023 were resistant to antibiotic treatments. Between 2018 and 2023, antibiotic resistance rose in over 40% of the pathogen-antibiotic combinations monitored with an average annual increase of 5–15%. WHO estimates that antibiotic resistance is highest in the WHO South-East Asia and Eastern Mediterranean Regions, where 1 in 3 reported infections were resistant. Too few new antibiotics are being developed to replace those that no longer work, leaving fewer treatment options for people with drug-resistant infections.
