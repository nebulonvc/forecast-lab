Computer and Internet Use in the United States: 2021. American Community Survey Reports, By Daniela Mejía, ACS-56, June 2024, U.S. Census Bureau. Among all households in 2021, 95 percent had at least one type of computer, and 90 percent had a broadband internet subscription. Smartphones were the most common computing device, as 90 percent of households had at least one. Eighty-one percent of households had a desktop or laptop computer, followed by 64 percent of households that owned a tablet. When the questions were first introduced to the survey in 2013, 84 percent of households had a computer of any type, and 74 percent subscribed to any type of internet. That year [2016], household computer ownership was at 89 percent and subscription to the internet was at 82 percent. By 2021, 95 percent of households had a computer and 90 percent subscribed to the internet. Urban residents were more likely than rural residents to use computing devices (96 percent of urban households compared to 93 percent of rural households) and were more likely to have a broadband internet subscription (91 percent of urban households compared to 87 percent of rural households).
