The worldwide demand for additive manufacturing (AM) is increasing due to its ability to produce more challenging customized objects based on the process parameters for engineering applications. The development of functional polymers and composites by 3D printing-based technologies has been explored significantly due to its cost-effective, easier integration into customized geometries, higher efficacy, higher precision, freedom of material utilization as compared to traditional injection molding, and thermoforming techniques. The market size of 3D printing was ∼4.4 billion US dollars in 2013, which has been reached up to ∼12 billion US dollars in 2018. The other example of the use of AM technologies in medical sector is the manufacturing of clear dental aligners. The use of 3D printers makes it easier to print customized dental aligners, which helps both the manufacturers and the consumers. It was also observed that 3D printing has increased the company's capacity ∼30% to produce custom-made orthodontic aligners.
