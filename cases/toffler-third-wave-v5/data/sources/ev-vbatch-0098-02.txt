In 2024, 71% of hospitals reported using predictive AI integrated with the electronic health record (EHR), up from 66% in 2023.

Small, rural, independent, government-owned, and critical access hospitals lagged in their adoption of predictive AI.

In 2024, 71% of hospitals reported using predictive AI integrated into their EHR. For the purposes of this study, predictive AI refers to using statistical analysis and machine learning to classify or produce a risk score for individuals (such as readmission risk prediction, early disease detection, appointment no-show, and treatment recommendations).
