Developing a National Measure of the Economic Contributions of the Bioeconomy, Tina Highfill and Matthew Chambers, Bureau of Economic Analysis, U.S. Department of Commerce, March 2023. NASEM’s recent report Safeguarding the Bioeconomy reviews the current state of the literature on bioeconomy measurement and proposes a biotechnology-centric definition of the bioeconomy: “economic activity that is driven by research and innovation in the life sciences and biotechnology, and that is enabled by technological advances” (NASEM 2020). This report constructs an estimate of the size of the bioeconomy under this definition using a NAICS-code-based classification of industries. Safeguarding the Bioeconomy estimates the bioeconomy’s value added in 2016 as $402.5 billion, or 2.2 percent of gross domestic product (GDP) that year. Developing a consistent, ongoing bioeconomy satellite account with a narrow focus on any of these visions is likely infeasible at this time due to both a lack of existing data on which to base such an account and a lack of consensus on practical measurement definitions.
