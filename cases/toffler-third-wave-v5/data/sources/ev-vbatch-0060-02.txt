In 2010, B Lab was part of successfully drafting and passing benefit corporation in Maryland, the first U.S. state to do so. This created a legal framework that allows companies to keep their values of care and community, not just shareholder value, at the forefront. Today, benefit corporation legislation is active in 52 jurisdictions around the world. “To build a sustainable, inclusive and resilient world, we need businesses and investors to recognize impact as core to what they are designed to deliver. The B Corp community has modeled this since its beginning.” Amit Bouri, Global Impact Investing Network Our global network Where we operate Our global network of B Lab and Sistema B organizations is known as the B Global Network, an interdependent system of global, regional, and national organizations that power the B Corp movement at the local level. We grow, engage, and mobilize our regional B Corp communities, ecosystems, and partnerships, and in turn reshape our local economies to prioritize people and the planet. Our network brings our global theory of change to life by driving policy changes with local governments, amplifying credible stories of business as a force for good, and changing the behavior of businesses in our regions.
