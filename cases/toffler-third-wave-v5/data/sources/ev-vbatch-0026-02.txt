Issued May 2013. P20-569. Population Characteristics. Computer and Internet Use in the United States, by Thom File, U.S. Census Bureau. The U.S. Census Bureau has asked questions in the CPS about computer use since 1984 and Internet use since 1997. This report begins with a summary of computer and Internet use in American households since 1984, while the second part addresses use specifically in 2011. Computer and Internet use at the household level has changed greatly in recent years (Figure 1). In 2011, 75.6 percent of households reported having a computer, compared with only 8.2 percent in 1984 (the first year that the Census Bureau asked about computer ownership), and 61.8 percent in 2003 (the last time the Census Bureau asked about computers prior to 2010). Figure 1 Household Computer and Internet Use 1984-2011 (Computer in household, in percent): 1984: 8.2; 1989: 15.0; 1993: 22.9; 1997: 36.6; 2000: 51.0; 2001: 56.3; 2003: 61.8; 2010: 76.7; 2011: 75.6. Although the majority of U.S. households reported having Internet use in the home in 2011, notable differences in Internet use persisted between demographic groups.
