World Bank World Development Indicators — SL.TLF.PART.ZS: Part time employment, total (% of total employment). Source definition: Part-time employment rate represents the percentage of employment that is part time. Part time employment in this series is based on a common definition of less than 35 actual weekly hours worked. Source Organization: Wages and Working Time Statistics database (COND), International Labour Organization (ILO), date accessed: September 28, 2026. Observed series values (% of total employment): OECD members: 27.01% in 2000 to 27.47% in 2025 (26 annual observations). United States: 30.78% in 1994 to 26.92% in 2025 (32 annual observations). European Union: 24.17% in 1991 to 34.95% in 2024 (34 annual observations). Germany: 19.66% in 1983 to 44.49% in 2024 (42 annual observations). United Kingdom: 35.86% in 1983 to 41.54% in 2025 (43 annual observations).
