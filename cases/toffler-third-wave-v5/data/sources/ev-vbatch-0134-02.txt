Democracy is back to 1978 levels for the average global citizen. The gains of the “third wave of democratization”, starting 1974 in Portugal, are almost eradicated. The world has 92 autocracies and 87 democracies at the end of 2025. 74% of the world population (6 billion) now live in autocracies. Only 7% of the world population (0.6 billion) live in liberal democracies. A record of 41% (3.4 billion) of the world population currently reside in autocratizing countries. Almost all aspects of democracy register far-reaching declines during the last decade – a dramatic reversal compared to 25 years ago. Repression of civil society has surged to affect 30 autocratizing countries (68%). Share of world population living in democracies: 2005: 50%, 2025: 26%.
