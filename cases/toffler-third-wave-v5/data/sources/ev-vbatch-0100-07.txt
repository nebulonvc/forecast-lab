Decline in office and administrative support work suggests certain tasks and skills have been replaced by automation (The Conference Board, October 19, 2022). Across all office and administrative support occupations, there has been a rapid decline in employment, over 2 million jobs lost between 2016 and 2021. This decline has been driven by financial clerks and secretaries which have lost respectively -10.3 and -8.7 percent of jobs between 2012-16 and 2017-21. In occupations that have fallen, their skills have been replaced by automated processes. Secretaries and assistants were historically employed for their administrative and logistical help in ‘appointment setting’, ‘travel arrangements’ and ‘office management’. However, these skills have been replaced with the rise of integrated calendar and office management systems.
