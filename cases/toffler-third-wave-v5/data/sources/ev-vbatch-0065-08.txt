European Commission - Directive on repair of goods. Directive (EU) 2024/1799 on common rules promoting the repair of goods was adopted on 13 June 2024. Member States had to transpose it into national rules and apply from 31 July 2026. The Directive aims at promoting more sustainable consumption by increasing repair both within and outside the legal guarantee. Manufacturers of products (e.g., fridges or smartphones) that are subject to reparability requirements in EU law and listed in Annex II of the Directive will have to repair those products within a reasonable time and for a reasonable price. In addition, manufacturers are prohibited from using contractual clauses, hardware or software techniques that impede the repair of goods listed in Annex II unless justified by legitimate and objective factors. Manufacturers that provide spare parts for these goods must do so at reasonable prices. The amendment provides consumers with an extra year of the legal guarantee if they choose to repair the product instead of replacing it under the legal guarantee.
