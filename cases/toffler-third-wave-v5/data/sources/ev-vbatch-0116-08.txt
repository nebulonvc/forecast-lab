Majorities of adults see decline of union membership as bad for the U.S. and working people (Pew Research Center, published March 12, 2024, updated with 2024 union membership data). Majorities of Americans see the large reduction in the share of workers represented by unions over the past several decades as a bad thing for both the United States and its working people. The share of U.S. workers who belong to a union has fallen since 1983, when 20.1% were union members . In 2024, 9.9% of U.S. workers were in a union .
