Computer and Internet Use in the United States: 2021. American Community Survey Reports, By Daniela Mejía, ACS-56, June 2024, U.S. Census Bureau. Access to the internet via computer or smartphone is an important part of many Americans’ day-to-day lives. People use the internet for completing personal and professional tasks and for interacting with others across vast geographic distances. From browsing social media to paying bills online and countless other activities in between, access to the internet is nearly essential for many Americans. In recent years, the COVID-19 pandemic has further highlighted the importance of having reliable access to the internet, as businesses adopted remote telework policies, health care workers offered telehealth visits, and schools and universities switched to virtual instruction. HIGHLIGHTS FROM THE ACS: Among all households in 2021, 95 percent had at least one type of computer, and 90 percent had a broadband internet subscription. Smartphones were the most common computing device, as 90 percent of households had at least one. Eighty-one percent of households had a desktop or laptop computer, followed by 64 percent of households that owned a tablet.
