Home-Based Workers in the United States: 2010 (U.S. Census Bureau, Current Population Reports P70-132, Issued October 2012). As communication and information technologies advance, workers are increasingly able to perform work at home. Table 1. Home-Based Worker Estimates: 1997–2010 (Numbers in thousands). American Community Survey (ACS) Work at home: 2000 total employed 127,156, work at home 4,160 (3.3 percent); 2005 work at home 4,793 (3.6 percent); 2010 total employed 135,906, work at home 5,815 (4.3 percent). Survey of Income and Program Participation (SIPP, worked at home at least one full day per week): 1997 total employed 132,229, work at home 9,241 (7.0 percent); 2005 work at home 11,313 (7.8 percent); 2010 total employed 141,646, work at home 13,401 (9.5 percent).
