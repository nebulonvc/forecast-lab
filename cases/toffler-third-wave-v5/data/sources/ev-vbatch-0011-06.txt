29 September 2025. World Nuclear Industry Status Report (WNISR in the Media, originally Renew Economy, 23 September 2025). Global report confirms and details nuclear power’s stagnation: Someone needs to tell the Coalition. The latest edition of the World Nuclear Industry Status Report paints a glum picture for the nuclear power industry — the number of countries building reactors has plummeted from 16 to 11 over the past two years — and gives the lie to claims by the Coalition that Australia risks being ‘left behind’ and ‘stranded’ if we don’t jump on board. Key WNISR global indicators: World Abandoned Constructions 95; Under Construction 73; Operating 414; Long-Term Outage 32; Closed 224 (Number of Reactors as of November 2026); 32.9 Mean Age of Reactor Fleet (as of November 2026); 8.8% Stable Nuclear Share in Electricity Production (2025).
