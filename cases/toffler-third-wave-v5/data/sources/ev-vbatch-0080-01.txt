Landsat 4 was launched from Vandenberg Air Force Base in California on July 16, 1982 on a Delta 3920 rocket. The sensors onboard the satellite collected data until late 1993, and the satellite was decommissioned on June 15, 2001. Landsat 4 was built and launched by NASA. NOAA initially oversaw the operations of the satellite but was eventually contracted out to the Earth Observation Satellite Company (EOSAT) in 1984. Despite numerous operations transfers, USGS EROS has remained responsible for the record and data keeping of the Landsat program.
