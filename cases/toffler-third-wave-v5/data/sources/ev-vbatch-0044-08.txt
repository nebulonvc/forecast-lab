World Automobile Production and Fleet, 1965-2021 | The Geography of Transport Systems. Source: US Department of Energy, Transportation Energy Data Book. The last decades have experienced a growing level of motorization, as reflected by the production of automobiles and the fleet of registered cars. Although car production has a behavior linked with economic cycles of growth and recession, there is a continuous growth of the fleet, with an annual car production of about 34-40 million vehicles in the 1990s, well above 40 million in the 2000s, and surpassing 60 million in the 2010s. By 2017, the number of registered automobiles exceeded 1 billion for the first time, twice 2000 figures and four times 1990 figures. While there are fluctuations in annual automobile production, the number of registration is steadily increasing.
