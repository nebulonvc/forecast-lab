AF&PA Details U.S. Paper Production and Capacity Trends (American Forest & Paper Association, May 16, 2025). Release of the 65th Paper Industry Capacity and Fiber Consumption Survey. U.S. paper and paperboard capacity declined by 2.0% in 2024, to 78.1 million tons. By comparison, the average annual rate of decline since 2015 was 0.9%. One bleached boxboard machine came online this month following conversion from printing-writing and a recycled boxboard machine is scheduled to come online in the fourth quarter of this year as part of a new mill. U.S. Printing-Writing and Tissue Capacity: Printing-writing capacity fell 6.9% in 2024, the steepest decline since the pandemic-induced drop of 14.9% in 2021. After printing-writing capacity dropped below 10 million tons for the first time in 2023, the level is now below 9 million tons.
