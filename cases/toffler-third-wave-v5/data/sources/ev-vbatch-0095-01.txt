Kalundborg Symbiose is the umbrella organization for seventeen public and private industrial companies in Kalundborg. Since 1972, this collaboration has developed the world’s first and leading industrial symbiosis, in which member companies systematically exchange water, energy, and materials.
The basic idea is that surplus resources from one company can be used as inputs in another company’s production. This reduces waste, resource consumption, and CO₂ emissions, while also generating economic benefits for the companies.
Today, the symbiosis serves as an international benchmark for circular production and green industrial development. At the same time, the collaboration contributes to growth and innovation in Kalundborg. Kalundborg Symbiosis also serves as a platform for learning, development, and inspiration for industrial symbioses worldwide.
