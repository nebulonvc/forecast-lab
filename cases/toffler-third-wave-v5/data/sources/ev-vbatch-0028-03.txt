Table A38. Mean hourly wages among employed U.S. adults ages 16-65, by sex, literacy and numeracy proficiency level, and educational attainment: 2012/2014. Total All adults $22.62, Males $24.45, Females $20.75. Literacy proficiency level: Level 1 or below 17.41 (Males 18.33, Females 16.15); Level 2 18.39 (Males 19.37, Females 17.41); Level 3 23.91 (Males 26.24, Females 21.78); Level 4-5 32.57 (Males 35.80, Females 28.82). Numeracy proficiency level: Level 1 or below 16.69; Level 2 19.38; Level 3 25.76; Level 4-5 34.56. Educational attainment: Less than high school 16.08; High school, no postsecondary degree 17.73; Postsecondary degree 29.71. NOTE: Both literacy and numeracy score boundaries are: Level 1 or below=0-225; Level 2=226-275; Level 3=276-325; Level 4=326-375; Level 5=376-500. Wages are hourly earnings, including bonuses. Estimates based on U.S. combined data from 2012 and 2014. SOURCE: U.S. Department of Education, National Center for Education Statistics, Program for the International Assessment of Adult Competencies (PIAAC), U.S. PIAAC 2012/2014.
