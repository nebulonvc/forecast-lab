Slums continue to grow amid a deepening affordable housing crisis. While the share of slum dwellers has fallen in some regions, the total number has surged to 1.16 billion worldwide (24.8 per cent of urban residents in 2024). Without accelerated investment, the slum population could exceed 1.2 billion by 2030. Housing affordability and security remain critical barriers to sustainable urban development. In 2024, about 1.16 billion urban residents – almost one in four – lived in slums or informal settlements, lacking access to safe water, sanitation, secure housing or adequate living space. Although the share of people in these conditions has slightly declined in some regions since 2015, rapid urban population growth has continued to drive up the total number of people affected, especially in sub-Saharan Africa and parts of Asia.
