World Bank Open Data API (https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/WLD/indicator/NE.TRD.GNFS.ZS?format=json&date=1980:2024&per_page=100) reports Trade (% of GDP) for World (WLD) from 1980 to 2024. Trade is the sum of exports and imports of goods and services measured as a share of gross domestic product. World Bank indicator NE.TRD.GNFS.ZS (Trade % of GDP, World): 1980: 38.63%, 1981: 39.07%, 1982: 37.06%, 1983: 36.22%, 1984: 37.51%; 2020: 51.87%, 2021: 56.50%, 2022: 62.15%, 2023: 58.07%, 2024: 56.72%. Across the 1980 to 2024 window, global trade as a share of world GDP expanded by roughly 18 percentage points, peaking above 62 percent in 2022.
