Our World in Data: Global Education and Mean Years of Schooling Long-Run Dataset (https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/mean-years-of-schooling-long-run.csv). The world has gone through a dramatic transition over the last few centuries, from one where very few had any basic education to one where most people do. Less than 1 in 5 adults has not received any formal education. Between 1980 and 2020, mean years of formal schooling rose substantially worldwide and across major economies: World increased from 5.31 years in 1980 to 8.84 years in 2020; United States rose from 12.28 to 13.32 years; United Kingdom rose from 8.52 to 12.90 years; Germany rose from 7.36 to 13.10 years; Japan rose from 9.60 to 12.83 years; and China rose from 5.74 to 8.99 years. This sustained expansion demonstrates that formal classroom schooling has lengthened rather than contracted since 1980.
