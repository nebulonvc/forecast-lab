How many children are homeschooled in the United States?
In the National Household Education Survey (NHES), students are considered to be homeschooled if all of the following conditions are met: their parents reported them being schooled at home instead of at a public or private school, their enrollment in public or private schools did not exceed 24 hours a week
, and they were not being homeschooled only due to a temporary illness. Homeschooled students include those ages 5 to 17 with a grade equivalent of kindergarten through grade 12. More students were homeschooled in 2019 than in 1999. Within these two decades
, the percentage of students who were homeschooled first increased from 1.7 percent in 1999 to 3.4 percent in 2012
. However, the percentage of students who were homeschooled was lower in 2019 than in 2012.
