Share of One-Person Households More Than Tripled from 1940 to 2020. June 08, 2023. U.S. Census Bureau. Over a quarter (27.6%) of all U.S. occupied households were one-person households in 2020, up from just 7.7% in 1940, according to recently released 2020 Census data. The share of people living alone increased every decade from 1940 to 2020 (Figure 1). The largest increase happened between 1970 and 1980, when the share increased from 17.6% to 22.7%. The overall share of people living alone inched up (from 26.7% to 27.6%) between 2010 and 2020.
