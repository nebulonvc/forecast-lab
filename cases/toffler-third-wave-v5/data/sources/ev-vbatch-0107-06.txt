Today, more than half of the global population, over four billion people, live in cities. Cities generate 80% of global GDP and accounted for 88% of private sector job creation between 2010–2020. By 2050, nearly seven in 10 people worldwide will live in urban areas. Subnational finance is crucial for enabling cities to respond to these demands. Local governments need robust financial systems and access to capital to invest in infrastructure, services, and economic development initiatives that create jobs and improve living standards. As of 2026, our active portfolio includes over 200 projects amounting to over $40 billion.
