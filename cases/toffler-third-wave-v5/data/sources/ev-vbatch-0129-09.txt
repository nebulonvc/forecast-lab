Timeline of announcements about the local government reorganisation programme.
A representation period opened for the 5 areas that received ‘minded to’ decisions on local government reorganisation on 29 October 2026.
The representation period runs from 23 November to 14 December 2026.
The Minister for Local Government, Devolution and Regional Growth wrote to council leaders in these 5 areas to invite them to submit further representations and evidence on the provisional conclusions and reasoning set out in the ‘minded to’ decision letters. This exercise follows the earlier statutory consultation and does not reopen or replace it. Other interested parties, including residents, are also able to provide representations. There is no need to resubmit evidence or repeat points already made.
A methodology note has been published as part of this representation process and is intended to provide information on how efficiencies and financial sustainability were assessed under criterion 2 for local government reorganisation proposals.
No final decision on local government reorganisation has been taken in these 5 areas. Further representations will be considered alongside previous consultation responses and all other relevant information before a final decision is made on which proposal, if any, to implement, with or without modification. Implementation would be subject to Parliamentary approval.
​An oral statement was made to parliament by the Minister for Local Government, Devolution and Regional Growth and a written ministerial statement laid in the house. This statement updated parliament on the review of the programme announced in September.
