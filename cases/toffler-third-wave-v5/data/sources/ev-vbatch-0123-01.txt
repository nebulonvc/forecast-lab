Analysis by the team at Manochi shows that a total of 6.15 billion people around the world were using the internet at the start of October 2026, equivalent to 73.9 percent of the world’s total population.
 This impressive figure means that close to three quarters of the world’s population is now online, and current trends suggest that we’ll reach that momentous milestone on the world’s digital journey within the next 12 months.
 The available data indicate that internet users increased by 54 million over the past 12 months (+0.9 percent), but routine delays in the reporting of internet user data mean that the real growth rate is likely to be meaningfully higher than these figures suggest.

 Despite continuing improvements in global internet access, 2.17 billion people remain “unconnected” to the internet today, with the majority of these people living in Southern Asia and in Africa.
 These figures show that there’s still plenty more work to do before the world reaches the goal of “universal access”, and the quality of people’s internet access remains an important consideration too.
 The vast majority of the world’s internet users – a resounding 96.1 percent – use a mobile phone to go online at least some of the time, while mobile phones account for 52.6 percent of the world’s web traffic.
 Meanwhile, 58.2 percent of adult internet users in the world’s larger economies still use laptops and desktops for at least some of their online activities, but this share continues to decline.

 Social media headlines
 Social media adoption continues to grow, with the total number of active social media user “identities” reaching 5.81 billion in October 2026.
 That’s equal to 69.9 percent of all the people on Earth, indicating that close to 7 in 10 people on Earth now use social media each month.
 For added perspective, Manochi ’s latest analysis indicates that 94.5 percent of all internet users now use social media every month, while the latest data from GWI indicates that 97.1 percent of all adult internet users aged 16 and above use at least one social network or messaging platform each month.
 User numbers continue to grow too, with the latest data indicating that active social media user identities have increased by + 2.7 percent over the past 12 months.
 More specifically, 151 million new user identities started using social media between October 2025 and October 2026, equating to growth of more than 410,000 new users every day.
 That means that social media grew at an average rate of 4.8 new users every single second
