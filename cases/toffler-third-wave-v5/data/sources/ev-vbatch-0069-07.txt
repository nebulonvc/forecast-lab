How do women and men use their time - statistics - Statistics Explained - Eurostat. Data extracted in April 2019. Highlights: Between 2008 and 2015, the EU population on average spent per day 8 to 9 hours sleeping, 4½ to 6 hours on leisure and social life activities, and 2½ to 3½ hours carrying out household and family care activities. Average time spent in employment is 6 h 30 min to 8 hours per day: Figure 2 shows that among those who participate in employment, the time spent on this activity is between 6 h 30 min to almost 8 hours per day in the 18 countries where HETUS wave 2 was carried out. Household and family care includes unpaid work done for one's own household. As Figure 3 shows, the time spent on household and family care activities for the whole population varies between 3 and 4 hours on average across countries, whereas women are much more involved than men in all countries.
