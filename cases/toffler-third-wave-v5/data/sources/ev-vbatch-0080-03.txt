Manufactured by Lockheed in Sunnyvale, California , the KH-11 was the first American spy satellite to use electro-optical digital imaging , and to offer real-time optical observations. [ 7 ] Later KH-11 satellites have been referred to by outside observers as KH-11B or KH-12, and by the names "Advanced KENNEN", "Improved Crystal" and "Ikon". Official budget documents refer to the latest generation of electro-optical satellites as Evolved Enhanced CRYSTAL System .
