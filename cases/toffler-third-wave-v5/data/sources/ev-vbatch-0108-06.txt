WTO | Regional Trade Agreements gateway. Regional trade agreements (RTAs) have risen in number and reach over the years, including a notable increase in large plurilateral agreements. Non-discrimination among trading partners is one of the core principles of the WTO; however, RTAs, which are reciprocal preferential trade agreements between two or more partners, constitute one of the derogations and are authorized under the WTO, subject to a set of rules. As of 1 October 2026, 388 RTAs were in force. These correspond to 642 notifications from WTO members, counting goods, services and accessions separately. In addition, the WTO Secretariat has also identified and verified, through the respective parties, at least 84 RTAs that are currently in force but had not yet been notified to the WTO by October 2026 (see WT/REG/W/200).
