In the long-run trends, household production's size relative to market production has declined. Compared to 1965, household production is smaller relative to GDP despite the 2020 increase in its relative importance. Including the household sector in GDP slows the growth rate of output. From 1965 to 2020, the average annual growth rate of nominal GDP was 6.3 percent. When household production is included, this growth rate drops to 6.1 percent. Household production has declined in significance over time as more women engage in market work. This sector accounted for 37 percent of the satellite account's output in 1965, but that declined to 25 percent in 2020 (table 2). The recent uptick in household production increased the relative size of the household sector. It was only 22 percent of the satellite accounts output in 2019. But it did not undo the longer-run decline in the relative importance of the sector.
