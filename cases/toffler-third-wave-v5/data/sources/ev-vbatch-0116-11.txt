Education and Skills Act 2008 (c. 25), Part 1, Chapter 1, Section 2: Duty to participate in education or training (England). (1) A person to whom this Part applies must— (a) be participating in appropriate full-time education or training (see section 4), (b) be participating in training in accordance with a contract of apprenticeship or an apprenticeship agreement , or (c) both— (i) be in full-time occupation (see section 5), and (ii) participate in sufficient relevant training or education in each relevant period (see sections 6 to 8).
