Typewriters reach the end of the line in the UK (The Guardian, November 21, 2012). The presentation of the last Brother CM-1000 to the Science Museum marks the end of the manufacture of typewriters in the UK. It was at 12pm on Friday that the last British-made typewriter was packed into its box at the Brother factory in Wrexham. The decision was taken six months ago to stop production, says Phil Jones, Brother's UK head. "Clearly, typewriters have been undergoing a decline in many years. There's always a point where it's not economically viable any more, and we always knew that time was coming." The factory had been making 300-500 machines a month, accounting for just 0.25% of the company's turnover.
