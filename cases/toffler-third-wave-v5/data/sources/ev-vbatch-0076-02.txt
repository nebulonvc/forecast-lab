For the full Adjusted and Not Adjusted estimates Time Series, see https://www.census.gov/retail/ecommerce.html. The Census Bureau of the Department of Commerce announced today that the estimate of U.S. retail e-commerce sales for the third quarter of 2026, adjusted for seasonal variation, but not for price changes, was $347.3 billion, an increase of 1.6 percent (±0.4 percent) from the second quarter of 2026. Total retail sales for the third quarter of 2026 were estimated at $1,941.9 billion, an increase of 1.1 percent (±0.2 percent) from the second quarter of 2026. The third quarter 2026 e-commerce estimate increased 10.4 percent (±0.9 percent) from the third quarter of 2025 while total retail sales increased 5.6 percent (±0.5 percent) in the same period. E-commerce sales in the third quarter of 2026 accounted for 17.9 percent of total sales. On a not adjusted basis, the estimate of U.S. retail e-commerce sales for the third quarter of 2026 totaled $335.7 billion, an increase of 0.6 percent (±0.4 percent) from the second quarter of 2026. The third quarter 2026 e-commerce estimate increased 10.4 percent (±0.9 percent) from the third quarter of 2025 while total retail sales increased 5.6 percent (±0.5 percent) in the same period. E- commerce sales in the third quarter of 2026 accounted for 17.2 percent of total sales.
