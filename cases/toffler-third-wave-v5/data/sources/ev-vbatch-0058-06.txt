Until 1976, the prices at which CMEA trade took place were based on the average of world price-s over the previous five years. Prices, once computed, remained fixed throughout the 5YP period. Subsequent to 1976, prices were based on a moving average of past prices. However, since the moving average was calculated for five years, this represented no change compared with the earlier procedure. Price determination rules for raw materials were directly established with reference to world markets. For example, for oil, the price of a particular grade from Rotterdam was that prevailing at Rotterdam, plus half the cost of transportation from that port to the importing CMEA country. The prices thus determined were however but starting points or "guidelines" for more detailed negotiations that took place in the course of annual foreign trade planning, at which stage negotiations resulted in further amendments. Thus, in the final outcome, with the exception of fuels and raw materials, there was often little systematic relationship between the pricing guidelines established in the initial negotiations and the prices at which CMEA exchange took place.
