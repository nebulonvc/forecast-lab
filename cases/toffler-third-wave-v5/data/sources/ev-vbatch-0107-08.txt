The Return of Industrial Policy in Data (February 12, 2024), Simon Evenett, Adam Jakubik (IMF), Fernando Martín (Global Trade Alert), and Michele Ruta (IMF). Industrial policies are any targeted government intervention aimed at developing or supporting specific domestic firms, industries, or economic activities to achieve national economic or noneconomic (e.g., security, environmental) objectives. In 2023 a total of 2580 unilateral state interventions were recorded; 71% are trade distorting. AEs accounted for 70.9 percent and EMDEs for 29.1 percent of trade distorting NIPs to date. Systemic players: China, EU, and US together account for 47.7 percent.
