DIGITAL ECONOMY. BEA’s Digital Economy Satellite Account measures the digital economy’s contribution to U.S. GDP, improves measures of high-tech goods and services, and offers a more complete picture of international trade. It includes valuing digital-enabling infrastructure, e-commerce transactions, and digital media. GDP Share of Total GDP: 10.0% of the U.S. economy, or $2.6 trillion, in 2022. Economic Growth: 6.3% annual growth in 2022 Outpaced overall U.S. economic growth of 1.9%. Jobs: 8.9 million jobs in 2022; 3.2% average annual growth, 2017–2022; $1.3 trillion in total compensation in 2022.
