Office/Occupier | CBRE | U.S. Real Estate Market Outlook 2024. An economic slowdown in the first half of the year and the normalization of hybrid working arrangements will continue to limit office demand in 2024. More than half of the respondents to CBRE’s 2023 U.S. Office Occupier Sentiment Survey said they plan to further reduce their office space in 2024. For pre-2010 buildings, absorption has been negative for the past four quarters. A recent increase in tenants that are actively looking for space across major markets suggests that leasing activity will rise by 5% in 2024 but will remain between 20% and 25% below pre-pandemic levels. Source: CBRE Econometric Advisors, Q3 2023.
