GitHub Blog — Octoverse 2024: AI leads Python to top language as the number of global developers surges (Published October 29, 2024). Similar to last year, we saw commercially backed and generative AI projects attract the most contributions in 2024. But where those contributions came from is notable with regions outside North America and Europe surging in overall activity. There’s a continued increase in first-time contributors to open source projects. 1.4 million new developers globally joined open source with a majority contributing to commercially backed and generative AI projects.
