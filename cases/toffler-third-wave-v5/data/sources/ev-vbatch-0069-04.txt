Actual and usual hours of work - Statistics Explained - Eurostat. Data extracted in May 2026. Highlights: In 2025, the average working week at EU level lasted 35.9 hours. This varied across the EU, from 31.9 actual hours of work in the Netherlands to 39.6 in Greece. Among full-time employed people in the EU, men worked on average 39.4 hours per week compared with 37.6 hours for women, with differences exceeding 3 hours in Ireland, the Netherlands and Greece. Belgium had the largest difference in usual weekly working hours between employees (35.2 hours) and own-account workers (43.2 hours). More specifically, it focuses on the average number of weekly actual and usual hours of work which employed people spent in their main job in 2025.
