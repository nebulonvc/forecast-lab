The difficulty of changing complex bilateral trade agreements, the use of precedent to establish prices, and the excessive focus on past results in preparing new plans 8 lead to a price structure that is more rigid than in market economies. In fact, the official CMEA pricing formula, adopted in 1975, codifies this rigidity by employing a five- year moving average formula for calculating prices. One important result of the price rigidity is that rapid changes can occur in the real. value of a transferable ruble relative to the dollar.
