Bioeconomy employment and value added: 2023 data - Infographic. Knowledge4Policy (K4P), European Commission Joint Research Centre (JRC). Available from 19 Nov 2025. Geographic coverage: European Union EU. Based on the dataset from Lasarte-López et al. (2025) and the methodology described in Ronzon et al., 2020.
