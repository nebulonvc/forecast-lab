In 1975, the cable television industry had 9.8 million subscriptions across 71.1 million U.S. households (or approximately 14%), which grew to 19.2 million subscriptions across the 80.8 million households (or approximately 24%) by 1980. [ 3 ] [ 4 ] By 1988, cable television subscriptions grew to 45.7 million across 91.1 million households (or approximately 50%), [ 4 ] while cable and satellite television subscriptions combined grew to 66.2 million across 99 million households (or approximately 67%) by 1995. [ 3 ] [ 5 ] While the number of cable television subscriptions began declining in 2001, combined cable and satellite subscriptions grew further to 90.5 million across 112 million households (or approximately 80%) by 2004, [ 3 ] [ 5 ] and combined subscriptions continued to grow to 95.5 million through 2007 after which combined subscriptions began falling during the Great Recession .
