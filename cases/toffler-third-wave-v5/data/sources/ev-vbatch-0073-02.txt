Intended additions to SNA
• Three new chapters are being prepared to introduce additional content on well-being and
sustainability into the SNA.
• Chapter 2 will highlight the significance of the topic from a national accounting
perspective and describe the need to extend and broaden the SNA and its links to other
areas of measurement
• Chapter 34 (Well-being) and Chapter 35 (Sustainability) summarise the measurement of
topics beyond the sequence of economic accounts.
• Topics include – unpaid household work, distributions of income, wealth and consumption, human capital,
health accounts, accounts for education and training, and environmental-economic accounting

In summary…
• The ISWGNA guidance includes detailed recommendations on the inclusion of unpaid
household service work in macroeconomic statistics
• The main recommendations will feed into the new SNA, although in a condensed form,
focusing on the main conceptual issues
• Countries will be encouraged to compile the results in the form of extended accounts
• However, neither the SNA or the guidance note have any mandated requirements, they simply
set out a conceptual framework (SNA manual) and make some recommendations (ISWGNA
note) of how to create these extended accounts
• International organisations may see if they will encourage regular compilation of these results
and/or make it part of their regular data collection
