Does Raising the State Compulsory School Attendance Age Achieve the Intended Outcomes? REL 2014-005 (Philip E. Mackey & Teresa G. Duncan, Institute of Education Sciences, 2013, ERIC ED544499). Maryland recently raised its compulsory school attendance age from 16 to 18 in two stages: from 16 to 17 at the beginning of the 2014-15 school year and from 17 to 18 at the beginning of the 2016-17 school year (Maryland Senate Bill 362, 2012). Against this background, this review addresses what changes have occurred in dropouts, truancy, and disciplinary actions in states that raised their compulsory school attendance age during 2002-11. Presently, findings indicate that there is insufficient evidence to build a case for or against raising the compulsory school attendance age.
