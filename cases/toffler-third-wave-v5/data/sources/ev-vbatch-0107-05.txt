Multinational enterprises in the global economy: Heavily debated but hardly measured. The OECD analytical AMNE database (May 2018). Activities under foreign control show growing production by foreign affiliates between 2000 and 2014. MNE activities at home and abroad: Almost one-third of global production is done by MNEs. MNEs also account for half of global exports, almost one-third of world GDP and about onefourth of employment.
