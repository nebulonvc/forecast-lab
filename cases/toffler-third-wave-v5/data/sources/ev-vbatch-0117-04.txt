Compulsory school attendance laws, minimum and maximum age limits for required free education, by state: 2017. Statutory age of required school attendance shows that roughly half of U.S. jurisdictions require attendance until age 18 or 19, including Arkansas (5 to 18), California (6 to 18), Connecticut (5 to 18), District of Columbia (5 to 18), Hawaii (5 to 18), Indiana (7 to 18), Kansas (7 to 18), Kentucky (6 to 18), Louisiana (7 to 18), Maryland (5 to 18), Michigan (6 to 18), Nebraska (6 to 18), Nevada (7 to 18), New Hampshire (6 to 18), New Mexico (5 to 18), Ohio (6 to 18), Oklahoma (5 to 18), Oregon (6 to 18), Rhode Island (5 to 18), South Dakota (6 to 18), Tennessee (6 to 18), Texas (6 to 19), Utah (6 to 18), Virginia (5 to 18), Washington (8 to 18), and Wisconsin (6 to 18), while several other states require attendance to age 17 (e.g., Alabama, Colorado, Illinois, Maine, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Pennsylvania, South Carolina, West Virginia) and the remainder to age 16.
