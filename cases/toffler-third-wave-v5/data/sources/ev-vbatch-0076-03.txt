Inventories Manufacturers’ and trade inventories for September, adjusted for seasonal and trading day differences but not for price changes, were estimated at an end-of-month level of $2,873.4 billion, up 0.5 percent (±0.1 percent) from August 2026 and were up 5.1 percent (±0.4 percent) from September 2025.
