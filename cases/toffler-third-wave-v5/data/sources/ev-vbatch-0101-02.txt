WASHINGTON – The American Forest & Paper Association (AF&PA) released the October 2026 Printing-Writing Monthly report.
According to the report, total year‑to‑date printing‑writing paper shipments decreased 10% through October compared to the same period last year. U.S. purchases of total printing-writing papers increased 5% in October compared to October 2025. Total printing‑writing paper inventory levels decreased 3% from September 2026.
Individually, across the 3 major printing-writing categories–uncoated free sheet (UFS), coated free sheet (CFS), and mechanical (MECH)–year‑to‑date shipments declined through October. The UFS operating rate hit the highest since March 2022. Compared to year-ago, CFS purchases increased 5%, while MECH purchases increased 2%.
