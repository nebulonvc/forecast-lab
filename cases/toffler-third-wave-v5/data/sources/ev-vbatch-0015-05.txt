FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: Thursday, November 19, 2026 Quarterly Retail E-Commerce Sales November 19, 2026 CB26-183 Notice of Revision: Quarterly retail e-commerce estimates were revised based on historical corrections and the results of the 2023 and 2024 Annual Integrated Economic Survey. Quarterly Retail E-Commerce Sales Report The Census Bureau of the Department of Commerce announced today that the estimate of U.S. retail e-commerce sales for the third quarter of 2026, adjusted for seasonal variation, but not for price changes, was $347.3 billion, an increase of 1.6 percent (±0.4 percent) from the second quarter of 2026. Total retail sales for the third quarter of 2026 were estimated at $1,941.9 billion, an increase of 1.1 percent (±0.2 percent) from the second quarter of 2026. The third quarter 2026 e-commerce estimate increased 10.4 percent (±0.9 percent) from the third quarter of 2025 while total retail sales increased 5.6 percent (±0.5 percent) in the same period. E-commerce sales in the third quarter of 2026 accounted for 17.9 percent of total sales. On a not adjusted basis, the estimate of U.S. retail e-commerce sales for the third quarter of 2026 totaled $335.7 billion, an increase of 0.6 percent (±0.4 percent) from the second quarter of 2026. The third quarter 2026 e-commerce estimate increased 10.4 percent (±0.9 percent) from the third quarter of 2025 while total retail sales increased 5.6 percent (±0.5 percent) in the same period.
