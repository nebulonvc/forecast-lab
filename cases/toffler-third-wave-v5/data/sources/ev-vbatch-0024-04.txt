The Home Video Explosion The rapid growth and diffusion of home video, its enthusiastic adoption by users, greatly alarmed the film industry. As table 3.2 shows, yearly sales of VCRs jumped from 802,000 in 1980 to 11—12 million per year during the second half of the decade. Sales of videodisc players, by contrast, remained sluggish, never substantially clearing 300,000 in any year. Sales of prerecorded videocassettes (chart 3.1) neared 50 million in 1985 and exceeded 200 million in 1989.
