World Bank Open Data Indicator NE.TRD.GNFS.ZS: Trade (% of GDP), Aggregate: World (WLD), 1980-2024. Source: World Bank national accounts data, and OECD National Accounts data files. Trade is the sum of exports and imports of goods and services. This indicator is expressed as a percentage of Gross Domestic Product (GDP) which is the total income earned through the production of goods and services in an economic territory during an accounting period. Reported World (WLD) values: 1980: 38.63%; 1981: 39.07%; 1982: 37.06%; 1990: 38.10%; 2000: 50.12%; 2010: 56.19%; 2019: 55.80%; 2020: 51.87%; 2021: 56.50%; 2022: 62.15%; 2023: 58.07%; 2024: 56.72%.
