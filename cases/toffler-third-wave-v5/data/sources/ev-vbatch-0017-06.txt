DIGITAL ECONOMY. BEA’s Digital Economy Satellite Account measures the digital economy’s contribution to U.S. GDP, improves measures of high-tech goods and services, and offers a more complete picture of international trade. It includes valuing digital-enabling infrastructure, e-commerce transactions, and digital media. Economic Growth: 6.3% annual growth in 2022. Outpaced overall U.S. economic growth of 1.9%. GDP Share of Total GDP: 10.0% of the U.S. economy, or $2.6 trillion, in 2022. Jobs: 8.9 million jobs in 2022. $1.3 trillion in total compensation in 2022. Latest statistics: This infographic reflects statistics published through December 2023.
