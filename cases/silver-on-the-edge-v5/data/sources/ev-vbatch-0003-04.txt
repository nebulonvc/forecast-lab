American Attitudes Toward Gaming 2026 November 16, 2026
88% of Americans say gambling is acceptable entertainment and a majority (52%) have gambled in the past year.
113 million American adults visited a casino in the past year with most crediting the industry with boosting local economies.
“Prediction markets” like Kalshi continue to misrepresent their product as investing while 75% of Americans agree that sports betting on a “prediction market” is fundamentally the same product as betting at a sportsbook.
A bipartisan 82% of Americans believe “prediction markets” should be regulated as gambling products rather than financial products. That view spans party lines, with agreement among 84% of Democrats, 82% of Republicans, and 80% of independents.
Methodology The online survey was conducted on Qualtrics September 2–18, 2026, among a nationally representative sample of U.S. adults.
