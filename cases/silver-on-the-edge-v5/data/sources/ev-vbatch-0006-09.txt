California Frontier AI Transparency Act Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act United States • California RAI-US-CA-CS5TFXX-2025 SB 53 Effective: 1 Jan 2026 In Force Checked 10 Sep 2026 California Frontier AI Transparency Act is In Force in United States as of 10 Sep 2026 , according to leginfo.legislature.ca.gov. Summary California SB 53 requires frontier AI developers to disclose model information, report critical safety incidents, and protect whistleblowers. Large developers must also publish risk frameworks and assessment summaries, with specified violations carrying civil penalties up to $1 million. The Act took effect January 1, 2026; CalCompute provisions depend on an appropriation. This law applies to "large frontier developers"—companies with over $500 million in annual revenue that have trained AI models using more than 10^26 computing operations.
