Q3 2026 Commercial Gaming Revenue Tracker 
The American Gaming Association’s (AGA) Commercial Gaming Revenue Tracker features state-by-state and nationwide financial performance data with breakdowns for individual gaming verticals. 
TOPLINE INDUSTRY TRENDS 
Record Third Quarter: Commercial gaming revenue reached $19.81 billion in the third quarter, a 4.6 percent increase over last year and the highest third-quarter total on record, with growth across all three major verticals. 
Q3 Closes Higher: September commercial gaming revenue totaled $6.49 billion, up 4.7 percent year-over-year. 
Gaming Taxes Grow: State gaming taxes increased 5.8 percent in the third quarter to $4.42 billion, bringing the year-to-date total to $13.63 billion (+6.7%). 
2 9 of the 3 7 commercial gaming states that have reported complete Q3 data saw annual commercial gaming revenue increases in Q3 of 2026. Oregon (+51.9%), New Hampshire (+30.7%), North Carolina (+23.6%) and Nebraska (+21.8%) reported the strongest growth. 
Traditional casino gaming expanded by 3.0 percent to $13.17 billion in the third quarter of the year, the highest third-quarter total on record and second highest ever . Both slot (+2.7%) and table (+3.9%) revenue increased in Q3. New York led the gains (+$167.9 million, +20.7%), including $129.8 million in table games revenue from Resorts World New York City, which began offering table games earlier this year , followed by Nevada (+$74.2 million, +2.0%) and Illinois (+$64.0 million, +12.8%). Iowa (-7.5%), Pennsylvania (-2.9%) and Maryland (-3.7%) posted the largest declines. For September, slot revenue grew 3.8 percent and table game revenue rose 2.6 percent .

Sports betting revenue totaled $3.58 billion (+3.8%) in Q3 with a handle of $38.15 billion (+8.2%). Hold slipped 39 basis points to 9.4 percent in Q3. Missouri, which began reporting sports wagering in December 2025, added $53.7 million in revenue and $778.2 million in handle; excluding Missouri, sports betting revenue rose 2.3 percent and handle rose 6.0 percent. 
September sports betting revenue rose 1.7 percent to $1.31 billion on handle of $15.74 billion (+1.5%), with hold of 8.3 percent, in line with last year. 
iGaming generated a record $3.05 billion in Q3 revenue, a 13.5 percent increase compared to the previous year. However, as markets mature and no new markets open, Q3 saw the slowest quarterly growth rate for iGaming since 2018. 
September iGaming revenue expanded 12.8 percent to $1.02 billion.
