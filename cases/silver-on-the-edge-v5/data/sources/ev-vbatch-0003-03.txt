Ethan Yau Nationality United States GPI Rank 318 WSOP Bracelets 2 Total Live Earnings $4,093,201 Data provided by The Hendon Mob Biography Ethan Yau is one of the absolute titans in the poker vlogging space and has gone from playing $1/$2 to some of the highest stakes games imaginable in just a few years. Yau has found success not just on YouTube, but also on the green felt itself and is a WSOP bracelet winner as well as two wins on the World Poker Tour . In addition to those titles, he's found success on the WSOP Circuit and has WSOPC five rings to his name.
At the 2026 WSOP, Yau won his second bracelet, defeating Adrian Mateos in heads-up to win the WSOP Online - Hybrid $5K Championship .
Yau regularly appears on poker live streams such as Hustler Casino Live and The Lodge Poker Club and took part in the former's Million Dollar buy-in cash game .
