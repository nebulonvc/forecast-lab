Important Update: Initial Payments Start November 23
The first payments from the Bartz v. Anthropic settlement are expected to go out during the week of November 23.

Only approximately 70 percent ($2,203.56) will be paid out now. This is just the first installment per work. The remainder will be paid out at a later time.
The settlement requires Anthropic to add money to the settlement fund within 30 days of its IPO, which happened last week. Once Anthropic has deposited the remaining funds, we expect the 30 percent balance to go out quickly.
Claims that show up as disputed in the portal will be paid out later:
In cases where the dispute is due to errors or where the only disputes exist on the publisher side (such as two competing publishers or imprints of the same publisher), they will likely be paid in December.

Bartz v. Anthropic is one of the major copyright lawsuits brought by authors against an AI company for using books without permission to train large language models. It was filed by nonfiction authors Charles Graeber ( The Good Nurse ) and Kirk Wallace Johnson ( The Feather Thief ) and thriller author Andrea Bartz ( We Were Never Here ).
In June 2025, Judge William Alsup of the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California ruled on summary judgment that using books without permission to train AI was fair use if they were acquired legally, but he denied Anthropic’s request for summary judgment related to piracy—finding that the piracy was not fair use. Judge Alsup scheduled a trial to determine Anthropic’s potential liability for piracy for December 1, 2025.
In July, the court certified a class of all rightsholders of books Anthropic acquired from LibGen and PiLiMi, provided the books were registered with the U.S. Copyright Office in a timely manner and have ISBN or ASIN numbers. Judge Alsup certified the class only for piracy, not the act of AI training, and as such the fair use ruling on training applies only with respect to the three named plaintiffs, not the certified class authors and publishers. The proposed settlement agreement came after an arms-length mediation with a neutral third-party mediator. The settlement agreement was preliminarily approved by Judge Alsup on September 25, 2025.
The deadline to file a claim was March 30, 2026. Final approval of the settlement was granted on July 20, 2026.
In September, the settlement administrator sent out notices to all claimants summarizing each of their claims and noting who else filed a claim for each title and what percentage of the award others claimed. The initial 30-day window for co-claimants to try and resolve any allocation disputes was extended to 60 days. Read more about the claims reconciliation process here.
As of late October, initial payments to claimants will go out during the week of November 23. Claimants who submitted allocation changes through the settlement portal will receive a notice before that summarizing the changes successfully submitted.
All Anthropic settlement claims that show up as undisputed in the settlement portal after November 20 will be paid out the week of November 23. Only approximately 70 percent ($2,203.56) will be paid out now. The rest will be paid after Anthropic makes its final payment (due 30 days after its IPO).
Claims that show up as disputed in the portal will not be paid out that week. In cases where the dispute is due to errors that can be easily addressed, they will be paid soon. Please provide corrections to the Settlement Administrator right away, so the claims can be dealt with expeditiously.
