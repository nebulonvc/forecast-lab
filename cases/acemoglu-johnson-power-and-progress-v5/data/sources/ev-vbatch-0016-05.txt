At the same time, 33% of Americans say they have “not very much” confidence in the media, and an equal 33% have “none at all.”
The latest results are from Gallup’s Sept. 1-17 Governance poll. The survey was completed just before the Trump administration denied White House access to several national news organizations beginning Sept. 18, accusing them of unfair reporting. Other major news organizations subsequently curtailed some White House pool coverage in response. Because these events occurred after Gallup’s field period ended, any effect they may have on Americans’ views of the media is not reflected in the current results.
Despite this year’s increase, confidence in the media remains near the bottom of Gallup’s more than 50-year trend. When Gallup first asked the question in the 1970s, about seven in 10 Americans said they trusted the media.
