DEMOCRACY REPORT 2026 – 10TH EDITION Executive Summary 1. Democracy in the World 2025 y Democracy is back to 1978 levels for the average global citizen. The gains of the “third wave of democratization”, starting 1974 in Portugal, are almost eradicated. y The level of democracy for the average citizen in Western Europe and North America is at its lowest level in over 50 years, primarily due to ongoing autocratization in the USA. y The USA loses its long-term status as a liberal democracy – for the first time in over 50 years. Autocracies and Democracies y The world has 92 autocracies and 87 democracies at the end of 2025. y 74%oftheworldpopulation(6billion)nowliveinautocracies. y Only 7% of the world population (0.6 billion) live in liberal democracies. The “Great Reversal”: 2000 vs. 2025 y Almost all aspects of democracy register far-reaching declines during the last decade – a dramatic reversal compared to 25 years ago. y Freedom of expression is the most hard-hit: 44 countries are declining in 2025. y Tortureisnowincreasinglyusedtosuppresspoliticaloppo­ sition–33countriesaresubstantiallydegeneratingin2025. 2.TrendsofRegimeTransformation y The world has never before seen as many countries autocratizing at the same time as during the last few years of the “third wave of autocratization”. y A record of 41% (3.4 billion) of the world population currently reside in autocratizing countries. y TheEuropeanUnionisgreatlyaffected. AutocratizationinEuropeaffectssevenEUmember statesandtwoofitsmainallies–theUKandtheUSA. 3. Autocratizing Countries y Nearly a quarter of the world’s nations are now on the list of autocratizers: 44 countries. y 44 autocratizers divide into 24 stand-alone autocratizers and 20 Bell-turns. y 10 new autocratizers identified in 2025. y Among the new autocratizers are five European countries: Croatia, Italy, Slovakia, Slovenia, and the UK. y Media censorship remains the most common tactic among autocratizing governments with 32 countries (73%) resorting to it. y Repression of civil society has surged to affect 30 autocratizing countries (68%).
