Augmented and Virtual Reality Headsets Market Insights: Q2 2026. Updated: October 30, 2026. Display Glasses Carry the Market as Headset Demand Cools. Q2 2026 Performance: Glasses Offset a Headset Decline. Worldwide AR/VR headset shipments grew 5.1% year over year in Q2 2026, according to IDC's Worldwide Quarterly Augmented and Virtual Reality Headset Tracker. Shipments declined 20.1% from the previous quarter as headset volumes weakened. Extended reality glasses drove the growth, rising 79.8% year over year to capture 46.0% of the market. Mixed reality headsets still held the largest share at 49.3% but declined 22.3%. Meta raised prices on Quest 3 and Quest 3S in April to pass through higher memory chip costs. Virtual reality devices fell 37.6%, while augmented reality grew 14.1% from a small base. Regional results diverged. The PRC grew 85.8% and Japan grew 88.2%, while the US and Western Europe declined 24.1% and 25.2%, respectively.
