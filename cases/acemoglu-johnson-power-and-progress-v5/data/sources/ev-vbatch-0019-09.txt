Online Deliberation Platform | Deliberative Democracy Lab
The AI-assisted Stanford Online Deliberation Platform is a video discussion platform designed for small group discussions. The platform is designed to facilitate a structured and equitable conversation with better opportunity for participants to speak up.
The AI-assisted Stanford Platform for Online Deliberation is a collaboration between the Stanford’s Deliberative Democracy Lab and Crowdsourced Democracy Team housed in the Department of Management Science & Engineering at Stanford University. The platform is designed based on the Deliberative Polling methodology to massively scale deliberation to allow unlimited number of participants to deliberate in small groups together simultaneously. Among its many features is an automated moderator that allows participants to form speaking queues, discuss in small groups with timed agendas, and allow for equitable participation. The platform is easy to use without any downloads, includes abuse prevention, and real-time analytics. The platform has been operates in 40+ languages (including Japanese, Chinese, French) and used in over 40+ countries, including for national Deliberative Polling events in Chile, Canada, and the United States. As of fall 2026, this platform has logged over 50,000 unique users and over 100,000 hours of deliberations.
To learn more, please also visit: stanforddeliberate.org or contact deliberationplatform@lists.stanford.edu for more information.
