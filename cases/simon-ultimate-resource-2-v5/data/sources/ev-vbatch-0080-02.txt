Agriculture, aquaculture, plantations, other rural sectors | International Labour Organization (ILO). Globally, about one billion people work in the agricultural sector , constituting approximately 28 per cent of the employed population. About 450 million are waged workers, many of whom depend on incomes from jobs in the plantation sector. While the share of employment in agriculture has declined in many countries due to technological advancements and economic shifts, it remains a critical sector for livelihoods, food security, and rural development. However, agriculture workers regularly face high levels of working poverty, food insecurity, poor health and safety conditions, and lack of labour and social protection.
