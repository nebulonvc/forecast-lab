World Bank World Development Indicators (WDI) API (lastupdated: 2026-11-11), indicator SP.RUR.TOTL (Rural population, 1996-2025): World (WLD) rural population increased from 3,213,684,390 in 1996 to 3,464,845,933 in 2025 (+7.8%). Low & middle income (LMY) rural population rose from 2,909,653,403 in 1996 to 3,183,634,484 in 2025 (+9.4%). Sub-Saharan Africa (SSF) rural population surged from 422,543,602 in 1996 to 731,422,742 in 2025 (+73.1%), and South Asia (SAS) rose from 847,615,563 in 1996 to 1,086,549,811 in 2025 (+28.2%; India from 717,468,852 to 941,424,277). By contrast, East Asia & Pacific (EAS) rural population declined from 1,235,428,920 in 1996 to 842,281,252 in 2025 (-31.8%), led by China (CHN) falling from 846,442,414 in 1996 to 473,414,440 in 2025 (-44.1%). Latin America & Caribbean (LCN) rural population declined from 129,643,128 in 1996 to 123,279,238 in 2025 (-4.9%), and High income (HIC/XD) fell from 304,030,987 to 281,211,449 (-7.5%).
