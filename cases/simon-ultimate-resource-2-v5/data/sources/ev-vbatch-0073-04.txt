World Bank World Development Indicators (WDI) official API data retrieved for 1996-2024 (sourceid=2, lastupdated=2026-11-11; indicators SP.DYN.TFRT.IN, SE.SEC.ENRR, SE.TER.ENRR, NY.GDP.PCAP.KD). From 1996 to 2024 in World Bank WDI data (last updated 2026-11-11), global gross secondary school enrollment rose from 56.56% to 77.28% and real GDP per capita (constant 2015 US$) rose from 7,202.7 to 11,894.3 while total fertility declined from 2.82 to 2.19. Global tertiary gross enrollment also increased from 16.39% in 1996 to 43.62% in 2024. In Low and Middle Income countries (LMY), total fertility fell from 3.14 (1996) to 2.33 (2024), secondary enrollment rose from 49.85% to 73.91%, tertiary enrollment rose from 9.91% to 38.79%, and real GDP per capita rose from 1,925.4 to 5,539.3 (constant 2015 US$). In Sub-Saharan Africa (SSF), total fertility moved from 5.94 (1996) to 4.26 (2024), secondary enrollment rose from 23.90% to 45.91%, and real GDP per capita rose from 1,181.7 to 1,588.9 (constant 2015 US$).
