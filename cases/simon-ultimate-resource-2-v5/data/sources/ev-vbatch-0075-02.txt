World Bank Open Data API — Population growth (annual %) for World (indicator SP.POP.GROW, 1996 to 2024). World Bank indicator SP.POP.GROW (World): 1996 annual population growth rate was 1.490%, declining to 1.271% in 2005, 1.184% in 2015, 0.927% in 2023, and 0.962% in 2024. These figures show that a single year's population increment in 1996 represented approximately 1.49% of the global population stock, and global annual population growth slowed substantially over the subsequent three decades.
