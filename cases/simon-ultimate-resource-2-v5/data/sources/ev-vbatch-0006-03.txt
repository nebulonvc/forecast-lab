Mhetras N, Gokhale D. Sustainable biodiesel production: importance of feedstock resources and production methods. RSC Advances (2025-07-25, PMC12302348). Vegetable oils, animal fats, microbial oils, algal oils, and waste oils are used as renewable feedstocks for biodiesel production. Different feedstocks have their advantages and challenges in relation to their availability, cost, energy content and sustainability. The major cost (80%) comes from the feedstock, and hence the feedstock selection seems to be a very crucial step for biodiesel production. B100 is seldom used as a fuel and is not regarded as practical due to the cost factors and availability. About 6.05 billion liters of biodiesel was produced in USA in 2021 and world's biodiesel production is expected to reach 40 billion liters in 2024 using food-based feedstocks.
