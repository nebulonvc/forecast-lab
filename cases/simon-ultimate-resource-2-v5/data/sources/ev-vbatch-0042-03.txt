IEA (2025), Renewables 2025, IEA, Paris. Heat accounted for almost half of total final energy consumption and 37% of energy-related CO2 emissions in 2024. Annual heat consumption expanded by 6% globally over 2018-2024. Renewable energy, excluding traditional uses of biomass, met only half of this increase, with its share in global heat consumption rising to 14% in 2024. Nearly 80% of global growth in renewable heat use was in the form of bioenergy (especially in industry) and renewable electricity (mainly in buildings). Despite domestic market contraction since 2020 due to Covid-19 lockdowns, China continues to dominate global solar thermal developments, being responsible for over 40% of consumption growth during the outlook period.
