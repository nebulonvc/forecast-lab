World Bank World Development Indicators (lastupdated: 2026-11-11), boundary check using NY.GDP.TOTL.RT.ZS (Total natural resources rents, % of GDP, 1996-2021) and NV.SRV.TOTL.ZS (Services, value added, % of GDP, 1996-2024). Global total natural resources rents as % of GDP did not fall monotonically every year, rising from 1.766% in 1996 to 5.017% in 2008 and 3.061% in 2021, while global broad services value added hovered around 63.3%-65.7% (64.402% in 1996 to 63.302% in 2024; OECD rose from 65.328% in 1997 to 69.819% in 2021; US rose from 71.809% to 76.256%). This confirms that while information, intellectual property, and R&D shares expanded strongly over 1996-2025/2026, the expansion was secular rather than strictly monotonic in every single calendar year.
