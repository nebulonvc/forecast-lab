Fish is Food – The FAO's Fish Price Index (PLoS ONE, published 2012-05-08, Tveterås et al.). Technological changes in salmon aquaculture, for instance, have driven down the relative price of salmon and dramatically expanded the salmon market. Figure 7 shows the aggregated FPI as well as the index disaggregated for wild and farmed fish between 1994 and 2010 using the mean in the 2002–2004 period as base. Price movements for wild and farmed fish are similar through the 1990s, but prices diverge at the turn of the century. The price index for farmed fish declines, and it subsequently has only a weak positive trend. In contrast, the food crisis of 2007–2008 had little visible impact on the aquaculture index. Price increases have been less pronounced in Asia compared to other regions because lower prices in aquaculture have dampened upward pressure on regional seafood prices.
