National Park Service - Entrance Passes and Reservations (nps.gov/planyourvisit/passes.htm). Reservations and Timed Entry: When planning a visit to a national park, check if you need a reservation. Some national parks use a timed entry system to manage vehicle traffic. Other parks use tour reservations to manage access to a building or natural feature. Reservations are typically made available through recreation.gov on a rolling basis. If you need a reservation, we recommend making it well in advance. America the Beautiful—the National Parks and Federal Recreational Lands Pass covers entrance fees and standard amenity (day-use) fees at lands managed by National Park Service, US Fish & Wildlife Service, US Forest Service, Bureau of Land Management, Bureau of Reclamation, and US Army Corps of Engineers. Annual Pass (US residents): $80.00; Non-Resident Annual Pass (Nonresidents): $250.00; Senior Annual Pass (US citizens and residents 62+ years old): $20.00; Senior Lifetime Pass: $80.00; Military, Access, and 4th Grade Passes: Free. Per NPS official visitation statistics (nps.gov/aboutus/visitation-numbers.htm), annual recreation visits rose from 265,796,163 in 1996 to 331,863,358 in 2024.
