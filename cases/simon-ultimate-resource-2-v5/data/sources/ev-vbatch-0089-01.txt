World Bank Open Data API (sourceid 2, World Development Indicators, lastupdated 2026-11-11): World Total Fertility Rate (SP.DYN.TFRT.IN) declined steadily from 2.82 births per woman in 1996 to 2.61 in 2005, 2.49 in 2015, 2.20 in 2023, and 2.19 in 2024. Across regions and income groups over 1996-2024, Sub-Saharan Africa TFR fell from 5.94 to 4.26, Low income countries TFR fell from 6.27 to 4.46, and OECD members TFR fell from 1.85 to 1.48. Over the same 1996-2024 window, World Total Population (SP.POP.TOTL) increased from 5,822,327,903 to 8,140,897,523, and World GDP per capita in constant 2015 US$ (NY.GDP.PCAP.KD) increased from 7,202.74 to 11,894.25.
