PMC13534520 (2026-08-04), Technoeconomic competitiveness of small modular reactors across U.S. power and industrial sectors. Small Modular Reactors (SMRs) are next-generation nuclear reactors proposed for United States deployment, but pathways for early scale-up remain uncertain. Small Modular Reactors (SMRs), with their novel compact designs and largely centralized fabrication, are expected to play a central role in the deployment of new nuclear capacity in the United States (U.S.) energy mix. While dozens of SMR designs exist, only a few are actively undergoing licensing, and none have yet been built for commercial applications in the United States.
