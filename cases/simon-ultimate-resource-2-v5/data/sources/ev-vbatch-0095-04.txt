U.S. EPA Air Quality - National Summary (Last updated on September 30, 2026): The graph below shows that between 1970 and 2025, gross domestic product increased 349 percent, vehicle miles traveled increased 197 percent, energy consumption increased 46 percent, and U.S. population grew by 67 percent. During the same time period, total emissions of the six principal air pollutants dropped by 78 percent. Despite great progress in air quality improvement, approximately 115 million people nationwide lived in counties with pollution levels above the primary NAAQS in 2025. In addition, from 2002 to 2023 emissions of air toxics declined by 50 percent, largely driven by federal and state implementation of stationary and mobile source regulations, and technological advancements from American innovators.
