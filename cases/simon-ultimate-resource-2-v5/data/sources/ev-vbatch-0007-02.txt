World Bank World Development Indicators (API v2, lastupdated 2026-11-11): Carbon dioxide (CO2) emissions excluding LULUCF per capita (t CO2e/capita, EN.GHG.CO2.PC.CE.AR5, 1995-2024): China (CHN) rose continuously from 2.79 (1995) to 9.32 (2024); Vietnam (VNM) rose from 0.48 (1995) to 4.27 (2024); Indonesia (IDN) rose from 1.20 (1995) to 2.87 (2024); India (IND) rose from 0.83 (1995) to 2.17 (2024); Bangladesh (BGD) rose from 0.18 (1995) to 0.72 (2024); Low & middle income (LMY) nearly doubled from 1.87 (1995) to 3.64 (2024); South Asia (SAS) rose from 0.74 (1995) to 1.98 (2024). Only Brazil peaked at 2.73 (2014) and edged down to 2.32 (2024), while Sub-Saharan Africa remained low at 0.70 (2024).
