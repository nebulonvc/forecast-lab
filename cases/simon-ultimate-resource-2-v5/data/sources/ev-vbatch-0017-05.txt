Our World in Data — Energy Institute Statistical Review of World Energy (2026)
Datasets: oil-proved-reserves.csv (World, oil_reserves_m3) and oil-production-by-country.csv (World, oil_production_twh).

Global proved oil reserves increased by 74.3% from 151.29 billion cubic meters in 1996 to 263.71 billion cubic meters in 2021, while global oil production rose by 39.6% from 39,151.24 TWh in 1996 to 54,673.59 TWh in 2025.

Selected annual values (World):
- Proved oil reserves (cubic meters): 1996: 151,286,800,000; 2000: 161,876,930,000; 2005: 203,320,560,000; 2010: 215,759,500,000; 2015: 263,811,920,000; 2020: 264,221,920,000; 2021: 263,711,720,000.
- Oil production (TWh): 1996: 39,151.24; 2000: 41,854.14; 2005: 45,723.04; 2010: 46,225.66; 2015: 50,739.49; 2020: 48,732.34; 2023: 52,583.20; 2024: 52,977.07; 2025: 54,673.59.
