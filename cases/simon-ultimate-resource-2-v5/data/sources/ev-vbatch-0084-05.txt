2025 LAND MARKET SURVEY, REALTORS Land Institute (RLI) and National Association of REALTORS (NAR), Released April 2026. The REALTORS Land Market Survey is a collaboration of the REALTORS Land Institute (RLI) and the National Association of REALTORS (NAR) that started in 2014. There were 453 responses, of which 91.4% reported a land sale in the last 12 month with the margin of error 2.2% at 95% confidence level.
