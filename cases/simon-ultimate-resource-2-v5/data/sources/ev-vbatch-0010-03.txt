Draft Exploitation Regulations are currently under negotiation by the ISA Council. Informal working groups, intersessional working groups and “Friends of the President” have been established by the Council as modalities to advance work on the Draft Exploitation Regulations. During a Council meeting in July 2023, the Council took stock of the progress made and agreed on a timeline. A road map was approved ( ISBA/28/C/24 ) to continue the elaboration of rules, regulations and procedures relating to exploitation with a view to their adoption during the thirtieth session of the Authority (2025). Additionally, it was agreed that a consolidated text would be provided for the continued negotiations by the Council. A consolidated text of the Draft Exploitation Regulations was released on 16 February 2024. The text contained a compilation of work carried out in the informal working groups of the Council. It became the central focus of the negotiations during the twenty-ninth session of the Council in 2024. In July 2024, the Council finished its first reading of the text, covering the preamble to draft regulation 107, with the full participation of ISA Member States and observers, in line with the July 2023 road map. Also, on 26 July 2024, the President of the Council introduced a revised road map. It was endorsed by the Council ( ISBA/29/C/9/Add.1 , Annex III) to guide its work during the thirtieth session in 2025. The road map focuses on advancing the Draft Exploitation Regulations through a Revised Consolidated Text and the associated standards and guidelines. In that regard, on 29 November 2024, the President of the Council issued a Revised Consolidated Text of the Draft Exploitation Regulations ( ISBA/30/C/CRP.1 ) and a Revised Suspense Document ( ISBA/30/C/CRP.2 ).
