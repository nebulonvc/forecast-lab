NBER Working Paper No. 21243, Issued in June 2015 (publication_date: 2015-06-08), Authors: Geetesh Bhardwaj, Gary Gorton, and K. Geert Rouwenhorst, Title: Facts and Fantasies about Commodity Futures Ten Years Later. Abstract: Gorton and Rouwenhorst (2006) examined commodity futures returns over the period July 1959 to December 2004 based on an equally-weighted index. They found that fully collateralized commodity futures had historically offered the same return and Sharpe ratio as U.S. equities, but were negatively correlated with the return on stocks and bonds. Reviewing these results ten years later, we find that our conclusions largely hold up out-of-sample. The in- and out-of-sample average commodity risk premiums are not significantly different, nor is the cross-sectional relationship between average returns and the basis.
