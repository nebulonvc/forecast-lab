U.S. Geological Survey, Mineral Commodity Summaries, January 2025 — BAUXITE AND ALUMINA (pages 42-43). Salient Statistics—United States: Bauxite — Price, average unit value of imports, free alongside ship (f.a.s.), dollars per metric ton: 2020: 30; 2021: 31; 2022: 32; 2023: 31; 2024e: 30. Events, Trends, and Issues: The average prices, f.a.s., for U.S. imports for consumption of crude dry bauxite and metallurgical-grade alumina during the first 8 months of 2024 were $30 per ton and $560 per ton, 5% less and 12% more than those in the same period in 2023, respectively. Import Sources (2020–23): Bauxite: Jamaica, 67%; Turkey, 9%; Guyana, 8%; Australia, 6%; and other, 10%.
