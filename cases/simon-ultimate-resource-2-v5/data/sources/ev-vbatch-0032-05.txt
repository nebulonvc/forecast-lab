City of Santa Barbara Water Commission Staff Report (October 20, 2022), Item 6a: Recycled Water Market Assessment Report (Final, September 2022):
The RWMA also compared the cost of recycled water to Direct Potable Reuse (DPR) and water produced at the City's Charles E. Meyer Desalination Plant (CMDP). The estimated cost of DPR in 2022 dollars was $2,057 per acre-foot (AF). In comparison, the estimated cost for ocean desalination from CMDP is currently $3,126 per AF, in 2022 dollars based on a production rate of 3,125 AFY. The cost desalinated water is expected to decrease due to economies of scale to approximately $2,450 per AF in 2022 dollars if production is expanded to 5,000 AFY.
Desalinated Water: The City's Charles E. Meyer desalination facility was constructed as a drought supply in the late 1980s. During the recent drought, the facility was reactivated and can produce up to 3,125 AFY. A policy change in the City's 2020 Enhanced Urban Water Management Plan changed desalinated water from a drought supply to a regular component of the City's water supply portfolio.
