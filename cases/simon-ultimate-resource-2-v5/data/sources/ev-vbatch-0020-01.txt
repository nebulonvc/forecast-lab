The FAO Food Price Index (FFPI) is a measure of the monthly change in international prices of a basket of food commodities. It consists of the average of five commodity group price indices weighted by the average export shares of each of the groups over 2014-2016. Release date: 06/11/2026. From the official dataset ffpi-data-2026-11.xlsx (sheet Annual_Real, 2014-2016=100) and food_price_index_nominal_real.xlsx (FFPI_Historical): Annual Real FAO Food Price Index values are 1990: 76.63; 1995: 83.62; 1996: 86.35 (Meat 93.59, Dairy 68.37, Cereals 92.82, Oils 77.60, Sugar 82.10); 1997: 82.39; 1998: 78.93; 1999: 68.76; 2000: 67.48; 2005: 77.17; 2006: 81.05; 2007: 99.13; 2008: 114.55; 2011: 118.73; 2015: 95.14; 2018: 94.05; 2019: 95.48; 2020: 100.20; 2021: 114.99; 2022: 123.10; 2023: 109.19; 2024: 106.03; 2025: 109.61; 2026 (Jan-Oct average): 112.49 (Meat 110.53, Dairy 102.54, Cereals 97.53, Oils 162.64, Sugar 84.45). Between 1996 and 2026, the FAO Real Food Price Index increased by 30.3 percent and stayed above its 1996 level in every single year from 2007 to 2026.
