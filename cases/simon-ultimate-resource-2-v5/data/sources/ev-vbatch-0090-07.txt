World Bank World Development Indicators (lastupdated: 2026-11-11), indicator GB.XPD.RSDV.GD.ZS (Research and development expenditure, % of GDP), 1996-2023. World Bank R&D expenditure as % of GDP rose from 1.965% (1996) to 2.597% (2023) globally, from 2.105% to 2.934% across OECD economies, and from 2.450% to 3.447% in the United States. Across the 1996-2023 span, global R&D intensity expanded by +32.2%, OECD by +39.4%, and the United States by +40.7%, despite minor interim dips such as the 2007 global trough of 1.925%.
