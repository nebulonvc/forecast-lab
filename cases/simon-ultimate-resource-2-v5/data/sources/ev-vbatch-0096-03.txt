The FAO Food Price Index (FFPI) is a measure of the monthly change in international prices of a basket of food commodities. It consists of the average of five commodity group price indices weighted by the average export shares of each of the groups over 2014-2016. From official FAO dataset food_price_index_nominal_real.xlsx (sheet FFPI_Historical, 2014-2016=100, released November 2026): In 1996, the FAO Nominal Food Price Index averaged 77.82 and the Real (MUV-deflated) Food Price Index averaged 86.35. In 2022, Nominal was 144.51 and Real was 123.10. In 2023, Nominal was 124.52 and Real was 109.19. In 2024, Nominal was 121.98 and Real was 106.03. In 2025, Nominal was 127.19 and Real was 109.61 (+26.9% vs 1996). In 2026 (year-to-date through October 2026), Nominal averaged 130.54 and Real averaged 112.49 (+30.3% vs 1996).
