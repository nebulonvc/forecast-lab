Farmland Values and Competitiveness in the United States and Brazil | farmdoc daily (16): 173, Department of Agricultural and Consumer Economics, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, September 25, 2026. Strong farm profits, supported by periods of high commodity prices, have contributed to substantial increases in farmland values in both countries over the past two decades. This article compares farmland values in the United States and Brazil from 2002 to 2025, using data from Iowa and Mato Grosso - leading soybean- and corn-producing states in their respective countries. In Iowa, the average farmland value rose from $2,083 per acre in 2002 to $11,549 in 2025, with particularly strong increases of 72% between 2010 and 2013 and 57% between 2020 and 2023. Farmland is also viewed as a stable investment amid broader economic and geopolitical uncertainty (Chandio, 2025). Farmland values increased in both Iowa and Mato Grosso between 2002 and 2025, supported by strong farm profits during periods of high commodity prices.
