World Bank Open Data API Indicator AG.YLD.CREL.KG (Cereal yield, kg per hectare, 1996-2024, FAO statistical series): Ethiopia (ETH) cereal yield rose from 1,213.1 kg/ha in 1996 to 1,760.3 kg/ha in 2010 and 2,631.6 kg/ha in 2024 (+116.9%). Sub-Saharan Africa (SSF) rose from 1,128.6 kg/ha in 1996 to 1,453.4 kg/ha in 2024 (+28.8%). Rwanda (RWA) rose from 1,263.0 kg/ha in 1996 to 1,965.8 kg/ha in 2010 and 1,784.0 kg/ha in 2024 (+41.3%). Malawi (MWI) rose from 1,390.6 kg/ha in 1996 to 1,906.9 kg/ha in 2010 and 1,877.5 kg/ha in 2024 (+35.0%). Zimbabwe (ZWE) fell from 1,258.7 kg/ha in 1996 to 733.4 kg/ha in 2010 and 1,069.8 kg/ha in 2024 (-15.0%).
