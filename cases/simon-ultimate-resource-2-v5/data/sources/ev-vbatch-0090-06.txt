U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) via FRED, series VAPGDPI (Value Added by Industry: Information as a Percentage of GDP, Quarterly) and VAPGDPPBS (Value Added by Industry: Professional and Business Services as a Percentage of GDP, Quarterly), 2005-01-01 through 2026-04-01 (86 quarters). US BEA Value Added by Industry: Information as a Percentage of GDP (VAPGDPI) rose from 5.0% in 2005 Q1 to 5.9% in 2026 Q2, while Professional and Business Services (VAPGDPPBS) rose from 11.1% to 12.7% (peaking at 12.9% in 2023-2025 Q1). Quarterly trajectory of VAPGDPI shows cyclical fluctuations rather than strict quarterly monotonicity: 5.0% (2005 Q1), 4.8% (2005 Q4), 5.0% (2010 Q1), 4.9% (2015 Q1), 5.7% (2020 Q2), 5.3% (2023 Q1), 5.6% (2024 Q1), 5.8% (2025 Q4), and 5.9% (2026 Q2).
