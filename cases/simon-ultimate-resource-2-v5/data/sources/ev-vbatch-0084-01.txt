Major Land Uses | Economic Research Service, U.S. Department of Agriculture (Updated: 8/14/2026). The Major Land Uses (MLU) data product classifies land use into seven major categories for the entire United States: total land, cropland, grassland pasture and range, forest-use land, special use land, urban land and miscellaneous land. Data from all 16 Major Land Uses reports have been combined into a set of files showing major land use estimates by region and State from 1945 to 2022. Data for total cropland used for crops are available annually in summary table 3, showing cropland use estimates from 1910 to 2025.
