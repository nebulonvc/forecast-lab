PubMed PMID 42893690 (Natural Product Research, published 2026-10-18): Taxus wallichiana zucc. (Himalayan yew): phytochemistry, therapeutic potential, biotechnology, and conservation perspectives. Advances in tissue culture, metabolic engineering, and endophytic fungal research offer promising alternatives for taxoid production, potentially reducing destructive bark harvesting. Despite its medicinal and pharmaceutical significance, Taxus wallichiana faces serious threats from overexploitation, poor regeneration, habitat degradation, and grazing pressure. Conservation approaches include ex situ propagation, genetic diversity assessment, habitat restoration, and sustainable utilisation of renewable foliage and endophyte-derived metabolites.
