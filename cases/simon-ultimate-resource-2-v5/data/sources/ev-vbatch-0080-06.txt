Planetary boundaries - Stockholm Resilience Centre (updated 2026, citing Richardson et al. 2023 and Sakschewski and Caesar et al. 2026). The planetary boundaries framework highlights nine global change processes where human activities affect Earth system functioning. All boundaries are now quantitatively assessed in the third major update to the framework, published in 2023. Six boundaries were transgressed and pressure was increasing on all boundary processes except ozone depletion. It stated that society’s activities have pushed climate change, biodiversity loss, shifts in nutrient cycles (nitrogen and phosphorus), and land use beyond the boundaries into unprecedented territory. In the 2026 update to the Planetary boundaries, scientists assess for the first time that Ocean Acidification is the seventh transgressed Planetary Boundary.
