World Bank Open Data (lastupdated: 2026-11-11), Indicator EG.ELC.NUCL.ZS: Electricity production from nuclear sources (% of total), World (WLD). Historical annual observations from 1996 to 2024 show a sustained decline in nuclear power's share of global electricity generation: 1996 was 17.63%, 1997 was 17.09%, 1998 was 17.03%, falling to 9.81% in 2021, 9.17% in 2022, 9.07% in 2023, and 8.95% in 2024. (Note: preliminary 2025 aggregate value of 15.16% reflects incomplete country reporting and is excluded from the continuous global trend.)
