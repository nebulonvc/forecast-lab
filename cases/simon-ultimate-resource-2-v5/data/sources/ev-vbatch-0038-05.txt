Global nuclear electricity generation reaches record high for second year running. Updated Monday, 7 September 2026. Nuclear reactors produced more electricity than ever before in 2025, breaking the record set in 2024, according to the latest edition of the World Nuclear Outlook Report, published today by World Nuclear Association. Performance: Global nuclear generation reached a record 2702 TWh in 2025 and the industry maintained excellent performance with an average capacity factor of 83.7%.
