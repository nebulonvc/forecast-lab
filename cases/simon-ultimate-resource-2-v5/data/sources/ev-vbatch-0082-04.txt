Land Use, Land Value & Tenure - Farmland Value. Economic Research Service, U.S. Department of Agriculture. Updated: 9/23/2026. Farm real estate (land and structures) accounted for $3.60 trillion (83.0 percent) of the total value of U.S. farm assets in 2026. Following a period of stabilization in farmland values from 2014 to 2020, farmland values began to appreciate in 2021, even after adjusting for inflation. Inflation adjusted farmland values stabilized in 2026. The value of U.S. farmland averaged $4,500 per acre in 2026, an increase of 3.4 percent over 2025 values, or 0.4 percent when adjusted for inflation. Over the previous 5-year period (2020 to 2025), the compound annualized growth rate (CAGR) was 6.8 percent, or 2.6 percent after adjusting for inflation.
