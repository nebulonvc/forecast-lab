Conventional gas: The most recent estimates of conventional gas reserves come from WEC (1998) for the end of 1996 and BP (1998) for the end of 1998. WEC gives total reserves as 177 Tm3 (6,534 exajoules) at the end of 1996, 147 Tm3 (5,450 exajoules) of which were proven recoverable reserves (table 5.3). The rest were additional recoverable reserves. The International Gas Union (IGU, 2000) reports total potentially recoverable reserves as high as 502 Tm3 (18,390 exajoules). Over the past 10 years reserve additions averaged 3.7 Tm3 (134 exajoules) a year, much higher than the 1997 production of 2.2 Tm3.
