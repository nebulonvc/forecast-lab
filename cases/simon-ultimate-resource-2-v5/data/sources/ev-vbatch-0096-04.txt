Levelized cost of energy (LCOE) for World from IRENA (2026), IRENA (2025), and IRENA (2024) processed by Our World in Data (constant USD per kWh). Global onshore wind LCOE declined from $0.2127/kWh in 1996 and $0.1839/kWh in 2000 to $0.1136/kWh in 2010, $0.0409/kWh in 2020, $0.0331/kWh in 2023, and $0.0343/kWh in 2024 (-83.9% from 1996 to 2024). Global utility-scale solar photovoltaic LCOE declined from $0.4077/kWh in 2010 to $0.1337/kWh in 2015, $0.0608/kWh in 2020, $0.0426/kWh in 2023, and $0.0430/kWh in 2024 (-89.5% from 2010 to 2024). Global offshore wind LCOE declined from $0.1904/kWh in 2000 and $0.2084/kWh in 2010 to $0.0807/kWh in 2024.
