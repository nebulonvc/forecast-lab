Following several years of preparation, the KAZA Elephant Survey (2022) commenced on 22 August 2022 and ran until 28 October 2022. The results are presented in a series of reports - An aerial survey of elephants and other large herbivores in the Kavango Zambezi Transfrontier Conservation Area, Volume I: Results and technical report and Volume II - Stratum Reports.
