U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) via Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED), series Y001RC1A027NBEA (Private Fixed Investment: Intellectual Property Products, Annual) and GDPA (Gross Domestic Product, Annual), 1996-2025. US Private Fixed Investment in Intellectual Property Products as a share of Nominal GDP rose from 3.14% in 1996 ($253.3B / $8,073.1B) to 5.60% in 2025 ($1,727.1B / $30,861.3B), increasing in 23 of 29 year-over-year intervals (+2.46 percentage points, or +78% relative increase). Selected annual milestones: 1996=3.14%, 2000=4.01%, 2004=3.58%, 2009=3.90%, 2010=3.84%, 2015=4.26%, 2020=5.32%, 2022=5.46%, 2023=5.45%, 2024=5.49%, 2025=5.60%. Cyclical year-over-year dips occurred in 6 of the 29 years: 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004 (post-dot-com bust), 2010, and 2023.
