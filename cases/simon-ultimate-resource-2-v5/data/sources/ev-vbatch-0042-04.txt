U.S. Energy Information Administration, Short-Term Energy Outlook Real and Nominal Prices, Release Date: November 10, 2026. Real Petroleum Prices are computed by dividing the nominal price in a given month by the ratio of the Consumer Price Index (CPI) in that month to the CPI in some base period. Consequently, all real prices are expressed in current dollars (constant November 2026 dollars) and any current month price may be compared directly with any past or projected real prices.
