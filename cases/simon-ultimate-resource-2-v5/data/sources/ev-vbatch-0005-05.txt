World Bank World Development Indicators (WDI) API (lastupdated: 2026-11-11), 1996-2025 global living standards and population series: World total population (SP.POP.TOTL) increased from 5,822,327,903 in 1996 to 8,215,424,893 in 2025 (+41.1%). World GDP per capita, PPP (constant 2021 international $, NY.GDP.PCAP.PP.KD) rose from $11,691.71 in 1996 to $21,913.51 in 2025 (+87.4%), with a temporary COVID-19 dip from $19,551.09 (2019) to $18,812.49 (2020) before rebounding to $19,875.57 (2021). World GDP per capita in constant 2015 US$ (NY.GDP.PCAP.KD) rose from $7,202.74 in 1996 to $12,130.66 in 2025 (+68.4%). World life expectancy at birth (SP.DYN.LE00.IN) rose from 66.52 years in 1996 to 73.48 years in 2024, despite a temporary COVID-19 decline from 72.87 (2019) to 71.22 (2021). World extreme poverty headcount ratio (SI.POV.DDAY) declined from 38.1% in 1996 to 10.1% in 2024. However, Low income countries (LIC/XM) real GDP per capita (constant 2015 US$) stagnated between 2010 ($778.45) and 2025 ($719.74), and PPP per capita stayed near $2,461.60 (2010) to $2,481.01 (2025).
