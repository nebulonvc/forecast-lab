The OECD report, Shrinking Smartly and Sustainably: Strategies for Action , reveals how regions can adapt to shrinking and ageing populations while protecting opportunity and quality of life. The OECD’s report, Shrinking Smartly and Sustainably: Strategies for Action , offers a comprehensive framework to help regions and cities respond to long-term population decline and ageing in ways that are economically viable and socially inclusive. Since 2001, over a third of remote regions in OECD countries have experienced population loss, and more than one in five urban areas have also seen declines. By 2040, the ratio of working-age people to those aged over 80 is projected to fall from 13:1 to just 7:1. These demographic shifts pose serious challenges for public services, infrastructure, housing, and community vitality. The report outlines seven strategic recommendations: Recognise demographic realities and plan ahead using evidence-based policies. Tailor responses to regional demographic profiles. Align policies with appropriate territorial scales. Adapt spatial planning and housing to demographic change. Foster age-friendly environments for inclusive communities. Strengthen coordination across sectors and government levels. Enhance fiscal resilience by doing more with less. Drawing on international case studies and policy dialogue, the OECD encourages governments to treat demographic change not as a crisis, but as a catalyst for innovation and sustainability.
