Ember, Global Electricity Review 2026 (published 21 Apr 2026), Chapter 3: Electricity demand and supply trends, Section 3.3 Solar. Solar met 75% of global electricity demand growth in 2025. As of 2025, 50 countries generate more than a tenth of their electricity from solar, up from just 15 in 2020. Global solar generation overtook wind for the first time in 2025, reaching a new record high of 2,778 TWh. Solar’s output in 2025 was almost 11 times higher than in 2015 (256 TWh). In the same period, its share rose from 1.1% to 8.7%. Over the past decade, solar generation has doubled roughly every three years. Solar accounted for 68% of all renewable capacity additions from 2019 to 2024. This is partly due to the sharp drop in solar panel prices, which fell by 90% between 2015 and 2024.
