World Bank World Development Indicators (WDI) official API dataset (lastupdated 2026-11-11), cross-country comparison of total population (SP.POP.TOTL) and real GDP per capita in constant 2021 international dollars PPP (NY.GDP.PCAP.PP.KD) and constant 2015 USD (NY.GDP.PCAP.KD) over 1996-2024. Among less-developed economies (World Bank UMC+LMC+LIC, N=120 for PPP, N=122 for constant 2015 USD): population growth CAGR (1996-2024) is negatively correlated with both 2024 GDP per capita level (r = -0.553 PPP; r = -0.502 constant 2015 USD) and 1996-2024 GDP per capita CAGR (r = -0.298 PPP; r = -0.270 constant 2015 USD). Less-developed countries with stationary or slow population growth (<0.5%/yr, n=19 PPP) had a median 2024 GDP per capita PPP of $18,714 and median annual per capita growth of +2.68%/yr, compared with $7,687 and +1.67%/yr for growing-population developing countries (>=0.5%/yr, n=101 PPP). Among more-developed economies (World Bank High-Income HIC, N=66 for PPP, N=68 for constant 2015 USD): growing-population HICs (>=0.5%/yr, n=37 PPP) had a higher median 2024 GDP per capita PPP of $62,398 (constant 2015 USD $41,605) versus $42,829 ($21,495) for stationary/slow HICs (<0.5%/yr, n=29 PPP; level r = +0.363 PPP), whereas stationary/slow HICs recorded higher median 1996-2024 per capita GDP growth (+2.21%/yr vs +1.28%/yr; growth r = -0.418 PPP).
