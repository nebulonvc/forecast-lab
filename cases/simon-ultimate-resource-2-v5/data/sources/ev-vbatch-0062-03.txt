World Elephant Day: The Journey of 54 Jumbos. The literal elephant in the room on World Elephant Day is this: Kenya’s elephant population has grown to over 42,000 individuals in 2026 from just 16,000 in 1989. Anyone who has followed this journey knows the success of Kenya’s elephant conservation journey is fairly close to Number 1 of the one thousand and one reasons to celebrate, but that does not mean the work is anywhere near done. Today, therefore, is a mark on the win column. It is a day to mentally high-five our conservation partners: rangers, communities, scientists, and the policymakers who have made this recovery possible. In October 2024, KWS translocated 54 elephants from Mwea National Reserve to Aberdare National Park, giving these jumbos a new home while helping mitigate human-wildlife conflict.
