World Bank Open Data (WDI / FAOSTAT) global series for World (WLD), 1996 to 2023-2024: Cereal yield (AG.YLD.CREL.KG) increased from 2,922.40 kg per hectare in 1996 to 4,229.28 kg per hectare in 2023 (+44.7%). Cereal production (AG.PRD.CREL.MT) increased from 2,041,810,222 metric tons in 1996 to 3,130,499,027 metric tons in 2024 (+53.3%). Food production index (AG.PRD.FOOD.XD, 2014-2016=100) rose from 63.78 in 1996 to 115.90 in 2024 (+81.7%). Over the same period, global population (SP.POP.TOTL) grew from 5,822,327,903 in 1996 to 8,140,897,523 in 2024 (+39.8%). By contrast, arable land as a percentage of land area (AG.LND.ARBL.ZS) changed only marginally from 10.56% in 1996 to 10.67% in 2023 (+1.0%), and land under cereal production (AG.LND.CREL.HA) expanded modestly from 698,704,868 hectares in 1996 to 744,312,437 hectares in 2024 (+6.5%).
