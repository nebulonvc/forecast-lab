The FAO Food Price Index (FFPI) is a measure of the monthly change in international prices of a basket of food commodities. It consists of the average of five commodity group price indices weighted by the average export shares of each of the groups over 2014-2016.

FAO Food Price Index (FFPI
averaged 133.9 points in October 2026, down 1.6 points (1.2 percent) from its revised September level. Price indices for cereals, dairy and meat products decreased from the previous month, while the vegetable oil price index increased only marginally and the sugar price index rose markedly. Compared to a year earlier, the FFPI stood 7.5 points (5.9 percent) higher but remained 26.3 points (16.4 percent) below its March 2022 peak.
