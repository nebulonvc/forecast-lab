World Bank World Development Indicators (WDI) official API dataset (lastupdated 2026-11-11) for 1996-2025. Global total population (SP.POP.TOTL) increased from 5,822,327,903 in 1996 to 8,215,424,893 in 2025 (+41.1%). Global average population density (EN.POP.DNST) increased from 45.83 people per sq. km in 1996 to 62.14 people per sq. km in 2023. Global rural population (SP.RUR.TOTL, national definitions) increased from 3,213,684,390 in 1996 to 3,464,845,933 in 2025 (+7.8%), even as the rural share fell from 55.20% to 42.17%. In High-Income countries (HIC), rural population decreased by 7.5% from 304.0 million in 1996 to 281.2 million in 2025. Weighted by national land area (AG.LND.TOTL.K2) across 207 countries covering 129.76 million sq. km: 83 countries with decreasing rural population from 1996 to 2025 account for 54.25 million sq. km (41.8% of covered land, including Russia -11.0%, China -44.1%, Brazil -29.4%, Argentina -14.9%), while 124 countries with increasing rural population account for 75.51 million sq. km (58.2% of covered land, including India +31.2%, DRC +103.8%, Sudan +96.4%, USA +12.5%, Canada +9.0%, Australia +26.1%).
