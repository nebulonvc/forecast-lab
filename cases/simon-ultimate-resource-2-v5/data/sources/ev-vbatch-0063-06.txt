What Innovations Are Shaping Global Copper Mining and Processing Today? — ProMineTech. Hydrometallurgical processing, such as solvent extraction and electrowinning (SX-EW), is gaining traction for low-grade ores. These techniques require less energy and produce fewer carbon emissions than traditional smelting methods. Bioleaching/Biomining: Microorganisms are being used to extract copper from ores in an eco-friendly way. This technology enables extraction from low-grade deposits previously considered uneconomical. Ore Sorting and Pre-Concentration : Technologies such as sensor-based ore sorting concentrate higher-grade copper ore more efficiently, reducing energy costs during downstream processing. IoT and Data Analytics : Internet of Things (IoT) sensors monitor every stage of mining and processing, enabling better operational oversight, reduced energy consumption, and improved yield extraction. Copper Recycling : Given copper’s 100% recyclability without loss in quality, emphasis on secondary copper production (from scrap material) is growing.
