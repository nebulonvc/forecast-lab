This page was first published in July 2020. We revised this page in August 2026 to reflect the change in how primary energy is measured. The energy system has transformed dramatically since the Industrial Revolution. It is based on historical estimates of primary energy consumption from Vaclav Smil, combined with updated figures from the Energy Institute Statistical Review of World Energy. We see that global energy consumption has increased nearly every year for more than half a century. The exceptions to this are in the early 1980s, 2009 following the financial crisis, and 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic. Global energy consumption continues to grow, but it does seem to be slowing — averaging around 1% to 2% per year.
