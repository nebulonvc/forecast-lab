NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory (GML) globally averaged marine surface annual mean carbon dioxide (CO2) mole fraction in dry air (parts per million, ppm), 1996-2025: 1996: 361.93 ppm (uncertainty 0.04); 2022: 417.08 ppm (uncertainty 0.09); 2023: 419.35 ppm (uncertainty 0.10); 2024: 422.79 ppm (uncertainty 0.09); 2025: 425.63 ppm (uncertainty 0.05). From 1996 to 2025, global annual mean atmospheric CO2 concentration rose monotonically by 63.70 ppm (+17.6%), reaching a new historical high in 2025.
