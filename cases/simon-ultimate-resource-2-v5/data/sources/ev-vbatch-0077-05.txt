World Bank Open Data (lastupdated: 2026-11-11), World (WLD) R&D inputs and patent output indicators from 1996 to 2024. Research and development expenditure (% of GDP, GB.XPD.RSDV.GD.ZS) increased from 1.96% in 1996 (1.96% in 1997, 1.97% in 1998) to 2.26% in 2019, 2.46% in 2020, 2.52% in 2021, 2.57% in 2022, and 2.60% in 2023. Researchers in R&D per million people (SP.POP.SCIE.RD.P6) rose from 1080.9 in 2000 to 1195.0 in 2005, 1267.1 in 2010, 1380.8 in 2015, 1523.8 in 2018, and 1651.6 in 2020. Patent applications by residents (IP.PAT.RESD) grew from 708,400 in 1996 to 2,330,000 in 2020, 2,530,000 in 2023, and 2,702,600 in 2024, while patent applications by nonresidents (IP.PAT.NRES) grew from 379,700 in 1996 to 1,022,400 in 2024, bringing total global patent applications from 1,088,100 in 1996 to 3,725,000 in 2024.
